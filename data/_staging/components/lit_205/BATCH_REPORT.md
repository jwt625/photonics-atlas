Fig. 2(c) / (d) per-wafer markers were not read (dozens of overlapping log-axis markers; the text gives no per-wafer values); an auditor may want per-wafer minima as figure reads. Q_i basis derived (Q = nu / kappa_0, regime determined from the data, D403 not triggered); propagation loss author_estimate (n_g not stated, D76) after the dB/m -> dB/cm conversion (D256a).
- (d) pfeiffer2018 wafer-3 2 um row carries only the Fig. 2(b) most-probable linewidth (no statistic: the mode of a fitted Burr distribution is none of mean / median / typical; definition in condition).

Table II responsivities entered at the target value with the printed bias; P_ON / P_SB entered as power_consumption_w (derived, nW -> W); (200, 200) standby 0.26 nW (table) vs 0.28 nW (text) noted.
