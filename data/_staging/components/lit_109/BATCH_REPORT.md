## Judgment calls

1. chen2016a 400 nm Ge device (separate row, 4 metrics from Fig. 9 labels / readings). The 400 nm design is that of
   the authors' earlier ref. [11] (20 GHz printed there, p.2), but Fig. 9 carries no citation and is presented as this
   paper's experimental comparison (19 GHz at -1 V, 29 GHz at -2 V). Entered with a note; if the coordinator reads it
   as reproduced earlier data, delete the row (D259 / D408).
2. chen2016a printed figure labels (18, 20, 7 GHz; 9.8 fF; 184 ohm) entered as `extracted_from_figure` with "no
   reading uncertainty (label)" (merged precedent). Wafer-scale "> 50 GHz" rows use `statistic: wafer_map` + gt.
3. lischke2015 Table 1 internal responsivities: `author_estimate` (GC loss of unstated origin) with qualifier `ge`
   (the paper calls them worst-case values). Bandwidth at -1 V entered twice: 67 GHz gt `measured` (instrument limit)
   and the authors' "at least 70 GHz" as `author_estimate` (D435). Reverse biases entered negative (Table 1 prints
   them negative; the text prints magnitudes).
4. lischke2015 grating coupler row (2.7 dB best, `author_estimate`, method unspecified): the only coupler value is a
   loss-budget term of unstated origin.
5. chen2019 responsivity 0.07 A/W entered on all three diameters: the paper states it for "the devices" without the
   diameter of the measured device. If the coordinator prefers D159, delete the three rows and keep the note.
6. chen2019 bandwidth conflict: 25 GHz at -3 V (abstract, p.4, conclusion) vs "highest 25 GHz at -4 V" (p.3) and the
   Fig. 6 inset (about 24 GHz at -3 V). Both printed values entered with the conflict in notes (D417).
7. smith2023 `q_intrinsic` and ring `prop_loss_db_per_cm` as `derived` (Eq. 4 with the coupling regime inferred from
   the width / gap trends of the paper's own data, Figs. 5, 7, 8). D403 allows `derived` only when the regime is
   determined from the data; the TM over-coupling is an argued inference (the authors print the under-coupled
   alternative). If the coordinator reads D403 strictly, switch these 9 rows to `author_estimate`.
8. smith2023 delay-line losses: Fig. 3(b) printed labels, `extracted_from_figure`, mean over 3 chips; 450 / 520 nm by
   vertical-scatter imaging (method `direct`), 630-850 nm cutback.
9. hausmann2014: one `resonator.ring` row for the ring family (R 20-30 um, D165), because only the Fig. 1(c) ring is
   identified (R 20 um) and the record Q 1.14e6 / 1.35e6 / 0.34 dB/cm are not tied to a device; a separate
   `frequency_converter.other` row for the OPO (D396, mckenna2022 precedent). Pump threshold and conversion efficiency
   `author_estimate` (powers "estimated in the waveguide"). Platform `other` (diamond on SiO2). Dimensions printed in
   the SEM caption kept `design_target` (measurement not stated).
10. liu2024 Euler and Bezier bends entered as `row_kind: design` rows (simulation-only comparison, Fig. 2(b)). Bend
    loss < 0.05 dB per FFC entered `derived` (cascade / 40); `min_bend_radius_um` holds the equivalent radius Reff
    (noted). Ring `q_intrinsic`, loss and coupling ratio `author_estimate` (D403).
11. selvaraja2014 fab org_imec from the acknowledgement that the imec p-line fabricated the wafers (D448: a named
    processing site). Within-wafer drop-wavelength spread 6.1 nm (3-sigma) with `statistic: wafer_map`; channel-spacing
    error 0.4 nm with `statistic: std`.
