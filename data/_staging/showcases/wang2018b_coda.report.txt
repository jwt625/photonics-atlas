3. WHAT EACH NUMBER RESTS ON
T-rail chapter (all [K]):
- Segment parameters (g, h, s, t, r, c) = (5, 6, 2, 6, 45, 5) um: stated, Fig. 1 caption p.2; the same set without g is
  repeated in Sec. II p.3 ("(h, s, t, r, c) = (6, 2, 6, 45, 5) µm as defined in Fig. 1"). Letter meanings read from
  Fig. 1(a) top view and Fig. 1(e) cross-section (basis figure):
    g = gap between facing caps across the waveguide (Fig. 1e; also "electrode gap g = 5 µm", p.4);
    h = stem length = open band between the channel body and the cap (Fig. 1a vertical arrows; Fig. 1e, body to cap);
    s = cap width across the line (Fig. 1a near the lower arm; Fig. 1e, small Au block);
    t = stem width along the line (Fig. 1a, top arrows over a stem);
    r = cap length along the line (Fig. 1a, bottom arrows);
    c = slit between neighbouring caps (Fig. 1a).
  Topology from the 600 dpi render of Fig. 1(a) (artistic, "not to scale"): one stem centred on each cap; slits midway
  between stems; facing T rows mirror-aligned across each gap (also seen in the SEM, Fig. 1b); T rows on both edges of
  both gaps (centre strip both sides, outer strips inner side only). Drawn proportions in Fig. 1(a) are not to scale:
  use the caption values.
- Period 50 um = r + c: derived. Channel-to-channel spacing 21 um = g + 2(s + h): derived, consistent with Fig. 1(e).
- Signal width 100 um: stated in Table I (p.3) for the simulated segmented design on quartz; the fabricated width is not
  separately stated.
- Stack (Sec. III p.3-4): NanoLN 600 nm x-cut film on 500 um quartz; 350 nm etched (slab 250 nm derived); 800 nm gold
  lift-off; 1 um SiO2 by chemical vapour deposition. Quartz permittivity about 4.5, loss tangent <10^-4 (p.3).
- RF loss at 50 GHz 2 dB/cm (segmented, LN on quartz) vs 7 dB/cm (regular, LN on silicon), 10 mm electrodes (Fig. 2
  caption), gold 800 nm, g = 5 um (p.4); fits 0.26 vs 0.69 dB cm^-1 GHz^-1/2 (p.4, Fig. 2). Note the comparison mixes
  substrates (as printed).
- RF phase index 2.23 measured (p.4); plain electrodes on quartz would give about 1.8 (p.3, statement).
- 20 mm: DC V_pi 1.35 V, V_pi at 1 GHz 1.3 V (Bessel method), 1.8 dB roll-off at 50 GHz, V_pi(50 GHz) 1.6 V (stated),
  ER about 20 dB, on-chip loss <1 dB (p.4). 10 mm: V_pi(1 GHz) 2.3 V, 0.8 dB at 50 GHz (p.4).
- "Not previously implemented [on LN] to improve RF performances" (p.3) is the authors' claim; the chapter line does not
  say "first".
- Coincidence worth knowing, not a claim in the story: the 2018 main device and the Kharel headline device are both
  20 mm; M. Zhang is an author of both papers (author lists).
Differential chapter (all [A] unless noted):
- Three-conductor travelling-wave electrode, 7.5 mm, 50 ohm design, "periodic capacitive loading", integrated NiCr
  terminations; 350 nm LN on silicon, commercial foundry MPW, C-band I/Q, only the I channel characterized (p.1 Sec. 2).
- Drive: driver output S̅ S S S̅ to four pads, the two inner pads connected to the central electrode, "no ground planes
  in between the signal lines" (p.1); Fig. 1(a) labels the three strips -, +, - with one arm in each gap (p.2). The
  electrode is "identical to the one of a modulator to be driven in a standard GSG fashion" apart from the input (p.1).
  This is the same scheme as US 12,619,109 FIG. 1E ("Differential CPW electrode, push-pull differential", S̅ S S̅).
- "The differential signal doubles the available voltage between the electrodes compared with single-ended drive" (p.2):
  basis of the 2x field arrows in the action.
- V_pi 3.26 V measured stand-alone; V_pi L 1.1 V cm "referenced to the single-ended signal" (p.1). Halving 3.26 V x
  0.75 cm gives 1.22 V cm, not 1.1; the paper gives no derivation (the atlas row records the same caveat). Shown with
  that note, not used on canvas.
- Modulator EO 3-dB bandwidth >67 GHz (equipment limit, Fig. 1b); assembly about 60 GHz at mid gain, terminations 35
  instead of 50 ohm (p.2, Fig. 1d). 140 GBd PAM-8, 347 Gbit/s net, 1.4 pJ/bit (p.3). Driver: 130 nm SiGe BiCMOS, 20 dB,
  605 mW per channel, simulated max differential swing 2.8 V per output pair into 50 ohm, >90 GHz (p.2).
Physics check (from [P] drawings and [A] text):
- Plain GSSG (dual coplanar strips) with one arm in each outer gap: FIG. 1C "same phase shift in both arms" (crossed
  out). SGS: FIG. 1D same. G S̅ G S G (dual CPW, arms in the first gap of each CPW): FIG. 1B push-pull, "2x the
  electrical power vs Case 1". S̅ S S̅: FIG. 1E push-pull. [A] p.1: GS̅GSG and GS̅SG "are not inherently compatible"
  with the three-conductor electrode; adaptations "often result in the loss of the push-pull effect".
  Simple check: x-cut, field along crystal z across the gaps. G(0) S(+v) S̅(-v) G(0): left gap field points from S to
  G, right gap from G to S̅: same direction in both arms, so both phases move together: no intensity modulation.

  split layouts work could not be read (Chen 2025: 403) or print no layout (Zhou 2026). The owner's literal ask
  ("G-S-G-S-G or GSSG") is therefore not met; the S̅ S S̅ scheme is shown because its conductor roles are printed.
- Differential-device segment sizes and cross-section (not printed by [A]); T-rail ridge width / sidewall (not printed
  by [K]); segment count along 20 mm (not printed); field / current maps (Fig. 1c, d artistic); a "first" for the
  differential drive (claimed by Chen 2025, not verified; an earlier OECC 2024 paper is cited but not found).
- No drawing / trace: [K] and [A] are licensed (no figure images); [P] FIG. 1E is public domain and shows the S̅ S S̅
  scheme, but it is a prior-art comparison sketch, not the demonstrated device. It could be cropped and traced for the

6. WHAT THE BUILDER MUST KNOW
- Two sources, two different stacks: the T-rail chapter is Kharel's device (LN on quartz, 600 nm, 350 nm etch, 0.8 um
  gold, 1 um cladding). Drawing T-rails on the 2018 silicon + 4.7 um oxide stack would misrepresent it (the authors say
  the slow-wave effect is undesirable there). The differential chapter is a composite: Aimone's drive scheme on the
  T-rail geometry of the previous chapter; label it (part detail says so; consider a short canvas note via
  dims[].note or the tooltip).
- Never draw ground between the two signals and never place both arms in gaps with the same field direction.
- S̅ is written with U+0305 (combining overline) as the sources print it; check the font renders it in Playfair /
  Source Serif / app sans. Fallback: "-S" as the patent drawings write it. Do not use "_" or "^" for it.
- Plan scale: 50 um period on a 20 mm line compressed about 20x would alias; draw true-size segments in the close-up and
  an illustrative count in the whole-chip view.
- handle.assembledAt: set to the start of fom (or the coda) deliberately; the default (start of the last chapter) moves
  orbit to the differential chapter.
- The coda chapters reuse existing parts (signal_line, ground_lines, ln_ridge, microwave_field); new ids: t_rail,
  quartz_substrate, diff_outer_lines, diff_driver. Geometry types t_segment_rows and role_change are new free-form types
  for the module, not runtime types.
