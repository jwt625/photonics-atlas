Method: patent page scans extracted with pymupdf; text pages re-OCRed (tesseract TSV) and every line mapped to

independently (numpy / scipy, scripts in the session scratchpad mcvd_audit/dig1b.py, dig2.py). No app run, no
network, no git.

VERDICT: NO-GO as is; GO after W1-W3 (all small). Sources, quotes, locators, recipe values, both digitized paper
figures and the core process topology (flow +x, burner below, forward traverse with the gas, fast return, soot
downstream, fusion behind the moving zone, barrier then core, collapse behind the hot zone) are right. The wrong
items: one on-canvas sentence that contradicts both sources and the animation itself, the graded-ring radii, and
a rotation arrow that turns against its own head.

Counts: wrong 3, imprecise 17, missing 3, ok 16. Plus 2 re-shoot checks (screenshots older than a runtime fix).

W1. "fuse on the next pass" (on-canvas line, soot chapter)
  file:     macchesney1974.yaml line 499 (chapters.soot.line) and line 500 (action "the following pass turns
            it into clear glass"); shown on canvas in d_p045, d_p050, l_*, i_* ("Soot and sinter").
  current:  "Particles form in the gas, settle downstream, fuse on the next pass."
  proposed: "Particles form in the hot zone, settle downstream, fuse as the zone passes."
            action: "... settles as white powder ahead of the zone; the advancing hot zone fuses it to clear glass".
  evidence: paper p.1280 right col.: "this powdery deposit is subsequently fused to a clear film as the burner
            traverses along the tube" (same traverse). US 4,217,027 claim 1 col. 10:50-58: particles "while
            traveling downstream" come to rest "within a region which extends from a position within the said hot
            zone, the moving zone serving the dual functions of: nucleation site ...; and consolidation site for
            previously produced particulate matter"; col. 7:60-64: the moving hot zone is responsible for
            homogeneous reaction, adherence, and "fusion of the deposited particles ... into a unitary,
            homogeneous glassy layer". The module already animates exactly this (ts line 92 barN: the new layer
            appears behind xb; line 93 powT: porous deposit only ahead of xb, same pass), so the sentence also
            contradicts the picture.

W2. Graded core rings drawn as equal-area annuli; the paper fixes equal radius increments
  file:     macchesney1974.ts line 700 (ringR: P2 * sqrt(1 - (i+1)/NG)); also SEC_FS line 146 (collapsed state
            maps rings with 1 - s^2, same equal-area rule; only reached if uGrade > 0 while the tube is shown).
  current:  core ring radii 1.400, 1.328, 1.252, 1.171, 1.084, 0.990, 0.885, 0.767, 0.626, 0.443 mm (outer ring
            0.07 mm wide, inner disc 0.44 mm radius); visible in i_explode_preform.png.
  proposed: ringR core entries P2 * (1 - (i+1)/NG) (radii 1.40, 1.26, 1.12, ... 0.14 mm: equal steps of 0.1 a);
            same rule in SEC_FS for the collapsed state (f = (1 - s) * 10 for e = 1). In the open tube the
            layers should then not be equal-thickness either (deposition time "proportioned according to the
            quantity of material", i.e. first layers thickest, areas ~ 19:17:...:1); acceptable to keep the tube
            view equal-thickness only if the cutaway is labelled visual (it is, via withheld).
  evidence: paper p.1281 left col.: "the preceding expression was evaluated in terms of radius segments in
            increments of 0.1 a for alpha = 2. Ten layers of equal thickness (producing ultimately equal
            increments of r in the fiber) were deposited by proportioning the GeCl4 flow ... Deposition times for
            each layer were proportioned according to the quantity of material required to form the layer."
            Direction of the grading (Ge rising toward the axis) is right (OK-11).

W3. Rotation arrow turns against its own arrowhead
  file:     macchesney1974.ts lines 528-539 (arc 0..1.55 pi, cone at 1.55 pi pointing CCW in the arc plane;
            arrow.rotation.y = pi/2) and line 1030 (arrowPivot.rotation.x = -p * 2 pi * 14).
  current:  after the y rotation the head points in the +x right-hand sense (local CCW = from world -z toward +y);
            the pivot turns in the -x sense as p grows, so while scrolling down the gap and head move backward.
  proposed: arrowPivot.rotation.x = +p * 2 * Math.PI * 14 (or mirror the arc/cone). Sense itself is free:
            patent FIG. 1 arrow 5a is "for example" (col. 3:63-64), so either sense is fine once head and motion
            agree.
  evidence: geometry of TorusGeometry (arc in local XY from +x CCW), ConeGeometry apex +y rotated by 1.55 pi ->
            (0.988, 0.156) = CCW tangent at 279 deg; R_y(pi/2) maps local x -> world -z, y -> y.

I4. Barrier : core deposit ratio flips between the tube and the preform

  evidence: col. 7:16-17 ("approximately twenty minutes"), 7:25-26 ("about one hour"); Example 1 col. 8:36-44.
