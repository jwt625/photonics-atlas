2 WHAT THE PAPER FIXES (fixed / assumed / withheld per part)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Locators: p.109 col. 1 = intro, col. 2 = structure and growth; Fig. 1 across the foot of p.109; p.110 col. 1 =
Table I, doping targets, preparation, contacts, heat sink, Fig. 2 method; p.110 col. 2 = Fig. 2, diode size, 311 K
thresholds, 25 K rise, 20 mW, 80 meV; p.111 col. 1 = Fig. 3; p.111 col. 2 = mirror coatings remark, acknowledgments.

Apparatus (Fig. 1, schematic, no scale): fused silica tube, furnace walls, single loop auxiliary heater (coil below
  the tube), graphite seed holder (bar, STOP at the H2-out end), seed set in its top near the stop end, graphite
  sliding solution holder (frame around the seed holder, four wells "SOLUTIONS 1 2 3 4" between five solid walls),
  push rod at the H2-in end, thermocouple from the stop end with its tip under the seed, H2 IN / H2 OUT arrows. FIXED
  (arrangement). At the start a solid wall covers the seed; pushing the holder toward the stop brings wells 1-4 over
  the seed in order: this is the reading of the drawing (seed under the leftmost wall, wells ordered away from it).
  WITHHELD: all sizes, gas flow, furnace type.
Process: cooling rates of 1-3 C/min established first; layer 1 grown from 840 to 830 C; layers 2, 3, 4 with 15 s
  each on the seed while cooling (p.109 col. 2). Table I melts (atom %): 1 Ga 92.0 Al 0.5 As 3.0 Sn/4.5; 2 Ga 94.0
  Al "..." As 4.0 Si/2.0; 3 Ga 95.9 Al 0.8 As 2.8 Zn/0.5; 4 Ga 95.7 Al "..." As 4.0 Ge/0.3. Thicknesses (typical run):
  3-4, <~1, ~1.5, ~1.5 um; vary up to 2x across a wafer. x ~ 0.2-0.4 chosen from the melt; doping targets (not
  measured): layer 1 n ~ (1-4)e18, layer 2 p ~ (1-4)e18 compensated, layer 3 p ~ (5-8)e18, layer 4 p ~ (3-5)e18 cm^-3
  (all four exponents read as 18 on the 2x render at 2.5x zoom; the text layer agrees). Small GaAs excess per melt.
  Si 1-15 mg/g Ga tried. FIXED. Layer 2 called the "active region" only by combining p.109 col. 1 ("narrower active
  region") with p.110 col. 1 ("second layer be thin and uniform"); spec says so.
Zn skin ~0.2 um p+ (p.110 col. 1). FIXED depth; WITHHELD conditions.
Contacts: Cr then Au on the p side, Sn-Pt-Sn on the n side, vapor plated onto the wafer. FIXED materials and sides;
  no stripe or pattern is described -> uniform films; WITHHELD thicknesses.
Diode (Figs. 2, 3): uncoated Fabry-Perot DH diode, 370 um long, about 80 um wide (p.110 col. 1-2). FIXED. WITHHELD:
  how the chip / faces were made, substrate thickness and orientation of this diode.
Mounting: Au sides bonded to diamond coated with Cr then Sn; In-coated contact pressed on the n side; diamond bonded
  to a Cu block; reported temperatures are the Cu block's (p.110 col. 1). FIXED topology (p side down). WITHHELD sizes.
Results: CW up to 311 K heat sink (abstract; "270-311 K" in the text); at 311 K Jth ~ 3.5 kA/cm2 CW, ~ 2.0 kA/cm2
  pulsed; junction ~25 K above the sink at 297 K (estimate; "~1.8 um" of AlGaAs in layer 3 and 15 % conductivity are
  the authors' hypothetical inputs); >= 20 mW at 30 % above threshold at 297 K; emission 80 meV above that expected
  for GaAs (attributed to Al carry-over into solution 2); lasing seen as "turn on" of several spots with an image
  converter microscope, or spectrally. Diode voltage ~2.6 V at 0.8 A (Fig. 2 label; text layer garbles it as
  "-2.SV"). Pulses 0.2 us width, 2 us interval (Fig. 2) = 10 % duty (text).
Other diodes (side panel only): square fully internally reflecting diodes Jth(300 K) as low as 1000 A/cm2 (text,
  p.109 col. 1) but the abstract prints "100 A/cm2" (checked on the 2x render: "100"); FP diodes 560 um long ~1600
  A/cm2; earlier lowest 2300 A/cm2.
Not in the paper (withheld): band diagram (none printed; the story has no band diagram), stripe, junction position,
  far field, polarization, efficiency, threshold of the Fig. 2 diode at 297 K.
Assumed (drawing only): layer 1 drawn 3.5 um (midpoint of 3-4), layer 2 drawn 1.0 um (printed bound), substrate drawn
  6 um and broken (thickness not stated), heights exaggerated; diamond / Cu / In contact neutral stand-ins; emission
  colour illustrative (near-infrared; 1.446 eV -> 0.857 um derived).
