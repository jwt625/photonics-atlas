I4. Model comb lines lead their stems by up to one stem
  file:     delhaye2007.ts line 1028 (a = clamp01(pen - i)), line 253 (penAt), comment lines 14-15 ("lights exactly
            when the plot pen draws its stem").
  evidence: PlotAssembler draws whole stems only: stems 0 .. floor(t)-1 at pen t (revealed(): n = floor(t)). Line i
            therefore appears in the stems at t >= i + 1, while the model ramps line i in over t in (i, i + 1), i.e.
            before its stem. Small in p (about 0.0015 per stem) but contrary to the stated sync.
  proposed: a = pen >= i + 1 ? 1 : 0 (or a short ramp starting at i + 1) for i >= 3; pair (i = 1, 2) unchanged.
