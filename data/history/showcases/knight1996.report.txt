3 PLOT DIGITIZATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Plot near_field: Fig. 2(a), p.1548 right col. (embedded raster img_p02_2.png, 796 x 1328 px; panel (a) = rows
0-725). Licensed: points only, no image in the build.
  Calibration: the SEM under the contours shows the hole lattice. Holes = dark blobs (Gaussian sigma 1 px,
  threshold 15 of 255, opening with a 5 px disk, area 80-700 px): 53 holes. Nearest-neighbour vectors clustered,
  integer lattice indices assigned, affine lattice refined by least squares: a1 = (83.63, 0.12) px, a2 = (41.89,
  -73.45) px, |a1| 83.6, |a2| 84.6, |a2 - a1| 84.6 px, angle 60.4 deg (near-isotropic, rows horizontal), fit rms
  4.75 px (0.13 µm). Pixel -> µm by the affine map that sends the lattice to an ideal triangular lattice with the
  printed pitch 2.3 µm (Λ/px about 0.0274 µm). Origin = the lattice site with no hole that is surrounded by six
  holes (the core), at px (430.41, 368.19); x right, y up (image y flipped). Caveat in the plot note: the caption
  calls the SEM portion "approximately scaled", so absolute µm values carry that approximation.
  Contours: four closed black lines. Inner three: dark mask (threshold 15, closing disk 2) -> each closed stroke
  component filled; stroke-centre line = the 0.5 level of d_in / (d_in + d_out) (distance transforms to the
  enclosed region and to the outside), sub-pixel via find_contours: an exact stroke-centre read of every printed
  pixel column, no interpolation. Outer contour: same with threshold 20 and closing disk 3 (at 15 its stroke has a
  break at lower right; the closing bridges it), same calibration. Resampling: every 0.06 µm of contour length the
  nearest real centre-line point is kept (axis span 8.4 µm, so 0.06 µm is about 1/140 of the span, denser than
  1/60). Points where the stroke is wider than 1.35 x its median width (the outer contour touching a hole: six
  places) are dropped, not filled: 57 dropped.
  Point counts (each closed by repeating its first point): innermost 50, second 114, third 166, outermost 279;
  air-hole centres inside the axis box 16 (style points).
  Checks: contour centroids within 0.06 µm of the origin (innermost (-0.06, -0.05), outermost (0.03, 0.04)); the
  outer contour dips toward each of the six nearest holes as the text says; nearest holes at 2.37-2.59 µm from the
  core site (the affine fit spreads the lattice error); replot compared by eye against the figure
  (cache/showcases/knight1996/nf_replot.png): shapes, D-shaped innermost contour and the six lobes match.
  Axes: x, y in µm, range [-4.2, 4.2] both. Equal aspect is needed for a true shape (open question O2).

  plus the words "increases approximately linearly with wavelength". Series: the two points (basis stated) and a
  dashed line between them labelled as the paper's words, not a measurement (plot note). Pen rule for the builder:
  the scene's output cone follows the NA only at and between the two printed points; nothing beyond them.

Not plotted: Fig. 2(b) is calculated (Fourier transform) and has no axis scale; Fig. 3 photographs have no scale.

4 GEOMETRY FOR THE MODEL
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.1 Scale chain (stated unless marked): rod 30 mm, hole 16 mm -> hexagon at most 26.0 mm across the flats
    (derived bound) -> cane 0.8 mm (as printed) -> stack 9 rings, 271 canes, about 13.2 mm across (derived) ->
    fused stack pitch ~50 µm, about 0.8 mm across (derived) -> fiber pitch 2.3 µm, 38 µm across the flats, core
    4.6 µm, holes about 0.35 µm (ref). The builder zooms between stages; the spec keeps real values in dims.

4.2 Ring count (Fig. 1, img_p02_1.png, 900 x 704 px, oblique SEM view):
    Hole detection: background-subtracted (median 13-15 px) local minima, face region only (excludes the bright
    sidewall and the black background). First pass (depth > 12): 211 holes; nearest-neighbour vectors
    (3.2, -33.3), (29.3, -13.1), (26.1, 20.1) px. Indices assigned; a projective (homography) map lattice -> pixel
    fitted (rms 0.12 pitch; the residual is lattice irregularity, not tilt). The empty site surrounded by six holes is
    the core. Second pass (depth > 5, accepted only within 0.35 pitch of a lattice site): rings 1-7 complete
    (6, 12, ..., 42), ring 8 47/48 (the missing site is at the chipped top corner, px (593, 44)), ring 9 28/54 at
    the fiber edge, mostly in the bright edge rim (median depth 12 vs 21 for rings 1-7: smaller or less resolved
    holes), beyond ring 9 only scattered noise outside the face.
    Outline: the face boundary mapped to lattice coordinates; minimum widths at normals 27-29, 88, 155 deg from a
    row direction, i.e. the outer flats are parallel to the rows of holes. Flat-to-flat 15.5 Λ (inside the edge
    rim) to 16.2 Λ (rim included) vs printed 38 / 2.3 = 16.5 Λ.
    Stack model: hexagonal canes, flats touching, N rings, outline flats parallel to the rows -> half width about
    (0.866 N + 0.29 ... 0.58) Λ (notch ... corner), mean 0.866 N + 0.43. With 8.26 Λ (printed) N = 9.04 -> 9.
    Result: 9 rings, 271 canes (basis figure + derived; not stated in the paper).
    Scale bar: the "10 µm" bar runs into the bright sidewall, its left end is not visible; not used. All lengths
    rely on the printed 2.3 µm pitch.

4.3 Hole size check (Fig. 1): half-depth area of 126 holes in rings 1-6, converted with the local homography
    Jacobian: equivalent diameter median 0.185 Λ (IQR 0.16-0.20). Blur (holes are 5-6 px across) biases this up;
    consistent with the authors' d/Λ ≈ 0.15 (Birks 1997), which the spec uses (0.35 µm, basis ref).

4.4 Test bench: laser box, objective, 1 m fiber (render shortened or coiled, length in hover), index-matching
    sleeve near the input (position assumed), output to the vidicon with a 60x objective (near-field chapter) or to
    a paper sheet several cm away (far-field chapter). All boxes are neutral stand-ins; no layout is stated.

5 CHAPTER SCRIPT RATIONALE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Ten chapters, two plot chapters (D1004 limit 3). The process follows the paper's order exactly: drill, mill, draw to
cane, cut and stack with the solid cane at the center, draw to fuse (pitch ~50 µm), draw a piece to fiber. The
lattice chapter is the reveal (pitch 2.3 µm essential). Then the test in the paper's order: launch with stripping,
near field (Fig. 2(a) plot), far field and single-mode range (NA plot). Physics: surface tension shrinks the
relative hole size in the final draw stages (shown in the final-draw chapter, not earlier); light in the cladding
is stripped at the index-matching fluid; the near field is at 632.8 nm (red); the far-field pattern keeps its
shape across wavelengths while the NA grows, outer spots slightly weaker at long wavelengths. Essential dims (4):
rod dia. 30 mm, hole 16 mm, cane 0.8 mm, pitch 2.3 µm. One amber emphasis: "*Single mode*". Lines carry no other
numbers. Spelling as printed: "fiber", "center" (US), "He-Ne", "Ti:sapphire", "vidicon".
