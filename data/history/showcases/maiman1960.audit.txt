W3. Laser output shown after the flash has died; steady beam with the lamp at 8 %
  file:     app/src/lib/history/showcases/maiman1960.ts lines 676-688 (flashOn, pump, lasing) and 720-738 (beam)
  current:  flash rises at flash-chapter q 0.10-0.18, holds to 0.45, then decays exp(-4.5 (q-0.45)); at the start
            of the pulse chapter (p = 0.78) flashOn = exp(-0.55 x 4.5) = 0.084, and it stays 0.084 to p = 1.0
            (seg() clamps). Lasing glow and beam start only in the pulse chapter (q >= 0.05 / 0.10), i.e. after
            the pump is ~92 % gone, and the beam then stays on as a steady line through the spectrum chapter.
            Free-running flash-pumped ruby lases while the flash holds the 2E population above threshold; output
            starts during the flash and ends soon after the pump falls (stored inversion is used up by the
            stimulated emission). Order flash -> output is right; the gap and the steady end state are not.
  proposed: start the beam while flashOn is still high (e.g. move the decay of the flash into the pulse chapter:
            flash held through pulse q < ~0.5, beam grows from q 0.1, both decay together), and at the end either
            fade beam + lamp out together or keep both at their "on" level as an explicit frozen frame. Keep the
            chapter wording ("Intense flash ..." then "694.3 nm").
  evidence: code above; desk_pulse_p084.png / desk_p090.png / desk_p100.png (beam on, lamp faint).
