2. WHAT THE PAPER FIXES (P)
- Name: Cobra = "commutateur optique binaire rapide" (p.289 col. 1). "original electrode configuration", abstract
  "novel electrode configuration"; no "first" claim.
- Theory (p.289 col. 1): coupled-mode Eq. (1), solution Eq. (2) for guide 1 excited; Fig. 1 plots |E1|^2 vs x for
  Δβ = 0 (full transfer at L0) and Δβ = c(12)^1/2 (partial exchange, back in guide 1 at L0). Total switching needs a
  coupler length that is an odd multiple of L0 (p.289 col. 2).
- Classical vs Cobra (p.289 col. 2, Fig. 2): classical = three electrodes, C axis in-plane across the guides;
  Cobra = two electrodes, C axis normal to the guide surface, opposite fringing-field directions in the two guides
  give opposite index changes (Fig. 3). Central electrode section 2000 Å x 2 µm, length ~several mm: fragile, high
  resistance. Principal effect on TM through r_zzz; r_yzy (printed subscript y, z, y; Fig. 3b lists r_yyy and r_yzy separately) gives polarity-asymmetric behavior.
- Fabrication (p.289 col. 2 - p.290 col. 1, Fig. 4): ~500 Å Ti by rf sputtering; masked (resist, light) and ion
  etched (ref. 7); diffused in O2 at 1100 °C for ~11 h. Electrodes (p.290 col. 2): 2000 Å gold, rf sputtered, optical
  superposition masking, ion etching, two 1 x 3 mm electrodes separated by 3 µm at the coupler center; ~2 dB/cm TM
  loss from the electrodes. Fig. 4 labels the film "Au or Al (2000 Å)".
- Geometry (Fig. 5 caption): a = 2 µm, b = 3 µm, c = 3 mm. Fig. 5 arrows: a across one strip, b between the facing
  strip edges (small print, read at 8x zoom), c along the dashed coupling-region outline.
- Test (p.290 col. 1): one guide excited with a rutile prism coupler, argon 5145 Å line, observation by fluorescence
  or diffused light. Coupling lengths with 2 µm strips: ≈ 500 µm at 2 µm separation, ≈ 1 mm at 3 µm (used for the
  switch). Marcatili model with strip width, 2 µm thickness, uniform Δn ~5e-3 ("rather pragmatic").
- Result (p.291 col. 1): essentially TM; 0 V: most light in the initially unexcited (straight) guide, as expected for
  L0 = 1 mm and a 3 mm coupling region; 6 V: switched back to the initially excited (curved) guide. ~3 x more
  effective for TM than TE; asymmetry with polarity observed. Abstract: "Efficient switching is observed with 6 V".
- Fig. 6 photographs (0 V / 6 V): the cached scan is bilevel; both photographs are black fields with a few white
  specks (146 and 2629 non-black pixels). No intensity information can be read from them.
- NOT in the paper: any measured switching curve, extinction ratio, crosstalk, insertion loss, speed, substrate
  size, bend radii, prism details, polarity used, Δβ at 6 V.
