            claimed to match". microwave_field.detail, append: "The velocity matching shown is the 2018 plain
            ground-signal-ground line's. T segments add capacitance and slow the microwave; Kharel et al. note that on
            LN on silicon with a thick oxide this slow-wave effect is undesirable and used a quartz handle (Optica 2021,
            arXiv v1, p.3). Matching of the drawn T-rail line on the 2018 stack is not claimed." t_rail.detail: add the
            same reason after "velocity matching of this combination is not claimed". Chapter line: see I1.
