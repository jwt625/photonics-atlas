2. WHAT THE PAPER FIXES (all basis stated unless noted)
Stack / cross-section (Methods p.5):
- Commercial x-cut LNOI (NANOLN), 600 nm LN device layer; final LN slab 300 nm (ridge height 300 nm: derived);
  buried SiO2 4.7 um; Si substrate 500 um.
- Waveguide top width 800 nm (single mode). EBL + Ar+-based RIE.
- Gold strip lines 1.1 um thick, PMMA 1.5 um lift-off, second aligned EBL. PECVD SiO2 cladding 800 nm. Facets diced
  and polished.
- Metal to waveguide edge spacing <2.5 um; simulated metal absorption loss <0.03 dB/cm.
- Group indices microwave and optical "both approximately 2.2"; impedance "near 50 ohm"; eps_RF 28, eps_opt 5 of LN.
- Crystal axes (Fig. 1d inset, Extended Data Fig. 5a; basis figure): x normal to the film, z in-plane across the gaps;
  micrograph axes z / y: propagation along y. TE excitation (Methods).
- Arrangement (Fig. 1d inset, Extended Data Fig. 5a, c, d; basis figure): G-S-G on the LN slab, one ridge centred in
  each gap (inset ridge centre 432.31 pt vs gap centre 432.18 pt), oxide above, buried oxide, Si.
Device / layout:
- Travelling-wave MZI; 50:50 Y-junction in, arms through the two G-S / S-G gaps, 50:50 Y-junction out (p.1).
- RF in at the light-input end, co-propagating; 50 ohm termination at the far end (Fig. 1d labels; Methods).
- Devices 20, 10, 5 mm active length (p.2-3). Total waveguide length >3 cm (Methods).
Results:
- Vpi 1.4 V (20 mm), 2.3 V (10 mm), 4.4 V (5 mm); Vpi.L 2.8 / 2.3 / 2.2 V.cm (Extended Data Table 1; Methods for the
  last two). ER 30 dB. Vpi measured with a 5 V peak-to-peak triangular drive.
- 3-dB EO bandwidth >45 GHz (20 mm, VNA), 80 GHz (10 mm), 100 GHz (5 mm; OSA method above 35 GHz); probes not
  de-embedded, so lower bounds.
- 70 Gbit/s directly from the CMOS DAC: 200 mV pp, BER <1e-6 (no errors in 1.1e6 bits), 0.37 fJ/bit; 60 mV pp,
  BER 3.4e-3, 37 aJ/bit. Binary = BPSK with the modulator biased at null (Methods).
- Amplified to about 2.5 V pp: 100 Gbaud binary BER 7.8e-5 (electrical BER 3.6e-5); 70 Gbaud 4-ASK 140 Gbit/s
  BER 2.1e-5; 8-ASK 210 Gbit/s BER 1.5e-2 (FEC limit 1.9e-2, 20 % overhead), 14 fJ/bit.
- On-chip loss <0.5 dB: upper bound, modulators (>3 cm waveguide) vs short bare waveguides, +-0.5 dB uncertainty.
- About 30 GHz/V bandwidth / Vpi (Fig. 4c).
Essential (on-canvas) dims, 3: LN film 600 nm (stack chapter), electrode length 20 mm (mzi chapter), Vpi 1.4 V
(vpi chapter; the story's one amber keyword "*1.4 V*").

8. NOTES FOR THE BUILDER
- Two scales: cross-section in um (film 0.6 um, gap 4.5 um, signal 50 um assumed, oxide 4.7 um) and length in mm
  (20 mm). Do not draw the device to scale along y; the plan view is schematic and compressed (say nothing on canvas
  that implies scale). Within the cross-section, stack heights and the ridge / gap geometry are to scale; the signal
  strip (50 um) dwarfs the ridges: frame the gaps (e.g. break the signal strip or zoom to one gap pair).
- One ridge per gap, centred; electrodes sit on the 300 nm slab (not on the buried oxide); cladding translucent and
  faint after its chapter.
- Physics: fields in the two gaps point in opposite z directions (signal at +V: field from S to both G), so one arm's
  index rises while the other's falls; flip both arrows together with the drive sign. Light and microwave travel the
  same way (RF in at the light-input end). Output brightness follows the transfer curve (bright at the maximum, dark
  at the minimum, 1.4 V apart).
- Plot axes: eo_20mm and its alternates share 0-100 GHz so the toggle compares like with like. data: log BER. fom: log
  Vpi axis (paper uses an inverse axis; note says so).
- The optical-mode spot and field arrows are illustrative (simulation figures, no printed mode scale).
- No figure images; no drawing pane (no public-domain drawing exists for this paper).
