2. WHAT THE PAPER ITSELF FIXES (P)
- Thin-wall fused-quartz tube (Amersil T08 Commercial Grade) held in a glass-working lathe, supported on both ends;
  rotating; heated by an oxy-hydrogen burner that traverses along its length; multiple passes.
- Gas: SiCl4, GeCl4, BCl3 carried in an O2 stream. Reaction at the tube surface and in the gas; gas-phase
  particles settle as powder downstream of the hot zone and are fused to a clear film as the burner traverses.
- Sequence: thin borosilicate barrier layer first; then GeO2-SiO2 or GeO2-B2O3-SiO2; tube collapsed to a solid rod;
  germania-rich layer forms the preform core.
- Fiber (Fig. 1): 723 m, core ~35 um, NA 0.235, loss minimum just under 2 dB/km at 1.06 um, ~lambda^-4 decrease;
  OH absorptions 0.72, 0.88, 0.95 um, < 10 dB/km at 0.95 um; fractionally distilled SiCl4 gave peaks ~2 dB/km
  above background at 0.95 um; NA up to 0.35 with other conditions.
- Graded index: 10 equal-thickness layers, GeCl4 flow in 10 increments, SiCl4 and BCl3 constant, time per layer
  proportioned; measured (interference microscopy) delta n max 0.0165, alpha 1.6 (Fig. 2).
- The paper prints NO temperatures, flows, speeds, rpm or pass counts. It does not use the words "MCVD" or
  "modified chemical vapor deposition" ("a new method derived from CVD").

3. RECIPE NUMBERS (all from US 4,217,027; HUD source)
- Tube 12 mm I.D. x 14 mm O.D. (6:60-61; 8:35-36); cut to 18 in (8:23-24); HF-HNO3 3 min, DI rinse 1 h (8:20-23).
- Rotation 100 rpm (6:62-63; 2:65-66). Hot zone ~2 cm, 45 cm/min, 100 rpm, 1600 C possible without distortion
  (5:15-20). Pyrometer on the outer surface; wall gradient up to 300 C (5:24-29). Hot zone ~2 cm, heated region
  extending 4 cm either side of the peak (8:04-06). Traverse 1-8 min per pass across examples (8:27-29).
- O2 flush: wall 1400 C, 5 min (6:63-7:01); flush flow 100-500 cm3/min (8:29-31).
- General Procedure barrier: ~10 % SiCl4, 3 % BCl3, rest O2, 1400 C, ~45 cm/min forward (direction of gas flow),
  rapid return ~30 s, ~20 min; then O2 only for several passes (7:01-19).
- Example 1 barrier: SiCl4 41 cm3/min, BCl3 12.5 cm3/min, total O2 250 cc/min, 16 passes, 1430 C (8:36-41).
- Example 1 core: SiCl4 32, GeCl4 48, O2 650 cc/min, 68 min, 1460 C (8:41-45). General Procedure core ~1450 C,
  ~1 h (7:20-26).
- Collapse: traverse slowed, temperature rises to ~1900 C with reactants flowing (nearly complete collapse), then
  flow stopped for final collapse (7:27-35; Example 1 8:45-47). Preform: GeO2-SiO2 core, borosilicate cladding,
  silica support layer.
- Draw: ~60 m/min onto a 30 cm mandrel at 60 rpm; fiber ~100 um OD, core ~37 um, ~0.7 km (7:38-50).
  Example 1: core ~40 um, 100 um OD, 723 m, 2 dB/km at 1060-1100 nm (8:47-51).
- Essential (on-canvas, D983): 45 cm/min traverse, 1460 C core deposition, ~1900 C collapse, just under 2 dB/km.
