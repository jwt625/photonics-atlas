2. What is fixed, assumed, withheld
-----------------------------------
Fixed by sources (basis stated / ref / figure):
- Rod: ruby, chromium in corundum (Nature p.493); "pink ruby cylinder 1-cm-diameter by 2-cm-long" (Smith); Nature
  says only "1-cm. dimensions". Both ends polished flat, parallel, evaporated silver (Nature + Smith).
- Output: 1 mm hole in one silver coating (Smith); patent: "coupled out ... through a hole in the reflective
  coating at one end of the rod" (col. 2:35-37), "nonreflective opening 32 in the end plating" (col. 5:1-2).
  Hole on the axis: figure basis only (FIG. 4, FIG. 5 schematics); the text does not give its radial position.
- Lamp: GE FT-506 xenon flash lamp, rod "mounted on the axis" (Smith); helical, rod coaxial inside (patent col.
  2:32-33, col. 4:69-70); three-ring coil (HRL photo caption, p.1 of the excerpt). Catalog FT 506 row: design
  voltage 900 V, max 1,000 watt-sec, 40,000 lumen-sec, outer bulb T-10 (heat resistant), base "Radio Octal",
  approx. source dimensions width 1 in, height 1 in, L.C.L. 2 1/8 in, M.O.L. 4 7/8 in, 1 flash per min.
- Reflector: lamp "placed inside a polished aluminum cylinder" (Smith); "outer enclosing cylinder 38 ... very highly
  reflecting inner surface for reflecting the pumping energy repeatedly through the rod 26" (col. 5:4-7).
- Power supply: "A power supply 36 provides the flashing energy for the tube 28" (col. 5:3-4); FIG. 4 wires it to
  both helix ends.
- Physics: pump "about 5500 Å" to 4F2, non-radiative to 2E, doublet "at 300° K. are at 6943 Å. and 6929 Å.",
  inversion condition, stimulated emission at 6943 Å, expected "(~10^8) reduction in line width when mode
  selection techniques are used" (Nature p.493-494). First laser action 1960-05-16 (Smith). Smith: the first
  laser showed "spectral and beam narrowing, together with lifetime reduction"; in mid-July 1960 three better
  crystals gave a sharp threshold and relaxation oscillations.
Essential (canvas) numbers, D983: rod 10 mm diameter, rod 20 mm length, 694.3 nm.
Assumed (rendering only, flagged basis: assumed): silver disc thickness 0.2 mm, lamp tube diameter 3.5 mm, cylinder
  inner diameter 34 mm / wall 1.5 mm / length 40 mm, power-supply box 40 x 24 x 24 mm and its placement, beam length
  120 mm, axial centring of lamp and cylinder on the rod.
Withheld: see `withheld` in the YAML (rod mount, cylinder size and ends, FT-506 bulb/base in the head, terminals and
  trigger, supply circuit/energy/voltage, threshold, output energy/power/duration/divergence, lifetime values, Cr
  concentration and c-axis, silver thickness/reflectivity, Fig. 2 absolute scale, cooling).
