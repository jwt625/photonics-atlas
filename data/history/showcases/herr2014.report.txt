2. WHAT THE PAPER FIXES (A)
Device
- "MgF2 crystal carrying two whispering-gallery-mode microresonators of different size (the smaller one with an FSR
  of 35.2 GHz is used)" (Fig. 1a caption, p.7). "crystalline MgF2 resonator" (p.2).
- FSR 35.2 GHz; coupled resonance width κ/2π = 450 kHz measured with modulation sidebands of a scanning laser;
  Q = 4×10^8; n0 = 1.37; D2/2π = 16 kHz, β2 = -9.39 ps^2/km (p.2, p.5). Finesse printed "F = 78 × 10^4" (p.5, checked
  on the page render); FSR / κ from the same sentence = 7.8×10^4: printed value kept, discrepancy in a dims note.
- A_eff 90×10^-12 m^2, V_eff 5.6×10^-13 m^3, n2 0.9×10^-20 m^2/W, γ 4.1×10^-4 /(m W) (p.5). Circulating power
  "several hundreds of Watts" for Pin 5-30 mW (p.5). T_R = 28.4 ps (p.4); "T_R = n_eff L/c" printed on Fig. 1a.
- Coupling: "evanescently coupled to the MgF2 resonator ... via a tapered optical fiber" (p.5). Not a prism.
- NOT in the paper: resonator diameter, fabrication method ("machined" / diamond turning / polishing appear nowhere;
  p.6 says only "TH and MLG fabricated the sample. CYW assisted in sample fabrication."), rim profile dimensions,
  which tier is the larger resonator, taper size / gap / coupling regime, mount.
Setup (Fig. 2d, p.8, block diagram + caption; p.5 Methods)
- Pump: fiber laser Koheras Adjustik, 1553 nm, short-term linewidth 10 kHz, amplified by an EDFA; scan box linked
  to the laser; FPC (three paddles); fiber thins past the resonator; output fiber branches to OSA; PD -> oscilloscope
  (transmission); FBG (pump filtered out, in transmission) -> PD -> mixer with LO (11.7 GHz, third harmonic) ->
  ESA, with a branch to the oscilloscope (beat sampled, down-mixed to 20 MHz, p.2).
Experiment
- Laser scanned "with decreasing optical frequency" (p.2). Fig. 2a (coupled pump 5 mW): thermal triangle with
  deviations as discrete steps (green shading). Spectra I-III at three scan positions (Fig. 2b); broad noisy beat
  then a single narrow beat (Fig. 2c). "the first step, that is the transition to low noise, coincides with the
  zero crossing of the PDH signal" (p.2, Fig. 2e, a separate 20 ms scan).
- Number of solitons controlled by detuning; steps = transitions to and between soliton states (abstract p.1).
  Per-step soliton counts are given only in simulation (Fig. 3c: "stepwise reduction of the number of pulses", p.3).
- Single soliton: smooth sech^2 envelope, 3 dB bandwidth 1.6 THz (>45 modes), TBP 0.315 -> 197 fs expected; beat
  note RBW-limited 1 kHz, SNR >60 dB; FROG pulses spaced 28.4 ps; multi-soliton states (2 and 5) structured (p.4,
  Fig. 4). FROG reconstruction 200 fs FWHM, error 1.7 % (Fig. 5a). Temporal magnifier x105.5, ~2.5 ps resolution,
  4 GHz sampling scope: pulses spaced by T_R, constant amplitude (p.4, Fig. 5c, 5d).
- Simulation parameters (p.5): 101 (and 501) modes, D1/2π 35.2 GHz, D2/2π 10 kHz, D3/2π -130 Hz, Q 2×10^8,
  Pin 100 mW, 193 THz.
- "First": none for the solitons ("Here we report the observation", p.1). The paper never says "dissipative Kerr
  soliton" (title_note says so).
