W1. Polarity-asymmetry coefficient: the paper prints r_yzy, the spec says r_yyy
  file:     data/history/showcases/papuchon1975.yaml line 220 (field_lines.detail: "r_yyy adds a polarity-dependent
            asymmetry"), line 240 (voltage.detail: "as expected from r_yyy"); papuchon1975.report.txt line 41.
            Visible in the side panel (field_lines, control voltage).
  current:  r_yyy
  proposed: r_yzy ("One expects ... a different behavior when opposite electrical polarities are applied, due to
            the r_yzy coefficient, which will lead to an asymmetric change of index between the guides.")
  evidence: p.289 col. 2, scan img_p02_1.png about x 1400-1580, y 2060-2130 at 6x: subscript y, z, y; the middle
            glyph is the same italic z as the three of "r_zzz" four lines above (x 1340-1440, y 1860-1910).
            Fig. 3b (p.290) lists both r_yyy and r_yzy as separate tensor elements, so the two are not
            interchangeable. text.md OCR "r y. y" is inconclusive; the scan is not.

W2. Switch chapter ramp: complete switching at half the voltage, then partly back
  file:     app/src/lib/history/showcases/papuchon1975.ts line 912 (d2 = 3 * S.v * S.v), line 625 (v ramps over
            switch-chapter q 0.32-0.66), line 1121 (hover "voltage rising: delta beta drawn proportional to the
            voltage"); yaml line 325 (switch.action), line 455 (withheld), line 421 (power_eq2 note).
  current:  delta beta proportional to V with (delta beta / 2c)^2 = 3 at 6 V. Output in the curved guide at
            3 L0 from Eq. (2) along the drawn ramp: 0 V 0.00, 1.5 V 0.30, 2.4 V 0.82, 3.0 V 1.00, 3.6 V 0.88,
            4.5 V 0.63, 5.4 V 0.88, 6.0 V 1.00. Scrubbing the chapter therefore shows full switching near 3 V,
            light leaking back to the straight guide (about 37 %) near 4.5 V, and switching again at 6 V, while
            the keyword says "Switched at 6 V". Cause: the Fig. 1 case delta beta = c*sqrt(12) is the paper's bar
            state for a coupler one L0 long ("all the power transferred to 2 has returned to 1 over the same
            distance L0", p.289 col. 1); for the 3 L0 switch Eq. (2) returns all light to guide 1 whenever
            s = sqrt(delta beta^2/4c^2 + 1) = 2m/3, first at s = 4/3, delta beta = (2 sqrt(7)/3) c = 1.76 c,
            and c*sqrt(12) (s = 2) is the second such state.
  proposed: (B, preferred: keeps the paper's own Fig. 1 value and the plot chapter consistent) keep c*sqrt(12)
            for the 6 V frame but do not interpolate delta beta through intermediate values: crossfade the 0 V
            pattern into the 6 V pattern while the voltage stand-in steps up; hover for the 6 V state: "6 V
            drawn for delta beta = c*sqrt(12), the Fig. 1 case; Eq. (2) also returns all light at 3 L0 for
            smaller delta beta (1.76 c); the delta beta reached at 6 V is not stated". Drop "drawn proportional
            to the voltage".
            (A) or use s = 4/3 at 6 V (basis derived from Eq. (2)): the ramp is then monotonic (output 0 -> 1),
            pattern dips to 0.44 at 0.75 and 2.25 L0, full return at 1.5 and 3 L0; needs a note that the
            plot's c*sqrt(12) curve is a different delta beta.
  evidence: eq2.py (scratch): P1(3 L0, 3 v^2) table above; s = 2m/3 solutions m = 2, 3, 4 give delta beta / c =
            1.764, 3.464, 4.944 with minima 0.4375, 0.75, 0.859. Kogelnik and Schmidt 1976 (cached, Sec. II,
            Fig. 2): for the single-section switch the bar states form concentric circles in the (L/l, Δβ L/π)
            switching diagram and "an increase in the voltage moves the point ... parallel to the Δβ L/π axis",
            so a ramp at L = 3 L0 crosses the first bar circle before the second. The 6 V end state itself is
            right (OK-9).
