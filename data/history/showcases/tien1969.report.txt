2. WHAT THE PAPER FIXES
Fixed (stated):
- Abstract (p.291): "novel prism-film coupler", also used to measure phase velocities; "Up to 50 % of the incident
  laser energy has been fed into a single mode". No "first" claim.
- Films (p.291 col. 1; p.292 col. 2 - p.293 col. 1): sputtered ZnO (argon-oxygen, sintered ZnO powder; c axes within
  a 5 deg cone of the normal; polished after growth) and e-beam-evaporated ZnS (not oriented); 800 to 30 000 A; on
  glass.
- Coupler (p.291 col. 1-2): beam totally reflected at the prism base at theta_3; film at distance S parallel to the
  base; "optical tunneling" by the evanescent fields in the gap; three coupling conditions (synchronous direction,
  matching polarization, S "in the order of half a wavelength"); beta = k n3 sin theta_3 (p.292 col. 1).
- Theory (p.292): Eq. (1)-(5) mode equation 2 b1 W - 2 psi12' - 2 psi10 = 2 m pi; linewidth 2 t1^2, finesse
  pi / t1^2; Eq. (6) buildup for 0 < x < L, Eq. (7) decay beyond L; typical t1 = 0.1; max about 81 % transfer;
  decoupling beyond L by a wider gap or by cutting the prism.
- Experiment (p.293 col. 1): rutile prism, alpha = 26 deg, optical axis parallel to y (TM couples via the ordinary
  ray, TE via the extraordinary ray in the prism); film pressed with adjustable pressure against the base, dust
  particles as spacers, substrate elasticity; linear polarized beam; screen; prism-film assembly and screen on a
  turntable, laser fixed; B3 spot always seen; at a synchronous angle: spot dip "occasionally down to 50%", bright
  film near the coupling spot, one or more m lines, one through the B3 spot; lines brighten, then broaden and shift
  with coupling; explanation by azimuthally degenerate and intermode scattering.
- p.293 col. 2: dark line in the B3 spot; aperture about 1 deg inside the prism; F about 50, t1^2 = 0.06.
- Table I (p.293) + p.293 col. 2 - p.294 col. 1: ZnO film W = 15 881 +- 60 A (fitted to the 6 lowest TE modes at
  6328 A), n1 fitted per group (1.9732 / 1.9779 / 2.0428 / 2.0485), n0 = 1.5127 (6328 A), 1.5206 (4880 A);
  n1 = 1.973 +- 0.001 vs 1.988 (Bond, bulk). Footnote a: some modes not observable with the 26 deg rutile prism.
- Half prism (p.294): prism of Fig. 1 cut along its plane of symmetry, right half removed, 90 deg corner = x = L;
  ZnS 1500 A film pressed by a pointed tool; beam enters the hypotenuse face from the upper left; streak about 1 cm;
  scratch ends the streak and radiates; two 90 deg prisms 3 mm apart (input / output), m line on the output screen.
  Fig. 2 caption: ZnS 1500 A, 6328 A, prism height 0.5 cm, viewed through the substrate.
From figures (Fig. 1, schematic): side-view topology (substrate, film, gap, prism with short vertical end faces,
  apex over the coupling spot, polarizer and an unlabelled biconvex element in the incident beam, tilted screen,
  face-on screen view with five lines, pressure tool under the substrate with upward arrows), theta_3 drawn about
  42 deg, drawn prism slopes 27.7 / 26.7 deg (consistent with alpha = 26 deg).
Derived (this story): Eq. (5) with an air cover (n2 = 1) and the printed W, n0, n1 reproduces every Table I theory
  value to +-0.0001 (script below): the theory used n2 = 1. Mode counts of the Table I film: 7 TE / 6 TM at 6328 A,
  9 / 9 at 4880 A. Prism height 0.5 cm holds for the Fig. 1 prism (the half prism was cut along its symmetry plane).
Assumed (presentational): drawn film and gap thickness (enlarged), substrate size, prism end-face height about 1 mm
  (Fig. 1 proportion 22/109 px), screen size, turntable rotation amount, scratch position, beam colour.
Withheld: 13 entries in the spec (synchronous angles, laser data, identity of the biconvex element, prism indices
  and size, gap values, turntable hardware, m-line count for a given film, L, screen, film/substrate size, losses,
  scratch position, Fig. 2 photo).

5. CHAPTER SCRIPT RATIONALE (9 chapters, 1 plot chapter, height 9)
substrate, film, prism (alpha + height on canvas), press (gap S label: the paper presses the film up against a fixed
prism; the story idea "prism lowered" is corrected), reflect (off-synchronous: total reflection, spot only), tunnel
(turntable turns prism + film + screen, laser fixed; at the synchronous angle spot dims with a dark line, film glows
near the spot, guided light in +x per Eq. (6)/(7)), mlines (Fig. 1 face-on screen, five lines as drawn), modes (Table I
plot, model left), free (half prism, streak 1 cm, scratch). Amber emphasis: "Optical *tunneling*" (the paper's term).
Essential dims (4): alpha 26 deg, prism height 0.5 cm, gap S about lambda/2, streak about 1 cm.
Physics notes for the builder: m lines appear only at a synchronous angle (all phenomena vanish if a coupling
condition is violated); the m lines are fixed relative to the screen (it turns with the prism) while the B3 spot moves
as theta_3 changes, sitting on a line when synchronous; under the full prism the guided glow is local (prism also
couples out beyond L); in the half-prism chapter the beam enters the remaining sloped face and the corner is x = L.
