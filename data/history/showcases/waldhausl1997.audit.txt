I1. Dose staircase and developed profile out of register (write / develop chapters)
  file:     waldhausl1997.ts line 185 (DOSE), 788-795 (shotRect: step 0 = highest dose at x0 ... x0 + 160 nm),
            186-197 (closeProfile: peak at x0, valley at x0 + 200 nm, long facet to x0 + 800 nm)
  current:  the highest-dose shot covers the steep side, where the drawn final depth runs 0 -> 250 nm. Mean drawn
            depth under each step, highest to lowest dose: about 100, 230, 167, 100, 33 nm, so the highest-dose
            strip ends up shallower than the second. During development (tint fades at develop q 0.55-0.8 while the
            profile grows 0.15-0.7) the viewer sees the deepest groove under the second strip and the brightest
            strip half under the peak.
  proposed: shift the staircase (or the profile) so depth follows dose: e.g. put the dose jump in the middle of
            the steep side (staircase origin at x0 + 100 nm) or at the valley (x0 + 200 nm), so the 200 nm
            inclination straddles the low-to-high dose step, which is how a blurred dose edge forms it. Keep
            "five steps, schematic" in hover.
  evidence: p.9384 col. 1: "the exposed regions of the resist are dissolved according to the varying electron
            dose"; p.9385 col. 1: "During the exposure process a second inclination of an approximately 200 nm in
            base length is formed."

  current:  straight beam through prism, liquid and plate at 52° from the surface, refracted at the prism face for
            an assumed prism index 1.457; hover explains that Fig. 3 draws a bend. Re-measured on Fig. 3 (p.9386,

  proposed: keep; hover may say "Fig. 3 draws the beam entering the prism at a shallow angle (about 19°) and
            bending twice; drawn here as one index-matched straight path (assumed)". "about 52°" can stay
