2. WHAT THE PAPER FIXES (fixed / assumed / withheld per part)
Key finding for the coordinator: the 1988 device is NOT the later AWG layout (input guide, input star / free region,
arrayed guides, output star, output guides). It is a bare sector of 31 concentric arcs; a broad Gaussian He-Ne beam
falls directly on its input plane, and the light leaving the output plane converges across the film to a focal plane
1 mm away. Fan-in / fan-out sections are only recommended (p.386 col. 1). The pick's story idea ("input guide, free
region, arrayed guides, output guides") would draw a device that this paper does not contain; the spec follows the paper.
- array: FIXED 31 guides, width 3 um, average radial spacing 6 um, R 910-1090 um, psi = 30 deg (p.386 col. 1); topology
  concentric arcs on one centre between two radial planes (Fig. 1, Eq. (1)). Closure: 30 x 6 = 180 = 1090 - 910 um.
  WITHHELD: individual R_i (Eq. (3) with a quadratic Phi and integers m_i, not printed). DRAWN equidistant at 6 um
  (basis: stated average); the paper's example says deviations of at most +/-5 % for m_i - m_(i-1) = 10.
- input / output planes, sector angle: FIXED (Fig. 1 labels, psi value p.386).
- other arrays: FIXED count 5 identical, the second excited (p.386 col. 1, Fig. 3); pitch FIGURE about 0.50 mm.
  Column alignment / offsets not resolved: drawn aligned.
- substrate: FIXED silicon (p.385 col. 2); size not stated -> stand-in.
- he_ne_beam: FIXED broad, Gaussian, He-Ne, lambda printed 0.6320 um (common He-Ne line 0.6328 um; kept as printed and
  flagged). Launch method and width WITHHELD; drawn as a band over the input plane (assumed).
- straight_light: FIXED qualitatively ("propagate straight forward or be scattered", p.385 col. 1); Fig. 3 streak.
- free_region: existence from Fig. 3 (beams visible between array and focal line); makeup WITHHELD.
- focal_plane: FIXED f = 1 mm design position (p.386 col. 1, Fig. 3 dashed line). Axis through the output-plane
  midpoint perpendicular to the output plane: FIGURE (Fig. 3) + physics of a symmetric quadratic profile; centre of the
  profile not printed (said in the panel).
- foci: FIXED order separation 66 um, theoretical, measured "compares well" (no number). Count of orders WITHHELD;
  drawn 3 (illustrative, labelled).
- wavefront / dispersion: FIXED Eq. (4) d alpha ~ psi d beta / beta; "1 % -> 0.01 psi; with psi = pi roughly 2 deg";
  linear focus shift with constant focal length (p.385 col. 2). DERIVED for this array: 0.01 x 0.5236 rad = 5.2 mrad,
  about 5 um along the focal plane per 1 % at f = 1 mm. Direction DERIVED (not stated): longer wavelength -> toward the
  inner (smaller-radius) guides, from n_eff dL + n_s d sin(theta) = m lambda neglecting waveguide dispersion, as Eq. (4)
  does. Drawn shift exaggerated and labelled.
- demux_prototype: FIXED 1.3 x 4 mm, five channels, 0.5 % spacing, "good focusing properties", channel separation
  better than 20 dB, receiver waveguides next to each other in the focal plane (p.386 col. 2). WITHHELD: its array
  geometry (count, radii, psi, f), receiver positions / spacing / width, the five wavelengths and how they were
  produced ("working at He-Ne wavelength"), crosstalk definition, placement on a substrate, orientation. Drawn as an
  empty outline only. Note: with the Fig. 3 array parameters a 0.5 % step moves the focus only about 2.6 um (derived),
  less than one 3 um guide width, so the prototype must have used a different design; drawing receiver guides on the
  Fig. 3 array would be wrong.

3. PLOT DIGITIZATION
Nothing digitized: the letter prints no measured or calculated curve or table (Fig. 1 and Fig. 2 are geometry
schematics, Fig. 3 is a photograph). Results are text only: order spacing "compares well" with 66 um; channel
separation better than 20 dB.
Derived plot (coordinator ruling D1011, 2026-11-11), id `dispersion`, hooked to the "wavelength moves focus" chapter:
- Eq. (4), p.385 col. 2 (the ruling said p.386; Eq. (4) is on journal p.385): d alpha ~ psi d beta / beta, with the
  letter's own reading "a wavelength shift of 1 % will cause an angular dispersion of 0.01 psi" (|d beta / beta| =
  |d lambda / lambda|, waveguide dispersion neglected as the letter does).
- y = f psi (d lambda / lambda) with the printed psi = 30 deg = 0.5236 rad and f = 1 mm (p.386 col. 1, Fig. 3 arrays):
  5.236 um per 1 %. Linear, as the letter states ("shift linearly with df").
- Range +/-1 %, the shift the letter discusses; the 66 um order spacing lies far outside and is not drawn.
- Sampling: 61 points, every 1/30 % = 1/60 of the 2 % axis span, exact evaluation (no reads). End points
  (-1, -5.236), (+1, +5.236).
- Sign: positive = toward the inner (smaller-radius) guides for a longer wavelength; derived from
  n_eff dL + n_s d sin(theta) = m lambda, not stated in the letter; said in the plot note.
- Basis derived; plot note says computed from Eq. (4), not measured, and that it applies to the Fig. 3 arrays, not the
  prototype (whose design is not printed). Same convention as the Cobra Eq. (2) alternate.
Figure measurements used as checks (not plotted), Fig. 3 photograph, crop of img_p03_1.png at (380, 680)-(1390, 1360):
- Scale: "1 mm" bar spans x 568/569 to 698/699 px over rows 630-638 -> 130-131 px = 1 mm (+/- 2 px, 1.5 %).
- Five white areas (threshold > 215, connected components): centroids y = 330.9, 397.6, 461.5, 527.3, 593.2 px ->
  pitch 66.7, 63.9, 65.8, 65.9 px, mean 65.6 px = 0.50 mm (+/- 0.03). Widths 64-93 px (0.49-0.71 mm; the excited one
  is enlarged by its glow), heights 39-49 px (0.30-0.37 mm). Computed plan extent from the stated geometry: 545 x 302 um
  (x from 0 to 1090 sin 30 deg; y from -1090 to -788). Agrees.
- Outgoing beam: ridge of maximum brightness (9-px smoothing) at x 430-655 px fitted -> 28.8 deg above horizontal
  (rms scatter 15 px from halftone noise), close to psi = 30 deg; confirms beams leave perpendicular to the output plane.
- Dashed focal line: about 0.95-1.05 mm from the excited array's right end (line fit contaminated by the overlay
  lettering; distance 125 px from a robust fit, about 130-137 px by eye). Consistent with f = 1 mm.
- Orientation: in Fig. 3 the guides turn upward (beams rise to the right); Fig. 1 draws the mirror image. The spec uses
  the Fig. 3 orientation.
- Closure check (report only): the 66 um order spacing implies n_s = f lambda / (dx dR) = 1000 x 0.632 / (66 x 6) =
  1.60 in the region beyond the output plane (small-angle grating relation, output pitch = 6 um), a plausible index for
  an Al2O3 film guide; not used in the spec.
