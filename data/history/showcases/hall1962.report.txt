2. WHAT THE PAPER FIXES (fixed / assumed / withheld per part)
-----------------------------------------------------------
Letter, p.366 col. 1: "The diodes can be described approximately as cubes 0.4 mm on an edge, the junction lying in a
horizontal plane through the center. Current is passed through the junction by means of Ohmic contacts attached to
the top and bottom faces. The front and back faces are polished parallel to each other and perpendicular to the plane
of the junction ... Current is applied in the form of pulses of 5 to 20 µsec duration with the diode immersed in
liquid nitrogen." p.366 col. 2: "its image in the metal disc on which the diode was mounted". p.367 col. 1: L-70
measured "at 8420 Å on the axis of the beam"; linear below 5000 A/cm^2, rapid rise near 8500, about ten times the
extrapolated low-current intensity at 20 000; widths 125 Å below threshold, "suddenly to 15 Å" above. p.367 col. 2:
instrumental resolution about 3 Å; added modes at longer wavelengths (Fig. 3); faces "0.32 mm apart in this diode"
(L-70); mode spacing about three times the one-half-wavelength estimate. p.368: L-75 pattern almost uniform in azimuth,
a few tenths of a degree vertically; the two "first" statements (quoted in title_note).
NOT in the letter: zinc diffusion, wafer, doping numbers, solders, which side is p, side-face finish, disc size or
metal, leads, Dewar / optics layout, repetition rate, beam angles for L-69 / L-70, power or efficiency, temperature
in kelvin.
