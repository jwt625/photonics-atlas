2. WHAT THE PAPER FIXES
Cross-section (Fig. 1 caption and labels p.616; text p.615-616), basis stated unless noted:
- n-type crystalline silicon slab = the silicon layer of the SOI wafer: ~1.4 um, active doping ~1.7e16 cm^-3, grounded.
- Gate oxide 120 A (text p.617: "thin gate oxide (12 nm)"), width ~2.5 um = rib width.
- p-type polysilicon rib: width ~2.5 um, thickness ~0.9 um at the waveguide centre, active doping ~3e16 cm^-3.
  Amorphous Si by LPCVD at 550 C, crystallized by a high-temperature anneal (temperature not stated).
- "wide (~10.5 um) polysilicon layer on the top of the oxide layers on both sides of the polysilicon rib"; Al contacts on
  top of it, to the side of the rib. Surface doping 1e19 cm^-3 on both the Si slab and the polysilicon for ohmic
  contacts.
- Oxide regions on either side of the rib (confinement, keep the field off the contacts). Buried oxide ~0.375 um; oxide
  cover on top, "not shown in Fig. 1", thickness not stated.
- Fig. 1 labels (basis figure): "5 um" from the inner edge of the left n-Si contact region to the rib edge; "1 x 10^19"
  on the n-Si contact regions and on the outer polysilicon; V_D on a contact; ground at the left n-Si region; axes
  x across, y up, z out of the page (propagation).
- Operation: n-Si grounded, positive V_D on the p-poly; charge layers accumulate on both sides of the oxide; Eq. (1)
  dN = eps0 epsr (V_D - V_FB) / (e tox t); V_FB = 1.25 V and t = 10 nm from DESSIS device simulation; Eqs (2), (3)
  index changes at 1.55 um. epsr is named but its value is not printed (so no density number is shown; withheld).
- TE efficiency ~7x TM; single-mode around 1.55 um; ~9 % of TE power in the polysilicon (simulation).
Device and results:
- Phase shifter lengths 1, 2.5, 5, 8 mm (Fig. 2, 1.55 um, free-space interferometer); VpiL ~8 V cm with V_FB = 1.25 V.
- Asymmetric MZI with two identical shifters (Fig. 3a), optical path difference ~16.7 um, Y junctions; 10 mm shifters,
  1.54 um, drive on one shifter: ER > 16 dB at ~7.7 V peak-to-trough; IL ~15.3 dB on state = 2 x ~4.3 dB lensed-fibre
  coupling + ~6.7 dB on-chip (mainly doped and undoped polysilicon; waveguide loss by cutback, no value printed).
- High-frequency device: asymmetric MZI with one 2.5 mm shifter in one arm; 1.558 um; 3 V d.c. bias; 0.18 V r.m.s. sine;
  15 GHz photoreceiver; on-chip voltage monitored; response normalized by on-chip voltage; 3-dB roll-off "in excess
  of" 1 GHz. 1 Gbit/s PRBS with 3.5 V digital pulses on 3 V bias (Fig. 5).
- Outlook (p.618): single-crystal Si via epitaxial lateral overgrowth could cut ~5 dB of loss (modelling); smaller
  waveguide, thinner oxide, graded doping. Not in chapters.
