2. WHAT THE PAPER FIXES
Fixed (stated):
- Abstract (p.9383): e-beam-lithography grating couplers for polymer slab and strip waveguides; maximum input
  efficiency 67 %; replication by embossing; all-polymer chip; blazed profile and variable depth (Gaussian-like
  out-coupled profile); focusing blazed grating 42 % into a strip; curvature correction. No "first" claim.
- Sec. 2 (p.9383 col. 2 - p.9384 col. 1): PMMA copolymer film (< 1 µm) on quartz, resist = waveguide, monomodal at
  632.8 nm; thin gold or aluminium layer against charging; ZBA 23 (variable shaped beam) with dose rising stepwise
  across each period; Ni electroplating ~300 µm + ~3 mm Cu; PMMA plates ~2 mm, 150 °C, molded under pressure; PαMS
  spin-coated over the embossed grating (Fig. 1), n_f 1.603 at 633 nm, 450 nm film; LION LV 1 continuous path for
  curved lines; development dissolves by dose -> blazed; depth by development time; strips: 20 µm flanking areas
  fully dissolved -> ridge.
- Sec. 3 (p.9384-9385): chip = input + output grating, 20 mm waveguide (Fig. 2); I0 reflected / transmitted into
  orders; TE0 only; Eq. (1); Tamir-Peng; calculated alpha 1.16 /mm; Table 1 (Λ 800 nm, t_g 250 nm, blazed,
  1 x 1 mm2, λ 633 nm, n_s 1.457, n_f 1.498, n_c 1, t_f 800 nm); Table 2 (41 %, 95 %, 65/35, 0.6 dB/cm, 14 %);
  alpha 1.48 /mm; second inclination ~200 nm base; replica n_s 1.489; Table 3 (46 %, 75 %, "74/36", 1.2 dB/cm, 12 %);
  alpha 0.75 /mm; 74 % to the cover; one-order grating Λ 250 nm, t_g 150 nm rectangular, from the substrate side,
  ~2 mm plate, prism + immersion liquid (Fig. 3), 67 %.
- Sec. 4 (p.9386-9387): Eq. (3)-(7); Fig. 4 alpha(z); Fig. 5 t_g(z) calculated + SEM-measured; 2 mm grating in 25
  subareas of 80 µm; ~80 nm zero offset (Fig. 6); near-field set-up (constant-depth input grating, 20 mm waveguide,
  CCD); Fig. 7 right edge = grating start, left edge = end; fit ~0.9.
- Sec. 5 (p.9387-9389): Eq. (8)-(15); Δn_eff ≈ 5 x 10^-3; pair of focusing gratings 2f apart (Fig. 8), f = 4 mm,
  diffraction limit 5.3 µm; Table 4 (40 %, 85 %, 65/35, 12 / 7 µm); alpha 1.2 /mm; strip chip 15 µm x 2 mm, focal
  points at the strip ends (Fig. 9); Table 5 (42 %, 85 %, 70/30, 12 / 7 µm, throughput 5 / 10 %); 10 / 5 µm strips
  < 3 % / < 1 %.

5. CHAPTER SCRIPT RATIONALE (12 chapters, 3 plot chapters, height 12)
substrate, resist (resist = waveguide is the paper's enabling point), write (dose staircase per period), develop
(*sawtooth*: the paper's word; amber emphasis), couple ("In and out", Fig. 2 physics and Table 2 in hover), master and
replica (Fig. 1 sequence, Table 3), one_order (the 67 % headline, Fig. 3), graded (Fig. 5 plot, Fig. 4 alternate),
nearfield (Fig. 7 plot; model brightness follows the trace at the pen), focus (Fig. 9, the 42 % strip result),
efficiency (bar plot of all five input efficiencies; master vs replica table alternate). Essential dims (4): Λ 800 nm,
t_g 250 nm, waveguide 20 mm, strip width 15 µm. Physics notes: in-coupling forward at the input grating; guided power
decays only under the output grating; cover orders brighter; one-order coupler is fed from the substrate and its
guided wave runs back against the beam's in-plane direction; focusing: converging fan to the strip start, diverging
fan from the strip end to the second grating, output nearly parallel.
