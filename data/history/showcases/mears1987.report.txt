2. WHAT THE PAPER FIXES
- Setup (Fig. 1, p.1027; text p.1027 col. 1). Signal: "modulated GaInAsP laser diode" = "1·54 µm GaInAsP DCPBH
  laser", thermoelectrically tuned 1.532-1.540 µm, modulation limited to a few hundred MHz by the mount, most data
  at 140 Mbit/s. Launch optics as drawn: diode, lens, intermediate focus, dashed "variable density filter", lens,
  fibre end in an "index-matching cell". Text only (not drawn): single-mode fibre coupler, loss 0.5 dB, splitting
  ratio 10 dB at 1.54 µm, second port = monitor for signal power and wavelength; index-matched butt-splice into the
  doped fibre, loss < 1 dB, index-matching oil. Doped fibre: 3 m, NA 0.2, λcutoff 1.4 µm, dopant ≃1e19 cm^-3,
  drawn as one loop. Pump: "reverse-pumped longitudinally with light from a CW DCM-dye laser", Fig. 1 box
  "Ar+ion pumped DCM dye laser". Output: lens, collimated beam, "dichroic beam-splitter (90% R at 1·54 µm; 85% T at
  670 nm)": pump transmitted from the dye laser into the fibre, signal reflected to a lens and the "GaInAs detector"
  (biased PIN, 50 Ω load of a Tektronix sampling head or Marconi spectrum analyser).
- NOT in the setup: no fibre wavelength-division coupler (pump and signal are combined / separated by the bulk
  dichroic plate), no isolator (feedback suppressed by index matching). The story idea in the brief ("WDM coupler")
  is therefore replaced by the dichroic beam-splitter.
- Results: threshold "a little over 10 mW"; 20 dB for about 20 mW absorbed; gain saturates thereafter; maximum
  observed 28 dB; gains include the ≃1 dB splice loss (fibre/fibre gain) (p.1027 col. 1). Fig. 3 at 60 mW absorbed:
  saturation begins above -20 dBm input (+5 dBm output), 3 dB compression at +7 dBm output (p.1027 col. 1-2).
  Highest gains in a 300 GHz band centred on 1.536 µm; > 10 dB expected over 4 THz (30 nm) from the fluorescence;
  gain unimpaired for pump 655-675 nm; 670 nm diodes, the 800 nm band or Er/Yb codoping (930 nm) suggested
  (p.1027 col. 2 - p.1028 col. 1). Noise near 100 MHz 200 pA/√(Hz), -42 dBm at 140 Mbit/s for 10^-9 BER; ASE
  50 µW; -62 dBm suggested by shot-noise calculation (p.1028 col. 1).
- Figure numbering: Fig. 1 setup; Fig. 2 "Amplifier gain against pump power and wavelength" = gain vs PUMP
  WAVELENGTH (640-690 nm) for three absorbed powers 16 / 40 / 100 mW (the text calls it the pump-power dependence);
  Fig. 3 power transfer; Fig. 4 gain spectrum vs signal wavelength with fluorescence. The brief's "gain vs pump power
  (key figure Fig. 4)" mixes two figures: no printed gain-vs-pump-power curve exists. The spec shows Fig. 2 as printed
  and a derived "peak gain vs pump power" alternate (3 Fig. 2 maxima + the stated 20 dB / 20 mW pair).
- No "first" claim ("we demonstate a third approach", p.1027; "a new technique", p.1028).
- Fixed / assumed / withheld per part: see the spec (15 parts; every dim carries basis + locator). Assumed values:
  stand-in sizes only (diode block, cell, coupler box, splice sleeve, plate thickness, beam colours). 15 withheld
  entries (coil size and turns, core / host glass, pump wavelength of the Fig. 3 / 4 runs, launched pump, gain
  distribution along the fibre, coupler type and monitor destination, output-end lead fibre, lens data and real
  distances, incidence angle, cell / oil type, use of the density filter, laser models, diode package, Fig. 4 error
  bars, isolators = none).

5. CHAPTER SCRIPT RATIONALE (10 chapters, 3 plot chapters, height 9)
intro; signal (diode, launch optics, filter); launch (index matching and splice: the paper's anti-lasing care, its
own paragraph); fibre (3 m label); pump (reverse pumping: pump enters the far end through plate and output lens,
runs against the signal); gain (pump on first, signal grows only in the doped section, the amber "28 dB");
detect (dichroic: signal reflected to detector, pump transmitted); then Fig. 2 (pump power / wavelength, with the
derived gain-vs-power alternate), Fig. 3 (power handling), Fig. 4 (gain spectrum; the milestone's key figure).
Drives follow the plot pen (D1006): Fig. 2 pump wavelength and series power, output from the digitized curve; Fig.
3 and Fig. 4 step through the measured points only, no interpolated states, the dashed guides never drive the model.
Essential dims: fibre length 3 m, maximum gain 28 dB, signal 1.54 µm. One amber emphasis: "*28 dB* gain".
Signal colour is illustrative (1.54 µm is infrared), labelled in the part detail and dims.
