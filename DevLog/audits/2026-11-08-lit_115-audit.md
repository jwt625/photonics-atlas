## Coordinator questions

1. velha2016 building-block values (2DGC 9.7 dB, TEGC 2.7 dB, MMI 0.5 dB, Ge PD 10 nA / 0.9 A/W): this chip's measurements or design-kit
   values? The paper does not say. Evidence: (a) p.2 puts "taken from the photonic design kit" in the sentence immediately before "the
   performances of the individual building blocks were determined"; (b) nothing in the paper shows a building-block measurement (no GC
   spectrum, no PD I-V, no MMI test); the only building-block test described (p.2, a 2DGC with two TEGCs) is a polarimeter trajectory check with
   no loss number; (c) round PD values and a GC bandwidth stated once for "the grating coupler". Nothing supports `measured`, and
   `specification` is for vendor datasheet values of purchased parts (D161), which a PDK origin would not confirm either. Recommendation:
   `author_estimate` with "origin not stated" (rule 4, D44; D418 nader2025 precedent), rows kept (the values describe these building blocks
   either way). Patched; to keep `measured`, drop the five metric ops and the replaced component-note sentence.

   page for imec ISIPP25G. Recommendation: process_name with the printed terms (patched) and Europractice to the org round as a fab org next to
   org_imec (Contract v7 / D398 / D439 MPW access provider named for the fabrication run). Alternative: Europractice appears only in the
   reference list, so treat it as not named for fabrication and keep fab org_imec only (process_name change stands either way).
