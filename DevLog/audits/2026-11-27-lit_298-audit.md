## Points checked (ok)

- hamrouni2024 m1 coupling ~1.5 %: p.3 "The waveguide used throughout the article achieves 1.5% of coupling efficiency"; p.4 gives
  it as the product of the objective transmission (~15 %) and the focus-to-mode overlap (~10 %), with "~1.5%"; neither term's
  origin is stated and no measurement of either is described (the 45 pJ on-chip maximum and every on-chip energy rest on the
  1.5 %). `author_estimate` per rule 4 (loss-budget terms of unstated origin) and D418 (nader2025, D167 not extended):
  agree. The p.3 statement alone would read as D167 `measured`, but p.4 shows the arithmetic and its inputs, which decides it.
  Scope `per_facet`, includes the objective transmission (a setup optic): correct and visible in `includes`. qualifier approx
  (p.4 "~"), 2090 nm inferred from the OPO (p.3-4), TE / TE00 (camera check of the TE00 mode, p.4), method loss_breakdown.
- hamrouni2024 m2 ~10 % overlap: `author_estimate`, `excludes` the objective; ok.
- hamrouni2024 m3-m5: p.3 Sec. 2 "top width of 1520 nm, a film thickness of 931 nm and an etch depth of 608 nm", labelled in
  Fig. 1(a) ("Schematic of the waveguide design"); no SEM / AFM: `design_target` (D402 for the film). Platform `lnos_rib`
  (931 nm film, 608 nm etch leaves a slab; sapphire substrate in Fig. 1(a)). On the coupler row these describe the facet
  cross-section (no taper stated); the edge_taper template takes `core_thickness_nm` and optional `width_nm` /
  slab, so the drawing is not guessing. Class `fiber_chip_coupler.edge` for an objective-coupled facet follows evans2015.
- hamrouni2024 poled waveguide not a row: Sec. 2-5 print no conversion efficiency, propagation loss, Q or pump threshold;
  the 360-2660 nm span, harmonic onsets (2 / 7 / 12 pJ), 22 pJ FH-SH overlap, simulated and deduced GVM / GVD and the f-2f
  SNR have no registry parameter; 5 mm length and the 6.30 +/- 0.135 um poling range would make a geometry-only row (rule
  9). Agree. The ~35 % output collection efficiency is from ref. [30] (rule 3). Yield "close to 100%": no parameter.
- hamrouni2024 fab empty: "We periodically pole" / "We fabricate" (p.2-3) with authors at three orgs (Neuchatel, Stanford,
  NTT Research; the first author holds both Neuchatel and Stanford), so the authors' org is not unambiguous (rule 12;
  evans2015 had all authors at Cornell). "procedures are similar to the one described in [29]" is a method citation, not a
  fab statement. Facilities: acknowledgment "Fabrication was performed at the Stanford Nanofabrication Facility, the Stanford
  Nano Shared Facilities ..., and the Cell Sciences Imaging Facility" (p.7): SNF + SNSF correct for a 2024 paper (pre-merger
  ids, D80d). NTT Research "financial and technical support": not a facility (D80c).
- kim2005 TA m1 1 W -> 30 dBm `derived` (D256), statistic best ("maximum average optical power"), 2.5 A DC, 16 mW input:
  this is the TA's own output (Fig. 4: TA block with its lenses, then a mirror); the ASE caveat is in the condition. ok.
- kim2005 TA m2 gain 17.96 dB = 10 log10(1000 / 16): both powers are at the TA's own planes (output after the TA, input as
  delivered to the TA), so it is the TA's external (lens-to-lens) gain, not a system value; it is a total-power ratio and the
  1 W includes an unquantified ASE share, as the note says. `derived` (arithmetic on printed powers, D166 / cheung2015). ok.
- kim2005 TA m3 3 mm -> 3000 um `derived` (D157); 974 nm inferred on all rows from the oscillator / CFBG centre (p.2, p.5). ok.
- kim2005 out-of-scope items agree with D706 (resan2005 / kim2004): CPMLL is a bench-top external cavity with HR, OC, slit,
  discrete MQW SA (Fig. 2(a)); IBTSOA in the cavity and as pulse picker has no value by itself; CFBG ~1600 ps/nm has no
  parameter (dispersion) and its 6 nm band no level; 56 % SMF coupling through aspheric / cylindrical / 40X objective optics
  is bulk optics; 194 mW, 2.0 nJ, 0.59 ps, 1.4 kW are system outputs; ref. [10] 238 nJ / 13000x is the authors' earlier
  LEOS paper (rule 3 / D259).
- fan2017 0 rows: p.6 Sec. 3 "25 mm long, 8.5 mm wide, and 1mm thick 5% MgO:PPLN" with seven gratings in an oven, focused
  with f = 200 mm / 100 mm lenses, 60 um waist: a bulk crystal in free space, no waveguide; D411 (kourogi1993, zhang2025e

## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. fan2017 0 rows (judgment a). **Agree.** Bulk 25 mm MgO:PPLN crystal pumped in free space; nothing chip-scale or
   fiber-coupled passive is characterized (D411).
2. hamrouni2024 coupling basis (judgment b). **Keep both rows `author_estimate`.** p.4 states 1.5 % as the product of two
   "~" terms without saying how either was obtained (D418; D167 not extended when the arithmetic is shown). Keep the 1.5 % row
   with the objective in `includes`: it is the only end-to-end input figure and every on-chip energy in the paper is
   referred through it; the 10 % overlap row is the facet-only term.
3. hamrouni2024 cross-section on the coupler row and the poled waveguide (judgment b). **Keep as entered**, plus the 78 deg
   sidewall (patched). A `frequency_converter.shg` row would hold only geometry (rule 9). If the registry later adds a
   supercontinuum-span or onset-energy parameter (gaps 2-3 below), the waveguide becomes a row and the cross-section should
   move to it with a cross-note.
4. hamrouni2024 fab / facilities (judgment b). **Fab empty, facilities SNF + SNSF: agree.** Multi-org "We fabricate" (rule 12);
   all three acknowledged facilities are in one "Fabrication was performed at" sentence; Cell Sciences Imaging Facility goes
   to the org round (facility role).
5. kim2005 preamplifier row (judgment c). **Delete (patched).** The 16 mW is measured after an isolator and a BPF (Fig. 4), so
   it is not the RWGSOA's output; only a "at least 16 mW" bound could be inferred, which the paper does not state. This
   matches D706 (no value of a purchased Sarnoff SOA alone). Alternative, if the coordinator prefers to keep the chip: keep
   m1 with `qualifier: ge` and a note that the plane is the TA input after OI + BPF; the patch would then drop the
   delete_components op and add that metric change.
6. kim2005 TA rows (judgment c). **Keep.** 1 W and 16 mW are the TA's own output and input planes; 17.96 dB is a derived
   total-power gain with the ASE caveat in the notes. Platform empty (no substrate printed); core material InGaAs now from
   p.2 (patched).
7. Sarnoff (judgment c). **No fab on any remaining row.** Sarnoff provided only the IBTSOA and RWGSOA (p.7), neither of which is
   a row after the patch; the TA maker is not stated. Keep "Sarnoff" on the org-round list as in D706 (it becomes a fab org
   only if a Sarnoff-chip row is added later, e.g. via pulse_energy_nj on amplifier for kim2004 / kim2005).
