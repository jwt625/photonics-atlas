## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. hao2019 tri-tilted "higher than -15 dB" (judgment a): keep as printed (`crosstalk_db` 15 `lt`). The typo reading is not compelled:
   at 90 % conversion up to 10 % of the power sits outside TE1, and a largest interfering mode above about 2.8 % of the input (crosstalk
   above -15 dB) is physically consistent; the paragraph frames the tri-tilted slot as a trade-off (different etch depth, shorter
   length). Strict alternative: delete m2 and keep the sentence in the component note (D159-style), if the coordinator reads the
   direction as ambiguous.
2. hao2019 Fig. 7 tolerance bounds as conditions on the TE0-TE2 row at 1550 nm inferred (judgment a): keep. Sec. 4 perturbs the Table 1
   design, optimized and reported at 1550 nm (Table 1 heading, Fig. 6); one physical design with operating points (rule 9).
3. hao2019 reflection < -35.0 dB (judgment a): keep out; `back_reflection_db` is limited to fiber_chip_coupler / chip_chip_coupler /
   micro_optics / fiber_interface (registry gap, already a recurring candidate for crossing / mode_converter / splitter).
4. hao2019 "as high as 90 %" -> `statistic: best`: keep (rule 7 analogue).
5. gupta2017 EL 1-1.5 dB on the WIDC family row (judgment b): keep. The sentence follows the D1 / D2 discussion, but the next sentence
   assigns the EL to the WIDC bend regions and the following paragraph says the deep-access WIDC EL "is reduced to 0.8 dB", which only
   reads as a comparison with shallow-access WIDCs. D1 (W 550 nm) is a different design with no row.
6. gupta2017 SEM W ~360 / G ~150 nm on the family row (judgment b): keep. W matches the D2 cross-section (350 nm), and Fig. 11(a) shows
   a short coupled section (a family member, not a 500 um test DC).
7. gupta2017 fab IIT Madras on all 10 rows (judgment b): keep. First person "we fabricated few test structures ... All the devices are
   defined ... EBL ... ICP-RIE" (p.5), all three authors at IIT Madras (rule 12); the 1x4 sentence ties its devices to the same flow
   ("as described earlier", p.6), the MZI uses "same WIDC design" and the rings the same two-step etch (p.7); one in-house flow (D656,
   D709). The GC row is covered by the same p.5 paragraph explicitly.
8. gupta2017 measured 3-dB length ~5 um in `coupling_length_um` measured (judgment b): keep; the condition text says it is the
   experimental 3-dB length (the lDC giving 50 %, Fig. 13(f) at 1550 nm), not a drawn dimension. Optional: add "Fig. 13(f), 1550 nm" to
   the condition.
9. gupta2017 Fig. 18 ring radius (judgment b): keep out (D159). "Larger ring radii (e.g., 100 um) were fabricated" is generic; a
   scale-bar estimate is not allowed (Contract v5). The 0.73 nm spacing is the Fig. 18(c) reading and is not cross-checked against a
   radius.
10. gupta2017 CNNP facility (judgment b): keep empty. "Carried out within the scope of the Centre for NEMS and Nanophotonics (CNNP) at
    IIT Madras, sponsored by MeitY" (p.1 footnote) is program wording (D80c), not "performed at" / "made use of". The lit_248 ruling
    (priyadarshini2024, D717) set CNNP as a facility on an explicit "fabricated using in-house facilities at the Centre" sentence, which
    this paper does not have. If the coordinator prefers lab-level consistency, set it only after the org round creates the id.
11. gupta2017 MZI / ring figure reads (judgment b): keep the figure-read approach; three readings corrected (table above).
12. morino2014 (judgment c): keep `split_ratio_pct` 48.98-51.29 % over the printed spans (a 0.2 dB window is not a 1-dB width; the
    bw_1db_nm precedents chen2022a / chung2023 / lu2015a hold +/-0.5 and +/-1 dB criteria, rule 5); keep the 43.5-50.5 % conventional-DC
    row on the LS 2.10 um design (the only conventional design in the paper, "designed for 50 % coupling" at ~1.55 um; Fig. 7 reads
    about 49.8 % at 25 C, consistent); keep platform empty (Si wire buried in SiO2 on a Si substrate, Fig. 2; SOI / BOX never stated;
