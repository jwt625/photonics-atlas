## Rulings on the batch report's judgment calls

- nan2025 fab org_east_china_normal_university: kept under D84a (the process is the authors' own, "detailed
  processes can be found in our earlier work", p.4; method developed by the authors, p.1-3); borderline as noted.
- nan2025 interlayer coupler not entered: endorsed. D80e would allow a fabricated row with simulated values, but
  Fig. 3(b)-(e) mixes dB/cm loss axes and "coupler" labels with a caption describing the crossing, so no value can
  be assigned to a component without guessing (D104 honesty).
- nan2025 p.6 "0.02 dB" vs Table 1 / abstract 0.002 dB: typo, not entered; endorsed.
- bock2010 fab empty: endorsed. "We used electron beam lithography to fabricate our structures" (p.6) satisfies D80b,
  but the authors are at NRC Canada (8) and the University of Ottawa (2, one dual-affiliated) and the place of
  fabrication is not stated (D72 unambiguity, D83c).
- nevlacsil2020 "~0.05 dB for all configurations" entered on each of the three MMI crossings: endorsed (one value
  stated for three physical designs; D46 concerns bands, not designs).
- nevlacsil2020 fab: "fabricated by ams AG on their SiN photonic platform" (p.6) is a rule-12 fab statement; blocked
  only by the missing registry org (see questions).
- wang2026 "excess loss" entered as crossing_loss_db: endorsed by the registry definitions (crossing_loss_db is the
  class-specific per-crossing loss); but see question 1 on the layout template.
- wang2026 abstract -0.048 dB treated as simulated: endorsed (Table III "-0.048 (sim.)").
- elsaeed2024 spans read from the plot axes with wavelength_inferred: endorsed (D46).
- huang2019a fab org_city_university_of_hong_kong: kept, borderline ("we fabricated ... with our in-house
  microfabrication facilities", p.2; three of four authors CityU, one UESTC), as D84b.
- qi2025 fab org_stanford_university with a Sandia co-author ("We fabricate", p.3) and SNSF + SNF facilities
  ("Device fabrication was performed at", p.7; 2025 paper, time-correct per D80d): endorsed.

  15 h bake not entered as retention (D83g): endorsed.
- xu2022 TE0-TE1 asymmetric coupler as splitter.directional_coupler: acceptable (dc template geometry; mode
  conversion in label / condition); see question 4.
- song2021a R 2 um bend losses as measured: endorsed; Fig. 2 shows the 0.078 dB normal-bend value matching the

## Open questions for the coordinator

1. Layout template `mmi` applies to crossing.mmi with primary `excess_loss_db` (per_component), but every crossing row
   in lit_05 (the first crossing data in the atlas) uses `crossing_loss_db`. Either widen the template primary for
   crossing.mmi to crossing_loss_db or repoint the crossing rows; recommendation: keep crossing_loss_db (registry
   definition "insertion loss per crossing") and change the template.
2. Registry orgs: add ams AG (Premstaetten, AT; name as printed "ams AG", nevlacsil2020 p.1 / p.6 Sec. 3), then set
   fab_org_ids on the six nevlacsil2020 components. Add National Research Council Canada (bock2010 p.1;
   org_national_research_council is the Italian CNR); fab stays empty unless the coordinator rules the NRC
   majority unambiguous. Grace Semiconductor Manufacturing Corporation (sheng2012 p.2) is not needed: no rule-12
   fab statement names it ("fabricated ... with 0.13-um CMOS technology", p.5, passive, line unnamed).
3. xu2022 `fp_retro_series` holds array-level values (regression over cavities of taper length 1.0-4.5 mm; maximum
   loaded Q of an unidentified cavity) beside the 1.5 mm and 4.0 mm rows. No decision covers array-level rows; keep as
   is or move the values to one of the design rows with a note.
4. xu2022 asymmetric TE0-TE1 coupler: splitter.directional_coupler (current) vs mode_converter.converter.
