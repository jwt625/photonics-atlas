## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. he2018 judgment (a). **Keep `mode_converter.other`** (D567; `mode_filter` subtype is the open registry candidate there).
   **Keep `le` on 1.0 / 2.3 dB**: p.6 prints the pair "in the wavelength range", the abstract bounds it "<=2.3 dB" and p.7
   "lower than 2.3 dB", and the Fig. 6(b) traces reach about 1.0 dB (TE0) and 2.3-2.5 dB (TE2, short-wavelength edge) at
   their worst. **Keep 25.6 dB `gt`** from "below -25.6 dB" (rule 7 maps "below" to a bound): Fig. 6(b) shows the worst
   in-band TE1 transmission about -28 dB near 1552.5 nm, so 25.6 dB is a conservative lower bound either way; `ge` would
   also be defensible by analogy with the TE0 row's printed ">=", but no rule prefers it over the printed "below". The
   reference level of both extinction ratios is not defined in the text (noted). Storing the abstract's "crosstalk" as
   `extinction_db` is right: the value is the suppression of the blocked mode (extinction_db def for mode_converter:
   mode-rejection / extinction ratio).
2. he2018 judgment (b), (de)MUX 0.7 / 1.3 dB. **Keep `measured`, scope and method `unspecified`, C band inferred.** The
   sentence carries no citation and sits in the experimental section about devices on this chip, which has (de)MUX-only
   reference structures #4, #9, #10 (Fig. 5); D167 treats a directly stated value of the paper's own device as `measured`
   without shown isolation (the same paper's GC 7.0 dB/port is handled that way). `author_estimate` is for values resting on
   an assumed, nominal or cited input, which is not shown here; the ref. [11] citation is for the design and the tapers only.
   Benchmark effect nil (no foundry; the rows carry the university fab).
3. he2018 judgment (c). **Keep fab `org_shanghai_jiao_tong_university`** ("We fabricated the devices" p.5, first person, all
   four authors at SJTU: rule 12) **and facility `org_center_for_advanced_electronic_materials_and_devices`** ("for the
   support in device fabrications", p.7): D609 zhang2019e (fab SJTU / facility AEMD), D733 xu2021c (AEMD kept as borderline,
   D80c), D708 wang2022c, D762 zhang2016a precedents; AEMD is a registered child of SJTU.
4. xu2013 judgment (d). **Keep**: 84 % `simulated` ("calculated", no measured counterpart), 1550 nm inferred with the
   reason in notes (rule 8; the design targets a resonance near 1550 nm); resonance wavelengths `extracted_from_figure`
   (my reads 1582.9-1583.0 and 1589.1 nm agree within the stated uncertainty); fab empty (D80b, D433). The only change is
