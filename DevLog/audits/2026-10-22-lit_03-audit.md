## Open questions for the coordinator

- Q1 brisson2023 fab_org_ids org_cea_leti: "We start the fabrication ..." (p.1) qualifies under D80b, but the authors are
  at CEA-Leti (4) and CEA-IRIG (2) and the paper does not say which unit fabricated; mapping the 200 mm line to Leti is
  outside knowledge. Options: keep org_cea_leti as borderline (note already says so), use the common parent org_cea, or
  empty (as for qi2024, authors at three orgs). Recommendation: org_cea or empty for consistency with qi2024.
- Q2 meng2023 amplitude-only (k-modulation) non-volatile PCM memory as phase_shifter.phase_change: no attenuator /
  amplitude-modulator class exists. Keep (nearest class, note present) or add a subtype such as
  phase_shifter.pcm_amplitude / a variable-attenuator class.
- Q3 qi2024 back-illuminated discrete InGaAs/InP SPAD in a butterfly package, PDE referenced to the MMF module input
  (ref_plane module), platform `inp`: in scope? The registry has photodetector.spad and photodetector.vertical
  (song2021 is likewise a discrete top-illuminated PD), so the rows validate; D80f covers fiber-only passive parts only.
  Recommendation: keep, and state in the contract that discrete fiber-coupled detector chips are in scope.
- Q4 (optional miss, not patched) song2021 Fig. 3(e) responsivity labels: 0.64 A/W points to the 30 um Si curve and its
  slope matches (about 2.6 mA at 4 mW), but the 0.57 label arrow points to the 20 um Si curve whose slope is about
  0.48 A/W, and the InP labels conflict with the text (40 um InP 0.57 A/W p.2 vs 0.51 label). The distiller left all
  out; entering only si_px30 0.64 A/W (measured, label) is defensible. Coordinator call.
