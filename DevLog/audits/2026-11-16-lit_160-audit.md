## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. siddharth2024 headline values on a kappa-unassigned row (D159). Recommendation: keep the batch layout (unassigned row
   edbr_8mm_piezo with threshold / power / SMSR / wavelength / tuning, three per-kappa rows with the linewidths only). The
   headline device is identified as a physical device (the 8 mm piezo E-DBR laser of chip D116_02_F9_C9, Fig. 2(a), (d),
   (f), Fig. 3(f)); only its grating strength is unstated. D159 deletes a value that could belong to either of two existing
   rows; here no per-kappa row can claim the values, and the separate unnamed-device row has a precedent (matsuo2013
   dynamic row, D422). Moving the values to notes (strict D159, as lihachev2022 D431, where no device was named at all)
   would drop the paper's main results; folding the linewidths into one row breaks rule 9. Caveat recorded in the row
   note (patch): the paper does not say the Sec. III and Fig. 3(f) values come from one grating, so the row is a
   design-family row of the chip (D165 spirit). The benchmark will show four 8 mm E-DBR laser rows for at most three
   devices.
2. siddharth2024 fab / facility. Recommendation: keep fab as batch (EPFL + Purdue on the 8 rows of the AlN-actuator chip:
   three 8 mm gratings, three per-kappa lasers, edbr_8mm_piezo, actuator; EPFL only elsewhere). The Purdue author made the
   actuators on the EPFL Si3N4 chip, the post-processed-chip clause of Contract v7 (D440 / D419) and the liu2020a precedent
   (same orgs, both on every row of the actuator chip) support it. Facility: the p.8 acknowledgment ("The chip samples were
   fabricated in the EPFL CMi, and in the Birck Nanotechnology Center at Purdue University") is a stated place (D448),
   not staff thanks (D84b / D72 do not apply), so both are facilities; but the two sites map to the two fabrication steps
   of the p.7 contributions. Birck should therefore follow Purdue: the patch removes Birck from the 7 EPFL-only rows (SIL
   chip, its grating and spiral, heater laser, the two tapers that exist on every chip, OFDR waveguide) and keeps CMi
   everywhere. To keep the batch reading (Birck on all rows), drop those 7 component ops.
3. siddharth2024 OFDR loss "1 dB/cm ... 2 dB/cm" (Supp. Sec. 5, p.15) vs Fig. S5(c) axis "Loss (dB/m)". Recommendation:
   keep 0.01 / 0.02 dB/cm `extracted_from_figure`, approx. The render shows points of 0.7-1.55 dB/m near 186-195 THz (1600 nm
   = 187.4 THz) and 1.9-2.6 dB/m near 225-237 THz (1300 nm = 230.6 THz); the main text gives 2.5 dB/m for the 5 um spiral
   (p.6) and dB/cm values would be 100 times that. The figure is the unit authority, so the figure basis is right; the
   printed text and the conflict stay in notes (D417).
4. wei2023 fab and 7.35 dB. Recommendation: keep both. Fab: p.14 contributions name Y. Bo (laser device) and M. Ming (FIB),
   Institute of Physics CAS, and A. He (design and fabrication of the SOI template), Shanghai Jiao Tong University: D395 /
   D439 (he2025), SJTU only on the separately fabricated fork-coupler test structure, IOP only on the HR coating. 7.35 dB:
   10 log10(6.8 / 37) = 7.357 dB, i.e. the WG-coupled maximum (210 mA, HR rear facet, FIB front facet) against the
   double-cleaved reference laser's maximum (250 mA, other device, no HR); it rests on the assumption that the reference
   laser's output equals the integrated laser's emitted power, so `author_estimate` (rule 4), not `derived`.
5. tang2021 fab and tuning range. Recommendation: keep fab empty: "The Si3N4 waveguide platform is used as our previous
   work [19]" with passive fabrication steps is the "our platform" case of D84a, not a fabrication statement (D80b; D433
