## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. (a) woods2020 insertion losses 19.6 / 20.0 / 25.6 / 23.4 dB, `device_total`, mean over 900-1500 nm, `derived`.
   **Keep as entered.** D80a: arithmetic on the source's own measurements is `derived` whether the batch or the authors did it;
   the printed dB values are the paper's conversion of two measured powers (mean collected power vs the ~110 mW on the facet).
   Scope `device_total` is right: free-space launch (40x objective), propagation and output collection, with no coupler
   separation (p.6 says coupling and propagation were not deconvolved). Mean-of-powers vs mean-of-dB is immaterial at this
   precision.
2. (b) GNLSE free-parameter losses. **Drop the clad 3-4 dB/cm, keep the unclad 5-6.5 dB/cm as `author_estimate`** (patched).
   The clad range is the window the authors allowed the free parameter to take (p.3 "we use", p.9 "variable parameters ...
   between 3 and 4 dB/cm"); the fits land on its 3 dB/cm floor in 7 of 9 subfigures, so the data do not determine it (D468:
   model input is not a device value; Fig. 7's 2 dB/cm already out). The unclad sentence reports the matching outcome; rule 4
   puts a result resting on a cited n2 and free coupled power at `author_estimate`. Alternative if the coordinator prefers one
   treatment: drop both (the unclad fits are not shown, and coupled power is degenerate with loss); either way the clad row keeps
   its insertion-loss metrics.
3. (c) woods2020 fab `org_university_of_southampton`. **Keep.** Rule 12 / D80b need a first-person fabrication statement plus an
   unambiguous own org: "Our fabrication process ... we deposit" and "waveguides that we have fabricated" with all authors in two
   units of one university (D154 parent org; ORC is a registry alias of the university). The 1.4 dB/cm "comparable" guide shares
   the row-level fab: it is measured in the fabrication paragraph and described with the same "our measurements".
4. (d) park2007a 5 dB facet and dependents `author_estimate`. **Keep** (gain 9.5 dB, IQE 50-55 % / 55 %, receiver 5.7 A/W, taper
   ~0.6 and 0.6-1.2 dB). The facet value rests on an unprinted reference insertion loss and an unstated facet / propagation split
   (D462 / D514 equal-facet assumption, D583 park2007); the taper loss also rests on a full-absorption assumption. Spectral width
   and 0.5 dB compression stay `measured` (relative quantities).
5. (e) RC-limited bandwidth 7.5 GHz. **Keep out** (notes only). It is a calculation, not the device response; the measured 3 GHz
   (hole-transport limited) is the bandwidth row. A second `bw3db_ghz` row at 7.5 GHz would read as the detector bandwidth in
   any best-value view. The capacitance used is not stated: 1 / (2 pi (50 + 80 ohm) C) gives 7.2 GHz with 0.17 pF and 4.1 GHz
   with 0.3 pF (audit arithmetic). ozdemir2021 entered a computed RC bandwidth only where no measured bandwidth existed.
6. (f) park2007a fab empty. **Keep empty.** Every fabrication sentence is passive, authors span UCSB and Intel, the dicing thanks
   names a person without an org (D80b, D84b, D154; park2007 precedent).
