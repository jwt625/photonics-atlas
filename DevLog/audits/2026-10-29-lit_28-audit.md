## Rulings on the batch report's judgment calls

- bauters2013 "LioniX BV" (p.8 Acknowledgment, 40 nm waveguide fabrication): renames / mergers are separate
  organizations in this registry; the registry holds only org_lionix_international (LioniX International BV), not the
  2013 entity. Not mapped by name; the five 40 nm rows keep fab_org_ids empty with the component note; reported for the
  org round.
- per_db vs extinction_db: per_db = "polarization extinction ratio, positive dB"; extinction_db = "notch depth, stopband
  contrast or on/off extinction". bauters2013 reports "on-chip polarization extinction" (p.6-7) of TE-pass polarizers and
  chen2021 the per-port polarization crosstalk (TM0- vs TE0-input power at one output, p.4): both are polarization
  extinction ratios, so per_db is right and crosstalk_db for polarization is not needed. Merged polarization.psr rows
  already use per_db (3 rows). Merged rows that disagree (follow-up, not edited here):
  `cmp_lit_wang2014_grating_polarizer_te-m1` (extinction_db 25, "polarizer extinction ratio") and
  `cmp_lit_wang2014_grating_polarizer_tm-m1` (extinction_db 30) -> per_db.
- nauriyal2019 arXiv v1 preprint: the source row carries arxiv_id 1810.09531 and access `arxiv`; values from the
  preprint are allowed (DevLog-011 merge item 8 analogue); the component note states it. Endorsed. Fab
  org_university_of_rochester ("We fabricate", p.4, all authors Rochester) and facility org_cornell_nanoscale_facility
  ("performed in part at", p.8) endorsed.
- bauters2013 org_aurrion on the four 100 nm rows: the acknowledgment thanks Aurrion, Inc. "for ... 100-nm-thick
  waveguide fabrication" (p.8) and p.6 places the 100 nm cores in a CMOS foundry; an org thanked for performing the
  fabrication is a fab statement, borderline under D84b; kept with the note.
- rasel2026 fiber_chip_coupler.photonic_wire_bond for fiber-PWB-fiber bonds with no chip, one row per offset: endorsed
  (sibling geometries, skill rule 9). Power handling 29 dBm gets qualifier gt (registry definition); the 20 dBm / 18-day
  row stays without qualifier (a tested level, not a limit). Fab empty (tool named, passive "fabricated", four orgs).
- gradkowski2023: link values on the connector row and lens tolerances on a micro_optics row endorsed (D396 spirit);
  loopback_half derived for per-coupler values endorsed, extended to m6 / m7 and the processed tolerances (patched).
  Table I values verified on the page_07 render (1.68 / 1.94 / 2.15 / 2.54 dB). "Lionix" fab of the reference PIC
  (2023, p.3) -> org_lionix_international endorsed.
