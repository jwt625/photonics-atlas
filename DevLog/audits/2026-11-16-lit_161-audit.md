## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. chen2023c asymmetric DC class. Recommend keep `splitter.directional_coupler` (+ `adc_ps` actuator, D396). The registry
   subtype reads "directional coupler (straight, bent, tunable)"; the paper uses the device as a "tunable beam splitter" /
   "quasi-continuously tunable directional coupler" with 32 levels (p.12, p.15), matching the same group's chen2024a qc_dc.
   chen2022c's GST 2x2 three-waveguide coupler was framed and used as a binary switch (D579, `switch_fabric.other`). The
   p.8 heading says "directional coupler switch": the `switch` tag keeps it findable.
2. chen2023c 40 um trimming MZI as `phase_shifter.phase_change`. Recommend keep. Its demonstrated function is
   non-volatile phase-error correction; the registry allows interferometer extinction on phase shifters; chen2024a MZIs used
   to show PCM phase steps are phase_shifter rows. A `switch_fabric.other` + actuator pair would duplicate the 30 um switch
   pattern with no switching data (only one direction of multilevel trimming).
3. Switching events halved to cycles. Recommend keep (`derived`): the paper itself equates 1,150 cycles with 2,300 switching
   events (p.33 Fig. S9 caption) and the parameter counts cycles (zheng2020 ring precedent, D586). Merged inconsistency to
   fix in a follow-up: chen2024a qc_dc_pcm-m1 holds 5,000 switching events as `endurance_cycles` 5000 `measured` (its own
   caption equates 52,600 events with 26,300 cycles) and sbs_mzi_60um-m4 holds 500 events; recommend 2,500 / 250 `derived`
   with the equivalence in notes, or an explicit ruling that events are entered as printed.
4. MZI "bar state" text vs figure. Recommend keep the condition as staged (figure: cross port high after SET, Fig. 2(f);
   the device starts in the bar state, Fig. 2(e), so a "complete spectrum flip" can only end in the cross state; the p.6
   wording is the error), with the D417 conflict note.
5. SNSF facility on every chen2023c row. Recommend keep. Rule 12 sets a facility for "a shared facility where the work was
   performed" and lists "performed at" as the trigger; "Part of this work was performed at the Stanford Nano Shared Facilities
   (SNSF)" (p.18) meets it and is not staff thanks (D84b / D72). D448 concerns where samples were processed (fab); it does not
   require a per-component facility statement, and facility is not per-component in Contract v7 (only fab is). The paper does
   not say which part was done at SNSF; the Stanford co-authors measured thin-film thermal conductivity (p.18), but that link
   is not stated and is not used. Same reading as merged chen2024a. Benchmark impact nil (rows have a fab; the
   affiliation-foundry rule needs no fab and no facility). The parallel Washington Nanofabrication Facility / Molecular
   Analysis Facility statement waits on the org round (below).
6. Fab `org_university_washington` from author contributions. Recommend keep: "R.C. simulated and fabricated the Sb2S3 silicon
   photonic devices" (p.18) names the fabricating author, whose only affiliation is the University of Washington (p.1)
   (D395 / D399; rule 12 "the authors' own org when the paper says the authors fabricated it and that org is unambiguous");
   Z.F. and J.Z. who "helped" are also UW only. Same as merged chen2022c / zheng2020.
7. shahin2025a. Recommend keep: Fig. 4 ranges verified (above), `extracted_from_figure` with min-max and n_samples 6 from the
   caption; `process_name` "imec 300-mm silicon photonics platform" is the printed "imec's 300-mm silicon photonics platform"
   (p.1) minus the possessive, fab `org_imec` from the same sentence (D455; merged shahin2025 prints "imec 300-mm Si-Pho
   platform"). Merged imec 300 mm rows carry 10+ spellings ("imec iSiPP300", "imec 300 mm SiPh platform", ...); grouping is a
   benchmark-config matter, not a distill fix.
