## Rulings on the batch report's judgment calls

1. konoike2020 (D259a): endorsed. p.1 Sec. I says the 8x8 switch and its 5.7 +/- 0.75 dB / ~1.8 dB results were
   demonstrated in ref. [12] (OFC 2020 Th3B.6, same authors) and that this invited paper analyzes the results obtained
   in [12]; Sec. III (p.3-4) presents that device and its data (FtF 5.7 dB, loss breakdown, crosstalk < -30 dB over
   > 90 nm, 17.4 / 3.88 mW, ~760 / ~420 mW, chip 8.5 x 4.5 mm) without marking any value as a new measurement. All are
   reproduced from [12] and stay out (zhang2023 precedent). What this paper measures itself and keeps: the intersection
   loss 0.024 dB 'measured through our latest fabrication tests' (p.5 Sec. IV). The 1.07 dB/cm and 0.0158 dB/180 deg
   stay author_estimate ('assumed'; the 'measured' clause is attached to the intersection loss in the sentence), the
   0.013 dB/90 deg and the per-MZI 0.13 / 0.21 dB rest on cited ref. [16] (skill rule 3), the 19 / 38 W estimates on the
   cited 1.9 W of ref. [6]. Fab AIST on the three test-structure rows: kept ('our latest fabrication tests', D84a; all
   authors AIST). TIA SCR facility: open (registry).
2. testa2018 double-MRR switch element: changed to switch_fabric.other (patched). D158 makes a single switch element
   switch_fabric.other; skill rule 10 / D400 keep wdm.mrr for rings used as channel filters in a WDM path. Here each
   matrix row already carries one demultiplexed channel (signal 'enters the switch matrix at the row lambda7 after ...
   an interleaver and an AWG de-multiplexer', p.8 Sec. VII-A), and the element moves it from row to column: its function
   is switching. Table I values allowed for switch_fabric stay (off / on loss, isolation at 200-1000+ GHz offsets as
   crosstalk_db); 1-dB bandwidth, tuning range, FSR and wafer spread are deleted and listed as a registry gap (below).
   Table I as this paper's own data: endorsed. p.3 presents Table I as 'specifications ... together with the measured
   performance'; refs [12]-[16] are cited for the element designs only (D80i), and p.8 ties the loss rows to this paper's
   full-chip PD-tracking test. Packaged-switch fab CEA-Leti + STMicroelectronics + UPV: endorsed under D399, each with a
   stated role: CEA-Leti fabricated the PIC (p.3 Sec. II 'fabricated in the CEA LETI facilities'); STMicroelectronics
   the EIC that is part of the packaged switch (p.4 Sec. III, BCD8sp 0.16 um); UPV (with TU Wien) the packaging (p.6
   Sec. IV). Chip-part rows (MRR element, interleaver, AWG) carry CEA-Leti only; the pigtailed grating row carries
   CEA-Leti + UPV (assembled row, D399). TU Wien missing from the registry (open).
3. zhang2019a fab TU/e: endorsed. p.2 Sec. II: fabricated in an IMOS MPW run 'carried out in the IPI-TU/e'; an MPW
