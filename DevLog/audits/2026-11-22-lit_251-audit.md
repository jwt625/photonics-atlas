## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. (a) gehl2017 Fig. 11 shifts: keep out (D159, plus D254). Sec. 8 opens on the 1 GHz AWG, then demonstrates spectral shaping on the
   10 GHz AWG, then tests "this device"; the 11.2 GHz/C of Sec. 7 is stated generically for both spacings. Fig. 11(b) (194.87-195.03 THz,
   about 1537.2-1538.5 nm; fit label "-11.2 GHz/C", the text drops the sign) matches neither Fig. 7 nor Fig. 8(a). That frequency test is
   weak on its own: Fig. 8(c) (194.161-194.171 THz, about 1543.96-1544.03 nm) and Fig. 8(a) (1545.03-1545.11 nm) of the same 1 GHz device
   already disagree by about 1.05 nm. The wording is the deciding evidence. If the coordinator wants the values in, the thermal drift is
   a waveguide-family property; enter it on both 10 / 1 GHz rows as `thermal_drift_pm_per_k` (about 88 pm/K derived at 1538 nm), or not
   at all. The > 30 mW power handling follows the same ruling.
2. (b) gehl2017 inverse taper: patched. The 4-5 dB is a two-facet total by the paper's own arithmetic, so the two rows are inconsistent:
   4 dB per input taper vs about 2-2.5 dB per facet. Keep both as `author_estimate` with conflict notes (D417, tossoun2024). The
   coupler is one physical design on every chip, so D159 does not remove m1, even though the power-test AWG is unidentified.
3. (c) gehl2017 fab / process: keep fab org_sandia_national_laboratories and process_name "Sandia MESA silicon photonics platform". It is
   assembled from the printed sentence in the style of merged stanfield2019 ("Sandia MESA low-volume CMOS production facility") and
   zhou2023a ("Sandia MESA CMOS production and research facilities"). Facility: leave empty (see Missing orgs).
4. (d) gehl2017 heater: keep `phase_shift_pi` < 2 `measured`. The phase comes from the interferometer calibration (Fig. 6, every
   diagonal term), and the 85 mW is an author estimate of the drive, stated in the condition. The 1.8 um row is patched to
