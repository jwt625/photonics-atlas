## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. Intrinsic (RC de-embedded) bandwidths (judgment (c)). Recommendation: basis `derived` on the 9 rows (in the patch). The values
   are not read from the measured S21: both papers remove the RC parasitics with the group's Cryo-VCSEL microwave model (chaw2025
   p.6 ref. [18]; wu2025b p.4 ref. [25], "measured and fitted intrinsic optical frequency response"). The merged rows of the same
   group and method (cmp_ofc2026_th1a3_cryo_vcsel_4p5um-m2 / -m3) are `derived`, as are cmp_ofc2026_w1b5 (three-pole de-embedding)
   and ramirez2022 (intrinsic S21); D394 puts de-embedded values at `derived`. The alasio2024 precedent the distiller cites removes
   pads only and the hsu2025 rows remove a photoreceiver response; keeping `measured` here would split one lab's values across two
   bases in the benchmark. "> 50 GHz" with qualifier gt is right in either case: the bound is inside the 50 GHz measurement range,
   no extrapolation (D435 not triggered). Revert = drop the `basis` / `notes` fields of the 9 bw3db_ghz ops.
2. Fab (judgments (a), (b)). Recommendation: chaw2025 UIUC kept (p.2 "We investigated ... by reducing the oxide-aperture size
   using carefully controlled process parameters, such as water vapor flow and temperature in the wet oxidation furnace", p.4 "our
   14-year oxide-VCSEL process experience", single-institution author list; D84a "our process"). wu2025b empty kept: only
   "designed and fabricated" and an agentless etch sentence (D80b); the per-paper rule overrides the same-group link. nishiyama2005:
   org_corning on the 12 VCSEL rows and the 2 bottom-DBR rows (in the patch). "Recent improvements in MOCVD system design ... allow
   us to achieve such precise control" (p.2) says the authors controlled the DBR growth, the same kind of first-person process
   statement as chaw2025; the author list is Corning only. The two top-DBR rows (e-beam evaporated a-Si / Al2O3, passive) stay
   empty under D515 (per-component fab). Revert = drop the 16 nishiyama2005 component ops.
3. RT temperatures and Fig. 1 labels (judgment (f)). Recommendation: keep empty. Only Sec. III-A defines room temperature (about
   20 C); 203 merged rows with a bare "room temperature" keep temperature_k empty. wu2025b Fig. 1 label block has no temperature tag
   (the inset puts Ith at about 1.55 mA at the lowest temperature, close to but not the label value).
4. nishiyama2005 single-mode 2.0 / 1.7 mW on the 5 um rows (judgment (e)). Recommendation: keep. The 5 um device is the only one
   stated single transverse mode over the whole temperature range (p.4, p.5); the 25 C curves of Fig. 6 (about 2.0 mW) and Fig. 10
   (about 1.74 mW) match; a single-mode 7 um device would give 3.5 / 2.8 mW, larger than the printed single-mode maximum. D159
   is not triggered: the paper's numbers tie the value to one aperture.
5. chaw2025 per-locator thresholds and the population row (judgments (d), report 5). Recommendation: keep. Fig. 3, Fig. 4(a) and the
   p.6 list are separate measurements with different printed values; each row names its locator and device role; the 0.30 / 0.29 mA
   pair at 40 K stays with cross-notes (D417 style). Population ranges are D165 array rows, re-read in the findings table above.
6. Platforms, babic1995 T0, dropped values (judgments (g), (h), (i)). Recommendation: keep all. imai2010 `gaas`: the printed active
   region is InGaAs / GaAs (p.1 abstract, p.1 intro), the same evidence level as chaw2025 (AlAs / GaAs DBRs, substrate not
   printed); not a D422 default. babic1995 `other`: InGaAsP / InP active layer fused between AlAs / GaAs and AlGaAs / GaAs mirrors,
   no single stack. T0 on the family row: the p.3 sentence names no device (D165 row, not a representative row, so D254 does not
   apply). chaw2025 Table I 6.8 um column (labelled "[15]") and the TDECQ 2.56 dB out (D259); nishiyama2005 top DBR 99.4 %
