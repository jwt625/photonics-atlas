## Coordinator questions

1. sacher2019 family-level ranges (asked). The crossing minimum 0.07-0.09 dB, the divider minimum excess loss 0.19-0.47 dB and the
   divider 1-dB bandwidth 70-172 nm are each printed once for all devices and polarizations, and Figs. 8 / 11 show device-to-device
   spread (divider minima from 0.19 to 0.47 dB: an envelope copy gives every divider a 0.19 dB best case). Recommendation (patched):
   D552 hu2020 ruling, i.e. delete the 11 copies, keep the ranges in notes, add per-device, per-polarization figure reads (14 rows,
   whose extremes reproduce the printed endpoints). Bounds printed "in all cases" (crosstalk > 20 dB, split within 1.1 dB) stay
   on every row (D552 lin2023). Alternative: keep the envelopes and drop the Q1 ops (op list in the patch header).
2. sun2017 fab `org_university_of_toronto` via "We thank the Toronto Nanofabrication Centre (TNFC) in device fabrications" (p.10).
   The acknowledgment names the place of device fabrication (an org, not staff); all three authors are at the University of
   Toronto. Recommendation: keep (D448 stated place; D84b borderline note already on the row). TNFC goes to the org round as a
   facility (parent University of Toronto), then into `facility_org_ids` of this row (D448 shared-facility clause).
3. wu2020a inconsistent insertion losses. The measured values do not all conflict: TE ~1.1 dB (band average) and 1 dB (conclusion)
   agree with the flat Fig. 7 TE trace; TM < 3 dB (band worst case) and 1.6 dB agree once 1.6 dB is read as the 1550 nm value. Only
   the abstract "< 1 dB for both polarizations" contradicts the paper's own TM numbers and trace. Recommendation (patched): keep all
   four measured IL rows with notes (D417), move m3 / m4 / m5 to 1550 nm inferred, keep the abstract bound out.
4. li2018a D-MWC TE2 "crosstalk" +9.08 dB measured / +12.01 dB simulated. The crosstalk port carries more power than the through
   port; as a suppression magnitude it would be negative, which the registry convention (positive suppression, c3) does not hold.
   Recommendation: keep in notes (no op); the TE2 loss rows (18.21 / 18.95 dB) already show the failure.
5. chen2006 platform `soi` -> `soi_rib` on the two fabricated rows (patched). The paper calls them silicon wire waveguides but
   prints rib ~0.34 um and slab ~0.02 um; recommendation: `soi_rib` (the drawing then shows the thin slab). The 2D-FDTD design rows
   stay `soi` (no cross-section). Alternative: keep `soi` (drop the two component ops).
