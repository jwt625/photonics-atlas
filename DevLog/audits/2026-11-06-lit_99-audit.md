  same. 36 um, 1.5 um ring = `resonator.ring` (Q-characterized, soliton source, D400); 18 um add-drop = `wdm.mrr` (comb-line selector in the
  filtering path, Fig. 4(a), D400 / D459); 36 um, 2 um comb ring = `resonator.ring`. Widths 1.5 vs 2 um make the two 36 um rings two designs

  against the dip width (about 2 pm). Filter Q_i > 1 M `author_estimate` (no fit shown, regime asserted): correct.

  (p.1, p.4, p.8 "approximate cutoff frequency"), `derived` with the conflict in notes, as the merged authors' time-constant conversions
