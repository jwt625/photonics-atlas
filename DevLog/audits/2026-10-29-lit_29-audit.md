## Rulings on the batch report's judgment calls

1. oh2023 fab org_harvard_university: kept (borderline, noted). Not passive only: App. C states an author-performed fab
   step ("we protected it by spin-coating", p.10) and "our cleanroom facilities" (p.9); the work was performed at the
   Harvard CNS (p.1). Authors split Harvard (4) / Corning (3), so D72 alone would not decide it, but D83c (the org
   where the fabrication was done) places it at Harvard. facility org_center_for_nanoscale_systems endorsed (performed
   in part at CNS); Harvard here is the fab org, not a facility (D82c not touched).
2. weigel2023 MWRN: reclassed to splitter.other (table above). Fab empty endorsed: fabrication in passive voice and
   "our technology" for the PolyBoard platform (p.1, p.3-4) is the D84a M1D.4 case; all authors at one org do not
   qualify (D80b).
3. zhou2022a CE basis author_estimate: endorsed. The per-coupler CE rests on "estimated transmission spectra of the
   input grating and ADCs" (p.4), an assumed input (skill rule 4); the bandwidths and peak wavelengths share that
   normalization. fab org_imec endorsed (MPW run at IMEC, p.4; acknowledgement "IMEC for device fabrication", p.5).
4. liu2025a angled-crossing ranges: endorsed. D159 bars a value whose sample identity is ambiguous between two
   components; here both printed values (per mode and polarization) belong to the one fabricated angled-crossing
   structure (C1-C4 crossings of Fig. 13, launch ports C2 / C4 or C1 / C3) and to the same quantity (maximum loss per
   two crossings), so value..value_max with statistic range is faithful without assigning ports (p.5 Sec. III).
5. wang2022b fab empty: endorsed. "The fabrication of the device was performed with CSiP180Al active flow technology"
   (p.3) names a process, not a fabricator (lit_24 gentry2015 ruling).
