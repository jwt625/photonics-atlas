## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. (a) zheng2018 hybrid-waveguide attenuation as `waveguide_platform.straight` `prop_loss_db_per_cm` (2700 / 76000 / 6200, plus
   added 70000) vs deletion under D250. Recommend keep: D250 rejected a phase-shifter parameter for per-length loss / extinction
   (a state contrast per um); the aGST and cGST values here are the absolute attenuation of one waveguide cross-section in one
   state, the same quantity as merged blundell2025 Sb2Se3-loaded rib rows (2800 dB/cm, audited lit_80). The per-length contrast
   (7.3 dB/um) and phase (nm/um) values stay out, as in zheng2020 (D586). The `phase_change` tag separates these rows in views.
2. (a') zheng2018 mode-mismatch row: recommend the patched `device_total` with both interfaces in `includes` (Eq. (1) round
   trip). Alternative: keep `per_component` and relabel the component as the interface pair. Halving to < 0.25 dB per transition
   is not recommended (the paper does not state equal interfaces).
3. (b) zheng2018 2 um GST ring, 33 dB extinction as `resonator.ring`: recommend keep (zheng2020 ring_r20_switch precedent; D400:
   no WDM path). No separate `phase_shifter.phase_change` row: the strip has no own metric beyond its 2 um length (rule 9).
4. (c) zheng2018 fab `org_university_washington`: recommend keep (first-person fabrication + stated place, D448; zheng2020).
   WNF facility missing from the registry (already reported D418 / D586).

   passive "was built" -> empty). Platform `other` (D424), IL `fiber_to_fiber`, isolation as `crosstalk_db`: keep.
