## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. bucio2019 review material (judgment a). Keep all three exclusions.
   - Table I "1000 x 300 nm 1.20 / 1.00 dB/cm *This work": the value sentence (p.2, "We have exploited this approach to achieve ...
     [19]"), the Fig. 3 caption ([19]) and the Sec. II-A opener ("Reference [19] discusses in detail the experiments") all attribute
     the measurement to [19] (the authors' 2017 J. Phys. D paper). "*This work" marks the group's platform against other groups'
     entries. D259 applies (D408: the value sentence attributes).
   - Fig. 12(c) Q_int 71,000-122,000: the section opener (p.7, "we employed this material for the fabrication of PhC waveguides
     [48] and high-Q PhC microcavities [49], [50]") attributes the fabricated cavities to [49] / [50], the design is "our PhC
     cavities [49]" and the harmonic part is a "brief review". Exclusion is consistent with D259a (values the paper reproduces from
     the authors' own earlier work), even without a "taken from" label on Fig. 12. Caveat: the value sentence itself carries no
     citation and the subsection says "Here we report the implementation"; if the coordinator reads D408 literally (value sentence
     or figure must attribute), the measured Q_int range would enter on a `resonator.photonic_crystal` row as `q_intrinsic`
     71,000-122,000 measured (resonant scattering), with the simulated 520,000 and V 0.77 (lambda/n)^3. Recommendation: keep out.
   - Stand-alone AMMI: Table V prints the row as "AMMI [37]", and [37] is the authors' own 2018 paper on the N-rich SiN angled MMI
     for O-band CWDM (reference list p.11); the Table V values (4 x 20 nm, < 1.5 dB, 16-30 dB, 18 pm/C) match the text (p.5). Keep
     out (D259: the table attributes). What remains (IAMMI, its MZI interleaver, GST switch, Si-rich converter waveguide and its
     tapers, 2 um waveguides) carries no attribution to earlier work in the text, figure or Table V (IAMMI row uncited). The IAMMI
     concept cites [33] (Hu 2013, SOI); the SiN device is new here.
