## Questions raised by the distiller (evidence-based recommendations)

1. matsuo2013 dynamic measurements (16.2 GHz at 200 uA, 1.6 V at 109 uA): recommend keeping the separate row
   (patched labels / notes only). Evidence: Sec. IV-B (p.7-9, Figs. 10-13) names no device, and the renders show the
   paper used at least three devices, not two: a = 430 nm emitting near 1567.8 nm (p.5 Fig. 6), an unidentified device
   near 1558 nm (p.7 Fig. 8), and a = 440 nm near 1589 nm at 25 C (p.7 Fig. 9(b)). D159 forbids attaching a value to
   one of two candidate rows; a row that describes "the device of the dynamic measurements" attaches the values to no
   named design, and the physical device is clearly a LEAP laser of this paper, so the values (the abstract headline)
   are not mis-attributed. Alternative (D159 strict): delete the row and move 16.2 GHz / 1.6 V into the a430 / a440
   notes; the cost is losing the paper's only measured modulation values.
2. chen2000 class: recommend fiber_chip_coupler.edge (as entered). Registry: edge = "edge coupler / spot-size
   converter (inverse taper, trident, cantilever, SiN/SiON/polymer SSC)"; micro_optics.vertical_taper = "vertical
   tapered waveguide / reflector to PD or fiber". All 10 merged vertical_taper rows are out-of-plane parts (ishii2003
   TIR mirror, zhang2024a laser-ablated 45 deg mirrors, OFC Th3C.3 suspended polymer tapers on PDs). chen2000 is an
   in-plane mode-size transformer at a chip facet for butt coupling to SMF (p.1 abstract, p.3 Sec. 2, p.8 Fig. 14),
   i.e. a polymer SSC.
3. hutchison2016 platform: recommend empty (patched). The text states only "0.4 um" Si thickness, a 0.2 um rib etch
   and a 300 mm CMOS facility (p.1-p.2, p.4); SOI, BOX or an insulator never appear, and Fig. 1(c) shows only
   "cladding removed". Setting soi would default from outside knowledge of the facility (D168 starnault2026 and OFC
   Th2A.6 "wafer stack not stated" precedents: platform empty). If the coordinator reads "silicon photonics device"
   (p.4) as enough, drop the three component ops.
4. Remaining items:
   - sysak2008 III-V grating loss / kappa: keep out (endorsed). p.6 prints "165 cm-1 and 3dB/100 um, respectively" for
     loss and kappa; read literally kappa would carry dB/length units, and the swap that makes the units fit is an
     interpretation (D156). kappa_per_cm exists for laser / reflector, so the gap is not the parameter but the source.
   - sysak2008 EAM values (> 5 dB DC extinction at -6 V, > 2 GHz at 6 V, 40 ohm, 200 um; p.8-9): keep out as an
     EO-atlas candidate (skill rule 2, D168 starnault2026 precedent); listed in the component notes. Note the
     conclusion says "up to 2 GHz" vs "greater than 2 GHz" in Sec. 5.2.
   - sysak2008 fab empty: endorsed. "we have fabricated" (p.1-2, p.9) with authors at Intel (two sites) and UCSB, two
     unrelated orgs (D154 / qi2024); the bonding help in the acknowledgment is to individuals.
   - acoleyen2009 heater P_pi author_estimate: endorsed (arithmetic above rests on the assumed resistance split; the
     14.28 pi total and 15 kOhm rows derived are correct per D80a / D256).
   - hutchison2016 SLSR from the figure label: endorsed as measured (judgment call 7).
   - chen2000 fab (raised by this audit): patched to org_university_of_southern_california on the D399 analogue
     (first-person process account in Sec. 3, all authors at USC). D80b lists "we developed" and passive
     "fabricated" as insufficient; Sec. 3 goes further ("the first method we use is O2 RIE with a shadow mask", "We
     also experimented with an aluminum shadow mask", a taper "made in our experiment"). Recommend keep; drop the op if
     the coordinator requires a literal "we fabricated".
