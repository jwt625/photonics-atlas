## Coordinator questions

1. luo2018 4.7 %/W basis. Recommendation: `measured` (patched). The paper's own sentence is a fit of its own data giving an on-chip
   value (D80a); the coupling corrections are not printed, and the visible output coupling loss is never stated. Rule 4's "loss-budget
   term whose origin the paper does not state" covers a printed term of unknown origin; here no term is printed, and the merged on-chip
   SHG rows in exactly this situation are `measured` (zhao2026 m1 "Coupling correction values not printed", lit_62 audit; wang2018,
   zhao2025a, lit_85 audit). `author_estimate` stays right where the paper calls its coupling losses estimated (chang2018a) or labels the
   quantity estimated (zhao2020, Q2). Alternative: keep `author_estimate` (drop the m1 op; then the zhao2026 / wang2018 / zhao2025a rows
   are the inconsistent ones and want a sweep).
2. zhao2020. Recommendation: keep as distilled (no op). (a) SHG values: the "detailed study ... will be presented elsewhere" sentence
   announces more data; the 2 x 10^3 %/(W cm2), 1.25 nm FWHM and Fig. 1(d) are presented as this paper's measurement with no citation
   (p.2). The D259 / D408 forthcoming-paper clause (taillaert2006, lit_57 Q4c) applies when the value sentence carries the citation to the
   other paper; this one does not. (b) `author_estimate` on the SHG value is still right: the Fig. 1(d) axis says "Estimated eta_norm" and
   the normalization uses facet losses of unstated origin next to cited propagation losses. (c) Facet losses `author_estimate`: a
   parenthetical setup fact with no method, in a paragraph whose other losses are explicitly "estimated ... based on previous
   measurements" (D418 nader2025, D167 not extended). (d) Fab `org_university_of_rochester`: the passive sentence names the org ("the
   waveguide structure fabricated in collaboration with the University of Rochester", p.1), which Contract v7 accepts, and the p.8
   acknowledgment thanks named Rochester co-workers "for collaboration on waveguide fabrication". UCSD stays out (poling passive, "we
   have recently developed" a recipe, D80b). Alternative for (d): none recommended.
3. hwang2023. Recommendation: keep the coupler, Q_i and on-chip rows as distilled; only the Q wavelength span is patched. (a) Coupler rows:
   both couplers are part of the fabricated OPO, so `fabricated` with design-only values is D80e; output DC `author_estimate` (design
   method not stated) and adiabatic coupler `simulated` (coupled-mode-theory design, p.9) follow D82a; the paper reports no measured
   coupler transfer (it names inefficient MIR transfer as a suspected loss, p.3). (b) Q_i `author_estimate` (D403; undercoupling comes from
   the ~1 % design extraction, Q_ex about 20e6 is not a fit result). (c) Threshold 80 mW and 15 % PCE `derived` (D394 / D166: on-chip via the
   own 33 % coupling and the own MIR / telecom calibrations). The 33 % coupling assumes negligible pump propagation loss (p.10): kept
   `measured`, assumption in the condition; if the coordinator reads that as an assumed input (rule 4), the facet m1 row would become
   `author_estimate` and the threshold / PCE / eta_DFG rows that rest on it too (4 rows), which I do not recommend (the assumption only
   makes the coupling a lower bound).
4. gaafar2024. Recommendation: keep (no op). (a) Facet losses `loopback_half` + `measured` are the lorenzen2026 ruling (D452) on the same
   authors' platform and wording (coupling losses "measured using passive (no gain layer) waveguides", p.6; per-facet value
   printed, total and halving not printed). (b) Fab per part: the amplifier row carries `org_ligentec;org_university_of_twente` (p.6
   "fabricated by Ligentec SA"; p.7 contributions "H.F., M.G. fabricated the silicon nitride chip", "K.W., S.M.G.B. developed and
