## Registry gaps confirmed (concrete skipped values)

- `passband_ripple_db` (wdm): jeong2013 in-band ripple 0.4 dB of the CROW-3 drop channel (p.7 Sec. 3.2, measured).
  Proposed definition: "peak-to-peak transmission ripple within the stated passband, positive dB (passband criterion
  in condition)".
- Directional-coupler coupling coefficient (splitter): camus2020 fitted kappa 0.29 mm^-1 (BGG) and 0.51 mm^-1 (silica)
  (p.6-7 Sec. 3.3), each equal to the CMT value. kappa_per_cm exists but is defined as a grating coupling coefficient;
  proposed: widen kappa_per_cm to splitter with the definition "field coupling coefficient per unit length of a
  directional coupler (CMT; fit vs computed in notes)", values 2.9 / 5.1 per cm derived. Values are family fits over
  the L series (D165).
- Split-ratio tolerance bandwidth (splitter): nikbakht2022 split within 3 dB +/- 0.3 dB over 1300-1600 nm (300 nm,
  measured, p.6 Sec. 3.1, p.8). Proposed `split_tolerance_bw_nm`: "full width over which the split ratio stays within
  the stated tolerance of nominal (tolerance in condition)". Check against the D81 / D250 rejection of "0.5 / 3 dB
  tolerances" before adding.
- Optical-input self-heating shift (resonator): wang2021 fitted dlambda/dP_input 1.52 pm/uW (p.5, Fig. 3(e)).
  Proposed: widen tuning_efficiency_pm_per_mw to optical input power (actuator in condition), value 1520 pm/mW
  derived.
- PhC hole radius (resonator, wdm, reflector): ashida2017 r 105 nm (both rows, p.3), radulaski2018 r 70 nm perturbed
  cavity (p.6; the L3 radius is printed as "a = 55 nm", ambiguous). Proposed `hole_radius_nm`: "air-hole radius of a
  photonic-crystal lattice (lattice constant: period_nm)".
- GC polarization selectivity (fiber_chip_coupler): zhang2013a TE GC "over 20 dB selectivity over TM" (p.5). Proposed:
  widen per_db to fiber_chip_coupler ("polarization extinction of a polarization-selective coupler"); single value.
- Platform value `sicoi` (4H-SiC-on-insulator): wang2021 four rows on platform other (p.2, p.9). Proposed:
  "SiC thin film (4H / 3C) bonded or grown on SiO2 / Si".
- Optional: heater_resistance_ohm for resonator / wdm (radulaski2018 4 kOhm entered on a separate heater row; see
  question 4).
- Not confirmed: all-optical switching time (radulaski2018 6 ps; identity issue), comb span (wang2021 1300-1700 nm;
  D81 rejected), nm/V^2 tuning sensitivity (model), mode_converter crosstalk bandwidth (covered, see rulings).
