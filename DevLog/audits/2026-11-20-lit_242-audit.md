## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. Fig. 12(a) text 60 dB vs caption 65 dB (i3o1): keep 60 with the conflict note (D417; tossoun2024, pita2023, zhao2019 precedents).
   The render supports 60: on-peak about -13 dBm, off-peak about -73 dBm. 65 could only come from the on-peak against the off-state
   floor; no op.
2. switch_4x4 m1, 10 dB mean IL with no conditions: keep, note (patched). It is printed twice (abstract, Sec. I) as this work's own
   measurement; Fig. 11 at 1285 nm / 100 mA / -4.2 dBm gives about 13 dB mean by eye, so the 10 dB likely comes from other
   conditions (or a subset of paths) the paper does not name. Dropping it would remove the paper's headline number; D417 keeps
   printed values with the inconsistency flagged.
3. Sec. III-A breakdown basis: keep `author_estimate` on all six (2.2 dB/facet, 0.13 dB/crossing, MMI 0.9 / 3.9 dB, SOA gain 13.7 /
   8.5 dB). Rule 4: each rests on a nominal input (the PDK-specified 3 / 5 dB/cm times mask lengths), and every later term inherits
   the earlier ones; D638 (xing2015 loss-budget terms) and D544 match. The waveguide term is small (about 0.5-1 dB of each Fig. 3
   bar), but the basis follows the origin, not the size. `derived` would misstate that the inputs are all measured.
4. 1x8 IL 6.8 / 5.9 dB and output powers -9.5 / -7.5 dBm: the external VOA (3 x 3.9 dB) is inside both IL values and the booster
   output power; in the pre-amp case it precedes the chip, so the printed -1.3 dBm "PIC input" is the emulated-switch input. Proof
   in the findings table (one circuit, only the VOA moves; OSNR explanation on p.9; OSNR unchanged after the combiner, p.10). Keep
   the rows: IL with `includes` naming the VOA (scope fiber_to_fiber of the emulated 8x8 path), output power with the plane in the
   condition (patched). If the coordinator prefers amplifier output powers to be chip-facet values only, the alternative is to
   delete soas-m1 (B, after the VOA) and keep m2; not recommended (condition now says what it is).
5. Four-MMI path widths 59.8 / 62.1 nm on the MMI row, single-MMI > 100 nm: keep. The authors attribute the narrowing to the MMI
   cascade (p.4), the condition says cascade of four plus facets / waveguides / crossings, and the same construction already
   holds the 37.4 nm SOA + 4 MMI path width on the SOA row. The > 100 nm bound is the plotted 1260-1360 nm span with every MMI port
   within about 2 dB of peak; `extracted_from_figure` with gt is right (text: "extends beyond the O-band spectrum"). Registry
   candidate: `bw_3db_nm` for `switch_fabric` (path optical bandwidth); once added, the 59.8 / 62.1 / 37.4 nm rows could move to
   the switch row.
6. psat_in > -4.1 dBm and on/off >= 29 dB: keep both. psat_in: no saturation over -14.1 to -4.1 dBm fiber input (Fig. 6(b) flat
   within about 0.3 dB), bound with ref_plane fiber and the reduced on-chip input in notes. On/off >= 29 dB: plane-independent
   ratio of two printed levels, `derived`, `ge`; notes now say the bound is the 1309 nm channel and the other channels block to
   about -59 to -73 dBm.
7. Rise / fall on the 1x8 path row, 1.5 V on the 600 um SOA row, fab / process / specification: keep all. Rise / fall: p.6 names
   the Fig. 21 test structure, and `rise_time_us` / `fall_time_us` allow only phase_shifter / switch_fabric. 1.5 V: statement covers
   all SOAs (condition says so); `forward_voltage_v` is amplifier-only and the 600 um SOA is the paper's reference SOA. Fab: the
   p.3 sentence names SMART Photonics for every growth and processing step (all rows). Waveguide 3 / 5 dB/cm `specification`:
   printed as "the specified waveguide loss" (Fig. 3 caption) of the PDK, as the chen2024 / catalalahoz2026a foundry-spec rows
   (D432, D614).
8. (auditor) SOA-less switch paths i4o1 / i4o3 fiber-to-fiber loss: Fig. 3 bars about 20.7 / 22.2 dB at 1285 nm (broken axis,
   +/- 0.3 dB); Fig. 4 peaks about -20.3 / -22.7 dB near 1297 nm; text: "added loss of 2.5 dB for the i4o3 path". Not entered by the
   distiller (figure-only, components extracted instead). Recommendation: leave out. The paths carry no switching element, so the
   value is not a switch-state IL, and the per-component terms derived from them are already rows; the readings are recorded here
   if a passive-network loss row is wanted later.
