## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. agazzi2010 fab (judgment (a)). Recommendation: keep the Al2O3 fab empty: "our lithographic equipment" (p.5) is first-person but the
   authors are at University of Twente (MESA+) and Ghent University, so rule 12 / D80b / D395 give no unambiguous org (Twente is likely
   from refs. [11], [23], but that is inference). ePIXfab ("multi-user run of the epixfab CMOS line [27]", p.4; MPW access provider, D398;
   the pilot-line owner is not named and is not inferred): after the org round set it as fab on the transition row and on the 13.5 mm
   amplifier row, which is that foundry chip post-processed with the Al2O3:Er3+ layer (D440 / D419); not on the butt facet (its guiding
   stack is Al2O3 only) or the two design rows. process_name is set on the amplifier row now (revert = drop that field).
2. agazzi2010 ~2.5 dB transition (judgment (b)). Recommendation: `author_estimate`, method `reference_structure` kept. D167 covers a
   directly stated coupling loss; here two path totals differ in Er-doped length (strongly absorbing at 1533 nm unpumped) and in a 4 mm Si
   wire, and the correction terms are not stated (the paper's only Si loss is cited); khope2019 (D440) is the same construction. Revert =
   drop the basis field.
3. Signal enhancement as `gain_db` (judgments (c), (e) 63 dB). Recommendation: delete (patched). The merged EDWA corpus never stores S as
   gain (eight papers, notes + registry gap); keeping these rows would put a no-net-gain device at 7.2 dB and a 16 dB net amplifier at
   63 dB in the amplifier views. If the registry adds `signal_enhancement_db` (amplifier; pump-on / pump-off contrast, wavelength, pump and
   signal power in condition), re-enter agazzi2010 7.2 dB (`measured`) + the three Fig. 5 reads (`extracted_from_figure`) and thomson2010
   63 dB with the earlier candidates (cai2021, chrysostomidis2021, vagionas2021, zhang2026, bonneville2024, li2025).
4. thomson2010 NF 8 dB at 1505 nm (judgment (d)). Recommendation: keep at 1505 nm with the D417 note. Fig. 5(b) has markers at 1505, 1510,
   1520, 1525, ... 1580 nm (first at about 7.9 dB), and p.6 states the saturation measurements over 1505-1580 nm, so "1525 nm" in the p.7
   range sentence is the inconsistent number; the figure supports the printed 1505 nm maximum.
5. thomson2010 added rows (judgment (e)). Recommendation: keep `pump_threshold_mw` 350 (Fig. 4 4x crop: the 350 mW marker sits on the 0 dB
   line, 300 / 400 mW markers at about -2.5 / +1.7 dB; nykolak1993 D665 and ahmadi2022 zero-net-gain precedent; fiber-to-fiber criterion in
   the condition). Keep `gain_bandwidth_nm` >= 75 nm `derived`: the registry definition takes a stated criterion, the net-gain statement
   covers the discrete tunable-laser points of Fig. 3 (1505 nm circle about +0.5 dB) and the range is the measurement limit (`ge`).
   63 dB: Q3.
6. umeki2011 (judgment (f)). Recommendation: keep all. Two modules of one design used in two classes: one row per class (D396; kazama2021
   SHG / OPA precedent). 5.0 dB IL stated once for the module type stays on the DPA row (signal path); SHG notes now cross-reference it and
   say the 23.4 % port-to-port ratio includes the module coupling. Deamplification as `gain_db` -10 with the gain definition in the
   condition (foster2006, akiyama2007, gagino2024 negative gain rows); the 21 dB extinction row carries the contrast. 23.4 % `derived`
   (ratio of two printed powers, D256). Fab org_ntt (rule 12, D399, kazama2021).
