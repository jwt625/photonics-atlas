   Recommendation: keep both rows. The single Lorentzian fit is a loaded Q of the 120 nm device, and the paper itself plots it

   series of the gap sweep needs it; the printed "Q_int" rests on the stated assumption Q_loaded = Q_int at 120 nm (p.4), backed
   only by simulation (Fig. 2(d)) and by a Q_C that is itself computed from Q_int (circular), so D403 gives `author_estimate`.
   Merged precedent for the pair: ashida2018 (q_loaded measured + q_intrinsic of the same value). Alternative (single row):
   keep only q_intrinsic author_estimate as in ilchenko1999 m4; this would leave the g120 resonator without a loaded Q while its
   series siblings carry one.
