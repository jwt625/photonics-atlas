## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. dai2012 fab `org_ucsb` (judgment (a)). Recommendation: keep. D80b is met by first-person fabrication ("In our fabrication",
   p.2; "We have designed and fabricated", p.3). UCSB is the only org every author prints and the sole affiliation of three of the
   four; hiratani2024 (D433) went empty because every author held the same two orgs (PETRA and Sumitomo Electric), which is not
   the case here (D452 common-org logic). Zhejiang University is the first author's additional affiliation only.
2. dai2012 IL rows (judgment (b)). Recommendation: keep the 4 figure reads as `extracted_from_figure`, `per_component`,
   `reference_structure`, with the patched values / notes. The paper prints no IL but plots normalized spectra and says the IL
   is high at the TM centre; the vector data give a stable local level (spread of local means 0.1-0.3 dB), while the ripple and
   the separate-reference normalization (traces up to +0.17 dB) are in notes. Keep the "e.g., <0.5dB" bound as `author_estimate`
   (origin not stated, D44) on both the MMI (`excess_loss_db`) and the bi-level taper (`insertion_loss_db`): the sentence names both
   parts, and it is the only performance value of either row. Alternative if the coordinator reads "e.g." as no value at all:
   delete both bound rows, which also removes the two component rows (no other performance metric, rule 9), sub-part geometry to
   the PBS notes.
3. feng2014 (judgment (c)). Recommendation: keep both printed ER rows with the D417 note (the values 26 / 16 dB are both on Fig. 3;
   only the port label is reversed, and the port is free-text condition, so no wrong number enters a structured field). `per_db`
   is right: the ER is defined at one output port as the TE-TM transmittance difference (D424 port-ratio rule; merged
   polarization.pbs majority, 95 per_db vs 2 extinction_db rows). Fab AIST: first-person process steps ("We develop a new planarization technique", "we employ
   chemical-mechanical planarization", p.2; "we also prepare a single wave-guiding structure", p.2) with both authors only at AIST.
4. sakaguchi2015 scope (judgment (d)). Recommendation: accept. The three free-space units have fiber in and fiber out (fusion-spliced
   to the MCF, p.4) like the merged jung2018 collimator pairs and oh2023 metasurface couplers; fontaine2019 (D642) failed on its camera
   output. The 36-core fiber is a transmission medium, not a component: no class fits and no merged fiber dB/km row exists; if the
   owner wants link fibers, that is a registry extension (fiber attenuation dB/km, cable cutoff, Aeff, core-to-core XT), not a
   distiller call. Platform `other` and class split (MUX `mode_converter.mdm_mux`, SDM DEMUX `fiber_interface.fan_out`, MDM DEMUX
   `mode_converter.mdm_mux`) follow merged precedents (anelli2024, eznaveh2018 lanterns; oh2023, shikama2017 fan-outs).
5. scarcella2017 (judgment (e)). Recommendation: accept. IL is defined as half the shunt loss (p.2 "-3.4 dB, which corresponds to IL
   = 1.7 dB"), so the 1.7 / 2.1 / 0.2 dB and 68 / 62 % rows are `loopback_half` `derived` (D542 / D554 stated halving) and the 3.4 dB
   rows `fiber_to_fiber` `measured`. The tolerances are on the halved IL (figure brackets checked), i.e. per interface, matching
   the registry definition; the notes say so. GC platform empty: SOI appears only in the introduction's general description of
   foundry silicon photonics (p.1), never for this chip (D423 strict reading).
6. scarcella2017 Axetris (judgment (f)). Recommendation: add Axetris (Axetris AG, vendor) in the next org round and fill fab on
   `cmp_lit_scarcella2017_ulens_pair` only: that row is the pair of purchased lens arrays (vendor of a purchased part, rule 12).
   On `cmp_lit_scarcella2017_pluggable_connector` keep fab empty with Axetris in notes: the connector is the Lego / Al-plate assembly
