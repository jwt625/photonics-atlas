## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. (a) yi2024b measured IL rows as author_estimate / loss_breakdown / de_embedded, not halved: keep. The extracted spectra rest on a GC
   term "estimated" with no stated origin and no reference structure (p.4), exactly the D428 sun2025 ruling (same group, same setup and
   wording). Halving to per-ADC would add an assumption (equal MUX / DEMUX loss, bus loss zero) the paper does not make. Crosstalk rows
   stay measured / direct: a port ratio within one launch, independent of the subtracted GC term.
2. (b) Simulated single-ADC values on the one MDM row with the ADC in the condition, scope per_component: keep. One row per physical
   design (rule 9); the ADCs exist on the fabricated chip (D80e); same construction as merged a2013 (one ADC stage, simulated) and
   sun2025 (MUX alone) and cheng2023a. A separate design row per ADC would split one physical device.
3. (c) Simulated CT > 18 dB over 1500-2000 nm with wavelength_inferred: keep, notes refined (patched). The figure supports the bound over
   the whole plotted span up to 2000 nm, and one value stated once is one row (rule 7). Alternative (band unspecified, no wavelength)
   loses information the figure does give; not recommended.
4. (d) Wb1 / Wb2 derived (D157 / D256: printed only in um, text and Table I) and Wa design_target (500 nm printed on p.2): keep. Coupling
   lengths printed in um and stored in um stay design_target. `width_nm` with the role in the condition is the only option
   (`bus_width_nm` is allowed only on resonator / wdm); sun2025 stores its multimode bus the same way.
5. (e) fab empty vs HUST: keep fab empty, facility set. D652 ruled on the identical acknowledgment ("for the support in device
   fabrication" at the WNLO / HUST facility) for cheng2023a: facility only; D448 needs a named place where samples were processed, and
   D634 says a facility's owner is not fab by that alone. The fabrication sentence is passive with no org (D80b). cai2024 carries HUST fab
   only because its abstract says the authors fabricated the devices (first-person rule 12). Notes patched to cite D652.
6. (f) Registry gap gap_nm on mode_converter: confirmed still open (`gap_nm` classes: splitter, resonator, wdm, waveguide_platform,
   chip_chip_coupler, phase_shifter, crossing, switch_fabric). Already logged as a registry candidate in D428 and confirmed in D533; add
   yi2024b g = 100 nm (p.2 Sec. II; Table I 0.1 um, all three ADCs) to the gap-fill list. No new gap.
