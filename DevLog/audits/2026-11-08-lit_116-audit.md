6.25 GHz step, 16.3 GHz roll-off (no parameters), 10 x 20 projections (p.7). Observation: p.3 "step less than 7 GHz" and p.9 "6.25 GHz" are
consistent.

song2013 (33 metrics ok). TO q-DOS values at p.5-7 confirmed: 1.6 nm (200 GHz) 3-dB width, about 30 dB extinction and on/off ratio, 37 mW,
0.4 A/mW = 40 pm/mW, about 0.11 nm/deg C, FSR about 3.2 nm, ng about 4.13 (authors' arithmetic from FSR and the about 385 um circumference),
geometry p.3-4 (220 nm / 2 um BOX, 500 / 400 nm widths, 300 nm gaps, 60 um coupling length, 10 um bend radius, about 60 nm slab). Fig. 7
rise / fall assignment verified on the render: (a) 4.3 us next to the rising edge at 50 us, 3.0 us next to the falling edge at 75 us; (b) 3.2 us
rising edge at 75 us, 3.7 us falling edge at 100 us. EO q-DOS: about 1 dB relative loss, -3 dB under injection, > 30 / about 10 / about 28 dB,
about 12 mW, 350 / 348 ps (p.8, Fig. 11 at 1601.4 nm) confirmed. The about 0.4 dB "transmission loss of waveguide" (p.5) stays out (D159):
Fig. 4(a) peaks sit near 0 dB normalized, so the sentence can be read either as the reference-waveguide share of its 6 dB or as the switch loss
relative to the reference (compare the EO paragraph "relative transmission loss about 1 dB", p.8); no per-coupler value derived. Abstract prints
the wavelength tolerance as +/- 6 A, the body as +/- 80 GHz (p.5): both are author estimates from the 1-dB width, not entered. D396 ring row
correct (switch_fabric does not carry fsr / drift / tuning efficiency). Edge-coupler row `soi_strip` is supported (channel waveguide for the
nano-taper, p.3).
