## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. tossoun2018 (PTL) vs tossoun2018a (JLT) (judgment (a)). Recommendation: keep all Sample A values only on the tossoun2018 rows and delete the
   JLT Sample A row (patched). The JLT names Sample A as the device "previously reported [11]" and every Sample A data set it shows is the PTL
   data: Fig. 8 (identical points, relabelled internal), Fig. 11 (PTL Fig. 9 normalized), Fig. 7 at 200 um (0.6 A/W) and, contrary to the
   report, Fig. 6 (same trace as PTL Fig. 3 within 2 %). D259 / D259a apply; D408 does not protect them (the JLT is not an extension of the
   PTL, and p.3 cites [11] for the Sample A dark current). The two papers stay separate sources with separate B / C rows.
2. Unassigned printed values (judgment (b)). Recommendation: keep both assignments. PTL "3 GHz at -3 V" -> 10 x 50 um: Fig. 9 is the only
   bandwidth device and its -3 V fit is 3 dB down near 2.7-3.1 GHz. JLT "1 nA at -1 V" -> Sample C 10 x 50 um: the p.3 range "1 nA to 100 nA
   under -1 V" matches the Fig. 6 Sample C (1.2 nA) and Sample A (113 nA) 10 x 50 um traces at both ends. PTL "0.49 A/W internal" stays in
   notes (D159): it equals the 20 x 200 um -3 V point (0.215 A/W) x 3.5 dB within reading, but the paper never says so.
3. D417 conflicts (judgment (c)). Recommendation: 10 x 10 um: no conflict (reading error, notes fixed). 20 x 100 um 0.15 vs 0.165 A/W:
   keep printed with note. Add the new 10 x 50 um -7 V note (2.2 GHz printed vs about 1.45 GHz from Fig. 9), value kept as printed.
4. Fig. 8 internal-responsivity reads (judgment (f)). Recommendation: author_estimate (patched). The plotted quantity rests on the
   calculated 7 dB loss, like the printed 0.81 / 0.84 A/W; reading it off a plot does not change its basis (D454 analogue for simulated
   curves). If the coordinator prefers extracted_from_figure for every figure read, revert the two metric ops.
5. Model-based values (judgment (d)). Recommendation: keep all three as author_estimate. nada2020 70.1 / 70.9 / 69.9 GHz: D435 (fit past the
   49.8 GHz limit). kinsey2001 "at least 320 GHz": a bound the authors infer ("therefore") from three fits whose mean is 313 GHz; the
   measured range row 270-340 GHz carries the data, so the bound stays author_estimate (rule 4, arithmetic not shown). yasuoka2005 390 GHz:
   a model limit from the 0.4 ps build-up time, not a measured MB product (measured: 170 GHz at M = 12); author_estimate with the condition
   as written. A new decision could state: "GBP / MB products computed from a fitted intrinsic time constant are author_estimate".
6. kinsey2001 wavelength (judgment (e)). Recommendation: keep band unspecified on the bandwidth and GBP rows (m2 / m3 patched to match m1).
   The title says 1.55 um, but the bandwidth was measured with a Ti-sapphire mode-locked laser, which cannot be 1.55 um; rule 8 inference from
   the operating band would misstate the measurement. QE rows keep the printed 1.55 um.
7. Fab and registry (judgment (g)). Recommendation: org_ntt (nada2020) and org_fujitsu (yasuoka2005) confirmed (first-person fabrication, all
   authors in units of one parent, D154). tossoun2018a stays empty despite the p.7 first-person sentence (two unrelated institutions; notes
   patched). Registry: lit:yasuoka2005 year should be 2003. p.1 footer prints "0-7803-7888-1/03/$17.00 (c) 2003 IEEE", the DOI is
   10.1109/leos.2003.1253144 (LEOS 2003 annual meeting, paper ThZ5, pp. 999-1000). The paper_id can stay; fix `year` in sources.csv
   (coordinator, registry edit).
