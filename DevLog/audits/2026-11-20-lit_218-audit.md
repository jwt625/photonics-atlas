## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. huang2016b basis of 1.05 A/W, 84 % and 70 % and the 15 dB grating coupler (judgment (a)). Recommendation: keep
   `author_estimate`. The paper calls the coupler loss "estimated" (p.2) and Supplement 1 is not cached, so whether it is a
   measurement cannot be checked; D517 (wen2022) made a coupler loss `measured` because the paper sourced a value to a
   supplementary measurement, which is not visible here. If Supplement 1 is cached and shows a measured coupler loss
   (cutback / loopback), switch the coupler row and these 3 rows to `measured`. The Fig. 1(e) reads stay
   `extracted_from_figure`. Coupler fab org_astar_ime: keep. The coupler is the input of the APD test path on the same die (p.2
   "Light couples from a grating coupler to a single-mode silicon waveguide that expands adiabatically to the ... APD
   waveguide"), and D515 / D463 split fab only where a part has another process or maker. Because the 15 dB is an estimated
   test-path value, consider listing the row in `exclude_values` of `config/benchmark.yaml` (D933) so it does not stand as an
   IME coupler figure.
2. huang2016b series rows (judgment (b)). Recommendation: keep the 15 figure-only rows (rule 9 sibling designs, each with a
   performance read; all reads verified above) and the 4 x 100 um 3.3 GHz marker as plotted. The marker is unambiguous, the paper
   does not discuss it, and its note says it lies far below the model; deleting a plotted measurement would be an editorial call.
   The 4 x 30 um row holding both the wire-bonded die and the Fig. 2(d) marker is fine (one design, conditions name the die).
3. Figure-read biases (judgment (c)). Recommendation: keep all. lenox1999 bias -21.5 / -23.9 V and breakdown ~24 V match Fig. 3
   (note on m2 corrected: about 8-9 nA, not 1-2 nA); franco2005 -34.1 V matches Fig. 2; campbell1988 keep the Fig. 3 marker read
   rather than the printed 40, since "as high as 40 ... before breakdown" names no device or bias and validate.py needs bias_v on
   avalanche_gain. Bias corrected to -63 V. The overlapping feature at about 40 / 63.7 V (possibly the 40) is recorded in notes
   only.
4. ye2015 "up to" (judgment (e)). Recommendation: drop `le` (patched). Each value is the measured point at the stated -10 V
   (Fig. 2(d) sweep ends), and `le` with bias_v -10 would claim a bound the paper does not mean. Rule 7's "up to" -> le fits a
   ceiling over unstated conditions. Revert = qualifier le on the two rows. Module row: keep ref_plane module on the chip row
   (D437). Fab Tyndall: keep ("we propose, fabricate and characterize", p.1; all fifteen authors at Tyndall, p.1; rule 12).
5. franco2005 platform (judgment (d)). Recommendation: keep `other` (zhang2022e precedent in D664) until the owner's
   bonded-APD platform sweep listed in D664 decides; `inp` would be the alternative (InP-lattice-matched epi, substrate
   removed, SI-GaAs carrier). Fab empty: keep (passive "was used to fabricate" / "Discrete APDs were fabricated", authors at
   three orgs, grower not named; D80b).
6. campbell1988 (judgment (f)). Recommendation: keep the two-row split (the 3 um AR-coated Fig. 4 device differs in absorber,
   substrate thinning and coating), the 3 um value as printed with the D417 note, and fab org_bell_labs as borderline
   (first-person "by thinning the substrate ... we have obtained", p.3; "assistance with device fabrication" thanks, p.5; all
   authors at AT&T Bell Laboratories; D84b). Dark current: the two Fig. 3 reads are added (miss 1); the printed population
   statements stay out (no bias). Revert = drop the campbell add_metrics m7 / m8 and the notes op.
7. ye2015 Fig. 2(c) leakage density of 100-500 um Design-1 mesas (distiller left out). Recommendation: leave out. These are
   leakage-mechanism test mesas plotted only as density versus D/4 (about 85 / 33 / 21 / 18 / 15 mA/cm2 at -5 V), with no other
   value for those sizes; the huang2016b series is different (the paper's own device-size study with QE and bandwidth).
