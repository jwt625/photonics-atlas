## Coordinator questions

1. piyush2026 P_pi about 29 mW (24 devices, heater length not printed) on the 160 um heater row. p.4 gives the value for the foundry W heater
   with no length; p.5 Fig. 3(b) chooses 160 um from a simulation at that power, and the 160 um heater has its own measured value (27.3 mW,
   p.12, m1). Recommendation: D159 / rule 1, delete the row and keep the value in the component notes (patched). Alternative: a separate
   component row "foundry W heater, length not printed" with the 24-device mean, fab empty (the run of those devices is not stated).
2. piyush2026 split deviations. p.18 Sec. 3.3 prints +/-0.15 dB at 1550 nm and +/-0.25 dB over 1545-1555 nm; the abstract (p.1) and
   conclusion (p.19) print +/-0.015 / +/-0.025 dB, the abstract over "a 40 nm" bandwidth that the measurement does not cover. Fig. 19(b)
   cannot settle it (trace noise of a few tenths of a dB on a 20 dB axis). Recommendation: keep out (D401; also internally inconsistent),
   both values and the 40 nm discrepancy in the notes (patched); no `il_nonuniformity_db` figure read.
3. Device assignment by inference. Recommendation: keep all three; D159 does not apply because each paper fixes the device.
   - fatema2026: the Fig. 4(a) caption prints the area (6000 um2); among the six rectangular sizes of Fig. 5(a) only 50 x 120 um matches.
     Circular APDs on SiN also exist (Fig. 4(b)), sizes not printed; the caveat is now in the note. The dark-current-density sentence
     (around 1e-12 A/um2) follows the 5 nA sentence and matches 5 nA / 6000 um2, so m3 stays with its bias note. To apply D159 strictly,
     delete m1-m3 of apd_50x120 and move them to notes.
   - tabatabaei2026: "the smallest PD" (p.6) plus the Fig. 6(b) legend (34 x 34 um2 = 1156 um2 is the smallest; 29 x 44 = 1276 um2). The
     vector read of the 34 x 34 curve gives 157 fF at -6 V, the printed value; the 29 x 44 curve reads 174 fF. Confirmed.
   - goyvaerts2020: the pooled dark-current and series-resistance statistics describe one coupon design (25 x 25 um GaAs p-i-n) printed on
     both test sites; the two PD rows differ only in the SiN grating under the coupon, which does not enter these electrical values. D465
     (statements about one device type kept on every row of the type) applies.
4. parra2020 IL "< 0.01 dB" and liu2024a scope. Recommendation: keep both as written. parra2020: p.8 says the measured loss was negligible
   "in agreement with simulations (< 0.01 dB)" and p.10 restates < 0.01 dB as the device IL; the number is the simulated bound (Table 2
   criterion), the measurement only bounds it qualitatively, so `author_estimate` fits rule 4 better than `measured`; `simulated` is the
   alternative. liu2024a: a lithographically patterned, SSMF-coupled passive polymer waveguide (platform `polymer`) is a chip-scale /
   fiber-coupled passive part under D411, not a bulk crystal part; board substrate (FR-4) does not change that.
