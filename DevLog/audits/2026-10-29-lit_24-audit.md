## Rulings on the batch report's judgment calls

1. gentry2015 fab org_ibm: removed. Rule 12 / D80b need a fabrication statement naming the org; the paper's
   fabrication statement names only "a CMOS foundry" (p.2 Sec. 2, "upon delivery from the foundry"), while "IBM 12SOI"
   is the process / PDK name (p.2, ref. [46]). No merged row uses "IBM 12SOI"; the merged foundry precedents that set
   fab from a platform name all have a fabrication sentence naming the org (xu2023a "fabricated on the GF 45CLO
   platform", elsaeed2024 "imec's iSiPP300", qiao2017 "180-nm CMOS process line of SMIC"); TriPleX rows without such a
   sentence (hosseini2015, garciablanco2025) are empty. process_name keeps "IBM 12SOI 45 nm CMOS".
2. gonzalezandrade2019 C2N as fab: removed. "The sample fabrication has been performed at the Plateforme de
   Micro-Nano-Technologie/C2N" (p.4) is a rule-12 facility statement; there is no authors-fabricated statement
   (p.2 passive). The registry has org_centre_de_nanosciences_et_de_nanotechnologies (research institute, the owner
   of the platform; not a facility_org_id per D82c) and no row for the platform itself, so facility_org_ids stays
   empty and the platform is reported. Same handling as merged lit:liu2018 (identical acknowledgement).
3. hosseinnia2026 R = 350 / 300 um "Q >= 1.0e7": q_intrinsic (patched). The skill default covers an unqualified Q;
   here "maintain" refers to the intrinsic Q of the preceding sentence (p.4 Sec. 4), and the paper discusses only
   intrinsic Q for its resonators.
4. hosseinnia2026 out-of-band rejection as crosstalk_db: endorsed. Registry crosstalk_db for wdm is the parameter when
   the type is not stated, wording in condition; merged precedents jeong2013 crow5 (40 dB) and wang2026b mrr filter
   (OBRR 20 dB) use it. The single-pole 16 dB (p.8) and the simulated 3-pole > 70 dB (Fig. S6) follow the same rule.
- hosseinnia2026 fab org_georgia_institute_of_technology: endorsed (borderline as noted). Abstract: the authors
  "design, fabricate and demonstrate"; p.3 Sec. 4 places the in-house process at Georgia Tech's IMS; authors at
  Georgia Tech and OEwaves, so D83c (org where fabrication was done). Facility org_institute_for_matter_and_systems
  endorsed: the printed "Institute of Materials and Systems (IMS)" at Georgia Tech is the registry facility
