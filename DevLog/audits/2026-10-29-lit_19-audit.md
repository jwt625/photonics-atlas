## Rulings on the batch report's judgment calls

1. abdulla2011 MEMS-tuned ring as resonator.ring: endorsed. One ring whose metrics are ring properties (FSR, Q,
   linewidth, notch depth) plus tuning_range_nm (allowed for resonator); the DWDM / switch use is only proposed
   (p.15 Sec. 5.2); D31 sends a ring to wdm.mrr only when used as a channel filter. fab org_university_of_twente for
   the MEMS post-processing endorsed (p.13 Sec. 5.1.3 "we could successfully etch"; all authors in two MESA+ groups of
   UT, D154 parent); the ring itself was fabricated by ePIXfab (p.4 Sec. 3.1), an MPW service under rule 12, missing
   from the registry (open question 2).
2. lischke2021 1550 nm values out (D259 / D259a): endorsed. The paper states its own new data are the 1310 nm
   measurements of a follow-up lot (p.1-2); the >= 110 GHz statement carries [2] (p.2, the authors' IEDM 2020 paper),
   the > 0.6 A/W internal responsivity carries [2] (p.3 Conclusion), and Fig. 4 is marked [2]. Fig. 1 (four chips,
   1550 nm) has no [2] in its caption, but only first-lot devices have 1550 nm couplers (p.1) and the sentence that
   states its result cites [2], so it is restated work (D80i). The abstract's "we demonstrate" does not override the
   in-text citation.
3. lischke2021 fab org_ihp kept borderline: endorsed under D84b / D84a. No sentence names IHP's SiGe or photonic
   BiCMOS line as the fab line (fabrication details referred to [2], p.1). What supports IHP: the acknowledgement of
   support from "IHP's cleanroom staff" (p.3), lot-level process statements by the authors ("fabricated with some
   changes in the processing, e.g. NiSi instead of CoSi2", p.1-2), and 5 of 7 authors at IHP / IHP Solutions. Keep
   with the existing note; never a facility_org_ids basis (D72).
4. lischke2021 internal responsivity 0.8 A/W author_estimate: endorsed (power "at PD" needs a coupler loss whose
   origin is not stated, Fig. 3 right; D44). The reversed order in the p.3 sentence is resolved by the Fig. 3 (right)
   slopes (about 0.8 at the photodiode, about 0.37-0.39 at the fiber tip) and the conclusion.
5. santini2017 SSC 1.5 dB as author_estimate: endorsed. The value sits in the design section ("allows low coupling
   losses with an optical fiber (about 1.5 dB)", p.2-3) without method or wavelength, and no SSC measurement is
   reported (all receivers measured without AR); D167 covers a per-facet loss reported as a result, D76 / D82a a
   performance value of unstated design / measured origin.
6. santini2017 receivers as photodetector.other: endorsed (merged precedent ofc2026 M4D.3 coherent-receiver PIC as a
   photodetector row; InP-native PDs stay photodetector.other, D152). Hybrid imbalance measured through the PD outputs
   on the hybrid row: endorsed (condition says so). Hybrid 4.9 dB excess as author_estimate: endorsed (8.9 dB total
   from measured values minus 4 dB resting on the AR-corrected 0.5 A/W estimate, p.4). Fab empty: endorsed (D84a:
   "our SOA-PIN integration platform" with passive process steps, p.2-3).
7. lahijani2026 crossing.other with crossing_loss_db from printed transmission (derived, D256 / D82d; simulated stays
   simulated, D83f): endorsed. An electrical-wire / waveguide crossing through a trench is not MMI, SWG or multilevel.

## Registry gaps confirmed (concrete skipped values)

- gap_nm for crossing, in-plane: lahijani2026 isolation trench 100 nm (tapered and curve-fitted EPCC, p.2-3, p.14),
  60 nm and 20 nm (designs, p.5 Fig. 4). Proposed: widen gap_nm for crossing to "vertical gap of a multilevel crossing,
  or width of an in-plane trench / gap the guided mode crosses (condition says which)", or a new geometry parameter
  `trench_width_nm` (crossing). The five deleted rows can be restored from this patch's delete list.
- thermal_drift_pm_per_k for reflector: hung2020 Bragg-wavelength shift 76.5 pm/C (strip CladMod, p.9 Fig. 6(b)),
  27.3 pm/C measured and 17 pm/C simulated (SWG CladMod, p.10 Fig. 6(d)). Definition as for wdm / resonator: "Bragg /
  stopband centre wavelength drift per kelvin".
- center_wavelength_nm for reflector: hung2020 Bragg wavelength about 1538 nm (p.9). "Bragg / stopband centre
  wavelength".
- back_reflection_db for mode_converter and crossing: hung2020 strip-SWG converter interface reflection about -24 dB by
  OFDR (p.7) and -21 dB simulated (p.6); lahijani2026 R < 1.4 % (tapered, p.3) and < 1.2 % (curve-fitted, p.16),
  simulated. Existing definition (positive dB below the input) fits.
- pdl_db for photodetector: santini2017 UTC PD PDL < 0.7 dB (p.3). "polarization-dependent variation of the PD
  responsivity".
- fill_factor (duty cycle) for waveguide_platform: jean2019 SWG duty cycle 40 % / 50 % (p.4 Table I), hung2020 SWG
  duty cycle 0.5 (p.4); subwavelength_period_nm and fill_factor for reflector: hung2020 SWG core of the CladMod
  reflector, 300 nm, duty 0.5 (p.9-10). Definition: fill_factor as registered (tooth / period, D253).
- Cladding-modulated reflector geometry: hung2020 corrugation width Wg 30 nm, core-to-cladding-waveguide distance d
  200 / 850 / 1050 nm, cladding waveguide width W2 230 nm (p.9-10). Proposed: gap_nm for reflector (distance between
  core and side / cladding waveguide of a cladding-modulated grating) and `corrugation_width_nm` (reflector, wdm:
  sidewall corrugation amplitude); W2 needs a role-specific width (e.g. `side_waveguide_width_nm`).
- Slow-light figures of merit (waveguide_platform.slow_light): jean2019 slow-light bandwidth (ng >= 10) 8.24-8.82 nm
  (SWG #1) and 7.24-7.47 nm (SWG #2) (p.5 Sec. IV-B); loss-per-delay 103.37 dB/ns fitted at 1575.64 nm and 32.15 dB/ns
  raw (p.7 Fig. 11). Proposed `slow_light_bw_nm` ("span between the band edge and the wavelength where ng falls to
  the stated threshold; threshold in condition") and `loss_per_delay_db_per_ns` ("alpha c / ng as reported").
- pull_in_voltage_v for resonator / phase_shifter (MEMS actuators): abdulla2011 9.2 V (40 um cantilever, p.7 Fig. 6)
  and 4.8-4.9 V (60 um, p.7 Fig. 7). "electrostatic pull-in (collapse) voltage of the actuator".
- Not proposed: jean2019 band-edge shift per nm of period (2.63 / 2.55 nm/nm, a design sensitivity), hung2020 optical
  overlap factor (simulated), santini2017 RC cut-off (computed from C and R).
