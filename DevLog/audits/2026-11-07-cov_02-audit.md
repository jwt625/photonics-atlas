## Rulings on the distiller's questions

1. zhou butterfly module class: keep `laser.els_module`. The abstract presents the module as the CW laser prototype for
   ELS (p.1); merged precedents put packaged ELS sub-assemblies with fiber output in els_module (sawamura2023 TOSA,
   ofc2026 W1B.3 / W3E.1 TOSAs). The COS stays laser.dfb (p.2).
2. mao2024 Fig. 1: keep the 2 mm row, the mounting row and the Fig. 1(a) reads. D408 removes values only where the
   value sentence or figure attributes them to the earlier paper. The earlier results are listed with explicit
   citations ([1] 2021, [5] 2023; p.1), while the comparison is introduced as given here, with the L-band lasers taken
   as the example (p.1 Sec. 2 last sentence to p.2 first lines); neither the caption nor the text cites a source for
   Fig. 1. Note: the Fig. 1(b) p-side-down curves coincide with the Fig. 1(a) 2.5 mm curves (0.81 / 0.63 W at 2 A), not
   stated, so the separate mounting row stays.
3. mao2024 platform: confirm empty (D422 / D423). SCOW is named (p.1), the material system is not; ref. [8] is an
   AlGaAs-InGaAs SCOW paper and does not fix this device. hatai (p.1), zhou (p.1), mehta (p.2) and xie (p.2) state
   InP-based epitaxy: inp correct.
4. Spacing errors as channel_spacing_ghz ranges: not acceptable as a centred conversion. The error reference is not
   200 GHz in levy (windows around the array's own spacing, p.6 Fig. 9(c)) and the mehta CS error is a per-channel
   placement deviation from a linear fit (p.4 Fig. 5(b)). Ruling applied: levy m3-m5 replaced by figure reads of the
   plotted spacings (statistic range, extracted_from_figure); mehta m3 deleted. hatai keeps its rows: the paper prints
   the spacings themselves (spacing frequencies within 400 +/- 100 GHz, 200 +/- 30 GHz spacing confirmed; p.2), and
   Fig. 2(d) points (about 345-455 GHz) sit inside 300-500. Gap 1 stays open with a definition that names the error
   reference (nominal grid, mean spacing or ring FSR) and its kind (+/- bound or peak-to-peak window).
5. levy2026 fab and platform: confirm empty. The Intel hybrid platform sentence is about the earlier array [33]
   (p.2); for this die only passive fabrication at a nominal spacing (p.4) and no acknowledgement (D80b, D422). The
   ring platform is not stated either (the Si p-i-n modulators are, p.4). See question 1 for the subtype.
6. mehta2026 O-NET: no fab or facility entry. D398 covers an MPW access provider named for chip fabrication support
   beside a foundry run; here collaborators at O-NET are thanked for supporting ELS packaging and evaluation-board
   development (p.5 Acknowledgment), with no statement that O-NET assembled the module (D80b needs one). Register O-NET
   in the org round (name as printed, p.5) and keep the note.
7. mehta2026 7.2 % efficiency: keep `wpe_pct`, no statistic, with the text definition (includes LD bias circuitry and
   PCB / parasitic losses, excludes TEC; p.3); the text names Fig. 5(e) as the module efficiency, the axis label (LD
   efficiency) is in notes. The figure does not settle the statistic: module-1's channel mean is about 7.21 %, all 24
   points about 7.13 % (added to notes). Keep the Fig. 5(e) range row.
8. mao2024 intrinsic linewidths: confirm `derived`. The authors convert the Lorentz-fitted -20 dB widths with a cited
   relation (p.2 Sec. 3, ref. [9]): model-based conversion of own data (D80a), and kHz -> Hz (D256); the zhuang2025
   rows are the same case. xie linewidths derived for kHz -> Hz only (D256): correct.

Fab orgs (D80b): org_huawei on the mao S-band row is correct (first-person fabrication sentence, p.2; all authors
Huawei; D399 for the L-band rows, whose fabrication is reported only in earlier work). All other rows empty: hatai
(passive, p.1), zhou (passive, p.1), levy (passive, p.4), mehta (passive, p.2), xie (passive, p.4). No facility
