## Rulings on the batch report's judgment calls

1. pfister2025 PWB transfer ratio 28.6 +/- 8.8 %: endorsed, no row. p.4-5 Eq. (1) defines Gamma_trans = eta_Lens,PIC /
   eta_Lens,NA, a ratio of two collection efficiencies (bare microlens into NA 0.6 as the reference), not a coupled
   power fraction; it can in principle exceed 100 % (p.5 notes the PWB collection window is wider than NA 0.6).
   coupling_efficiency_pct ("coupled power fraction at the stated scope") does not match; insertion_loss_db would need a
   defined input power. Gap kept (below).
2. chowdhury2024 cavity fab = UBC: endorsed (borderline). p.3 "fabricated through a multi-project run via SiEPICfab";
   p.13 Sec. 4 "fabricated in the SiEPICfab UBC in-house process flow". The MPW / process service (SiEPICfab) is the
   fab org under D83d / D398 but is missing from the registry; the printed statement itself names UBC ("UBC in-house"),
   so UBC is not a name-only mapping. The SBQMI ANF is the shared facility (p.13 Acknowledgments, "technical support
   ... to conduct the fabrication and assembly process flow"), correctly in facility_org_ids and never a fab basis
   (D84b / D72). When a SiEPICfab org row exists, fab should move to it (UBC as parent). DFB rows: org_macom as vendor
   of the purchased die is rule 12; on-chip DFB facility ANF covers the assembly (same acknowledgment); fiber-PWB
   control has no stated site (empty, endorsed).
3. chowdhury2024 WBG Fabry-Perot cavity as resonator.bragg: endorsed (two waveguide-Bragg mirrors around a cavity,
   p.4 Sec. 2.1, Fig. 2(a); `bragg` = Bragg resonator).
4. yulaev2024 fab = NIST: endorsed (borderline). Contract v4 needs an explicit fabrication statement: p.2 "We fabricated
   the MS-PIC emitter in two separate steps" names the authors; every author lists NIST (Yulaev and Ropp also UMD, whose
   role p.4-5 Acknowledgments is the PREP administration), and the work was "performed in part at the NIST Center for
   Nanoscale Science and Technology" (p.5), so the fabrication site resolves to NIST (D83c). CNST as facility_org_ids is
   correct ("performed in part at").
5. yulaev2024 -6.2 dB on the MS-PIC emitter row as device_total: endorsed. p.2: -1st-order power "relative to the
   power in an input fiber"; the row label and core_material define the MS-PIC as the full chain (edge coupler,
   expander, grating, metasurface), includes lists them; abstract -6.3 dB noted. slsr_db 10 dB: endorsed; the registry
   definition for free_space_emitter is "main lobe over the highest sidelobe / grating lobe, as stated", and the 0th /
   -2nd diffraction orders are grating lobes; the highest is the 0th at about 10 dB below (Fig. 1(e) caption; text
   "at least a factor of 10"). m2 metasurface-only 2.4 dB simulated on the same row is acceptable with includes.
6. chang2022 85 % / 0.8 dB at inferred 852 nm: endorsed. The headline carries no wavelength (p.1 Abstract, p.8 Sec. 6);
   fiber alignment and Ttot were optimized at 852 nm (p.5), the design wavelength (p.2): wavelength_inferred true with
   the reason (skill rule 8; D393 does not apply, nothing is stated with the value). The mismatch with the Fig. 2(b) TE
   symbols (81-83 %) is recorded in notes. Basis derived (T = sqrt(eta Ttot), D80a) endorsed.
7. verdier2017 RSOA wavelength from a figure peak: fixed (table above). A value printed in the text keeps its basis;
   its wavelength condition comes from the stated operating band when none is stated (rule 8), never from a figure
   reading, so the extracted_from_figure question does not arise for the value.
