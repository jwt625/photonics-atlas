## Rulings on the batch report's judgment calls

1. pathak2014 two rows per design (5 nm and 1 nm mask grid): endorsed. Skill rule 9: the two grid exports were
   fabricated side by side as separate devices (p.3 Sec. V) and Table I (p.4) reports separately measured IL, XT and
   CXT for each grid, so each grid version is a different fabricated design with its own measurements. FSR basis
   corrected (above). XT as crosstalk_db (single-channel crosstalk floor, p.2 Fig. 3 definition, no adjacent /
   non-adjacent contrast): endorsed. 4- and 8-channel IL `band unspecified`: endorsed (no spectra, no wavelength).
   Abstract averages (-26 / -23 dB) left out: endorsed (D254). Fab org imec: endorsed as borderline. "We fabricated
   those devices on a 200mm SOI wafer" (p.3 Sec. V) names the authors (D80b); all authors at Ghent University-imec or
   imec (sub-units of one parent, D154) and the design targets "IMEC's passive silicon photonics platform" (p.3 Sec.
   III); bogaerts2006 precedent. The Optical IO Industrial Affiliation Program (p.4) is a program, not a facility.
2. xia2026 / zali2026 separate amplifier.soa rows per SOA switch path: endorsed (D396: one device in switch and
   amplifier functions, one row per class, cross-noted). zali2026 gains author_estimate: endorsed; p.5 Sec. 4.2 states
   the passive losses used in the gain calculation are "measured directly or referenced in the literature" (rule 4,
   not D166). xia2026 on-chip gains 30 / 23 dB author_estimate: endorsed; the de-embedding is not shown and does not
   close for port 2 (5.5 dB net + 6 + 6 + 11 dB = 28.5 dB vs 30 dB printed, p.4-5).
3. Borderline fab orgs. zali2026 org_smart_photonics: endorsed. "we design, fabricate, and demonstrate" (p.3 Sec. 2)
   plus "the active-passive butt-joint integration process of the SMART Photonics commercial platform is utilized"
   (p.4 Sec. 3) names the foundry platform that made the chip (rule 12; two authors at Smart Photonics); TU/e not added
   (no contribution statement places fabrication there). arafat2024 org_tyndall_national_institute on the printed-PD
   row only: endorsed (D399; "We transfer printed the PDs ... on the SOI circuit", p.2; all authors Tyndall; the SOI
   circuit is only "fabricated using a silicon-on-insulator (SOI) wafer", passive, D80b; the Munster Technological
   University thanks is to a person, not a facility). xia2026 fab empty: endorsed (only "the PDK of the foundry", p.4;
   the "we designed, fabricated" sentence is about the earlier report, D399).
4. arafat2024 GC duty cycle 4 % kept as 0.04 (derived) with a flag: endorsed. The duty cycle is printed once (p.2) with
   no definition; the Fig. 4(a) inset is an unlabelled SEM micrograph (never measured off a micrograph); no second
   statement contradicts it, so D159 does not apply; D253 keeps undefined duty cycles as printed with a note.
5. xia2026 ER > 60 dB (conclusion, p.8) left out: endorsed. The results (abstract, p.2, p.4 Sec. 2.2) give > 35 dB
   for both the gate-only and the both-blocked definitions; the conclusion's 60 dB has no supporting measurement in
   the paper; the supported bound is entered (D159-style, noted on the component).
6. arafat2024 port suppression 11 / 9.5 dB as extinction_db: endorsed (photocurrent ratio between the two output PDs,
   compared by the authors with the 25 dB ER; Fig. 5(b) bars consistent).

   single wafer-site channel centred near 1290 nm, matching the text (p.4, p.14). One row per filter configuration with
   the two channel periods as conditions: endorsed (only one channel is characterized statistically; the second
   channel device has no separate measured values).
