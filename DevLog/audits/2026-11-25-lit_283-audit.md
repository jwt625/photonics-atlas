## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. (a) shtyrkova2019 215 fs. Keep `pulse_width_fs` 215 `derived`. D84c covers it directly: the merged wang2024b rows use the same
   "supports a pulse duration" wording (computed from the authors' own sech2 spectral bandwidth, no autocorrelation) and are
   `derived`, as are byun2009 and karunakaran2024 transform-limit rows. The only non-measured input is the sech2 shape, which is the
   authors' standard fit of their own spectrum (D80a), not an assumed or cited numeric input, so `author_estimate` does not apply.
   The condition already says "transform-limited ... no pulse-duration measurement"; Fig. 10(b) prints "~215fs" next to the 17 nm
   arrows.
2. (b) shtyrkova2019 three chirped-grating rows `fabricated`. Keep (D80e). The p.3 sentence covers "all the lasers and test
   structures described in this work", each chip carries 9 full MLLs (p.12), and the three gratings are presented as designs of
   the laser's end mirror; only which design sits in the measured laser is unstated, and the component notes say so. All three
   values are `simulated` either way, so `row_kind` changes only the fabricated / design split. Alternative (not recommended):
   `design` on the -46,000 and -30,000 rows and `fabricated` on -38,000 because the Sec. 4 model uses it; the model choice is not a
   statement about the measured laser. Figure check: Fig. 6(b) zooms put the -38,000 curve at about 94.5-95 % and the -30,000
   curve at about 90 % at 1.85 and 1.95 um, so the printed bounds over 1850-1950 nm hold only marginally (edge readings added to
   notes; no D417 conflict at the reading uncertainty).
3. (c) shtyrkova2019 fab. Keep CNSE on the passive rows and CNSE + `org_mit` (fab) + `org_mit_microsystems_technology_laboratory`
   (facility) on the two gain-bearing rows (D448: the sentence names the place where the film was deposited). NLI heater: the patch
