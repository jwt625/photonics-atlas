## Coordinator questions

1. arianfard2020 comparison designs (four comparison BPFs, MZI interleaver). Recommendation: keep (notes patched only). They are the
   paper's own designs and simulations: "the above filters were designed based on the same SOI wire waveguide ... and had the same ER
   and FSR" (p.15); "The MZI and the parallel MC-SLR resonator are designed to have a small CS of about 100 GHz" (p.9). No value is taken
   from refs. [33], [41], [77, 78]; only the architectures are, and the 3-C-SLRs foil is the authors' own earlier architecture
   re-simulated at matched ER / FSR, not a restated value, so rule 3 / D259 / D408 do not apply. Precedent for simulated comparison
   designs as `design` rows: chen2006 plain crossing, ma2023 direct crossing, liu2024 / shang2023 bends. The weakness: the four BPF rows
   have no geometry and one value each, constrained to another device's ER / FSR (foils, not proposed devices). Alternative: delete the
   four bpf_cmp_* components (4 metrics) and keep the MZI (it has a full Table II column).
2. arianfard2020 Fano device class. Recommendation: keep `resonator.other` (no op). `resonator.photonic_crystal` ("photonic-crystal /
   Fabry-Perot cavity", used for wu2018 cascaded SLRs, D454) fits only the t_b = 1 limit; the top bus adds a feedback path (hybrid FIR /
   IIR, p.4-5), so the device is not a pure FP cavity. The cited n_g 4.3350 and 2.4 dB/cm (p.5, "values obtained from our previously
   fabricated SOI devices [33, 40]") correctly stay out (rule 3, D259) and are quoted as model inputs in conditions.
3. arianfard2020 thermal drift ~64 pm/K (il_100ghz m10). Recommendation: delete (done in the patch). It is lambda x dn/dT / n_g of
   two cited inputs (64.3 pm/K vs 64.1 pm/K read), independent of the filter design, read off a plot, and never printed; rule 3 excludes
   values computed from cited values. Counter-precedent: melati2022 echelle 80 pm/K and heermeier2025 design-independent dlambda/dT
   rows were kept (D456), but those are printed numbers. To reverse: drop the delete_metrics op.
4. yang2018a orgs and Q basis. Recommendation: (a) fab `org_caltech` kept (explicit author-contribution fabrication statement, single
   affiliation; D80b / D395); (b) facility KNI removed (done in the patch): KNI appears only beside the DARPA award with no use wording,
   the D83e situation (KNMF named only in funding lists). guo2023 kept KNI because its acknowledgment says the nanofabrication "was
   performed at" KNI; this paper does not. To reverse: drop the four `facility_org_ids` changes (borderline-kept with note, D80c). (c)
   Q basis: loaded 214 M `measured` (Fig. 2(c) label, caption "loaded"), all Q0 values `author_estimate` (D403): the regime is never stated;
   the dips (TE about 0.05, TM about 0.55 transmission) do not fix it without a gap series. Endorse as entered.
5. lake2016 efficiencies and platform. Recommendation: keep as entered (no op). (a) %/W `derived` (D82d / D256; only /mW printed). (b)
   Two maxima: 4.4e-4 /mW is the measured highest-efficiency point at Pcrit (p.4; consistent with 1.5e-4 / 0.35 mW); 3.8e-4 is the P^2
   fit to data scaled to double resonance (Fig. 4(c)), which the abstract (">") and conclusion ("maximum") quote; both kept with their
   definitions and `statistic best` on the measured point (D417 style; the conflict is in the component notes). (c) Platform `other`:
   the guiding stack is stated (undercut GaP microdisk), it is just not in the enum (not `gap_on_insulator`: no insulator, undercut);
   D422 / D423 apply when the stack itself is not stated (SOI never said), not here. Precedents: hausmann2014 diamond, thiel2026 InGaP.
