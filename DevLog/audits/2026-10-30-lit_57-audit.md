## Questions raised by the distiller (evidence-based recommendations)

1. barwicz2016 Fig. 6 (-1.3 dB, 0.8 dB penalty over 100 nm): keep out (D259a). Evidence: p.4 "The resulting optical
   performance with self-alignment is presented on Fig. 6 for a metamaterial converter in the O-band [16]"; Fig. 6
   caption ends "More information is found in [16]"; [16] = the authors' OFC 2015 Th3F.3 on that O-band converter. The
   value sentence and the figure both attribute the device and its data to [16], which is the D408 exception, so D259a
   applies (muthuganesan2023 Table 1 precedent, D417). barwicz2016 is an overview of several own conference papers
   ([12], [16], [17], [21]-[25]), not the journal extension of [16]; the abstract headline does not change the
   attribution. No component row (no other metric).
2. barwicz2016 fab: org_ibm_watson on the chip-to-chip row (patched), polymer interface empty. Evidence: p.9 "where most
   of the photonic chips were fabricated" (MRL, IBM T. J. Watson) followed by the only exception, the Fig. 6 device at
   the IBM Microelectronics Foundry; the Fig. 13 assembly uses a Si photonic die and a Si photonic secondary die. Same
   lab statement gives org_ibm_watson on 14 merged barwicz2017 rows (own lab, D404). The polymer interface guides mostly
   in the polymer ribbon, whose fabricator is not stated (refs. [19], [20], [25], an AGC co-author), so "photonic
   chips" does not cover the component. If "most" is judged too weak (D159 analogue), drop the port B op.
3. Orgs: Heriot-Watt University, University of St Andrews and Institut fur Mikroelektronik Stuttgart (IMS) are absent
   from organizations.csv and org_aliases.csv (no name, alias or substring match; checked 2026-10-30). Needed: Heriot-Watt
   University (thomson2007 p.1 affiliation of all authors; p.2 "we use ultrafast-laser waveguide-inscription to
   successfully fabricate": fab org on both thomson2007 rows once registered, rule 12). Not needed for a role: St Andrews
   (taillaert2006 p.2 affiliation; D154), IMS (zaoui2012 p.1 affiliation; fabrication "according to an SOI wafer flow
   concept" with ref. [12] by IMS authors, but no statement names IMS as fabricator; D154), IBM Microelectronics
   Foundry, Essex Junction (barwicz2016 p.9, only the unentered Fig. 6 device), Asahi Glass / AGC Electronics
   (barwicz2016 p.1 affiliation).
4. Judgment calls:

      (p.3) and Sec. 3 (p.6-7) give the same 630 nm / 70 nm / 10 deg / 220 nm / 1 um BOX / air design and p.7 compares
      the measurement with "the simulation results"; skill rule 9 sets row_kind design only for components that exist

      grating and are separate design rows: correct.

      coupling efficiency"; the Eq. (3.1) two-coupler method is described for SOI (p.6). Simulated 34 % / 54 % are
      single-coupler model values (per_coupler).

      measured a coupling efficiency of 69 % on a coupler with a gold mirror" carries ref. 20 (Van Laere, to be
      published): the data belong to that publication (rule 3) and the device is not described (no grating, stack,
      wavelength or method), so a row would be a bare number with unspecified scope. Alternative if the coordinator
