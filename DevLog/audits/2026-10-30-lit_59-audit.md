   `organizations.csv` / `org_aliases.csv`; `org_astar_ime`, `org_national_institute_of_standards_and_technology` and

     (p.1): not a fab org (D80c).

     org_national_institute_of_standards_and_technology) and set facility_org_ids on the 7 fabricated chiles2017 rows
     (not the ipc_design row); fab stays NIST (ruling 3).
   - Freescale Semiconductor, Inc.: mekis2010 p.2 Sec. III A: the Luxtera CMOS photonics process "is based on the 0.13
     um CMOS process available at Freescale Semiconductor, Inc." and the wafers "are processed in the semiconductor
     fabrication plant (or, fab) from the front end through the backend". Recommend: register (company, foundry role)
     and set fab_org_ids on the 3 mekis2010 rows; the statement names the process owner and says the wafers run in its
     fab (rule 12 foundry). Not org_luxtera: "We have developed a silicon photonics platform" (p.1) is a development
     statement (D80b).

   year, "authors state they fabricated ... 200 mm SOI ... 248 nm DUV", one joint Ghent University - IMEC affiliation,
   no acknowledgment): fab empty, "org not unambiguous"; (b) bogaerts2007a / bogaerts2006: org_imec through an
   acknowledgment of the IMEC P-line processing, pathak2014: org_imec with imec-only co-authors; (c) vandekerckhove2025
   / desmet2020 / liu2025b: Ghent University (plus imec where imec is the named foundry) when the text or author
   contributions tie the processing to Ghent. bogaerts2007 has none of the (b) / (c) evidence and is the (a) case;
   "both orgs" would be the only lit row assigning both from a joint affiliation alone. Alternative if the coordinator
   prefers attribution: org_imec only (the 200 mm / 248 nm DUV line), which needs a ruling that also re-opens
   brouckaert2007 and dumon2004.
3. PSR crosstalk: recommend one rule by definition, not by the paper's word. per_db stays for port ratios (wanted /
   unwanted polarization power at one port, or the ER / isolation of a PBS): lu2015 Eqs. 9-12 (P_TE,through /
   P_TM,through), chen2021 (TM0-input vs TE0-input power at a port), cheng2023 (ER = TM0 over TE0 transmission). A
   leakage of the wrong input mode into a port normalized to the input power (guan2014a measured, p.3 / Fig. 7(b);
   gao2026 simulated through-port crosstalk below -30 / -24 dB, p.6) is crosstalk_db, which needs polarization added to
   its classes (registry extension; two papers, one measured value, definition unambiguous with the stated wording in
   condition). Until then guan2014a m6 is out with the value in notes (patched, D401 pattern), and a gap-fill re-adds it
   as crosstalk_db > 20 dB (1290-1330 nm, derived, p.3 / p.4 Fig. 7(b)). If the coordinator rules per_db for both,
   drop the delete op and the guan2014a component op. Follow-up check: merged lin2022 psr_mode_evolution-m2 ("cross
   talk ~23 dB", wrong-output wording) should be read against the same rule.
