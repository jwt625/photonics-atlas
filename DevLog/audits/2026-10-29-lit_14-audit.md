## Rulings on the batch report's judgment calls

- One physical device as several class rows. davenport2016 SOA vs FP laser: endorsed; the laser rows describe the
  same die before AR coating (p.9 "Prior to AR coating, the devices lase"), threshold / slope efficiency are
  laser-only parameters, and Table IV reports both roles. otterstrom2019 amplifier / isolator / passive resonator:
  endorsed; gain and NF (amplifier), isolation_db (nonreciprocal) and Q / FSR / loss / coupling (resonator) cannot sit
  on one row. Rule 9 ("one row per physical design") is not met literally; both follow the wang2021 class-role split
  endorsed in the lit_10 audit, whose question 3 (convention) is still open. white2022 isolators plus the
  `sin_ring_d200` array row is the same pattern (q_intrinsic not allowed for nonreciprocal).
- matsumoto2018 Table I per-interface misalignment excess as insertion_loss_db per_interface with
  includes / excludes: endorsed as the only registry-conformant encoding (excess_loss_db not allowed for
  chip_chip_coupler; gap below); basis changed to author_estimate (see findings).
- matsumoto2018 AIST fab: kept on the two SSC rows and the butt-coupler row (p.2-3 names AIST as fabricator of the
  300 um Si inverse taper, the core of the dual-core SSC; the butt row is borderline because the SOA-side SSC
  fabricator is not stated, noted on the row). Removed from the 4x4 switch row (D80b, see findings). The SOA rows stay
  empty (SOA fabricator not stated).
- pintus2017 Fig. 8 and circulator 11 / 6.7 dB: D259a applied strictly. [25] and [35] are the authors' own papers
  (Huang et al., co-author of this paper); the 11 / 6.7 dB sentence carries [35]; Fig. 8 is data on the [25] device.
  Rows removed; the R 35 um isolator is deleted (no metric left). The cascaded isolator (28 dB, new device, p.6),
  the simulated single-ring circulator (9.15 dB, own model at the computed optimum 9% coupling) and the two-ring
  design (18.3 dB simulated) stay: their inputs are design / material parameters (couplings from [36], Faraday
  constant from [24]), not cited results, so rule 3's "computed from cited values" does not apply to own simulations.
- guo2016 fab: changed to org_yale_university (see findings). Facilities Yale SEAS cleanroom and YINQE are not in the
  registry (question 1).
- davenport2016 fab empty: endorsed (thanks to a named person for fabrication assistance, no affiliation printed;
  no first-person fabrication statement; facility not named).
- davenport2016 Table IV Ps not entered: endorsed. Ps is the Eq. (1) material gain saturation power (p.2); Table II
  heads the same numbers "Ps,out", and the abstract (14 dBm) / conclusion (16.25 dBm) print saturated output powers

- davenport2016 conflicts (3-dB width 66 / 69 / 65 nm; facet reflection 0.28 vs 0.28%): text values entered,

## Registry gaps confirmed (value actually skipped, proposed definition)

- radius_um, gap_nm, bus_width_nm for frequency_converter: guo2016 R 30 um, pump bus 0.8 um at 0.6 um gap, SH bus
  0.175 -> 0.125 um at 0.5 um gap (p.3). Same definitions as for resonator.
- radius_um, gap_nm for nonreciprocal: pintus2017 cascaded isolator R 20 um, gap 200 nm (p.5), circulator R 20 um
  (p.6); white2022 ring D 200 um (p.6, currently on the resonator row only). Same definitions as for resonator.
- isolation_threshold_mw (nonreciprocal): white2022 12.9 / 6.5 mW (p.9). Definition verified in the paper (p.5):
  "input power required to isolate by 3 dB"; proposed "input power at which a nonlinear isolator reaches 3 dB
  isolation".
- isolation_bw_mhz (nonreciprocal): otterstrom2019 2.5 MHz at > 10 dB, 350 kHz FWHM of the nonreciprocity peak
  (p.4 Fig. 3(c)); "frequency width over which isolation exceeds the stated level (level in condition)". Conversion
  to isolation_bw_nm would need an inferred wavelength.
- pump_threshold_nj (frequency_converter, laser): mutter2024 325 nJ (p.3); "optical pump threshold as pulse energy
  (pulse width and repetition rate in condition)".
- nonreciprocal_split_nm (nonreciprocal): pintus2017 0.302 nm at 200 mA on the 230 nm-gap cascaded sibling (p.5,
  Fig. 12); "CW-CCW resonance split from the nonreciprocal phase shift (drive / field in condition)". The 0.32 nm at
  140 mA (p.4 Fig. 6) is on the [25] device and would fall under D259a.
- mfd_chip_um for nonreciprocal: lapointe2025 9.4 um at the rotator-waveguide output (p.5 Fig. 3(a)).
- excess_loss_db for chip_chip_coupler: matsumoto2018 Table I 2.3 / 2.8 dB per interface (p.5); would replace the
  insertion_loss_db + excludes encoding.
- back_reflection_db for waveguide_platform: davenport2016 fitted taper-tip field reflectivity r = 1e-3 (p.8; power
  -60 dB, derived). Also proposed in lit_11.
- thermal_resistance_k_per_w for amplifier: davenport2016 11 K/W (p.9, thermoreflectance), stated for "the devices"
  without a design; entry would need a ruling on a value shared by four sibling designs (D254).
- power_handling_dbm for nonreciprocal: lapointe2025 IL and isolation unchanged for 1-15 mW input (p.5), i.e.
  11.8 dBm ge (derived); same definition as for the allowed classes.
- center_wavelength_nm for frequency_converter: mutter2024 backward wave 1514.6 nm, forward wave 1688.7 nm (p.3);
  "output wave centre wavelength (which wave in condition)".
- Not confirmed: amplifier gain bandwidth in frequency (otterstrom2019 "10 MHz to sub-MHz" is a trend with an
  unquantified end, p.5); guo2016 temperature acceptance FWHM 7.7 C (p.4, single value; would need a new
  temperature-width parameter); lapointe2025 Faraday figure of merit 346 deg/dB (single value); frequency_converter
  extinction (guo2016 prints a linear 0.75 without its definition).
