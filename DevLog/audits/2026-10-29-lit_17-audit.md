## Rulings on the batch report's judgment calls

- psaila2024 glass waveguide bridge as setup equipment: endorsed. p.5 Sec. III: it is the probe used to couple in and
  out of the bridge + PIC assembly ("used to couple in and out", periscope test configuration, Fig. 11); rule 13 / D160
  (djogo2026 probe precedent). Its measured 34.7 x 31.6 um beam (p.5, Fig. 12) correctly stays a note on the bridge row.
- mangal2019a fab org_centre_for_microsystems_technology: kept as borderline under D84b. The only basis is the
  acknowledgment (p.6) thanking CMST staff for the microlens RIE and die lapping, i.e. an org's own staff helping with
  device fabrication, which D84b places at that org for fab_org_ids (never facility_org_ids, D72). Rule 12's "staff
  thanks are not entered" governs facility_org_ids; the first and last two authors are CMST, so no foreign org is
  implied. Note on the row already says borderline.
- ibrahimi2021 2.539 GHz vs "6th harmonic" of 360 MHz: keep as printed with the note (m2). The frequency is printed
  twice (p.6 text, p.7 Fig. 16 caption) and the conclusion says "up to the 2.5 GHz repetition rate"; the inconsistency
  is in the harmonic index (2.539 / 0.36 = 7.05), not in the measured RF line, so the value is unambiguous.
- chen2023 gain chip / SOA properties as specification: endorsed. p.3 Sec. 2.1 names the parts (Thorlabs SAF1126C;
  SOA "developed by ShiJia Photons") and states their resistance, length, mode size, gain > 25 dB as part properties
  with no own measurement; rule 4 defines specification as the datasheet / nominal value of a purchased part, which
  does not require a cited datasheet.
