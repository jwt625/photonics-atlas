## Coordinator questions

1. baumgartner2020 fab (report Q1). The paper gives a first-person fabrication account (p.3-5: "We used molecular direct wafer bonding",
   "we fabricated shorter and narrower devices", "We fabricated 2-um-long photodetectors") by authors at IBM Research - Zurich, ETH Zurich
   (two institutes) and IBM T. J. Watson, with no author contributions, so no single own org (D154). The acknowledgment thanks "the BRNC
   OpTeam for support in fabricating the devices" (p.6). Recommendation: keep `org_binnig_and_rohrer_nanotechnology_center` as the D84b
   borderline fab (staff thanked specifically for fabrication support; xie2024 / bhasker2026 construction), facility empty (D72).
   Alternative: empty fab (iadanza2024, where the BRNC operations team was thanked only "for their help and support"). IBM Research -
   Zurich is not supportable: no sentence names IBM as the fabrication site (D80b); the IBM-fab precedents accepted in the lit_01 / lit_04
   audits rest on author contributions. Note for the benchmark: no merged row has BRNC as fab yet (it appears only as facility).
2. morgan2021 dark-current bias (report Q2). Recommendation: keep both rows with `bias_v -2` and the existing "bias not printed" notes.
   Type II: the Fig. 2(d) trace reads about 0.085-0.09 mA at the -2 V end of the sweep, matching the printed 90.8 uA, so the value is the
   reverse end of the plotted I-V. Type I: "< 1 nA (measurement limited)" is read off the same sweep (flat trace), and -2 V is its largest
   reverse bias, so the bound holds at -2 V. Alternative: delete the Type I row only (its -2 V is not separately confirmable on the mA axis).
3. morgan2021 2.9 dB/cm and 8.4 dB (report Q3). Recommendation: own measurement, keep `measured`. The values are stated as "was measured" /
   "was found" with the paperclip method on devices "fabricated on the same run" as the detectors; ref. [4] ends the calibration sentence
   and is the Al2O3 platform paper also cited on p.1 for the waveguides, i.e. the source of the paperclip / waveguide design (D80i). The
   distiller's note placed the citation on a different sentence; corrected in the patch. The responsivity rows built on these losses stay
   `derived`.
4. chen2022c class (report Q4). Recommendation: keep `switch_fabric.other` for the 2x2 and 1x2 devices. The paper names and measures them
   as routing switches with bar / cross states, ER and crosstalk (D158 / D407 single switch elements; merged PCM precedents ros2022
   `mzi_switch_2x2` and hu2020 `si_gst_cdc_switch_p376` are `switch_fabric.other`). chen2024a's QC-DC is a multi-level tunable coupler with
   a split ratio, which is why it went to `splitter.directional_coupler`. Sibling `phase_shifter.phase_change` rows for endurance stay
   (D396) until `endurance_cycles` is allowed on switch_fabric.
