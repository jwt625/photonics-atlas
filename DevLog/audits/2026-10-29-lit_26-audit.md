## Rulings on the batch report's judgment calls

1. zhang2020 fab "Institute of Microelectronics, China" -> IME CAS: not kept. The text names no parent org (p.4 Sec. 3,
   abstract "multiproject wafer foundry"), the bare name is not unique (the registry already maps the alias
   "Institute of Microelectronics" to A*STAR IME), and resolving it to CAS rests on outside knowledge (rule 12
   unambiguity, D154 spirit). fab_org_ids empty with the printed name in notes.
2. Facility thanks: chen2026a org_ucsb_nanofabrication_facility kept: p.7 acknowledges "the support of the entire UCSB
   Nanofabrication facility and its staff", a thanks to the facility itself (lit_09 cheung2023 precedent, D80c); KNI
   appears only in the funding sentence and as a staff thanks: not entered (endorsed). li2026
   org_shanghaitech_material_and_device_lab kept: p.10 thanks the lab itself (with the Core Facility Platform of
   Electronics) "for their technical support" (D80c borderline, noted); Max-Optics is a company, not a facility
   (endorsed).
3. xu2018 split ratio "3 +/- 0.27 dB" stored as 47.1-53.3 %: endorsed. Fig. 10(b) (p.10) plots "Power Splitting Ratio
   (dB)" for the bar and cross ports (about -3.2 and -2.85 dB), computed from the MZI extinction ratio with the formula
   of refs. [12,19]; that method yields the port fractions of the coupler output (bar + cross = 1), so the dB values
   are per-port power fractions, not per-port insertion losses, and 10^(-0.327) / 10^(-0.273) = 47.1 / 53.3 % is a valid
   derived form of split_ratio_pct. Simulated m5 / m7 (Fig. 7(a), 8(a)): normalization of the FDTD port powers is not
   stated, but the simulated IL is < 0.08 / < 0.13 dB (under 3 % relative), kept simulated. No dB split-deviation
   parameter is needed for this paper.
4. Granularity: spencer2015 per-design rows from Table 1 endorsed (skill rule 9; each of the 24 designs has its own
   kappa / FWHM / peak R). lin2019: the family row is endorsed for the family-level statements (excess loss < 0.5 dB
   for all designs, PSR 0 to -18 dB, simulated values; D165), but per-design measured PSRs are readable in the Fig. 3(a)
   page render (the distill's "mirrored" note applies only to the flipped embedded image), so the 11 Table 1 designs
   get their own rows (rule 9) with the measured PSR read off the figure plus the three simulated PSRs the text states.
5. Other calls endorsed: chen2026a ring-down QL 276 M derived (authors' tau -> Q, D80a), 0.07 dB/m author_estimate (ng
   not stated; D256a for the unit conversion), D165 array row, Southampton ORC as wafer preparer in notes only (rule 12),
   fab org_caltech (p.7 contributions: two Caltech-only authors "developed, fabricated, and annealed" the rings);
   zhang2020 1524 nm inferred from the Fig. 5(a) major resonance (1524 nm / 1.20 pm = 1.27e6, consistent with QL);
   li2026 SLL -18.2 dB not entered while the Fig. 7(d) label 17.9 dB on the theta scan at 0 deg stays (label tied to an
   arrow on that lobe; text pairs -18.2 / -17.9 with Delta theta / Delta phi: conflict recorded in the OPA notes),
   DC target ratios author_estimate (D82a); lin2019 and xu2018 fab org_applied_nanotools (p.3 / p.8 name Applied
   Nanotools for the EBL fabrication); xu2018 IL as half the MZI excess (derived) and MZI ER >= 24.2 dB as
   extinction_db; spencer2015 fab empty (passive voice, authors at UCSB, Johns Hopkins, Morton Photonics), kappa
   derived (T-matrix fit with an assumed plane-wave interface reflectivity: model extraction, D80a), FWHM pm -> nm and
   peak R dB -> % derived (D82d), design period 528 nm on post and sampled rows (first-order Bragg period, p.2), the

## Registry gaps confirmed (value actually skipped, proposed definition)

- crosstalk_db for waveguide_platform: li2026 dense AGF array, measured below -20 dB to nearest and second-nearest
  neighbours over 1500-1600 nm (p.6 Fig. 4(c)). Def: power coupled to a neighbouring waveguide of a dense array,
  positive dB, neighbour order and propagation length in condition.
- Split-ratio wavelength variation for splitter (new `split_ratio_variation_db`, or wdl_db widened to splitter):
  li2026 AGF DCs, maximum power variation below 0.77 dB over 1500-1600 nm (p.8 Sec. 2.2); lin2019 simulated
  split-ratio variation below 1 dB over 100 nm (p.1). Def: max minus min of a port's split ratio (dB) over the stated
  span.
- kappa_per_cm for resonator: spencer2015 lambda/4-shifted grating, fitted kappa 30 cm-1 (p.6 Sec. 4.2). Def: grating
  coupling coefficient of a Bragg / phase-shifted grating resonator.
- gap_nm for reflector: spencer2015 post-to-waveguide gap 0.8-1.6 um on 8 rows (p.2, p.6 Table 1). Def: lateral gap
  between a side perturbation (post) and the waveguide core.
- Width-perturbation depth for reflector (new `corrugation_width_nm`): spencer2015 Delta w 0.2 / 0.25 / 0.3 um on 16
  sampled / high-order rows and 1.2 um on the lambda/4 grating (p.2, Table 1, p.6). Def: full difference between the
  wide and narrow waveguide widths of a sidewall-corrugated grating. Not in the batch report.
- diffraction_order widened to reflector: spencer2015 3rd / 5th-order gratings, 8 rows (p.2, Table 1). Def as for wdm
  (grating order). Not in the batch report.
- Lower priority: pitch_um for waveguide_platform (li2026 750 nm array pitch, p.5; already on the OPA row as
  free_space_emitter), wdl_db for waveguide_platform (li2026 through-power variation below 1.5 dB over 100 nm, p.6;
  D250 deferred it for an ambiguous +/- value, this one is a stated max variation), taper_length_um for reflector
  (wang2017 2 um, p.8), per_db for reflector (wang2017 simulated > 16.5 dB over 1433-1763 nm, p.8), sampling period of a
  sampled grating (spencer2015 T 40.128 um, p.2).

  change (< 0.07 dB, 280-380 K, simulated only).
