## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. resan2005 / kim2004 at 0 rows (judgment 1): keep 0 rows. resan2005 is a free-space sigma-ring cavity with two intracavity dual-grating
   double-pass compressors with telescopes, a SA mirror (described in ref. [7]), PBS / wave plates (Fig. 1), external SOA and bulk
   compressor; every printed value (185 fs, 323 MHz, 14 mW, 230 W) is a system output. kim2004 is the same kind of bench-top
   colliding-pulse external-cavity laser plus an X-CPA chain. D411 scope and the D642 fontaine2019 precedent apply; every merged
   `laser.ecl` / `laser.tunable` external cavity is chip-scale (Si / Si3N4 / LNOI circuits). The purchased Sarnoff SOAs are chip-scale and
   rule 12 would make Sarnoff their fab, but neither paper prints a performance value of an SOA by itself (resan2005 Fig. 2 is an
   emission spectrum only; kim2004 prints 154 / 238 nJ per stretched pulse with no gain or power per amplifier), so a row would have
   no allowed metric (rule 9). If `pulse_energy_nj` is added on `amplifier` (Q6), kim2004 3 mm inverse-bowtie SOA (154 nJ, 50 A peak)
   and 4 mm broad-area amplifier (238 nJ, 80 A peak; p.2, Fig. 3) become rows with fab Sarnoff (org missing); revisit then. The CFBG
   (2000 / 4000 ps/nm) stays out until a dispersion parameter exists (D81 / D250).
2. bimberg2009 HHI fab (judgment 2): keep on the facet row only. The thanks name HHI staff for "assistance with the AR-coating of the
   QD SOAs" (p.4), a facet process; D84b gives a borderline fab for staff thanks at a named org, and Contract v7 places an org named
   for one part's process on that part only (D515 / D463; wei2023 audit: IOP on the HR coating only; mangal2020: a fab org for one
   part not extended to AR-coated GC rows). The SOA processing is passive with no org (D80b), so the three SOA rows stay empty. The
   printed name has a typo ("Heinrich-Hertz-Instiute"); the org id is right.
3. bimberg2009 gain per length (judgment 3): keep in notes. The registry has `gain_db_per_cm` on `amplifier` (46 merged rows), so this
   is not a parameter gap. But the 10 / 7 dB/mm come from a "basic comparison" made before the 2 / 3 mm samples were fabricated
   ("Thus, 2 mm ... and 3 mm ... samples were fabricated", p.1). So they belong to unnamed earlier samples of each material, not to
   the 2 mm or 3 mm rows (D159). The p-doped value is also a family value across the 2 mm and 4 mm siblings (D254). The rows' own
   numbers do not reproduce it: 22 dB / 2 mm = 11 dB/mm, 23.5 dB / 3 mm = 7.8 dB/mm.
4. `bw3db_ghz` on `amplifier` for XGM (judgment 4): keep. The registry lists `amplifier` in the parameter's classes, with the definition
   "3-dB electrical / modulation bandwidth". The XGM small-signal response (pump modulation to probe modulation, S21 in optical units)
   is the SOA's gain-modulation bandwidth, and the -3 dB criterion is visible: the -3 dB line in Fig. 4(b) and the -3 dB tick in
   Figs. 2-3 (the 40 GHz traces sit just above it where the text says "beyond 40 GHz"). The XGM definition, pump / probe settings and
   the 40 GHz instrument limit are in the conditions. Optional registry note: name XGM / cross-gain response in the def for amplifiers.
5. juodawlkis2009 (judgment 5): (a) keep `ref_plane module`. The values run pigtail to pigtail through the laser-welded package, and
   merged packaged-SOA rows use `module` (starnault2024 bulk / QW / QD SOAs, kazama2021). (b) Keep 90 % / 0.5 dB as `author_estimate`.
   The figure is the paper's own headline estimate (abstract "The estimated coupling efficiency ... is 90%", conclusion). It is
   computed from this paper's measured 0.8 W and a pre-packaging 0.9 W whose sentence ends with ref. [12], the authors' LEOS 2007
   conference paper. Same construction as D429 (ahmadi2022 net gain on a cited input) and D581 (kaspar2015, "previously presented"
   noted as the weak point). Strict rule 3 / D259 would delete m1-m2. The coupler row would survive on its measured mfd_chip_um
   rows, so either ruling is safe; keeping is recommended. The 0.9 W itself stays out. (c) Keep 6.5 um `mfd_fiber_um`
   `specification`. It is a parameter of the purchased lensed fiber (rule 4 example), the paper calls it "spot size" with no
   diameter / radius statement, and the note says so. Lensed-fiber datasheets usually quote a spot diameter, but that is not stated
   here, so no change.
6. park2007b (judgment 6): (a) keep 5 dB per facet `author_estimate`. D583 ruled the same sentence of the same device that way in the
   PTL version: the per-facet number comes from an insertion-loss measurement whose split into facets and a waveguide loss at "long
   wavelengths" is not given (theurer2019, D437: D167 not extended). This OFC text prints even less (no total). (b) Keep the rows; do
