## Points checked as asked

- hatori2014 class split (D396): correct. The 1.55 um trident design appears as fiber_chip_coupler.edge (lensed-fiber chain test, p.3 Fig. 4,
  polished facets), chip_chip_coupler.butt for the LD test on a polished facet (p.3 Fig. 5), a separate butt row for the flip-chip interface
  (dry-etched facet, about 1 um unfilled gap, p.4: different facet process, so a different physical interface), and the 1.3 um design (200 nm Si,
  120 nm tip, p.5) with its own butt row. The tip-width population rows (D165) are fine: Fig. 6 re-read (2 px per nm, 52.8 px per dB): trident
  points at about 131 / 151 / 169 nm and 2.73 / 2.68 / 2.73 dB; inverse taper at about 160 / 169 / 179 / 189 nm and 4.47 / 3.64 / 4.00 / 4.55 dB;
  Fig. 12 at 110 / 120 / 130 nm about 4.11 / 3.91 / 3.91 dB. Batch ranges match within the stated uncertainties. The tip_series_1550 row keeps
  `statistic: range` with a single value (all three points read the same); left as is.
- hatori2014 waveguide-referred powers `author_estimate` (0.26 W/A, > 2 mW): correct. Fig. 7 and Fig. 13(a) are labelled "light power in Si
  waveguide"; the paper never says how the on-chip power was referenced (no reference structure, no subtracted coupling term for these curves;
  D460 franken2025, D431). Threshold currents (11 mA, 5.1 / 14.9 mA) and T0 about 70 K stay measured (independent of referencing).
- hatori2014 fab AIST on the 7 Si-side rows: acknowledgment (p.7) thanks the TIA-SCR Management Office, AIST, for technical support and device
  fabrication; the "we fabricated" sentences (p.3, p.4, p.5) are by authors at PETRA, AIST and the University of Tokyo (all also PECST), so no
  single org (D154 / D395). D84b (thanks to an org for device fabrication: borderline fab kept with a note) covers exactly this; kept (Q1). The
  laser rows stay empty (LD maker of the QW array not stated; the QD LD of the butt test is "fabricated at QD Laser Inc.", p.5).
- katsumi2018 classes: chip_chip_coupler.evanescent is right: the nanobeam cavities are made on the QD wafer and the GaAs wire waveguides on a
  second wafer, then transfer printed (p.4, p.17-18 Supp. S1); mnaymneh2018 (transition) had the waveguide defined under the placed emitter.
  Resonator rows per cavity (D396) fine. Basis of eta_exp: see finding above and Q3. Fig. 5 cavity peaks re-read: L at about 906 nm, R at about
  898 nm, consistent with the printed QD detunings (QD-A 904 nm + 2.0 nm, QD-B about 899.8 nm - 1.8 nm; p.29-30). Simulated 924 nm (a = 230 nm,
