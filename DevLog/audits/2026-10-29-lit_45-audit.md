## Rulings on the distiller's questions (evidence-based recommendations)

1. kittlaus2020a no nonreciprocal row, ~15 dB contrast out, serpentine as mode_converter: accept. The 15 dB (p.9
   "about 15 dB", p.10 "15 dB"; Fig. 5(i) annotation ~16 dB at the lobe peak) is the ratio of the frequency-shifted
   modulation efficiency for forward vs backward probing, measured on the sideband; the carrier passes in both
   directions and no port-to-port transmission isolation is measured (the paper itself says unity-efficiency
   modulation is needed for an isolator, p.1 / p.10, and calls the device a four-port circulator only "under its
   current design" in principle, p.10). This is exactly the sohn2018 quantity ruled not isolation_db in lit_33. With
   this second confirmed value, recommend adding `conversion_contrast_db` (classes mode_converter, nonreciprocal;
   definition as in the lit_33 record: ratio of the frequency-converted output for the phase-matched vs the
   non-phase-matched probe direction at the same drive, positive dB; level / band in condition) and entering kittlaus
   15 dB (over the 0.8 nm FWHM band, p.9) on the serpentine mode_converter row (the row that holds its other metrics;
   a nonreciprocal row would need its own class-specific metric, D396).
2. Figure-based attributions: both endorsed, no D159.
   - koyaz2026 1.8 %/W: in the p.5 Fig. 5(b) render only the blue (w = 1.30 um, legend) trace reaches 1.8 %/W (top of
     the axis, about 1981.55 nm); the orange 1.26 um trace peaks at about 0.85 %/W. The "up to" maximum over both
     waveguides is therefore the 1.30 um value. Caveat recorded in notes: 1.8 is the peak of the unaveraged trace with
     ~0.01 nm Fabry-Perot fringes; the averaged envelope peaks at about 0.8 %/W.
   - xu2026 23.9 dB/cm: three independent figure checks tie Fig. 3(b) to the 1.5 mm waveguide (pump 41.6 mW = last
     Fig. 3(b) point; Fig. 3(a) pump-off dip about 9.7 dB / 0.15 cm = about 65 dB/cm vs alpha_wg,abs 63.6 dB/cm, and
     pump-on peak about 13.7 dB vs about 91 dB/cm x 0.15 cm; Fig. 3(c) about 3.6 dB at 1.5 mm = about 24 dB/cm, while
     2.4 mm and 6 mm give about 23 and 22 dB/cm). Evidence written into the m1 notes.
3. Jet Propulsion Laboratory: confirmed missing (no row or alias in organizations.csv / org_aliases.csv; only
   org_caltech and org_caltech_scherer_nanofab_group, no NASA row). The affiliation prints "Jet Propulsion
   Laboratory, California Institute of Technology", so the parent is printed. Recommendation: add an org row for JPL
   (parent org_caltech) in the next org round and set it as fab_org_ids on the 5 kittlaus2020a components (p.11 author
   contributions); if no new row is wanted, org_caltech is the printed parent (D154 analogue). Not patched here.
4. Registry gaps: confirmed at their locators (see next section). Two refinements: the koyaz BWSHG 1980.4 nm is
   printed for the 1.26 um waveguide only (p.6); the 1.30 um peak (about 1981.5 nm) is figure-only. The kittlaus
   1525.4 nm is stated as the centre operating wavelength set by the 6.14 deg IDT (p.9) and matches the forward lobe
   in Fig. 5(i), so it is a measured response centre, not only a design value.
