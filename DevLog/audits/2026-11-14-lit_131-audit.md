## Coordinator questions

1. ren2025 on-chip PDE 13.8 % as `author_estimate` (asked). The mean photon number before the DLCS rests on 8.3 dB = 12.3 dB measured minus 4 dB
   for the output facet, where 4 dB is half of an 8 dB two-coupler reference: equal facets on two different paths and a symmetric on-chip
   splitter are assumed (p.6). Rule 4 and D462 (li2025a efficiencies resting on an assumed coupling loss) apply. Recommendation: keep
   `author_estimate`; APP and jitter stay `measured` (they do not depend on mu).
2. ma2024 PWB 2-3 dB as `author_estimate` (asked). The 12-13 dB total is the paper's own comparison, but the PWB share subtracts a 10 dB PLC
   loss the producer supplied and the paper did not measure; the conclusion's verb "measured" does not change the basis (rule 4). Recommendation:
   keep; the total stays `derived` on the PWB row (`device_total`).
3. ma2024 PLC class `wdm.other` vs `splitter.other` (asked). The paper calls the part a mux / combiner of the WDM transmitter and lists MMI, AWG and
   cascaded Y-branch muxes only as options (p.1); the type is not stated. The Fig. 5(a) schematic draws a three-level binary tree, and the 10 dB
   loss is close to the 9.03 dB of an ideal 8-to-1 power combiner, but both are inference from a schematic and a number. Recommendation: keep
   `wdm.other` (stated function, type unknown in notes). Alternative: `splitter.other` if the coordinator reads the schematic as the device type
   (one op, given in the patch header).
4. ma2024 kappa 93 / 30 /cm and kL 0.75 (asked). `kappa_per_cm` exists for laser, and the values describe this laser's grating, but the sentence
   carries ref. [24] and the paper reports no own determination; kL is the cited kappa times the 250 um cavity. D80i: cited unless clearly the
   paper's own result. Recommendation: keep out (notes). Alternative: one `author_estimate` row (30 /cm after sampling) under the D80i borderline
   clause.
5. ma2024 21.9 GHz current (asked). Text: 40 to 100 mA gives 15.8 to 21.9 GHz (p.5); Fig. 13 caption and legend stop at 70 mA, and the 70 mA trace
   crosses -3 dB near 22.0 GHz (audit read). The figure supports 21.9 GHz at the highest plotted current. Recommendation: keep as entered with the
   conflict in the condition (D417); do not set a bias.
6. sun2026 figure-derived rows (asked). (a) S1-S4, S6, S7 geometry from the Fig. 4 axis labels (`extracted_from_figure`, label units not printed;
   S5 / S8 text values fix the unit and match their labels): keep. (b) Means / std from Fig. 4(a): reproduced within 0.15 %: keep. (c) 26.5 % on S5:
   stated in text (p.2), not only inferred from Fig. 4(b): keep. (d) "more than half above 15 %" as `median` `gt` 15, n 168: a logical restatement
   consistent with Fig. 4(d) (51 %): keep `measured`; alternative is notes only. (e) D153 stack rows added on the six designs without them (24 adds)
   and on the morenopozas2026 antenna (2 adds), following the D568 precedent: keep; to drop, remove those adds (listed in the patch header).
