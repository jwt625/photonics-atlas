## Coordinator questions

1. chen2024c fab `org_amf` (all 4 rows). The only fabrication evidence: passive "fabricated on a multilayered SiN-on-SOI platform" (p.3) and the
   author contribution "G.L. support the wafer fabrication process" (p.8) with G.L. at the printed "Advance Micro Foundry Pte. Ltd." (p.1, p.8,
   p.10). The supply review left the same statement out as no fabrication statement; that contract records vendor / product usage and has no
   wafer-fab role, so it is not a conflict on this field. Reading under the component rules: D80b counts author contributions as fabrication
   statements, and D84b places the fabrication at an org when that org's own people are credited with helping fabricate; here the only
   person tied to the wafer fabrication is the foundry author, and no other fabricator is named (D395 does not apply: there is no main
   fabricator whose assistant this would be). It is a process-support verb, not wafer supply (D428 would make a supplied wafer a note).
   Recommendation: keep `org_amf` as borderline fab with the existing note, `process_name` empty. Alternative: empty fab on the 4 rows (the
   chip then falls to `family_override` / no column in the benchmark); no affiliation-foundry rule covers AMF, so nothing else would place it.
2. xu2022a `org_sjtu_pinghu_institute_of_intelligent_optoelectronics` on the ECL row. "For device characterization, the chip is packaged by
   SJTU-Pinghu Institute of Intelligent Optoelectronics" (p.5) and Fig. 6 calls the result the assembled device of fiber array, RSOA and PIC.
   That is a named maker of the assembly, so D399 (assembled rows where stated) applies and D450 (unnamed assembler) does not.
   Recommendation: keep on the ECL row only (with `org_amf` for the PIC), not on the OPA / ring / antenna / bus rows, whose metrics do not depend
   on the package. The org exists (from ofc2026 batch_5); every author is also affiliated there, which does not change the reading.

   place a laser whose repetition rate and RF linewidth are set by the unattributed Si3N4 cavity into the SMART column. Recommendation: keep
