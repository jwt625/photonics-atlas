## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. lee2014a (judgment a): rule in. The distiller reads D642 as "free-space parts without fiber I/O are out", but D642 removed
   fontaine2019 as a bench-top assembly of purchased parts; it does not cover lithographic flat optics. Merged precedents keep
   chip-scale free-space polarization elements without fiber I/O: yang2024 (free-space Si-on-sapphire metasurface PBS at normal
   incidence, `polarization.pbs`, platform `other`, IL derived from %), lai2025 (GaN metasurface circular polarizer,
   `micro_optics.metalens`), zhang2025g (LC metasurface, D618 "in scope (chip-scale, D411 / D424; lai2025 / yang2024)"). The
   four GMR polarizers are holographically patterned, RIE-etched subwavelength gratings (5 x 5 mm devices on 1-inch substrates),
   which is the same kind of part. The patch adds them as `polarization.other`, platform `other`, fab empty ("we fabricate", p.11,
   authors at four orgs, D395): center wavelengths (~1565 / ~1584 / ~1711 / ~1499 nm), ER (printed 160:1 and 360:1 converted;
   16.9 / 19.3 dB derived from the printed ~% transmittances; band bounds > 15.0 / > 15.1 dB), TM-pass IL (derived from %),
   measured grating depth / residual layer / Si film, 5 x 5 mm footprint. Design (RCWA) values stay in notes (design geometry
   differs from the fabricated one; four extra design rows were judged not worth it). The sputtered-Si3N4 sentence swaps TE / TM
   (p.8); Fig. 9 render confirms TM passes. If the coordinator keeps lee2014a out, drop the lee2014a entries before applying
   (the patch header says which).
2. debevc2022 (judgment b): keep as entered. IL `< 2 dB measured` is the authors' own bound from their measurement (p.14, Table 1
   bracketed), with the per-port readings (> -1.5 / > -1.3 dB, up to +1.3 dB) and the "not accurately determined" caveat in the
   notes; entering the per-port readings instead would overstate precision the authors disclaim. 100 nm row from Table 1 only is
   right (its curves are in the uncached Supplement 1); plus the IL_sim miss above. Separate design rows with FDTD values are
   right (fabricated PBS-2 changed ff and polarizer N, p.11). TE / TM GC rows with the tilt as a condition are right (one design
   each, tilt is a measurement setting), now `per_coupler`. Fab `org_applied_nanotools` with process_name "NanoSOI Fabrication
   Service" as printed: right (davis2020 precedent).
3. ma2020 (judgment c): keep `polarization.compensator` (registry subtype "polarization compensator / controller"; merged
   velha2016, chang2023 controllers). PDL ~1.5 dB `derived` is right under D80a (the authors' difference of two fitted BER

   right (D83d / D398).
