## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. guo2024a EXC02 at 1550 nm (judgment a): keep the two range rows. The five printed pairs (p.8) repeat 1.27 (-19.48) and cannot be mapped
   to four conversions (D156 / D159 for the per-conversion values), but every pair belongs to EXC02, so a range over the printed values
   carries no sample-identity ambiguity. Fig. 7(e)-(h) at 1550 nm (desired mode 0 to -2 dB, worst unwanted mode about -19 to -22 dB) does
   not resolve the mapping either. Fab empty: confirmed (passive sentence, D80b / D433).
2. Basis of chen2021c vs guo2024a (judgment b): keep both. chen2021c defines the result by printed formulas (L_conv = L3 - L1 - Lmux,
   Lmux = (L2 - L1)/2, p.5): D80a, derived whether the authors or the batch did the arithmetic. guo2024a describes a reference-group
   normalization without a formula and prints the normalized values; the same authors' guo2023a rows were kept `measured` with
   `reference_structure` (lit_250, D716). Strict alternative under D80a: the seven guo2024a measured IL rows (exc01 m1 / m3 / m5 / m7 /
   m9, exc02 m1 / m3) -> `derived`; the CT rows are ratios inside one output measurement and stay `measured` either way.
3. zhang2015a derived bandwidths (judgment c): keep. The paper itself treats the delay window as the bandwidth (11 nm = 1533-1544 nm for
   the 500-550 grating; dispersion = delay / window for all three), and the Fig. 5 reflection plateaus match the windows. If accepted, the
   simulated 20 nm row (patch) follows the same convention.
4. zhang2015a spectral shapers (judgment d): keep as non-rows. "the fabricated spectral shaper ... around 30 dB" (p.4) follows the Fig. 6(d)
   paragraph but sits after a sentence about both measured responses (plural); with two shapers fabricated, D159 applies. The value is a
   fiber-to-fiber total including grating couplers and Y-branch splitting, so even a resolved identity would add little.
5. zhang2015a fab (judgment e): keep `org_imec` + `org_cmc_microsystems`. CMC "enabling the fabrication of the device" is the D398 / D83d
   MPW-access role (zhang2015 precedent, D539). ePIXfab is a broker named for fabrication ("accessed via ePIXfab"), a fab org under
   Contract v7 once registered; it is already on the open org-round list (DevLog-007, D458-D470 open items). Set it on all three rows in
   the org-round fix batch.
6. ma2017 (judgment f): keep the figure-read notch depths (`ge` 49 dB uniform, ~24 dB apodized, `extracted_from_figure`), the C band
   inferred from the stated 1554 nm design Bragg wavelength (rule 8, spectra plotted vs detuning only), the apodized C-SBGW as a non-row
   (its only value, 31.2 ps delay, has no parameter), and fab `org_applied_nanotools`.
7. Dry-run WARN exc02-m11 / m7 (judgment g): keep both. 0.69 dB is printed for TM0-to-TM2 and for TM2-to-TM0 (p.5); the rows differ in
   `mode` and Fig. 3 panel. The duplicate check does not compare `mode`.
