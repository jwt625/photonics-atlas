## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. xu2019b (judgment a). **Class `switch_fabric.other`: keep** (single 1x2 / 2x2 elements, D158 / D407; chen2022c
   precedent). **IL scope `per_component` + `reference_structure`: keep** (p.9 normalization to straight waveguides with
   the same grating couplers on the same chip removes couplers and access waveguides). **No phase-shifter row: keep** -
   the GST is switched once by annealing the whole chip (p.6), so there is no actuator component with its own metrics;
   the ~2 nJ is an estimate via ref. 15 and stays out. **WNF missing: confirmed** - neither "Washington Nanofabrication
   Facility" nor "Molecular Analysis Facility" is in `organizations.csv` / `org_aliases.csv` (only
   `org_university_washington` and the GWU facility); same open item as D418 / D604. Add both to the org round as facility
   orgs (parent `org_university_washington`), then a fix batch sets `facility_org_ids` on both xu2019b rows. Platform
   patched to `soi_strip` (paper says strip waveguide).
2. ros2019 (judgment b). **`phase_shifter.phase_change` on `sin` with n_levels / retention: keep** (D155 amplitude-only
   PCM memory; li2018 precedent; platform from the 330 nm Si3N4 rib). **Grating coupler ~20 % `author_estimate`: keep**
   (Methods setup aside with no method, D418 / D437; D167 covers loss values stated as results). Retention without
   qualifier: acceptable (printed 10^4 s is the demonstrated drift-free span, entered as stated; the merged rows split
   between no qualifier and `ge` for "up to <duration>" spans, e.g. ofc2026 th2a6 `ge`, chen2022 / zhang2024b none). Owner
   call only if a convention sweep on demonstrated-duration retention rows is wanted.
3. densmore2009 (judgment c). **5 dB/cm `author_estimate`: keep** - D608 limits D394 (`measured`) to an unquestioned bulk
   Fresnel reflectance; here the facet reflectivity ~20 % comes from a 3D FDTD simulation (p.6), the dagostino2015 /
   chen2021b / cheung2014 pattern. Method patched to `direct`. **Fab empty with SOITEC in notes: keep** (passive
   "devices were fabricated on unibond SOI wafers obtained from SOITEC", p.6; "we have developed" p.3 is design wording,
   D80b; SOITEC is a wafer supplier, Contract v7). Platform patched to `soi_strip`.
4. dong2010a (judgment d). **Fab `org_kotura`: keep, with the note.** It is stronger than the bare D84b case: p.4 is
   first-person ("We patterned the waveguides ... We then deposited ...") by six Kotura and three Sun Labs / Oracle
   authors, and the p.7 acknowledgment names three Kotura staff "for their work in fabricating of the device", which
   places the fabrication at Kotura (D84b, D463, D469; merged lit:dong2010 rows carry `org_kotura`). **21 mW per FSR
   `derived`: keep** - the paper shows the arithmetic ("Considering the FSR of 19 nm, we achieve ... 21 mW", 1.10 mW/nm x
   19 nm; D80a: explicit arithmetic is `derived` whether the authors or the batch did it). This differs from merged
   dong2010 m1 (2.4 mW per FSR read directly, `measured`) for that reason; the abstract / Table 1 restatements do not
   change the basis.

## Registry gaps confirmed

- Thermal crosstalk between neighbouring tuned elements: dong2010a measured < 0.5 % (0.03 vs 7.75 nm, rings 15 um
  edge-to-edge, p.6), simulated 1.5 % / 3.0 % with / without trenches for a probe waveguide 15 um away (p.4). Same
  candidate as lit_303 (zhang2019f 5 % at 38 um); the proposed `thermal_crosstalk_pct` definition is fine.
- `coupling_length_um` on `switch_fabric`: xu2019b 24 / 35 um (now `length_um`, `design_target`; chen2022c uses the
  same workaround). Recommend allowing it.
- Spiral-arm geometry on `phase_shifter` (densmore2009 2 um wire pitch, 12 um heater pitch, 65 um area radius, p.3-4, p.7,
  p.9): low priority, single source.
- PCM film thickness on `phase_shifter` / `switch_fabric` (xu2019b 20 nm GST + 11 nm ITO, p.5; ros2019 10 nm GST + 10 nm
  ITO, p.12): not reported by the distiller; values sit in `core_material`. Note only.
