# Audit lit_01 (2026-10-22)

Every metric row was checked against the paper text at its locator. Where a value comes from a figure, or the
extracted table is garbled, it was also checked against the page render: garciablanco2025 Fig. 4(a), kuznetsov2025
Fig. 2(e) and Ext. Data Fig. 5, zhang2023a Fig. 5(b), mckenna2022 Table I, cheng2025 Supp. Fig. S6, and

Component rows were checked for:
- class / subtype
- platform
- fab_org_ids / facility_org_ids (rule 12, D72 / D80b-d / D82c / D83c / D84)
- registry org ids
- missed values, including the D153 layout geometry

The patch also adds two batch-wide consistency passes:
- **D111 / D82d:** a design dimension entered after a unit rescaling is `derived`.
- **D43 precedent:** an equal split of a two-facet measurement is `loopback_half`.

## Open questions for the coordinator

- **Q1. Basis of internal / on-chip amplifier gain and NF.**
  - Values where the paper states the subtraction of its own measured coupling / absorption / propagation terms:
    - garciablanco2025 edwa m1, m5
    - vazquezcordova2014 spiral m1 (both)
    - zhang2023a amp m1, m2
    - kuznetsov2025 780nm-m2 and 790nm-m6 (internal = off-chip + measured IL)
  - These are entered as `measured`; read literally, D80a makes them `derived`. The fiber-to-fiber gains are measured either way.
  - Recommendation: one class-wide ruling. Either derived per D80a, or measured, treating the pump-probe internal-gain procedure as a standard measurement like a Lorentzian fit. Not patched.
- **Q2. ronn2019 orgs.** C2N (Universite Paris-Sud / CNRS) fabricated the waveguides (p.9 contributions), and Micronova Nanofabrication Centre is thanked for providing the facilities (p.9). Neither is in the registry.
  - Recommendation: add both orgs, then set fab C2N on the 7 ronn2019 rows (waveguide fabrication per the contributions) and facility Micronova on all 7 ("providing the facilities" is a facility-use acknowledgement, D42 / D80c).
  - Where the ALD Er:Al2O3 deposition was done is not stated; Micronova is the only facility named (p.9).
- **Q3. Merged-data D111 consistency.** About 50 merged geometry rows keep design_target after a unit rescaling (vs about 9 derived, count by the group-1 reader). This patch applies D111 / D82d to all 27 lit_01 rows.
  - Recommendation: a convention sweep over the merged batches. Alternatively, amend D111 so that a unit rescaling of a design dimension keeps design_target; then the 27 lit_01 ops should be dropped before merge.
- **Q4. mckenna2022 classes.** The SHG racetrack is resonator.ring and the OPO is laser.other, because frequency_converter allows no Q, FSR, threshold or absolute efficiency.
  - Recommendation: keep both classes now. Reclass both in one sweep if the registry extends frequency_converter (q_loaded, q_intrinsic, fsr_ghz, pump_threshold_mw, absolute conversion efficiency %); then also enter the ~3.7 um poling period (p.1).
- **Q5. song2025a routers.** The four-port and 5x5 routers are filed as nonreciprocal.circulator. Recommendation: add nonreciprocal.other and move them there.
- **Q6. starnault2026 platform.** The platform is empty on all 3 rows. The paper states x-cut TFLN (p.10) but not the etch / rib geometry, and there are no EO rows to inherit from (D82e). Recommendation: keep it empty. Alternatively, rule that HyperLight-foundry TFLN rows default to lnoi_rib with a note.
- **Q7. starnault2026 modulator metrics.** Vpi 2.25 / 2.5 V and EO bandwidth 102 (text) vs 104 GHz (Fig. 1 caption) have no home in this atlas. Recommendation: add the paper to the EO-atlas candidate list.
- **Q8. shi2024 lensed-fiber 4.9 dB/facet (m1).** It is entered as derived, but the paper shows no arithmetic: it says it calibrates "using a pair of lensed fibers" and "estimate[s]" the loss (p.8). Recommendation: basis measured, keep per_facet, quote the word in notes. Not patched.
- **Q9. mckenna2022 SHG-device coupling 26 % / 11 % (m5 / m6).** The split method is not stated (p.8). Recommendation: keep per_facet with a note; do not assume loopback_half.
- **Q10. Optional misses, not patched:**
  - garciablanco2025 EDWA cladding ~7 um (~1 um TEOS + ~6 um PECVD, p.5).
  - garciablanco2025 best ~1.3 dB/facet read off Fig. 4(a), which would be extracted_from_figure.
  - li2025 narrow-section width 1 um (p.4) and min bend radius 400 um (p.3). The amplifier class has no layout template and min_bend_radius_um is not allowed for amplifiers.
  - vazquezcordova2014 Table 1 simulation inputs (p.9): not entered.
  - song2025a 3.5 dB "junction loss": origin and per-junction vs total not stated.
  - cheng2025 two-ridge cross-section: width_nm cannot hold two ridge widths.
  - cheng2025 dual-waveguide -12.29 dB: scattering efficiency in the text, IL in Supp. Table III.
  - Recommendation for all of these: do not enter.
