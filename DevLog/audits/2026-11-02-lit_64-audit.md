## Questions raised by the distiller (evidence-based recommendations)

1. lihachev2022 intrinsic linewidth about 25 Hz (p.1, p.2) and SMSR 60 dB (p.3, Fig. 2(d) > 60 dB): no resonator is named. Fig. 2(d) is
   captioned the SIL DFB emission only. pi x 8 Hz2/Hz = 25.1 Hz points to the 2.45 GHz device (floor p.3), but that relation and
   assignment are not printed. Recommendation: stay out, in notes on all SIL rows (D159). If the headline is wanted, it needs a ruling
   that this arithmetic identifies the device: sil_2p45ghz linewidth_hz 25, author_estimate.
2. wang2026f thresholds from Jth x ridge area: recommendation keep. Geometry and Jth are both printed for the same device, the 3 mm
   check (589 A/cm2 <-> printed 265 mA) closes, and Fig. 5(b) shows the 50 C kink near the derived 882 mA. No merged precedent either
   way (no derived threshold_ma row from a density). If rejected, delete 15x1000, 15x1500, 15x2000 (their only performance metric, D49)
   and 15x3000-m2; the Jth values are already in notes.
3. zhang2025c fab: recommendation stay empty. p.2 says the PIC is on the SMART Photonics generic platform and p.2 names an InP foundry
   only for compatibility; no fabrication sentence (D80b, D84a). Merged SMART rows print one (andreou2019 p.3, chen2025 p.2, zali2026);
   guzman2019 in this batch does (p.2). process_name kept.
4. kharas2020:
   a. Coupling loss 1.6 / 2 dB `derived`: recommendation keep. Ratio of own responsivities with two stated assumptions, both about the
      own devices, none cited or assumed from outside (p.9): D80a "model-parameter extraction with stated assumptions", not rule 4
      author_estimate. The paper's word "estimated" is in the source; +/- 1.0 dB is in notes.
   b. 0.23 nm in `stopband_width_nm` with "level not stated": recommendation keep (6 merged rows of this form, dolgaleva2011,
      giuntoni2009; D82g reserves bw_3db_nm for a stated 3-dB band).
5. wang2026f temperature_k: recommendation stay empty (no RT number printed; skill rule 1), with the figure evidence now in notes. If the
   coordinator accepts the 20 C points of Fig. 5(c) and Fig. 6(b) as identifying RT, 293.15 K goes on the RT rows of 15x3000 and the
   three siblings.
6. lihachev2022 actuators `phase_shifter.other`: recommendation keep (dong2021 and hosseini2015 piezo rows are other; the two merged
   phase_shifter.mems rows are gap-moving NOEM devices), although the paper calls the actuator MEMS-based. Chip mechanical modes on
   the actuator rows, with the note that they are chip modes: endorsed. FSRs author_estimate: endorsed (D76).
7. guzman2019 line spacing in `channel_spacing_nm` next to repetition_rate_ghz: endorsed (D83b; the paper says repetition rate and the
   nm spacing is printed separately; pulses are not characterized, so 100 GHz rests on the electrical tone, Fig. 7(b)).
