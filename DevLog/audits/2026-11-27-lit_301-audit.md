## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. herr2012 (judgment a). **Keep all four rows and the fab / facility.** The paper is a research article, not a review: Fig. 1
   is new data and nothing in Fig. 1 or its caption is attributed to an earlier paper (D408 tests attribution). The p.2
   "[24]" is Levy et al. (Nature Photonics 2010, Cornell), attached to the platform name "Si3N4 micro-ring resonators" in
   parallel with "MgF2 resonators [19-21]"; the caption restates "200 MHz Linewidth" for this resonator without a reference,
   and the SiN samples were made at CMI-EPFL (p.9), so the width is the paper's own (note supplement added). 200 GHz ring:
   **keep FSR `author_estimate` and radius `typical`** (label "typ. radius 100um" sits on the image of that ring; D254 is not
   triggered because the label is tied to one device). MgF2 out (D411). Fab `org_epfl` + CMi facility: D448 / D83c.
2. ye2023a (judgment b). **Keep budget terms `author_estimate`, MMI 0.06 dB per_component, 12.66 dB `derived`.** New fact: the
   four terms sum exactly to the total (2.5 + 0.36 + 0.2 + 9.6 = 12.66 dB), so at least one term (likely the 9.6 dB antenna
   term) is a remainder of the measured total, which supports `author_estimate` (D702 "origin not stated"). The 12.66 dB is
   the authors' explicit arithmetic on two stated measurements: `derived` under D80a; the 0.02 dB recomputation gap stays in
   notes.
3. xiang2022a (judgment c). **Keep kappa ~10 /cm `author_estimate`.** Eq. (1) is a cited formula ([20]) and its n_eff input is
   not stated (the paper only plots calculated n_eff vs width, Fig. 3), so the value rests on an unstated input (rule 4);
   D80a `derived` needs stated assumptions. he2024 ~10 /cm author_estimate precedent. Arithmetic check: n_eff about 3.2 with
   0.73 nm / 1546 nm gives 9.8 /cm (audit only).
4. ashtiani2019 (judgment d). **Keep the through / coupled reading (D156 not triggered).** The tie rests on more than
   plausibility: the second number rises 5 -> 43 % along the row as an equal-power tap must (last coupler near an even split),
   the pair sums are 86-89 % (simulated excess), and the reverse reading would send 81 % of the row power into the first
   element, contradicting the stated equal-power design (p.2, p.4 Sec. 3); the p.6 order "transmission and the coupling
   coefficients" matches. Supplement added to the dc notes. **43 dB: keep `de_embedded` + `author_estimate`** (coupled
   power "set", origin not stated). **Fab `org_astar_ime` and process_name as printed: keep** (named-process sentence,
   Contract v7, aflatouni2015 precedent).
5. ito2019 (judgment e). **Agree.** The prism lens is a 24.0 x 18.7 mm acrylic part (bulk, off-chip): 40 x 4.4 deg, 0.15 deg,
   4256 points, ~0.1 deg delta-phi, 0.30 deg step and the 9 dB fiber-to-beam total stay out (D411 / D558, zhang2021a).
   **MZ < 0.2 dB `simulated`: keep** ("theoretical", D82a calculated). The 0.3 dB LSPCW taper is cited to [18] (rule 3).
6. xiang2022a "250 kHz at 100 mW" (no change). The abstract / p.2 power and the Fig. 11(b) lowest point (about 87 mW) differ;
   the m19 note already records the figure position. If the coordinator wants it explicit, a D417-style note "printed 100 mW,
   Fig. 11(b) lowest point about 87 mW" is enough; the value stays.
