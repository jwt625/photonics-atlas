## Questions raised by the distiller (evidence-based recommendations)

1. ahmadi2022 1.1 dB/cm, 4.5-4.6 dB per facet, net gain.
   Evidence: p.4 gives 1.1 dB/cm as a fixed fit input, determined from a ring cavity Q on the same sample; p.7 App. A gives the same value
   from the Q-factor reported previously in [14], the authors' own earlier laser paper (p.2), and computes 3.6 dB and then 4.6 dB per facet
   from it; p.7 calls the result calculated. The distiller cites D159, but no second component is involved: the better grounds are rule 3 and
   D259 / D408 (the value sentence attributes the value to the earlier paper).
   Recommendation: keep 1.1 dB/cm and 4.5-4.6 dB out (endorsed); the measured 12.7 dB total goes in (patched). Keep the net-gain rows
   (amp_3p25cm-m4, m5; amp_1p2cm-m3, m4, m5) as author_estimate: they are the paper's own headline results, combine its measured SRS
   gain with the loss model (p.3), and skill rule 4 covers results resting on a cited input; the component notes record the dependence
   (Fig. 3(b)-(c) low-power asymptotes about -3.3 and -1.2 dB match 1.1 dB/cm x length). If rule 3 is read strictly ("computed from them"),
   those five rows go as well and both components keep only SRS gain and geometry.
2. kazama2021 fab. The distiller left fab empty on "developed". p.3 Sec. 2 has a first-person statement that the authors formed the taper at
   both waveguide ends; p.2 Sec. 1 refers to their own development and optimization of the fabrication process; all authors are in NTT
   Corporation laboratories (p.1). Recommendation: org_ntt on all four rows (patched; D80b, D84a, D154; umeki2010, goh2001 precedents). If
   the module assembly (passive, p.5) should stay out under D399's logic, empty fab on `ppln_opa_module` only.
3. kazama2021 PPLN-1 and PPLN-4 values (7.5 / 6.9 dB at 0.5 W; 1.3 W SH from 2.6 W). The text never ties these units to the characterized
   waveguide; it runs the characterized design as PPLN-3 (p.5-7). D159 does not strictly apply (they are named units, not an ambiguous
   sample), but the merged shimizu2023 note treats the separate SH-pump module as setup, not a row (rule 13). Recommendation: notes only,
   no 50 % row (endorsed, no patch). The module-equals-PPLN-3 reading is endorsed: the p.7 calculated curves use the 1200 %/W, 0.6, 0.7
   and 1.0 dB of the characterized waveguide.
4. zeng2026 6.6 mm device. The distiller put the 2.1 dB in a note and missed the 6.2 dB/cm. Rule 9 makes a different length a sibling row.
   Recommendation: accept the new row (patched); if declined, delete `cmp_lit_zeng2026_yb_alox_amp_6p6mm` and the 6.2 dB/cm goes in the
   55 mm row's notes.
5. Other zeng2026 calls: all endorsed. On-chip rows derived; 71 % measured with the reference plane not printed (Fig. 2(e) axis, render);
   26.5 dB ref_plane unspecified (Fig. 2(b) caption does not say on-chip, unlike Fig. 2(c)-(d)); 8.7 mW author_estimate (correction of the
   lensed-fiber path not printed, p.19); 1.0 dB symmetric with the p.20 asymmetry in notes; 0.7 nm author_estimate. laser.dfb follows the
   paper's name; the geometry is two Bragg gratings around a 60 um cavity with gain throughout (p.4, p.19, tag dbr_mirrors).
6. yan2020: IL `measured` + reference_structure + de_embedded endorsed (the paper states IL as a result, no printed arithmetic; D167
   analogue). Both crosstalk ranges kept with their locators (the text range and Table 1 differ in the paper itself, p.8 / p.9). Rotator pair
   0.7 dB author_estimate, on_chip_excess, not halved: endorsed (the authors' attribution is partial, TE and TM devices are separate, p.8).
