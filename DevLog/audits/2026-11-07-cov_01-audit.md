   benchmark only.

   performance value of unstated design / measured origin is author_estimate). The Si values are PDK library values

   origin is not stated (p.2 Sec. III-A). The SiN values are stated as the achieved result of the PECVD process
   ('yielded', p.2 Sec. III-B), and the EC is a demonstrated device (D167: a per-facet loss stated directly is
   measured). Benchmark effect: TSMC SiPh wg_loss stays empty (author_estimate is filtered, D901); the SiN WG (platform
   sin) forms a TSMC SiN cell that stays hidden below min_rows_per_column; the SiN EC 1.2 dB (scope unspecified) and the
   SPGC / PSGC losses (scope unspecified) stay out of edge_il / gc_il by the row scope filter.
