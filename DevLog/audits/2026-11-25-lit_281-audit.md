## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. levy2011 (judgment a). (i) -35 dB as 0.0316 % `author_estimate`: keep (unstated coupling corrections, D256a). (ii) 0.101 %/W:
   delete (patched). The paper never reports a normalized efficiency; the distiller picked the top, thermally limited Fig. 5 point,
   and the same arithmetic on other Fig. 5 points gives up to about 0.18 %/W. Rule 4 permits arithmetic on printed values, but a
   point-dependent quantity the paper does not state is the distiller's construction (rule 1); readings kept in component notes.
   (iii) Fab: `org_cornell_university` under D84a (patched): refs. 5 and 14 are the authors' own fabrication papers, so "the same
   fabrication process previously described [5, 14]" is "our previous process"; all authors are at Cornell and the work was
   performed in part at CNF. Alternative (strict D80b, as almeida2003 / ji2017 with CNF only): fab empty. Facility CNF either way.
2. porcel2017 (judgment b). (i) 0.4 % `author_estimate`: keep. Rule 4 lists a cited input as `author_estimate`; rule 3 removes values
   taken from earlier work or computed by the batch from them (D611 arianfard2020: batch-computed from cited dn/dT, deleted). Here
   the authors printed 0.4 % from their own measured powers with a cited loss inside the coupling estimate, the D746 wang2025d
   pattern (coupling losses with a subtracted cited 0.12 dB/cm, kept as `author_estimate`). (ii) Fig. 7 siblings: keep
   `extracted_from_figure` (vector reads confirmed) as SH / 10 mW with empty ref_plane (D673 ayan2023: figure-read conversion rows,
   empty ref plane). (iii) Population row (P0 1.22 mW, 6.02 / 2.44 dB): keep. (iv) Fab `org_lionix_international`: keep, and it is
   not a D84b borderline case: p.6 names the process with its org ("proprietary TripleX process from LioniX International",
   Contract v7) and p.17 credits LioniX staff with the "fabrication of the samples" (a stated place, D448); notes restated by the
   patch. (v) process_name "TripleX": keep as printed (Contract v7); the merged rows also carry printed variants ("TriPleX",
   "TriPleX technology", "LioniX TriPleX ..."), so any spelling merge belongs in the benchmark view, not the data.
3. wang2017a (judgment c). (i) 41 / 6.8 %/W/cm2 `author_estimate` (D608): keep. (ii) 0.412 / 0.017 %/W: keep as `author_estimate`
   (D256a). Unlike levy2011 they restate a printed result: they equal the printed %/W/cm2 times the printed length squared and the
   Fig. 4(c) / 4(d) experimental peaks, plotted by the paper in W^-1 (the stored-unit form, D256). Recommendation on coordinator /
   distiller conversions in general: keep a computed row only when it restates a printed result in the stored unit (unit, dB <-> %,
   per-length <-> total with a printed length); a new quantity the paper does not report (levy2011) goes to notes. (iii) Widths
   `design_target` (patched). (iv) Sidewall: enter 50 deg derived (patched; Fig. 1(a) draws theta from the vertical, merged practice
   converts). (v) 3.0 dB/cm and 4.8 dB per facet `author_estimate`, fab Harvard + CNS: keep.
4. yu2005 (judgment d). (i) 23 %/W -> `author_estimate` (patched): the 72 % factor has no stated origin and no index or reflectance is
   printed, so D394 (bulk Fresnel as a stated model input) does not reach it; the 45 % input term alone would have allowed
   `derived` (own reference structure, D166). Alternative if the coordinator reads "because of end-facet reflection" as a stated
   bulk Fresnel term: keep `derived`. (ii) 3.4 %/W `measured`: keep, with the Fig. 3(a) conflict note (the plotted data imply about
   20 %/W; plot reference planes not printed). (iii) 125 / 27 %/W `simulated`, four growth-temperature loss rows, fab Stanford:
   keep. (iv) Pump 1559.1 nm (p.6 body and Fig. 3(b)) over the abstract's 1.568 um: keep with the D417 note.
5. Free-space lens coupling as `insertion_loss_db` per_facet on converter rows (yu2005 m5, porcel2017 population m2 / m3; BATCH_REPORT
   judgment 7): keep; no fiber or separate coupler part is involved, and per_facet is the scope the merged lensed-fiber rows use
