## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. doerr2010 4.2 dB / 67 nm `loopback_half` `derived` and borderline fab `org_astar_ime` (distiller judgment (a)).
   Recommendation: keep both. The paper states the square-root step itself (Fig. 4 caption, p.2-3), which D516 / D542 make
   `derived` (gehring2019, marchetti2017, zhou2022). The acknowledgment thanks two named IME staff "for discussions and
   fabrication" with the org and country printed; no other fabrication statement exists and the authors are at Bell Labs, so
   D84b borderline fab with a note applies, as for mao2008 (D616, IME "assistance in sample preparation"). Alias request:
   "Institute of Microelectronics, Singapore" -> org_astar_ime (the existing bare alias "Institute of Microelectronics" collides
   in wording with the CAS institute; the country disambiguates). Alternative: fab empty if the coordinator reads "discussions
   and fabrication" as staff thanks without a stated place.
2. larrea2018 ~2 dB per facet `author_estimate` and borderline fab `org_upv` (judgment (b)). Recommendation: keep both. Eq. (4)
   subtracts 2.5 dB of propagation loss taken from a cutback on a different sample (+/- 1 dB/cm, i.e. +/- 0.25 dB per facet) and
   assumes equal facets; Pout is not printed, so the value rests on a transferred input (rule 4; bao2024 D605 equal-facet and
   bonneville2020 D580 estimated-loss precedents). `derived` would be the alternative if the coordinator treats the sister-sample cutback as the
   paper's own measurement of this chip's waveguide (same process stated). The Fig. 7 row keeps `extracted_from_figure` (reading
   mode) with the same caveat in notes. Fab: passive "proposed, designed, fabricated and demonstrated" plus thanks to "the
   fabrication team of the Nanophotonics Technology Center", all authors at NTC-UPV: D84b borderline, kept on org_upv until an NTC
   unit org exists (D533 garciarodriguez2017); then re-point.
3. zhong2014 6.4 / 4.7 dB `measured` `loopback_half`, fab `org_university_washington`, custom-stack design row (judgment (c)).
   Recommendation: keep. The paper plots per-coupler "Coupling Efficiency" from the pair without printing a total or the halving,
   which is D452 (lorenzen2026, D167) `measured`; Fig. 5 confirms the -6.4 dB is a single-coupler figure. The acknowledgment names
   the place ("fabricated at the University of Washington - Washington Nanofabrication Facility"): fab UW and facility WNF by
   D448; WNF is still not in the registry (queued since D420), so the facility waits for the org round. The custom-stack row is a
   separate simulated design (different BOX / cladding), `row_kind: design`, as the doerr2010 Bragg-mirror row (rule 9). Variant
   caveat added to the fabricated row's notes (patched).
4. gui2021 thermal phase shifter < 0.3 dB / ~20 kHz `author_estimate` (judgment (d)). Recommendation: keep the basis (no
   measurement shown; velha2016 precedent for an unstated bandwidth criterion), but the band is not unspecified: Fig. 4 labels
   both external lasers in the C band, so rule 8 gives `c_band` + `wavelength_inferred` (patched on m1; m2 is a thermal bandwidth
   with no wavelength condition). Platform and fab empty stand.
5. bundalo2022 loss budget, MOB, connector and modulator (judgment (e)). Recommendation: keep as entered.
   (i) Table II terms `author_estimate` + `loss_breakdown`: the paper only says "the corresponding transmitter losses are shown in
   Table II", no origin per term (D428 / D437). (ii) MOB 8.9-9.5 dB `measured` vs Table II 2.0 dB: they cannot both describe the
   laser-to-GC loss (2.0 + 2.6 = 4.6 dB); the paper does not reconcile them, so both stay with the D417 note and `scope:
   unspecified` on the 2.0 dB row; deriving a module output from either would be speculation. (iii) Placement / gluing ~0.5 dB
   `author_estimate`: printed next to the measured 8.9-9.5 dB but with no stated procedure; `measured` per D167 is defensible,
   the conservative basis is fine. (iv) Connector ~1.5 dB per interface `measured` ("showed to be about 1.5 dB", relative to
   direct fiber-GC coupling, IMEC + KIT lenses; the tested module used IMEC lenses on both sides, noted). (v) Modulator 7.30 dB at
   quadrature, 1289 nm optimum and the eyes stay out (rule 2): EO-atlas candidate (bundalo2022 has no EO-atlas row). (vi) Fab:
   Europractice appears only in the title of ref. 15, not in the fabrication sentence: not a fab org here (Contract v7 names
   brokers "named for fabrication"); process_name keeps "iSiPP50G" as cited.
6. larrea2018 `tol_1db_longitudinal_um` > 20 um add (audit miss 4). Recommendation: accept. The printed 1.5 dB bound minus the
   printed 0.5 dB optimum is a 1-dB window that the simulated curve has not reached at 20 um (Fig. 4(b) about 1.4 dB there);
   `simulated` by D83f, qualifier `gt`. Alternative: drop the add if the coordinator wants 1-dB tolerances only when the paper
   names the 1-dB criterion.
