## Coordinator questions

1. deng2025 measured IL (distiller judgment 1-2). The abstract prints < -0.96 dB for both polarizations; the conclusion and Table 2 print
   -0.80 / -0.97 dB; the Fig. 7 traces bottom at about -0.80 (TE) and -0.96 dB (TM). Fig. 5 shows a single crossing and a 10-crossing cascade,
   and the text never says which structure gave the spectra or that a division was made. Recommendation: keep the conclusion / Table 2
   bounds with the abstract value in notes (D417), scope `per_component`, method `reference_structure`, basis `measured` (D167 / D452: no
   arithmetic shown).
2. deng2025 `mmi_width_um` 1.50-1.93 um range (judgment 3). The arm is a 13-segment width profile, not a rectangle; Table 1 labels the
   widths "Length" but p.2 calls them the optimized widths. One merged precedent stores a range in `mmi_width_um` (zhou2025a mzi_coupler-m9).
   Recommendation: keep the range row (value + value_max, statistic range). Alternative: delete m17 and keep the widths in notes, leaving the
   `mmi` template without its required width.
3. gao2025 simulated TM tolerance bound < 0.17 dB vs Fig. 3(c). Confirmed: the 85 deg curve reaches about 0.18 dB at 1580 nm (500 dpi);
   the TE bound 0.19 dB is consistent with its curves (highest about 0.175 dB). Recommendation: keep 0.17 dB as printed with the D417 note.
   Fab `org_sitri` accepted (fabrication sentence names SITRI); the printed expansion is an alias candidate for the org round.
4. xu2025 maximum IL 1.26 / 1.42 dB of a single crossing from the crosstalk structure (two MUXs and two DEMUXs) vs cutback 0.11-0.53 dB.
   The paper assigns both numbers to a single crossing; the Fig. 7 bar traces sit around -1 to -2 dB, so the CT-structure normalization
   likely leaves MUX / DEMUX residue, but the paper does not say so. Recommendation: keep m7 / m8 as printed (`le`, method `unspecified`,
   conflict note; tossoun2024 precedent in D417: identity clear, numbers disagree); m8 keeps 1550 nm as `wavelength_inferred`. Alternative:
   delete m7 / m8 and move the values to the component notes as path values.
5. xu2025 fab (judgment 3). The fabrication sentence is passive; the acknowledgment thanks named engineers of the Center of Optoelectronic
   Micro & Nano Fabrication and Characterizing Facility, WNLO, HUST for support with device fabrication. Recommendation: keep
   `org_huazhong_university_of_science_and_technology` as D84b borderline fab, facility empty (staff thanks never set a facility, Contract
   v7). Note the merged cai2024 rows carry the same facility org because that paper thanks the facility itself, not its staff.
6. zhao2022 MUX + DEMUX pair row (judgment 5). The (de)multiplexer design comes from the authors' ref. [9], but the about 1.0 dB / < -20 dB
   values are measured on this paper's chip as its reference PIC (p.7, Fig. 9(d)-(f)), so D259 / D408 do not apply. Recommendation: keep
   (`mode_converter.mdm_mux`, `excess_loss_db` device_total). The other zhao2022 conflicts (TE1 0.16 vs 0.15 dB/MWC, simulated TE1 < 0.10 vs
   the -0.13 dB label, 10-mode 0.37 vs 0.36 dB) stay as entered with their notes.
7. jin2024 (judgments 1-4). (a) Per-GC CEs from input + output GC structures printed without arithmetic: keep `loopback_half`, `measured`
   (D452, D567). (b) Initial apodized GC (MFS 30 nm, -2.15 dB, p.3): keep out. It is the starting design, not fabricated, and its MFS and CE
   equal the Table 1 entry of ref. [20]; Fig. 3 does plot an Initial GC Sim curve, which is the only argument for a design row. (c) UGC
   simulated -2.6 dB: keep. Fig. 3 plots the paper's own UGC Sim curve peaking near -2.6 dB; ref. [23] in Table 1 is an apodized 180 nm MFS
