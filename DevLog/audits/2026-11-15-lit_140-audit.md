## Coordinator questions

1. tran2019 fab (distiller Q1). The paper names the process with its org: Gen. 1 lasers "were built upon the standard process-design-kit for the
   heterogeneous Si/InP developed at UCSB" (p.4) and the designed laser "is then fabricated in a standard Si/InP heterogeneous process [35],
   [36]" (p.5); Gen. 2 is "fabricated in a modified version of the Si/III-V heterogeneous process" with process changes the paper describes
   (p.10). Contract v7 counts an org named with the process ("on imec's 300-mm platform", D455) and D84a counts a device made with the authors'
   own process; five of six authors are at UCSB. This differs from szelag2017 / D433 (platform named with passive verbs, no org) and
   morichetti2021 (PDK only cited). Recommendation: `org_ucsb` on all 12 rows as patched (benchmark column UCSB heterogeneous Si/InP).
   Alternatives: Gen. 1 only (drop the five gen2 ops; Gen. 2 says "the ... process" without repeating UCSB), or keep empty (drop all 12 ops).
   No facility is named; the staff thanks (p.12) do not set one (D84b).
2. lihachev2024 laser row fab (distiller Q2). Packaging by A.B. and A.V., both with EPFL and Deeplight SA affiliations; the site of the
   packaging is not stated, and the packaged laser also contains a purchased Thorlabs RSOA. Recommendation: keep fab empty (D83c, D399
   assembled rows only where stated; hiratani2024 / D433 precedent). The two facilities on the laser row are the chip's fabrication sites
   (p.7): keep, or move them to the chip rows only if the coordinator treats assembled rows like fab (D399 by analogy).
3. poulton2019 electro-optic phase shifter as `phase_shifter.other` (distiller Q3, rule 2). The class label reads "Phase shifter (non-EO)",
   but rule 2 keeps only modulator metrics (Vpi, EO bandwidth, modulator IL / ER, VpiL) in the EO atlas, and this paper prints none: the rows
   are OPA-element values (2.4 dB average IL over 2 pi, 2 pi reach, 2 uW static power). poulton2019 is not an EO-atlas source, so there is no
   host row to duplicate. Precedent: gagino2024 InP EO phase modulator in an OPA (D451), lu2016a / gao2025a p-i-n phase shifters.
   Recommendation: keep the row and class; list it as an EO-atlas candidate only if the EO atlas widens to OPA phase shifters.
4. oldenbeuving2012 design PHR row (audit). Deleted as patched because the 0.63 / 2.0 dB design belongs to a kappa^2 = 0.44 design that the
   characterized mirror is not (designed 0.3-0.4, fit 0.39). Alternative: keep it as a design requirement with `qualifier gt` and a condition
   naming the kappa^2 = 0.44 design (remove the delete_metrics entry and the mrr_mirror op).
