## Rulings on the batch report's judgment calls

- Heater + ring split (dong2010, wang2016) into phase_shifter.thermo_optic and resonator.ring / wdm.mrr rows (oz2023
  precedent): endorsed; D396 (one physical device, functions of different classes, one row per class with a
  cross-note) covers it. wang2016 large-ring heater geometry sits on the wdm.mrr row (heater_length_um is allowed for
  wdm); no large-ring heater row because its only performance value is the 33 uW/GHz gap value.
- dong2010 org_kotura: endorsed, and stronger than borderline. p.7 thanks three staff "from Kotura Inc. for their work
  in fabricating of the device": a statement naming Kotura staff as having fabricated the device places the
  fabrication at Kotura (D84b; contract v4 "a statement naming the org as fabricator"), 6 of 9 authors Kotura, and
  p.3 cites the authors' own earlier process (ref. [14]). Not a facility_org_ids basis (D72).
- dong2010 tuning efficiency 4.8 / 2.7 nm/mW as pm/mW `derived`: endorsed (D256 prefix conversion). Q ~8000 entered
  on both radii: endorsed (stated once for "our devices", lit_05 nevlacsil2020 precedent; not a D254 typical value
  of a family).
- maeder2022: org_eth_zurich ("we fabricated", p.2, all authors ETH) and facilities ScopeM, BRNC, FIRST ("support for
  nanofabrication from", p.4) endorsed (rule 12, D80b). bw3db_khz 40 kHz from 0.35 / tau_r as `derived` (authors'
  explicit formula, D80a) and > 50 kHz on all six Fig. 5(a) devices (legend verified on the page render) endorsed.
- wang2016 Fig. 5(b) caption 24 / 47 us rise / fall for the larger rings vs p.10 "We did not measure the temporal
  characteristics of the larger microrings": not entering is endorsed. The paper contradicts itself about whether the
  measurement exists, so the values cannot be tied to a measured device (D156 / D159 spirit); kept as a component
  note. The R 1.7-5.9 kohm / dC 0.12 nF RC inputs stay out (ring size of the measured banks not stated, D159).
- wang2016 fab org_sandia_national_laboratories: endorsed ("fabricated as part of a multi-project run ... at the MESA
  facility at the Sandia National Laboratory", p.4: rule 12 MPW service / named fabricator). The MESA facility is
  missing from the registry (only MESA+ Twente exists); see questions.
- wang2016 FSR 850 GHz as author_estimate: endorsed (D82a; p.5 states the FSR and bandwidth specifications were
  inherited from the testbed specification, measured vs design not distinguished).
- zhu2019: fab empty endorsed (passive "The devices were fabricated", p.2; all authors A*STAR IME; D80b). "Modulation
