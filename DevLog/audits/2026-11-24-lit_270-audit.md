## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. samusenko2016 Fig. 11 coupling-length series (judgment a, last item): the batch left it out (sample / cladding unstated, "points
   overlap at 0 um"). The markers do not overlap: eight coupling lengths each carry one Q square and one ER circle, all resolved on
   the raster. The series is a distinct, identifiable set of test rings; its wafer and cladding are unknown, which is a stack gap
   (cladding empty, notes), not an ambiguity between two entered components (D159 does not bite). Recommendation: accept the added
   row (18 metrics). Alternative if the coordinator reads D159 as covering unknown stacks: delete `cmp_lit_samusenko2016_ring_lc_series`
   (one delete_components op) and keep the Q about 2.7e5 at 0 um as a note on the BS rows.
2. samusenko2016 q_intrinsic basis (judgment a): keep `derived` on ring_bs3_teos (Fig. 11 red labels = this ring, on the
   over-coupled side of the series; D403 satisfied). ring_bs2_teos: keep `derived`, but the evidence is indirect (the red labels are
   BS3; the text calls "the resonators in the circuit" over-coupled from the 35 um zone vs 10-20 um critical; BS2 couplers are
   over-coupled at every tested length, Fig. 7). The strict D403 alternative is `author_estimate` for BS2 only. Both printed Q_i
   values are reproduced by the over-coupled relation (4.6e5 / 3.3e5 vs 4.5e5 / 3.8e5), which supports the regime. Notes patched.
3. samusenko2016 FSR 0.67-0.70 nm on all four rings (judgment a): keep. The range is printed for "the" measured rings jointly, so
   each ring's FSR lies inside it (same logic as lit_265 wang2017c 28-39 nm); `statistic: range` with the joint wording in notes is
   honest. Alternative: keep only the FEM 0.7 nm in notes; not recommended (measured value lost).
4. samusenko2016 TEOS-ring extinction (judgment a): keep both. BS2 1.8 dB from the Fig. 9 dip (about 0.66, 10log10); BS3 2.8 dB
   from the Fig. 11 red circle (raster -2.81 dB), with the Fig. 9 dip (about 0.52, 2.84 dB) agreeing.
5. samusenko2016 fab / facility (judgment a): keep empty. Acknowledgment (p.8) thanks "the MNF Facility of Fondazione Bruno Kessler,
   Centre for Materials and Microsystems, for device fabrication": under D448 FBK is fab and the MNF facility is facility. Neither
   is in `organizations.csv` / `org_aliases.csv` (searched Kessler, FBK, Trento; only org_university_of_trento exists, which is
   the first author's other affiliation and not named for fabrication). "Fondazione Bruno Kessler" already sits in
   `lit_headers/org_requests.csv` (bernard2021, limongi2026 headers). Org round: FBK (fab) and its MNF facility (facility, child
   of FBK); then a fix batch adds them to the 18 fabricated samusenko2016 rows plus the added series row (the FEM design row stays
   without).
6. zhuang2011 sideband filters (judgment b): keep `wdm.interleaver`. The response is half-band periodic (-1 dB passband 50 % FSR),
   the second filter's architecture is cited from an interleaver paper (ref. [29] "optical interleaver based on double-ring
   assisted Mach-Zehnder interferometer"), and the merged same-group zhuang2016 2RAMZI and rizzo2020 / rizzo2023 / cheung2022 RAMZI
   rows are wdm.interleaver. Keep 25 dB per curve (`extracted_from_figure`; orange about 25.5 dB, blue about 24.5-25 dB) and the
   -1 dB passband 3.35 GHz `author_estimate` on both (the 50 % FSR label sits where both curves cross -1 dB; GHz value rests on the
   design FSR). Keep the 4.5 % / 10 % transition-band labels out (D156): the dashed edges tie 4.5 % to the blue (double-ring)
   curve and 10 % to the orange (single-ring) curve, opposite to the printed device names; the double ring being the more selective
   filter (p.8) suggests the names are swapped, but that is an inference. process_name "TriPleX technology" with fab empty: keep
   (v7 named process without a named foundry; LioniX BV is an author affiliation only, D80b).
7. chen2006a (judgment c): keep `wdm.other` (multi-channel all-pass ring dispersion compensator with on-chip PBS / PBC; not a
   channel-filter wdm.mrr, D400) and `glass` (the paper calls Hydex a "high index contrast glass material system"; merged `glass`
   rows are ion-exchange and Er-doped glasses, `silica` rows are doped-silica PLCs; no Hydex precedent in data/components). If the
   coordinator wants Hydex grouped with doped-silica PLCs, `silica` is the alternative; `other` is not needed. IL spread 0.4 dB
   (`il_nonuniformity_db`, Fig. 4) checked: keep.
8. jin2017 tuning (judgment d): keep neither v_pi_v nor phase_shift_pi. The plotted data support about a full FSR, not pi: at 15 V
   the 86 um device shifts 0.38-0.39 nm = about 0.93 FSR, i.e. about 1.9 pi round-trip phase, so the results sentence "pi phase
   shift ... 16 V" conflicts with the paper's own data and with abstract / conclusion. Keep tuning_range_nm 0.412 `derived` (value
   = the FSR label, from the text's full-FSR statement), note patched to say the 16 V point is not plotted. Keep the slopes 25 / 21
   pm/V (`extracted_from_figure`, endpoint averages over 0-15 / 0-20 V); caveat for notes if wanted: the 86 um response is
   superlinear (about 13 pm/V over 0-5 V). fsr_nm 0.412 `author_estimate` (origin of the label not stated; the 0 V dips are 0.41 nm
   apart, consistent).
