## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. su2022 fab `org_imec` (distiller judgment a). Recommend keep. This is not the D433 case (a PDK only cited, no fab statement):
   the devices are imec PDK cells and the paper twice names imec as the device's manufacturer, including a process fact about this
   photodiode ("According to the manufacturer's description, the underlying photodiode is properly protected by the silicon dioxide
   overlay during the etching process", "the manufacturer's (IMEC's) model", p.4). That is the noun form of "manufactured by IMEC"
   (velha2016 precedent, fab org_imec). Keep the AWG fab empty: the manufacturer sentences are about the photodiode (Contract v7,
   per component), although the AWG is integrated on the same chip (p.3 "integrated AWG").
2. su2022 cross-section withheld (judgment b). Recommend keep withheld (rule 3): the paper says the PDK gives no cross-section and
   the structure "is followed by the design shown in the previous work [29]"; the 1 um Ge width, 0.45 um intrinsic height, ~400 nm Ge,
   220 nm Si, 2 um BOX, doping and 13.4 um electrode separation are that assumed structure, not a property known for this device. The
   patch removes the 220 nm from core_material for consistency. Only the 40 um active length (also stated in Sec. I as the authors'
   own design) and the 40 x 10 um footprint stay.
3. su2022 responsivities (judgment c). Recommend keep as staged: 0.79 A/W `author_estimate` on_chip (15 dB AWG + lensed-fiber term
   of unstated origin; neither reading of the printed end points reproduces it: slope 0.39 A/W, ratio 1.1 A/W at 0.4 mW), 0.63 A/W
   `measured` fiber-referred (end points give 0.62 A/W; the paper says coupling loss is not removed), conclusion 0.64 and abstract
   0.63-0.74 in notes (D417).
4. ahmad2002 per-mirror losses and T-branch (judgment d). Recommend keep both. The paper states the values per mirror ("per corner
   mirror", "These measured values of loss per mirror", p.2), so no division is ours to show; measured with the stated wording (D167
   analogue). The Fig. 1 SEM of two mirrors in series is an example image, not a stated test structure. T-branch ~1.5 dB stays
   `excess_loss_db` per_component (Fig. 3 normalized transmission sums both arms, about 0.7, so the 3 dB split is not in it); the
   note on the output corner mirrors and on Fig. 3 (1.5 dB matches the 2.2 um2 point at about 0.71 better than 2.4 um2 at about 0.66)
   is sufficient.
5. pruessner2007 sample identity (judgment e). Recommend keep on the W 6 um rows (D159 not triggered): Fig. 1(b), captioned as the
   L_C 12 um device with W = 6 um in Sec. 2, carries the same Q labels (2,626 / 4,584) as Fig. 4 and Sec. 3.2, the 28 nm FSR equals
   the 1575.7-1604.3 nm spacing, and the Fig. 3 tuning starts at about 1604 nm. The W 5 um sample has a different resonance (1614.5
   nm). The 125 kOhm resistance "for our L_C = 12 um long cavity" also goes with this device (notes; registry gap below).
6. blascosolvas2024 grating 11.7 dB (judgment f). Recommend `author_estimate` (patched): the taper term is not measured but rests on
   a stated no-adiabatic-loss assumption (rule 4; D612 precedent for a stated no-loss assumption). Scope per_coupler with set-up loss
   in `includes` is right. Overrule to `derived` only if the coordinator treats an own-measurement-times-length term as arithmetic.
