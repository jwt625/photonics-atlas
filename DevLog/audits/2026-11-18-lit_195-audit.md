## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. liu2024b (judgment (a)). Recommendation: keep the row (patched note). The premise in BATCH_REPORT is wrong: the
   letter's ref. [16] is an Appl. Opt. 2023 paper, and merged lit:liu2023a (Optics Express, published 2023-12-28; this
   letter received 2023-11-29) is not cited at all. D408 / D259 therefore do not remove anything: no value is attributed
   to earlier work, and the numbers differ (EL < 0.18 vs < 0.22 dB; 5.2 % vs +/-0.051), although the Table I design (all
   widths, gaps and lengths) is identical and both papers may report one chip (not stated). Parameter: `coupling_length_um`
   is right for the new rows. The `dc` layout template (component_layouts.yaml) applies to `splitter.adiabatic` and
   requires `gap_nm` + `coupling_length_um`; the region III length at constant G2 is the coupling section, and this row
   already uses `taper_length_um` for the 10 um access tapers Lt1 / Lt2, so L there would put two lengths under one
   parameter. The merged liu2023a rows (adc_50_50 m7 78 um, adc_70_30 m7 70 um, adc_90_10 m7 79 um, all
   `taper_length_um`) are not this audit's to patch: migrate them to `coupling_length_um` in a coordinator fix batch and
   add the reciprocal cross-note to cmp_lit_liu2023a_adc_50_50. Merged `splitter.adiabatic` rows hold 15 `taper_length_um`,
   11 `coupling_length_um` and 7 `length_um` rows: worth one convention sweep (mode-evolution section at constant gap ->
   `coupling_length_um`; access tapers -> `taper_length_um`).
2. du2019 (judgment (b)). Recommendation: keep as distilled. 0.35 is a power quantity: it is computed from measured port
   powers as 2C3 / (C2 + 2C3) (p.6), whatever the p.4 amplitude naming; it equals R / (R + T), so the 35 % power
   reflectivity holds for a lossless PTE (note added, basis `derived` per D256 stays). Fab: the acknowledgment names "the
   prototype device", and the paper uses "prototype device" for the coupled-cavity configuration (Fig. 1, Fig. 2(a)), so
   device A and the two Qc devices carry `org_cornerstone`; devices B and C are separate test structures ("also
   fabricated", p.4) and stay empty under the per-component rule. Alternative (not recommended): all five rows, from p.5
   "fabricated on the 340 nm SOI platform", which only implies CORNERSTONE. Qc 170 / 450 as sibling rows with
   `author_estimate` Q and figure-read mean extinctions: agreed (D159 does not apply; the Qc devices are named by their
   Qc, device A's Qc is not stated).

   on assigning the whole ~0.25 dB MZS loss to two identical FACs (lossless arms assumed, no FAC test structure):
