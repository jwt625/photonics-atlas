## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. li2017a Harvard University not on fab though fabrication was "performed at" Harvard CNS (D448 reading; judgment (a)).
   Recommendation: keep fab org_mit only, facilities MTL + CNS. D448 put UCSB / Honeywell on fab because the acknowledgment named
   those orgs as the places where samples were processed; here the named places are two shared user facilities, which rule 12 /
   Contract v7 route to `facility_org_ids`, and D82c keeps the owner of a named facility out of the facility role (IBM / BRNC);
   it does not make the owner a fab either. The fabricator is named (H.L., MIT; D395 / D399: fab = the org of the authors the
   fabrication sentence names). D83c (fabricator with two affiliations -> org where the work was done) does not apply: H.L.
   has one affiliation. Merged precedent: MIT-fabricated work at CNS keeps fab MIT
   only (lit:he2026, lit:zhang2019 org_mit;org_mit_lincoln_laboratory with CNS facility). Alternative: org_mit;org_harvard_university
   if the coordinator extends D448 to the owner of a named shared facility (would also touch the merged rows above).
2. li2017a TE 590 dB/cm basis and polarizer arithmetic (judgment (b)). Recommendation: 590 dB/cm `measured` (patched): the ER
   formula and slope are the paper's own extraction over its own MZI length series with no assumed or cited input (D80a), as
   ring fits and the merged fringe-extinction rows (li2026, lucia2026). Method `cutback` kept for the length-difference slope
   (the paper contrasts its MZI method with a classical cut-back only on coupling immunity). Alternative: keep `derived` (drop
   the op) if the ER-to-absorption conversion is read as explicit arithmetic (Contract v4). The TM 20 dB/cm stays `derived`
   (difference of two rings, rule 4), and polarizer IL 0.8 dB / ER 23 dB stay `derived` (printed correspondences of the two
   losses over 400 um; the paper states no separate polarizer IL measurement; the end-fire far-field ER 21 dB is the measured
   row).
3. li2017a thermo-optic switch split into `phase_shifter.thermo_optic` (graphene heater) and `resonator.photonic_crystal`
   (cavity) per D396 vs `switch_fabric.other` (judgment (c)). Recommendation: keep the split. D158 / D407 cover 1x2 / 2x2 MZI
   switch elements and ring elements routing demultiplexed channels in a switch matrix; this is a 1x1 resonant intensity switch
   with no port routing. `tuning_efficiency_pm_per_mw` is not allowed on switch_fabric and `rise_time_us` / `fall_time_us` are
   not allowed on resonator, so a phase_shifter heater row (yan2017 graphene-heater precedent) plus a resonator row keeps every
   value; the crossing stays its own `crossing.mmi` row.
4. li2017a Fig. 4(d) bias conflict and mid-IR modulator (judgment (d)). Recommendation: keep 1.5 V on m1 / m2 with the conflict
   note. Fig. 4(c) reads about 108 mA/W at +1.5 V and about 0 mA/W at 0 V, and Fig. 4(b) gives about 0.6 mA/W at zero bias,
   while Fig. 4(d) shows about 100-110 mA/W at 2185 nm and up to 250 mA/W: the spectrum cannot be a zero-bias sweep, so the
   caption is right and Supp. Sec. XV (p.46) is the error (D417 note). The modulator stays out (rule 2); it is not in the EO atlas
   `devices.csv`, so list li2017a as an EO-atlas candidate (8 dB/mm over 2.05-2.45 um, 7 us / 23 kHz, 0.5 pF, 6.4 GHz projection)
   as D168 / D612 / D614 did.
5. nejadriahi2021 platform, fab, facility and cited loss (judgment (e)). Recommendation:
   (i) Platform `sin` kept: every merged Si-rich SiN row uses `sin` (bucio2017 n 2.54, popovic2006 n 2.18, nielson2005,
   croce2026, veiga2026, boynton2020); the enum has no SRN value and `other` would drop the rows from the SiN family. The n = 3.1
