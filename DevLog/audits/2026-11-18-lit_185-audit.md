   shi2024). D418 (nader2025) concerned a loss-budget term of unstated origin, which neither sentence is.

   determined, and the dip depth is symmetric in the two branches), q_loaded 1e6 `measured`; threshold ~0.036 mW `measured`

   drop both rows as label-only, but ye2019 / boust2020 precedents keep printed comb spacings. Fab `org_ucsb` from the author
   contributions (D80b, D395).

   rows, and its geometry is carried by the facet row with the identity note.
