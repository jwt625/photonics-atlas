## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. Class of the two tunable dispersion compensators (judgment (a)): keep `wdm.cascaded_mzi`. The subtype text is "cascaded MZI /
   lattice filter", which is exactly both structures (takiguchi1996 9 + 8 MZI lattice; doerr2005 four two-arm stages); `wdm.other` is
   "other WDM / band multiplexer" and would say less. Neither row carries WDM channel metrics, and the two IL rows are distinguishable by
   scope / ref_plane (fiber_to_fiber 14.4 dB chip; fiber_to_fiber module 5.5 dB incl. circulator). Patched: common tag
   `dispersion_compensator`. If more TODCs arrive, a `wdm.dispersion_compensator` subtype (or a view filter on the tag) is the registry
   candidate.
2. zheng2023a LN etch depth / sidewall (judgment (b)): `design_target` (patched). The values are the design-section simulation
   cross-section, set "according to our fabricated waveguide profiles" with no method or measured number; liu2024 m8 (same wording, 60
   deg) is merged as `design_target` after the lit_109 audit. Contract v5 reserves `measured` for SEM / AFM-measured dimensions.
3. zheng2023a platform (judgment (c)): keep `lnoi_loaded_si` for the whole LWDM and the VAC. Light passes Si MMIs / delay arms and the
   bonded LN phase-shifter sections; Fig. 1(a) shows the LN film over each stage block; Table II labels the platform "SOI/LN" (p.3);
   valdez2023 Si MMIs under bonded TFLN are `lnoi_loaded_si`. `soi` would describe only the filter-defining arms. Si-LN VAC as
   `waveguide_platform.transition` (rule 10) ok. IL conflict: keep the results-section 7.5 dB with the conclusion's 7.6 dB in notes.
4. zheng2023a LN phase shifter (judgment (d)): no row, as staged. The registry `phase_shifter` class is "non-EO"; VpiL (about 3 V.cm DC
   measured, 2.32 V.cm simulated, p.3) is an EO-atlas quantity (rule 2), and a v_pi_v of about 30 V would be derived from it for a 1 mm
   section, not a stated value. EO-atlas candidate; no phase_shifter VpiL gap needed for this paper.
5. takiguchi1996 monitor tap (judgment (e)): keep the separate `splitter.other` row with 4 % `author_estimate`. Same construction as
   the merged cmp_lit_akiyama2021_tap_coupler (15 %, splitter.other, author_estimate) and cmp_lit_castro2025_monitor_tap_mmi; the tap is
   a distinct physical coupler (rule 9) and "coupling ratio was 0.04" (p.5) does not say design or measured.
6. FSR basis (judgment (f)): keep `author_estimate` on both (D82a). takiguchi1996 is explicitly the design ("we set the delta L value
   to 4.138 mm so that the FSR ... becomes 50 GHz", p.3); doerr2005 "The free-spectral range is 100 GHz" (p.2) gives no method.
7. ong2020 (judgment (g)): keep as staged. Median + range rows per Table I cell group with IQR in notes (no IQR statistic in the enum;
   not worth one for a single paper); IL `de_embedded` / `reference_structure` with the couplers in `excludes`; fitted geometry
   `derived` (D80a); Compoundtek only in `process_name` with fab empty (design rules cited, no fabrication statement; Contract v7).
8. Fab and scope (judgment (h)): takiguchi1996 fab empty (staff thanks without an org, D543); zheng2023a CUMEC on the Si-based rows only
   (per-component fab, D515 / D463; the conclusion's "we have designed and fabricated" names three author orgs, note patched);
   doerr2005 `org_lucent_bell_labs` (first-person "We made" + one author org). doerr2005 5.5 dB: keep on the TODC row as a module-level
   value; scope `fiber_to_fiber` (patched) with ref_plane `module` and the circulator / connector in `includes`.
