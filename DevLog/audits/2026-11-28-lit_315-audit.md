## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. (a) Ring Q from Fig. 13(e, f) vector markers, panel (f) on the panel (e) scale. **Keep both.** The independent marker read
   reproduces every stored range within the stated uncertainty; for (f), the frame height matches (e) within 0.4 pt and the
   grid lines align, and the alternative calibration on (f)'s own frame moves the values by about +3000 (inside +/-5000).
   The shared-axis assumption is written in the row notes, which is the right place for it. `q_loaded` with note (unqualified
   Q), statistic `range` over the 13 bias points, 0 V value in condition: fine.
2. (b) Three `phase_shifter.other` rows incl. the damped device; per-polarization slopes. **Keep all three rows and the four
   slope rows** with the patched conditions. The damped row is a different substrate stack (XeF2-roughened backside + PU / Ni
   layer, D32) and the paper calls it only "the fabricated device", so merging it into the Disk actuator would assert an
   identity the paper does not state (D159 spirit). Its two resonances are valid on their own; moving them to the Disk row as
   a "post-damping" condition is the alternative if the coordinator prefers fewer rows. Slopes: the printed labels are
   +60 V shifts, so the stored values are 0 to +60 V averages; the patch says so and gives the full-range chord in notes
   (do not replace them with figure-read chords: the label arithmetic is traceable, the chords are not printed).
3. (c) Sub-ns response time as `rise_time_us` 0.001 `lt` `author_estimate`. **Keep.** The claim is printed three times (p.1,
   p.4, p.11) and in the Fig. 4(b) caption; no edge time is shown and 20 ns pulses cannot resolve 1 ns, so `measured` would be
   wrong and `author_estimate` with the "not a 10-90 % edge" condition is honest. Notes-only is the stricter alternative.
4. (d) Fab EPFL + Purdue and facility CMi + Birck on all rows; OEM Group as a note. **Keep both.** D448 (place statement =
   fab + named shared facility), liu2020a / siddharth2024 identical construction; OEM Group sputtered the Mo / AlN films
   "through foundry services" (p.15), a film-deposition service like the lihachev2022 case ruled note-only in D431. No org
   request needed.
5. (e) AlN stress-optic modulator values as EO-atlas candidates. **Agree.** The eo-modulator-atlas holds `stress_optic` rows
   (khalil2026-a, montifiore2026-a..d) and no tian2020 row; queue tian2020 there: 3-dB modulation bands 250 MHz around
   4.13 GHz and 260 MHz around 8.7 GHz (p.14, Fig. 4(f), TM, damped device; abstract p.1, p.4 and the Fig. 4 caption print
   250 MHz for both, a conflict to note there), multi-octave S21 1-6 GHz (p.6, Fig. 2(b, c)), > 20 dB resonance /
   anti-resonance contrast (p.8), 6 GHz square-wave drive (p.13). `bw3db_khz` here is a baseband cutoff and must not take
   passband widths.
