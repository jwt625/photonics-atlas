## Coordinator questions

1. Ps of the gain-compression fit as `psat_out_dbm` (beeck2020, gasse2019; asked). In G = G0 (1 + Pin/Ps) / (1 + G0 Pin/Ps) the output power
   approaches Ps once G0 Pin >> Ps while Pin << Ps (Pout = Ps (1 + Pin/Ps) G0 Pin / (Ps + G0 Pin)), so the model itself makes Ps an
   output-side scale; at 3-dB compression Pout = G0 Ps / (2 (G0 - 2)), about Ps/2. Merged precedents enter the same model parameter as
   `psat_out_dbm` with "not a 1-dB / 3-dB point" in the condition (liu2019 qd_soa_on_si, cao2025 qd_soa_3mm). Recommendation: keep as entered.
2. thai2018 T0 = 117 K (asked). The registry definition says threshold current; here the threshold is an optical pump power density (fit
   15-230 K, condition says so). The quantity is the same exponential threshold law; babichev2025 / hakobyan2025 already store T0 of a threshold
   current density. Recommendation: keep, and widen the `characteristic_temperature_k` definition to "threshold current, current density or
   optical pump density (which one in condition)" in the next registry extension. Strict alternative: delete m3 (value to notes).
3. kumar2025 PIC-to-PIC edge coupling as `chip_chip_coupler.butt` (asked). Butt coupling between separate dies across a 10 um gap; merged
   precedents use `.butt` beyond laser-to-PIC (barwicz2017 Si-to-InP chip-to-chip couplers, sundaram2025 PPKTP waveguide to PIC).
   Recommendation: keep, and widen the subtype text to "butt / edge coupling between separate dies (laser-to-PIC, PIC-to-PIC)".
4. Fab empty on souleiman2024 and gasse2019 (asked). souleiman2024: the only first-person sentence is the wafer bonding (p.2), with authors at
   III-V Lab, Telecom SudParis and CEA-Leti; device fabrication is passive and points to ref. [21] (p.3). Three orgs: not unambiguous (rule 12,
   ronn2019 lit_01 precedent). gasse2019: "we present the design, fabrication and characterization" (abstract, p.2) is a presentation sentence
   like "we developed" (Contract v4 / D80b); processing is passive; all authors at Ghent University - imec. Recommendation: keep both empty.
   Alternative for gasse2019 only: treat the abstract sentence as a first-person statement and set the Ghent University - imec org.
5. beeck2020 ring-laser operating wavelength (audit miss). Added as about 1550 nm from the abstract (D393), with the Fig. 9 peak reads
   (1555.5-1577 nm) in notes; the printed value is loose against the figure. Recommendation: keep. Alternative: drop the add and keep the
   wavelength in notes only.
6. D153 geometry misses (audit). Added 18 design-dimension rows stated in the text (souleiman2024 Si height / BOX on four rows, SOA length;
   lee2022 gain / phase section lengths; beeck2020 spiral length and two waveguide cross-sections). Recommendation: keep all (rows feed the
