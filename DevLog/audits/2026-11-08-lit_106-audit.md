## Coordinator questions

1. kiani2023 platform of the TeO2:Tm3+-coated Si rows (2 disks, 2 lasers, edge coupler): keep `other`, or `soi` (Si holds 67-74 % of the mode
   power, p.2-3)? Recommendation: keep `other`. The gain layer is part of the guiding stack (15-19 % confinement is what makes the laser work) and
   the rows are post-processed foundry chips that the benchmark excludes anyway (D933); `soi` would rank a coated hybrid as a plain AMF waveguide.
2. ferrotti2016 fab: empty on all 9 rows (passive "are fabricated", authors at CEA-Leti / STMicroelectronics / INL, staff thanked by name with no
   org)? Recommendation: keep empty (szelag2017, D433; D84b needs a printed org).
3. coratozanarella2026 Fig. 2(b) micrograph shows chip-surface text "k2 = 10 %", "FSR = 38.00 GHz", "tip = 100 nm": enter as MRR design values?
   Recommendation: no. They are layout text written on the chip, not figure annotations or stated values; the text states only that the MRR FSR
   was chosen "close to" the about 38 GHz FPLD FSR, and the tip belongs to an edge coupler with no row.
4. kiani2023 extinction 3.2 / 3.5 dB: keep as printed with the pump-vs-laser ambiguity in notes? Recommendation: keep; the printed numbers reproduce
   the 52 / 55 % absorbed fractions exactly, so the value is the paper's own and only its resonance label is unclear.
5. coratozanarella2026 FPLD power / MFD / FSR `measured` although the characterization sits in the uncached Supplement 1? Recommendation: keep;
   the Fig. 1 caption and p.9 Methods say the authors characterized the FPLD (Supplement 1 Sec. 2), so these are not datasheet values.
