## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. (a) luo2017 platform `suspended_lnoi` for the undercut LN microdisk. **Keep.** After the HF undercut the WGM rim of a
   45 um disk on a pedestal is free-standing (air above and below the guided mode), which is what distinguishes
   `suspended_lnoi` from `lnoi_strip` (fully etched film on oxide, mode sees the BOX). Same group, same wafer type and same
   undercut chemistry already merged as `suspended_lnoi`: liang2017 nanobeam (300 nm x-cut, Rochester), li2019 PhC cavities;
   also jiang2020 (Stanford) released LN. The undercut SiN disks of doolin2015 are `sin` only because no suspended SiN value
   exists, so they are not a counter-precedent. Recommend recording a decision: undercut / pedestal LN microdisks and
   microrings released from LNOI are `suspended_lnoi`; disks whose rim still rests on oxide (wedge on full BOX) stay
   `lnoi_strip` or `lnoi_rib` as the paper states.
2. (b) Pedestal height as `box_thickness_um` 2 um. **Keep.** The pedestal is the undercut BOX of the wafer, and the
   parameter is defined as buried oxide / lower cladding thickness; merged precedents store pedestal heights the same way
   with the role in condition: singh2015 AMTIR-1 microdisk m7 (about 2 um silica pedestal), li2024a Ta2O5 microdisk m9
   (4.7 um SiO2 partially undercut to a pedestal). `design_target` is right (wafer BOX, D402). No new parameter needed unless
   a layout template later has to draw the undercut width (not printed here).
3. (c) SHG efficiency 0.36 %/W `derived`, ref_plane `unspecified`. **Agree with both.** The paper prints only 3.6e-6 /mW, so
   the %/W form is a conversion (D256; 3.6e-6 /mW = 3.6e-3 /W = 0.36 %/W, arithmetic checked, and it reproduces the
   last Fig. 3(b) point: 3.6e-6 x 1.29^2 = 6.0e-6 mW = 6.0 nW). Fig. 3(b) axes say only "Fundamental Input (mW)" and
   "Second-Harmonic Output (nW)"; no taper transmission, coupling or collection correction is printed, and the text blames
   unoptimized "light extraction" (p.5), so neither on-chip nor fiber plane is stated. Condition range fixed in the patch.
4. (d) carmon2004 toroid Q 2e7 and microsphere Q 2e6 as `q_loaded` `author_estimate`. **Agree.** Both are bare caption
   statements with no linewidth, spectrum or extraction method (bino2018 precedent, D633; ilchenko1999 405 um m4). For the
   toroid the Q is not one of the two stated fit parameters (p.4 caption names only I_h/K and I_h/Cp; p.5 "The fit parameters
   ... were the heat capacity and heat conduction"); it is an input that sets the Lorentzian width in Eq. (3)-(4), and the
   Fig. 1(a) data are thermally broadened at 1.8 mW, so the cold-cavity Q is not measurable from them. `measured` would
   overstate it.
5. (e) luo2017 `q_intrinsic` `author_estimate` (D403). **Agree.** The dips are shallow (Fig. 1(b) about 0.71, Fig. 1(c)
   about 0.80) and the text states neither the coupling regime nor the fit model; for T = 0.71 the under- and over-coupled
   solutions give Q0 of about 1.09 Q_L and about 12.7 Q_L, so the regime decides the value by an order of magnitude and is not
   determined from the data (D403, D418; li2019 same group same treatment). `typical` statistic follows the printed "two
   typical examples" (rule 7).
6. (f) luo2017 fab empty, facility CNF. **Agree.** p.3 is passive with no org (D80b, D433, Contract v7); the same-group
   liang2017 row (same "performed in part at the Cornell NanoScale Facility" wording, passive fabrication) has fab empty and
   facility CNF, while li2019 has fab `org_university_of_rochester` only because its text is first-person ("we deposited").
   D448 does not apply: the acknowledgment names the shared facility, not the authors' org, and the merged CNF rows keep
   such sentences as facility only.
