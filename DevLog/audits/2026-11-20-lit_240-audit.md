## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. paredes2021 IL scope and CTP (judgment 1): keep `de_embedded` + method `unspecified`. The paper states the spectra are normalized
   (p.7) and the through traces sit at 0 dB, so the couplers are removed; only the reference is unnamed, which `method: unspecified`
   and the excludes text say (ding2013 is the `de_embedded` link precedent; yang2026a went `unspecified` because no normalization was
   stated at all). Fab `org_applied_nanotools` correct. CTP: a facility, not borderline (rule 12 "work performed there"; thanks go to
   the facility for its characterization, not to staff; D640 gu2021). Org round: "Core Technology Platform Facility (CTP)", New York
   University Abu Dhabi, facility, lit:paredes2021 p.9; then set `facility_org_ids` on the paredes2021 row. "NYUAD Photonics Lab" is a
   group lab (no entry).
2. paredes2021 O-band crosstalk (judgment 2): keep 17.0 dB with the D417 note. Re-read on a 3x crop of Fig. 7(a): I2-O1 peaks at
   about -12.9 to -13.5 dB near 1.278 and 1.328 um, so neither the 17.0 dB O-band figure nor the 16 dB headline holds on that trace;
   the identity of the value is clear (D417: printed kept, conflict noted; ji2022a / horst2009 precedents).
3. paredes2021 tolerance (judgment 3): keep < 0.7 dB `simulated` with the conflict note. Fig. 5: 1310 nm curve about -1.2 to -1.25 dB at
   -10 nm, about -0.6 dB at +10 nm; 1550 nm above -0.25 dB. Same D417 treatment.
4. jiang2021 port vs mode labels and ref. [7] (judgment 4): keep the values by port. The port assignment is the one the results
   paragraph states and the figures confirm (Fig. 2 paths, Fig. 6 panels, 84 nm read in Fig. 6(a) for I1); the abstract / conclusion
   mode labels contradict p.2 (I1 is the first-order path) and are recorded in the component notes; `mode: TM0` (the launched mode at
   both ports) is literal. Ref. [7] splitter values stay out (D259: attributed to the authors' 2020 JLT). The TWC simulated values
   (p.3) carry no citation and stay in, although the multimode-access TWC is the authors' ref. [32] design; D259 needs an attribution
   in the paper.
5. jiang2021 GC 9.2 dB/port (judgment 5): keep `loopback_half`, `measured`, method `loopback`. The Fig. 7 peak label -18.4 dB at
   1.55 um is exactly 2 x 9.2 dB, which only a two-coupler transmission explains; `per_coupler` would claim an individual isolation
   the paper does not show, and `unspecified` discards that evidence. Basis stays `measured` (stated per-port value, arithmetic not
   shown: D167 / D452 lorenzen2026). The three Fig. 7 labels stay `unspecified` (caption "the grating coupler", values match two).
6. jiang2021 scopes, platform, wavelengths (judgment 6): keep all. Fig. 8 loss rows `unspecified` (no normalization stated for Fig. 8,
   unlike Fig. 6; the near-0 dB I2-O2 trace suggests GC normalization but the paper does not say so); splitter TM0 < 3 dB
   `unspecified` (p.6: O1 not normalized). Platform `soi` (device-layer etch depth not stated, only the 70 nm GC etch; D422 / D434).
   Centre-wavelength rows at 1550 nm with `wavelength_inferred: true` (centre wavelength 1.55 um stated for the design, p.3; the GC
   sentence just before gives 1550 nm, p.5).
7. liao2016, wang2022c, class, registry (judgment 7): keep all. Residual through-port TE1 as `extinction_db`: the registry defines it
   for mode converters as mode rejection with the type in condition (ahmmed2020 rejection rows), and the paper names only the two
   slot-port terms crosstalk. "IL less than -0.1 dB" (p.4, conclusion) stays out: the path is not identifiable (rule 1; the TE0
   through loss is already m1). wang2022c fab empty (D80b) and AEMD facility (cai2021); TE1 conventional ADC kept as a `design` row
   (own simulated IL, not on the chip). All (de)MUX links and mode splitters `mode_converter.mdm_mux` (liao2024, yu2025).
