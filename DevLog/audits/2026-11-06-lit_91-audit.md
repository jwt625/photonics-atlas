## Open questions for the coordinator

1. tsakyridis2020 class. Recommendation: `switch_fabric.other` with the gain row deleted (patched). The SOA-MZI is one all-optical gate
   used for wavelength conversion and power equalization (switched port, gate wording, index term on burst-mode switches), not an amplifier;
   D158 / D407 class by function; SOA gate switches are already switch_fabric (rombouts2025). Gcw1 = 2 is one internal SOA's operating
   point and the model input, so it is not a component gain. Alternative A (distiller): keep `amplifier.other` and m1 (drop the class, notes
   and delete ops). Alternative B: `frequency_converter.other` by function (merged FWM converters foster2006, kuyken2011, ye2021), but
   neither extinction_db nor gain_db is allowed there, so the row would have no metric and be deleted (D49).
2. maeda2024 8.3 dB SOA gain and SMSR 35-40 dB. Recommendation: keep them out (no patch). The paper is the journal extension of [13] on
   the same devices, but Fig. 10 and Fig. 8 carry [13] in their captions, which is the D408 figure clause applied in D455 and D465. Weak
   point on record: the p.5 and p.7 sentences cite nothing, and the unattributed Fig. 11 (same SOA) shows about 8.7-9.2 dB at 10 mA, so the
   gain is represented. If overruled, add gain_db 8.3 (10 mA, 1280 nm, Pin -7 dBm fiber, derived per D166, p.5 Sec. III-B) on soa_l140 and
   smsr_db 35-40 (range, about 30 mA, 25-80 C, measured, p.5 Sec. III-A) on dr_ld_dfb200.
3. itoh2024 Fig. 2(a) thresholds. Recommendation: add the four range rows (patched); the reading is as reliable as the batch's own Fig. 2(b)
   and Fig. 4(b) reads. If overruled, drop the four add ops and the four phase-series note ops.
