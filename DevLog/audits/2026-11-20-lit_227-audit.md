   Fig. 12 point is tied to its diameter and etch condition by the legend, and no value is moved to a device it may not belong to (the

   points). The simulated value is Fig. 7(b) of the 2a = 3 um design (D80e; condition says ideal disk, alpha_s = 0). The sibling rows

   one org).
2. fujita1999 resonator row: 30 cm-1 as 130.3 dB/cm `author_estimate` and the 0.6 nm doublet as `mode_splitting_ghz` 67.7 derived
   (judgment (b)): keep both. The loss follows the merged Q -> dB/cm precedents with an unprinted index (karunakaran2024, li2026b; D76)
