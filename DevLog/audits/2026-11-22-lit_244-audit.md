## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. (a) sato2007 fab: keep empty. Rule 12 / D72 need an unambiguous own org ("all authors at one company or institution"), and
   the printed affiliations are NTT Photonics Laboratories (5) and Hirosaki University (1). D154 (authors over unrelated orgs stay
   empty) and the hiratani2024 ruling (D433, dual affiliations) apply. The biography (with NTT until 2006; manuscript received
   2006-11-02) and the thanks to "members of NTT Photonics Laboratories for valuable discussions" (not a fabrication or place
   statement) make org_ntt very likely, but a majority-plus-biography rule would be a new decision; if the coordinator wants it,
   take it as a ruling and apply org_ntt to both sato2007 rows with the biography in notes. Recommended: keep empty.
2. (b) faugeron2011 fab: keep empty. "We have developed a structure" (abstract) is not a fabrication statement (Contract v4), and
   "We have fabricated" (p.3 Sec. V) names authors at Thales Air Systems (2), III-V Laboratory (6) and SUPELEC (1): D154 /
   zhang2023f (D618) precedent. The thanks to a III-V lab member for "fruitful technical support" names no process or place (D72,
   D84b needs thanks for help with fabrication).
3. (c) chen2002 RIN: keep -155 `lt` `measured`. The registry stores RIN negative "as reported"; adding the conventional sign is a
   sign normalization like rule 5 / D33 for losses, which keeps the basis (764 merged measured `_db` rows carry a "sign as printed"
   style note). D82d / D256 cover unit and prefix conversions, not sign conventions. The printed form is in the notes.
4. (d) menna2001 far field: keep out (D159). The paragraph (p.1-2) is about "the lasers" of both sections, the far-field sentence
   says "The laser far-field". Its 1.6 A end point matches the section-A figures (Figs. 1-2 end at 1.6 A; Fig. 3 runs to 2.0 A),
   which hints section A but does not state it. Values are in both components' notes. If a ruling ever allows a shared-waveguide
   value on sibling rows (same ridge, only the grating period differs), these 31 / 11.5-14.0 deg are the case; not recommended now.
5. (e) Laser facet to lensed fiber / lens assembly as `fiber_chip_coupler.edge` on `inp`: keep. Same treatment as merged
   andreou2019 (InP laser facet to lensed fibre), cao2025 and bakonyi2003 (SOA facet to lensed fiber) and D554. Scope per_coupler
   is right (one facet into one fiber, printed as a fraction of the emitted power). faugeron2011 1552.1 nm and menna2001 c_band
   are the lasers' own emission, flagged inferred with reasons; acceptable either way (rule 8).
6. (f) "as high as" / "as low as" -> `statistic: best`: keep (rule 7 literal; chen2010 75.8 % precedent, D622). Note that sato2007
   prints the same 8 mW without "as high as" in the abstract and that each value is one laser's; `best` here marks the authors'
   emphasis, not a population maximum. If the coordinator wants `best` only where several devices are compared, it would touch
   four rows (sato2007 dfb m2 / m5, fp m1; sato2008a dfb m3) and is a rule change, not an audit fix.
7. (g) menna2001 rows: keep. Sections A and B carry different grating periods (sibling designs, D32); the T-cBN and Cu C-block
   mounts are packaging conditions of one design, kept in `condition` with the 10-15 C heating note.
