## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. dumon2004a fab (judgment a): patched to empty. The batch's own precedent pick (pathak2014a) does not fit: there all authors print
   one org; here two printed orgs (UGent department "Ghent University - IMEC" and IMEC vzw) with no named place, exactly the
   split for which the merged journal paper lit:dumon2004 was left empty. Alternative if the coordinator prefers imec: D154 parent
   rule (both affiliations print "IMEC"), but then lit:dumon2004 should be changed in the same ruling for consistency. Taper
   values: keep `le` `author_estimate` (the bound rests on a theoretical facet reflection, an assumed input; same as the merged
   lit:dumon2004 taper rows). Also note for the record: dumon2004a (LEOS 2003) and lit:dumon2004 (PTL 2004) report the same wire
   and taper numbers; no dedup rule exists, both kept with a cross-note (taper length 200 um here vs 300 um in the journal).
2. orcutt2011 fab (judgment b): keep `org_texas_instruments` only. D440 adds the authors' org for first-person post-processing
   only when that org is unambiguous; the post-processing sentences ("we use post-foundry processing", p.6) are spoken by an author
   list at MIT (RLE, MTL) and Texas Instruments, and no sentence names the place (D154). Recommend listing the four orcutt2011
   rows in `exclude_values` of `config/benchmark.yaml` (D933): every value was measured after the authors' XeF2 substrate
   removal on process-development wafers, so it is not a TI-process value.
3. orcutt2011 55 dB/cm (judgment b): keep both rows. The printed sentence (p.8) attributes "approximately 55 dB/cm from 1520 to 1580
   nm" to both Fig. 3(b) and 3(c); 55 is the ring-model best fit (Fig. 3(c) caption; derived, D80a), while the Fig. 3(b) cut-back
   means read 47.3-53.2 dB/cm (separate `extracted_from_figure` row, D417 conflict note already present). Method `unspecified` on
   the 55 row is right because the sentence names both methods.
4. orcutt2011 "5 um mode size" (judgment b): keep `mfd_chip_um` 5 `author_estimate` with the note (definition and level not
   printed); it is the only beam-size figure for the coupler. Deleting is acceptable if the coordinator wants only 1/e^2 MFDs.
5. orcutt2011 crosstalk and drift (judgment b): keep "less than 15 dB" as 15 `gt` suppression: Fig. 5(a) adjacent drop curves lie
   at about -17 to -20 dB (normalized to the off-resonance through port) at each neighbouring drop peak, and below -25 dB on the far

   citation (D408 / D80i scope); [33] is cited two sentences earlier for the defect-state absorption concept, and only the dark
