## Coordinator questions

1. rudenkov2025 scope (distiller Q1): a 34 mm depressed-cladding waveguide written by an ultrafast laser inside a polycrystalline
   Cr:ZnS sample, free-space coupled. Recommendation: keep (no op). D411 removes discrete bulk resonators (polished crystalline
   disks / WGM parts, tebeneva2024, zhang2025e); a lithography-free written waveguide in a bulk host is the merged amorim2017 case
   (fs-written guides in a fused-silica block, kept in lit_30) and the D163 fs-written waveguide rows. Platform `other` is right
   (ZnS has no enum value). Alternative: delete the eight components if the atlas should exclude written waveguides in crystals.
2. cheung2015 fab (distiller Q2): first-person fabrication ("we employ photoresist reflow", p.7; "We fabricated several flared SOA
   devices", p.11). The only work affiliation printed is UC Davis (affiliation 1); affiliation 2 of Kawakita is a "Currently with
   Furukawa Electric" present address, which the header contract records as `role: present_address` (ofc_headers/CONTRACT.md,
   "currently with"), separate from work affiliations, as this batch's own jung2021 rows do for the KIST present address. Recommendation: `org_university_of_california_davis` on all 7 rows (D72, applied in the patch);
   facility empty (no acknowledgment section).
3. cheung2015 Pout - Pin gain rows (distiller Q3): Pin (2 / 0.1 mW) is the stated on-chip SOA input (EDFAs compensate the input
   coupling loss, p.9) and Pout the on-chip output the paper's own WPE formula uses; the paper speaks of "SOA chip gain" (p.12).
   Recommendation: keep the five rows as `derived` (D166 / D394 arithmetic on the paper's own on-chip powers), no op; the flared
   row keeps the 11.24 vs 11.42 dBm note (8.23 vs 8.41 dB).
4. subramanian2012 gain vs "as discussed in [11]" (distiller Q4): Sec. V opens "Ta2O5 rib waveguides 2.3 cm long ... were used for
   gain measurements as discussed in [11]", and p.1 says "we reported the first measurements of gain in Er:Ta2O5 waveguides [11]"
   ([11] = the same four authors, "High index contrast Er:Ta2O5 waveguide amplifier on oxidised silicon", Opt. Commun. 285, 2011).
   The measurement is attributed to an earlier journal paper of the authors, not a conference paper this work extends, so the
   D408 extension clause does not apply; the attribution sits in the section's lead sentence rather than the value sentence, which
   D424 (pathak2012: "body cites the authors' own paper") treated as enough. Recommendation: delete m1-m3 (applied in the patch;
   values kept in component notes); keep the theory rows m4 / m5 (this paper's model) and the passive rows. Alternative: keep
   m1-m3 under the narrow D408 test (remove the three delete_metrics and the subramanian2012 `components` entry).
