   keep it in notes (patched to cite p.3 as well).

   printed design rules, the p.3 6.63 um does not: 33 x 160 nm + 2 x 25 nm = 5.33 um (4 x 4, stated 33 periods); 27 x 200 nm +
   2 x 5 nm = 5.41 um (5 x 5); 15 x (250 + 183.7) nm + 2 x 50 nm = 6.61 um (6 x 6, matching both 6.6 and 6.61); 6.63 - 0.01 um is
   not a multiple of 200 nm. Period counts for 5 x 5 / 6 x 6 are not printed, so this check stays in the audit, not in data.
