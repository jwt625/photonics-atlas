## Points checked as asked

- jung2025 Fresnel subtraction (judgment 1): endorsed. p.7 Sec. 2.1 subtracts a 0.15 dB Fresnel loss from all following experimental data as an
  inherent air-polymer property; the term is calculated, not measured, and the text does not say whether it is per coupler or per backloop, so the
  raw transmission cannot be restored. Absolute losses (tir_coupler m1-m3, mtp m3 / m4) rest on that input: author_estimate (rule 4; sun2025
  D428 analogue for a subtracted term). D394 does not apply (there the reflectance is a model input of a fringe fit, here a subtracted loss).
  Differences and spreads cancel a constant offset: mtp m1 0.37 dB derived (difference of two own measurements), m2 0.11 dB derived, std rows
  (tir m4 0.05 dB, mtp m6 0.14 dB) and wdl rows (0.15 / 0.36 dB) measured. jung2026 (merged, same group) keeps the Fresnel reflection inside its
  measured coupler transmission (jung2026 p.10 Methods), so its measured basis is not a precedent against this.
- jung2025 alignment tolerance (judgment / m7): endorsed. Fig. 4(d) re-read at 300 dpi: on-axis point about -0.73 dB, points -0.9 (-1 um),
  -2.05 (-2 um), -1.0 (+1.5 um), -2.65 (+2 um); 1 dB below the on-axis point falls at about -1.72 and +1.72 um. The Gaussian fit (maximum about
  -0.22 dB, about -2.5 dB at +/-2 um) gives about +/-1.3 um. The printed "-1 dB alignment tolerance of 4 um" (p.10) matches neither a half-width
  nor a full width (about 2.6-3.4 um) under either reading, and p.10 ties it to the authors' ref. [25] ("consistent with"). The figure read
  (1.7 um, extracted_from_figure, +/-0.3 um) with 4 um in notes is the D432 treatment (chen2024: figure over a contradicted print); Q2.
- jung2025 fab empty (judgment 3): endorsed. Printing is first-person (p.10, p.15) with no named person or site; authors at Heidelberg University
  (two institutes) and University of Munster (one author at both): D154 / D395 give no unambiguous org (itoh2024, D468). The acknowledgment
  thanks one person for SEM images only; no facility.
- jung2025 classes: TIR coupler `fiber_chip_coupler.other`, platform polymer (jung2026 / yu2023 merged precedent); mode-transfer section as
  `waveguide_platform.transition` with platform other (merged asch2025, dangel2018, fard2021 Si / SiN-to-polymer transitions use other);
  0.12 dB per transition measured (stated directly, D167 analogue), wavelength inferred from the coupler span (no wavelength printed; Supplement A1
  not in the file). MTP row `fiber_interface.connector`, platform empty (D422), 0.37 dB `per_interface` (D441 sutherland2019). Coupler bw_1db
  `> 100 nm` on the coupler row with the package in condition (D163): Table 1 prints ">>100 nm" for the packaged path; the bare coupler's own
  Fig. 3(b) spectrum also stays within 0.15 dB over the whole 100 nm span, so the lower bound at the span edge is measured, not a D435
  extrapolation (contrast jung2026 "> 900 nm" with a printed edge past the data, D459).
- jung2025 reference GC figure reads: Fig. 4(c) at 300 dpi: peak about -4.3 dB near 1549 nm; -5.3 dB crossings near 1535 and 1563 nm; m1 / m2
  confirmed.
- marchetti2017 / zhou2022 loopback_half derived (judgment 5): endorsed. marchetti2017 p.7 Methods states the arithmetic (fiber-to-fiber
  transmission minus the spiral-measured propagation loss, divided by two) and prints the subtracted inputs (alpha per chip, 5.2 mm); zhou2022
  p.3 states the equal split between input and output couplers with the short waveguide neglected. Rule 4 lists halving as derived; cheben2015
  (merged, D441) is the precedent. lorenzen2026 (D452, measured) differs: no arithmetic was stated there. Bandwidth rows measured (read off the

- marchetti2017 fab org_university_of_southampton: endorsed. p.8 author contributions name C.L. as the fabricator, at the University of
