## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. benedikovic2016 row split (judgment a): keep 9 fabricated rows. The linear grating with a 500 um adiabatic taper and the focusing
   gratings (confocal elliptical trenches) with a 40 um taper or a 17 um slab are different physical layouts (rule 9), and the paper
   gives each its own spectrum (Figs. 6-8). The headline -2.7 dB (uniform) / -2.5 dB (apodized) is a statement over three layouts and,
   for -2.5 dB, two apodization designs: notes only (D159). The 3-row alternative would turn layouts into conditions and still leave the
   apodized headline unassignable. The figure reads support the headline (uniform FPR ripple-mean about -2.65 dB; two-step linear about
   -2.5 dB). Fab empty: keep (SEM banner is not a fabrication statement; benedikovic2015 / cheben2015 precedents).
2. benedikovic2016 fringe back-reflection (judgment b): notes only (patched; 9 rows deleted). The estimates are per group, the paper
   derives reflection from ripple magnitude, and Fig. 6(a) shows ripple from about 0.15 to 0.4 dB peak-to-peak inside the linear group,
   so 6 % on the continuously apodized linear row would be a per-device claim the paper does not make (D552 hu2020: group summaries with
   visible device spread are not copied to device rows). This also matches the distiller's own D159 handling of the -2.7 / -2.5 dB
   headline. Alternative if the coordinator prefers to keep data: restore only the 6 curved rows (Figs. 7(a) / 8(a) ripple is similar,
   about 0.15-0.25 dB) and keep the linear 6 % in notes.
3. benedikovic2016 simulated values and design geometry on the linear rows (judgment c): keep the simulated CE / back-reflection and
   the Table geometry (period, n_periods, SWG pitch, fill factor) on the linear rows only: the 2D model describes straight trenches
   and the paper prints no focusing-grating periods. Wafer stack and coupling angle are stated for every fabricated / measured
   structure, so they were added to the six focusing rows (misses).
4. chen2020b (judgment d): keep all three calls. `loopback_half` + `measured` per D452 / D167 (back-to-back pair in Fig. 6(a), no total
   and no arithmetic printed); polarization `both` (P and S traces coincide on the Fig. 7(a) render); no-mirror 4.07 dB `derived` at
   1310 nm `wavelength_inferred` (Fig. 7(a) shows the no-mirror peak at about -4.1 dB near 1308 nm, so 1310 nm and 4.07 dB agree with
   the plot).
5. wang2017b (judgment e): conditions patched to name the 2D curves (vector check, table above). Keep 41 nm (body p.7 and conclusion
   p.9; the abstract's 40 nm in notes). Fab `org_applied_nanotools` correct; CMC not entered (tool access only). Optional, no op:
   `process_name` "Applied Nanotools electron beam lithography" as in merged wang2018c; most merged Applied Nanotools rows leave it empty.
6. zou2015 (judgment f): keep the `reflector.other` design row. The grating reflector is designed and scored as a part of its own
   (Sec. 2.2, Fig. 3, reflectivity > 92 %), like the merged itoh2024a metal reflector and nishiyama2005 bottom DBRs; zhang2014 kept its
   DBR in notes, so the two batches differ, but a sweep is not needed. 3 % back reflectivity: enter on the P_GR 890 nm row (patched). The
   sentence follows the two final designs (-0.88 dB CD 100 nm with P_GR 890 nm, -0.90 dB CD 150 nm), and the distiller already placed the
   2 % on the CD 150 nm row by the same text order; holding back the 3 % only is inconsistent. Strict alternative: both values in notes.
   Losses at 1550 nm inferred: keep (Figs. 2(b) / 4(b) peaks at 1547-1556 nm). Fiber angle 8 deg as printed: keep.
