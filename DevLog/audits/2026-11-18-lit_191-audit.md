## Points checked (ok)

- Crosstalk 24 / 22 dB (p.3 text; Fig. 4 caption "at a wavelength of 1530 nm"): printed -24 / -22 dB stored as positive
  magnitudes (rule 5), `measured`, method direct, drive -0.97 V / 1.01 V in `bias_v` with current and power in condition.
- Crosstalk > 20 dB over 1520-1540 nm (p.3 "In the 20 nm wavelength range around 1530 nm, the CT value remains below -20 dB";
  Fig. 5, eight traces 1-1' / 1-2' / 2-1' / 2-2' on / off, "20 dB" arrow near 1532 nm): qualifier gt, span 1530 +/- 10 nm
  noted as the reading of "around". On the render all leakage traces stay below -20 dB between 1520 and 1540 nm. Fig. 5 is
  normalized to the reference waveguide; crosstalk is a port ratio, so the normalization does not affect it.
- xt_bandwidth_nm 20 (same sentence): stated range, no bound qualifier, consistent with merged yang2010_switch_4x4 m7 (stated
  window, no qualifier). On Fig. 5 most traces stay below -20 dB well beyond 1520-1540 nm, but the paper states only the 20 nm
  window; the value is not re-read from the plot.
- On-chip IL about 3 dB (p.3 "measured on-chip insertion loss (without the coupling loss) was about 3 dB around 1530 nm for
  both states"; "To remove the influence of the grating couplers, the spectra were normalized by a reference waveguide
  fabricated near the switch"): `de_embedded`, ref_plane on_chip, method reference_structure, `measured`, approx, 1530 nm
  (D393). Fig. 5 upper traces sit at about -3 dB near 1530 nm. Includes the five author terms (sum 0.2 + 0.2 + 0.4 + 0.6 +
  1.6 = 3.0 dB).
- Drive power 3.88 / 7.07 mW as power_consumption_w 0.00388 / 0.00707 W `derived` (D256: the W form is not printed; ikeda2020
  m1-m4 precedent). The printed powers are themselves V x I of the printed drive (0.97 V x 4 mA, 1.01 V x 7 mA), so `derived`
  holds on either reading. Not p_pi_mw: each drive moves the mismatch by pi/2 from the pre-biased state (p.2-3).
- 10-90 % times 5.32 / 5.41 ns as rise_time_us 0.00532 / fall_time_us 0.00541 `derived` (D256, ns -> us). Fig. 6 (p.4):
  first edge 1-1' rising with 1-2' falling, marked 5.32 ns; second edge 1-1' falling with 1-2' rising, marked 5.41 ns; matches
  the text "rising (falling) ... falling (rising) ... for 1-1' (1-2')". Drive 50 MHz square wave with about 170 ps edges in
  condition. No wavelength stated for Fig. 6 (registry requires none). Abstract "about 5.4 ns" is the same measurement.
- n_inputs / n_outputs 2 (title p.1, Fig. 1(a) p.2), 340 nm Si (p.2-3, Fig. 3(b) label) and 2 um BOX (p.3) `design_target`
  (SOI wafer, D402).
