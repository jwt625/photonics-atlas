## Rulings on the batch report's judgment calls

- Intrinsic Q basis (lucia2026 vs li2024a). Merged precedent is mixed: 24 derived q_intrinsic rows include several
  computed from loaded Q and transmission without a regime determination (gao2021, wang2021, li2026a, zhang2023a),
  boust2020 (critical coupling assumed) is author_estimate, mckenna2022 (regime set by power-dependent lineshapes) is
  derived. Rule applied to both papers (skill rule 4): Qi from 2 QL / (1 +/- sqrt(T)) is author_estimate unless the
  coupling regime is determined from the data. lucia2026 (p.7-8, "assuming undercoupling" / "assuming overcoupling",
  Fig. 4-5 captions) stays author_estimate, including the 0.24 dB/cm loss computed from that Qi (ng not stated);
  li2024a m2 (p.4-5, undercoupled branch used without a regime statement) -> author_estimate. The merged rows need a
  sweep (question 1).
- lucia2026 C2N platform: facility_org_ids empty endorsed. The registry holds C2N only as a research institute
  (org_centre_de_nanosciences_et_de_nanotechnologies), and the owner of a named facility is not a facility (contract
  v4, D82c); no platform org exists. Reported for the org round (question 2). RENATECH ("partly supported by the
  RENATECH network and the General Council of Essonne", p.9) is a support / network acknowledgement, not a place where
  the work was performed: not a facility even if added (D80c). Fab empty endorsed: fabrication passive (p.2-3), "we
  have therefore implemented racetrack resonators" is not a fabrication statement (D80b).
- dumon2004 racetrack as wdm.mrr plus resonator.ring: endorsed (D396; taddei2019 precedent in the lit_23 audit). The
  paper presents the racetrack as an add-drop filter (add-drop crosstalk 20 / 25 dB, drop loss ~3 dB, p.3) and extracts
  finesse and cavity round-trip loss from the same spectra (p.2-3); finesse and prop_loss_db_per_cm are not allowed for
  wdm, crosstalk_db is not allowed for resonator. Cross-notes present on both rows.

  (p.13 Sec. 4; Fig. 5(b)), would sit on ring_gap550_t3. Proposed `thermal_shift_mhz_per_mw` (resonator, wdm):
  "resonance frequency shift per mW of optical power dropped into the resonator (self-heating), as reported";
  alternative: widen `tuning_efficiency_pm_per_mw` to dropped optical power (would need a MHz -> pm conversion,
  derived, about 1.50 pm/mW at 1560 nm).

  p.6 Sec. 3.2), currently only in labels and notes; needed to draw the gratings. Proposed: "sidewall modulation
  amplitude of a corrugated waveguide grating, w(x) = w0 + dw f(x); the paper's definition (one-sided amplitude vs
  full step) in condition".
