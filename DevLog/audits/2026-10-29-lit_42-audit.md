## Rulings on the batch report's judgment calls

1. cohen2024 D408: applied as above (coordinator ruling). The WSS and filter rows stay: their values are measured on
   the WSS chip in this paper (p.4-7). The Fig. 6 isolation sentence (> 25 dB) carries no citation; the preceding
   grid-alignment sentence and Fig. 7 cite [22] (CLEO); m3 kept under D408 (value sentence not attributed), Fig. 7
   OSA values already not entered.
2. morichetti2021 fab: fixed to empty (coordinator ruling, gentry2015 / lit_24).
3. pommarede2017 fab org_cea: endorsed. p.2 Sec. III: "The fabrication uses the standard platform at CEA [8]. It
   starts with 300 nm-thick SOI wafers on a CMOS 200-mm pilot lines processing tools", followed by the process
   steps. The sentence locates this device's fabrication at CEA (not only a platform name), on the process of the
   authors' own org (5 of 7 authors at CEA LETI; D84a "made with the authors' own process"); org_cea is the org the
   text names (D154 parent level).
4. cohen2024 bend / other classes for the test structures: moot (rows deleted).
5. ding2017 MZI element as switch_fabric.mzi_mesh: endorsed (D158, cell characterized as part of the 4x4 mesh,
   p.4 "a single MZI on this chip"); lu2016a stand-alone 2x2 MZI as switch_fabric.other: endorsed (D158). EO phase
   shifters: ding2017 QCSE rows deleted (only modulator metrics); lu2016a p-i-n keeps p_pi_mw and length; the
   morichetti2021 p-i-n VOA keeps its added-loss vs bias, 80 ns / 400 ns times and length: a VOA is not a modulator
   and its attenuation is its function (phase_shifter.other, D155 analogue).
6. cohen2024 filter width as bw_3db_ghz: endorsed; the Fig. 5 caption calls the same filter width the "3 dB
   linewidth" (p.5); the abstract's FWHM wording is consistent.
7. munk2019 Tower-Jazz: empty fab endorsed (renames are separate orgs; no name-only mapping to
   org_tower_semiconductor).
