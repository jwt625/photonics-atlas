## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. tanaka2009 design-only offset crossings; intro 90 deg values (about 1.0 dB / -12.4 dB, p.2) left out. Recommendation: keep
   as is. The three rows are the paper's own 2-D FDTD results with printed values at their locators. The 90 deg values carry
   no citation and no method, sit in an introduction sentence surrounded by refs. 3-6, and Fig. 2 (20-70 deg) cannot confirm
   them; component notes already hold them. Alternative: a fourth design row "simple 90 deg crossing" with both values
   `author_estimate` and "origin not stated", which adds an unverifiable comparison point.
2. badri2019 return loss (C band < -12 dB, O band up to -7 dB, simple intersection -13.0 dB). Recommendation: keep out, add the
   paper to the registry-candidate list for `back_reflection_db` on `crossing` (now ma2013, shang2017, ni2025a (lit_11, lit_38,
   lit_59 audits) and badri2019, all simulated). D250 deferral still fits (no measured crossing reflection yet); propose the
   widening once a measured value appears.
3. pita2021 coupling efficiency `measured` / `per_coupler` / `reference_structure` vs `author_estimate` + `loss_breakdown`
   (D428). Recommendation: keep `measured` / `reference_structure`. D428 (sun2025) covers a subtracted term of unstated origin
   with no reference structure; here both subtracted terms are the paper's own calibration measurements (-3.65 dB GC,
   1.4 dB/cm, 8-waveguide regression, p.3), and the plotted plateau (about -5 dB, Fig. 2) sits near the -3.9 dB simulation,
   so the subtraction was made even though the feed length and arithmetic are not printed (D167 / D452: no arithmetic shown
   -> `measured`). `includes` now names the taper (patched). Fab `org_applied_nanotools`: keep ("fabricated in a dedicated run
   at Applied Nanotools (ANT) facilities", p.3, names the org; not a shared facility, D404); no `process_name`.
4. xie2024a measured CE scope. Recommendation: `per_coupler` with method `unspecified` (patched), extraction and taper /
   waveguide handling in notes. The paper presents "the coupling efficiency of the GC" and plots it against the per-GC
   simulation (Fig. 4), so the referent is one GC; only the extraction is missing. Merged precedent: hung2020 polysi_gc-m1
   ("extraction from the GC pair not described", per_coupler / unspecified; 75 merged measured GC rows use that pair).
   `scope: unspecified` is for papers that do not say whether a value is per coupler or per pair (ding2022, hu2025a).
   Alternative: keep `unspecified` (revert the scope ops and the two note ops).
5. xie2024a moving-average peaks (-4.5 +/- 0.7, -7.1 +/- 0.77 dB). Recommendation: keep the added rows. They are printed
   measured results, and the paper's own bandwidth boundaries refer to them; without them the only measured CE rows are the
   ripple-inflated raw maxima. Alternative: notes only (delete m17 / m14 adds).
6. xie2024a fab and facility. Recommendation: fab empty (passive "is fabricated", p.5; all authors at CU Boulder; process advice
   from named people is staff thanks, D84b), `facility_org_ids` empty until the Center for Integrated Nanotechnologies is in
   the registry, then `fix_<name>_orgs` sets it on both rows ("This work was performed, in part, at", p.7). Sandia National
   Laboratories (in the registry) and Los Alamos National Laboratory (not in the registry) are the operators named in the
   facility sentence, not facilities (D82c).
