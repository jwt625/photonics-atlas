## Rulings on the batch report's judgment calls

- "3 +/- 0.25 dB" splitting (kim2021 p.5 Sec. III: 3 +/- 0.25 / 0.3 / 0.13 dB, mapped by the authors from the MZI
  extinction; singh2021 p.2 and Abstract: 3 +/- 0.25 dB over 1530-1600 nm, Fig. 3(b)-(c) per-output splitting in dB):
  not covered by imbalance_db. The registry definition is the port ratio |P1/P2| in dB; a +/- deviation of each output
  from the 3 dB level is a different quantity, and turning it into a port ratio needs an assumption (lossless,
  complementary outputs, worst cases at the same wavelength). Kept out; gap confirmed below. kim2021 simulated
  "imbalance of less than +/- 0.18 dB" (p.4) is the same +/- form and stays out too. See question 1 (lit_24 entered a
  similar value as an insertion_loss_db range).
- taddei2019 band unspecified: endorsed. No measurement wavelength anywhere; the 200 THz / 1.5 um figure (p.1) is an
  example in the introduction, and the operating band is not stated (skill rule 8).
- taddei2019 13 dB filter IL scope unspecified: endorsed. p.3 Sec. III prints the 3 dB/facet coupling and the "overall
  insertion loss of the filter" separately without saying whether the facets are inside; the 0.014 dB/cm estimate from
  it stays author_estimate (model curves of Fig. 2, p.3).
- taddei2019 first CROW ring as its own resonator.ring row: endorsed (D396: one physical device characterized in a
  function of another class; finesse and the notch FWHM belong to resonator). Finesse basis corrected (table above).
- tian2018 fabricated-series family row: endorsed (D165 array-level row). It carries the measured S range 25-90%
  (p.7, abstract 1:0.67:1 to 1:18:1) and the lowest IL ~0.2 dB at 1550 nm of an unidentified device (p.3), which belong
  to no single design row (D159); the device-type footprint bound was added there. "Most measured ILs below 1 dB" (p.7)
  stays out (not a bound on all devices).
- tian2018 designed-unequal Port 2 / Port 1 ratios as imbalance_db: endorsed (the definition is a port power ratio;
  ghosh2012 precedent); 3.2 and 12 dB are printed annotations in Fig. 7(b) / (c), so basis measured stands.
- frellsen2016 1.7 dB scope unspecified: endorsed. Halving is stated for the two-mode device only (p.5, p.7); the
  Fig. 6(b) reading favors the unhalved mirrored-pair value, recorded in the note; no enum value fits a two-device pair,
  so no scope change and no halving by the audit.
- teng2009 fab org_cea_leti: endorsed (p.7 "fabrication ... was done by CEA-LETI"). ePIXfab ("through ePIXfab") is an
  MPW access provider and therefore also a fab org (D83d CMC precedent, D398 TSRI); missing from the registry, so it
  goes to the org round; notes updated.
- kim2021: facility National Nanofab Center (p.4 Sec. III "using the fabrication facilities at the National Nanofab
  Center"; p.6 acknowledgement on the fabrication process there) is a facility_org_ids basis (rule 12 "made use of");
  missing from the registry, org round. fab_org_ids empty endorsed (passive wording, GIST / DGIST authors).
