## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. ye2014 laser S21 > 6 GHz and waveguide PD 0.3 A/W / 120 nA (judgment (a); D259 / D408). Recommendation: keep both. D408 removes a
   value only where the value sentence or figure attributes it to the earlier paper. The Fig. 2 caption puts [18] on the "Inset: back-to-back
   eye-diagram" sentence only, and the p.2 S21 sentence has no citation; the Fig. 3 caption ("Measured responsivity and leakage current")
   and the 0.3 A/W / 120 nA sentence carry no citation, while [22] sits on the next sentence (bandwidth > 10 GHz, correctly not entered).
   That [18] and [22] are the authors' own letters on the same device families makes overlap plausible, but [18] / [22] are not cached and
   the cached paper presents both figures as its own measurements (rule 1). Laser notes now state the inset-only citation.
2. ye2014 1x4 MMI 1.54 dB `author_estimate` (judgment (b)). Recommendation: keep `author_estimate` (D76 / D82a: performance of unstated
   design or measured origin) and row_kind fabricated (p.3 "Passive functions such as ... splitters ... were developed" on the platform),
   with the uncertainty in the component notes (patched). Alternative if the coordinator reads "designed ... obtained by optimizing" as
   simulation: basis `simulated`, row_kind unchanged (D80e only if the part exists on the chip; not stated either way).
3. ye2014 surface-normal PD split (judgment (c)). Recommendation: keep two rows. p.3 ties the 0.94 A/W to "the high speed photodiode"
   and the leakage to "a 50 um diameter device"; the capacitance clause is coordinated in the 50 um sentence ("... (0.1 A/cm2) and the
   capacitance was < 0.3 pF"), and < 0.3 pF fits a 50 um mesa (audit parallel-plate estimate, eps_r about 14 assumed: 50 um disk over 2 um about 0.12 pF
   plus pads; an RC limit of about 10.6 GHz at 50 ohm, consistent with the ~6 GHz device). Merging into one row would assume the
   high-speed device is the 50 um one, which the paper does not say (D159).
4. ye2014 hybrid 2.2 dB scope (judgment (d)). Recommendation: `on_chip_excess` (patched), not `device_total`: the value is normalized to a
   straight waveguide (fiber coupling and straight loss removed) and includes the self-referencing MZI, exactly the merged lu2015a pattern
   (on_chip_excess, includes "MZI test circuit"). `device_total` precedents (zhao2022 MUX-DEMUX pair) are values for an assembly of several
   functional parts.
5. nakata2002 GBP 180 GHz (judgment (e)). Recommendation: keep `measured`. p.2 states it as this device's high-M result ("In higher M
   region ... the GB product reached 180GHz") with Fig. 3 drawing the 180 GHz line through this device's points, and the conclusion claims
   it ("applying the dry-etching technique enabled achieving ... a high GB product of 180GHz"); "which was also previously reported [7]"
   compares with the earlier WG-APD, so D80i's "clearly reports it as its own result" exception applies. No arithmetic printed, so
   `measured` (rouvie2008 asymptote precedent).
6. li2015 Rj and process_name (judgment (f)). Recommendation: keep both. Rj sits in series with Cj in the Fig. 3(b) inset circuit (one
   branch R_j - C_j, parallel to C_p and R_o - C_o), so it is a series resistance in the registry sense (definition: method in notes);
   check: 1 / (2 pi (50 + 32) ohm x (40.5 + 14) fF) = 36 GHz, matching the printed ~37 GHz electrical bandwidth at 1 V. process_name
   "standard 0.18 um CMOS" is the printed "standard 0.18 um-CMOS foundry" minus the word foundry: it names a node (Contract v7, D536;
   merged precedent "0.18 um standard CMOS processes" with SMIC). Verbatim "standard 0.18 um-CMOS foundry" would also be fine; no op.
7. duan2012 platform, fab and figure-read biases; hayashi1998 biases (judgment (g)). Recommendations: duan2012 platform `other` (kang2008 /
   kang2008a vertical Ge/Si APD rows; light is not guided and the SOI layer is only the n contact; the fard2017 `soi` rows are lateral Si
   PDs in the SOI layer itself); fab empty ("we report a Ge/Si APD fabricated using the SEG approach" plus passive steps names no
