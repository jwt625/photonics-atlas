## Coordinator questions (rulings on the distiller's 1-4 and orgs)

1. EO comb class. Recommendation: keep `laser.comb` (subtype described as "comb source"; Kerr microcombs, which are not
   lasers either, already sit there: ji2024, ye2019, girardi2025) with the `eo_comb` tag the batch already sets on
   zhang2019b, hu2022, ling2024 and wang2025a. Registry round: widen the `comb` description to "comb source (comb laser,
   Kerr microcomb, EO comb generator)". No new subtype: `frequency_converter` would split comb rows by mechanism and the
   comb parameters repetition_rate_ghz and pump_to_comb_efficiency_pct are laser-only (channel_spacing_ghz: wdm and laser).
2. wang2025a 6.5 dB fibre-to-fibre loss. Recommendation: enter on the comb row as `insertion_loss_db`,
   `fiber_to_fiber`, measured (patch m7). Rule 2 keeps modulator metrics out to avoid duplicating EO-atlas rows;
   `lit:wang2025a` has no EO-atlas source, so nothing is duplicated, and the paper's Table 1 (p.8) reports it as a comb
   generator figure beside, not as, the PM Vpi. Its per-facet and per-cm terms are already entered and the total
   anchors them. To keep the distiller's call, drop the add op and the comb notes op.
3. zhang2024c overall WPE "beyond 18.8 %" (p.4; abstract 19 %). Recommendation: leave out (agree). It spans the SIL
   microcomb, its pump and 34 injection-locked DFB outputs (Fig. 2(g) dashed line); no component row holds that
   system, and a parallel-source row would be built for one value. If the coordinator wants it, a `laser.comb` system
   row with `wpe_pct` 18.8, qualifier gt, n_channels 34.
4. hu2022 on-chip efficiency / power (30 %, 32 %, 20 mW). Recommendation: keep `measured` with ref_plane on_chip
   (agree). The paper states that both spectra show on-chip power after taking the different in- and out-coupling facet
   losses into account (p.3 Fig. 2(c) caption); no assumed or cited input is named, and D167 treats directly stated own
   loss values as measured. Unlike li2025a (D462) the paper never calls the facet loss assumed. Consequence applied in
   the patch: ling2024 8.5 mW "on chip" moves to `measured` for the same reason.
5. D167 vs rule 4 for coupling losses. The patch makes directly stated per-facet / interface losses `measured`
   (helgason2023 ~2 dB, ling2024 ~6 dB x 2) and keeps wang2025a 1.0-1.25 dB/facet and 0.27 dB/cm `author_estimate`
   because they are printed as the breakdown of a stated total. Confirm this split.
6. Fab / facility orgs (D80b). Rulings: zhang2019b, hu2022, ling2024, helgason2023 fab orgs as entered (named
   fabricating authors, unambiguous org); wang2025a empty (passive "Our TFLN PICs are fabricated", authors at two
   orgs; org_liobate exists in the registry but the paper does not say Liobate fabricated); zhang2024c empty
   (unnamed commercial CMOS foundry; DFB sources not stated). NANOLN (registry org_jinan_jingzheng_electronics) stays a
   wafer-supplier note.
