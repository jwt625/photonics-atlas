## Rulings on the distiller's questions

1. shim2026 Supp. Sec. 7 Q values. Enter on the Device B row (patched, 6 rows). D458: D159 needs two candidate rows;
   Device A has none (it serves only the thermoreflectance study, p.21 Supp. Sec. 6), the optical measurements use
   Device B (p.5), and the coupler that sets the high ER was designed for the 7.1 mm octagonal round trip (p.18 Supp.
   Sec. 5). Q_i rows author_estimate (D403). Fallback (keep the distiller's call): drop m6-m11 and the notes op.
2. shim2026 p_pi_mw = 4 x P_pi/4. Accept as `derived`. The paper defines the phase as linear in Delta n_eff over L_h
   (p.5 Eq. (5)) and fits Delta n_eff linearly in P; every P_pi (183-474 mW) lies inside the measured 0-620 mW range
   (Figs. S13 / S14), so D435 is not triggered. No `p_pi_over_4` parameter needed.
3. wu2023a route extinction and 0.15 dB per unit. Keep on the switch_fabric row. `extinction_db` covers switch on/off
   extinction; the values are bar / cross route states of MZI unit (5) of the mesh (p.4 Fig. 3(b)), and the 0.15 dB is
   a passive budget of DCs plus waveguide (p.3) with no modulator-specific term. V_pi, rise / fall time, capacitance and
   energy stay out (rule 2; the paper has no EO-atlas row, so they remain in the batch report only).
4. BRIGHT Photonics (gatkine2022 p.8 Acknowledgments). Note only, not a fab org under D398. The acknowledgment credits
   BRIGHT Photonics and Ligentec jointly for design and fabrication help on "one of the chips" tested, without saying
   which; the body names Ligentec as the fabricator of this AWG (p.3), already on the rows. D398 needs the provider
   tied to this component's fabrication run; here the chip identity is ambiguous (D159 spirit). Registering the org is
   optional (company, design house / MPW access) and not needed for this batch; the component notes already carry it.
5. hu2021b two-resonator q_intrinsic. Keep `author_estimate` (D403). The four-parameter fit of the bus transmission
   (p.11) cannot separate the over- and under-coupled solutions by itself, and no phase or ring-down data are shown;
   the strongly over-coupled statement (p.3) is the authors' design claim. Unlike ji2024 (D465), no data-side regime
   evidence is printed. gamma also stays author_estimate; the loaded linewidth kappa stays measured.
6. li2019 arXiv v1. Accept as the same work (D433 / D462); journal version unread, re-check values if it is cached.
