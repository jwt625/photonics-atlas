## Coordinator questions

1. park2007 5 dB facet loss and the gain / Psat / NF basis (distiller judgment). The paper says the coupling efficiency "is measured to be
   -5 dB from the measured insertion loss of 20 dB at long wavelengths" but never states the remaining 10 dB in-device term. Audit
   arithmetic (not data): the cited 15 cm-1 waveguide loss of ref. [3] over 1.36 mm gives about 8.9 dB, so the split plausibly rests on
   that cited value (rule 3 / rule 4 "cited input"). D167 covers a per-facet loss stated directly without shown arithmetic; here the
   arithmetic is half shown and its second term is unstated, which is the D437 theurer2019 case (value not tied to an isolating
   measurement; dependents follow). Recommendation: keep facet m1 `author_estimate` / `loss_breakdown` and the chip-referred rows (m1 13
   dB, m3 11 dBm, m7 5-8 dB, and m5 after this patch) `author_estimate`; m3 also lies past its data (D435). Alternative (D166 / D167):
   facet m1 `measured`, m1 / m5 / m7 `derived`, m3 stays `author_estimate` (D435). m8 7.5 ohm `author_estimate` matches ma2024 m15 (D576);
   most merged series-resistance rows are `measured`, so `measured` (D167 analogue) is the other defensible reading: no op.
2. zhang2019c fab and loss budget. (a) Fab: "Y. Z. and Q. D. fabricated the isolator devices" (p.20): org_mit is the org common to both
   (D452 chang2026). "C. W. and T. F. deposited and characterized the magneto-optical thin films": C. W. is UESTC-only, and the garnet
   deposition is a process step on the devices, which under D80b (fabricators at two orgs) would add `org_uestc`; but the sentence is
   separate from the device fabrication sentence, may refer to the characterization films (Supp. Fig. S2), and names no site.
   Recommendation: keep org_mit only, with the existing note. Alternative: add `org_uestc` to `fab_org_ids` on all 8 rows. CMSE facility
   stays a missing org (also D535). (b) Loss-budget rows (MMI 0.7, junction 0.25, garnet 2.2-3.2, bare Si 0.36 dB): keep
   `author_estimate` / `loss_breakdown` (origin "parameterized in Supplementary Section 7", not cached; no measurement shown; D428 /
   D544 precedents). `wavelength_inferred` 1574.5 nm is fine (the budget explains the IL across the 10-dB band around that point).
3. yu2025a 41.4 % vs 44.44 % vs 49 %. Fig. 3 shows 41.4 % is the fit at the operating pump; Table S2 is the measured series (best 44.44
   %); 49 % is the fit maximum beyond the data. Recommendation: keep all three as entered (m1 `measured` fit inside range, D80a; m2
   `best` from Table S2; m3 `author_estimate`, D435), with the D417 note on the "up to 41.4 %" wording. Alternative: drop m2 (single
   table point) and keep only the printed operating value.
4. zhou2024 converter with no row. The only converter outputs are 0.7 mW MIR after the filter, the 2.4-4.2 um span and 0.87 % RIN; no
   efficiency is printed, and a computed 0.7 / 150 mW would mix reference planes the paper does not define. Recommendation: no converter
   row (rule 9; registry gaps as reported); keep the facet row with 60 % `measured` (D167) and 15 um width.
5. yang2020a reflectivity basis (new, patched). The paper never prints "measured 56 %" in words, but Supp. Fig. S1 measures every
   reflector by FP finesse and both labels match the measured points while the simulated trajectories end near 0.6 / 0.99.
   Recommendation: accept the patch (`measured`). Alternative: `extracted_from_figure` would be wrong (the values are printed labels).
