## Coordinator questions

1. lu2018 Q_i / losses `author_estimate`. Recommendation: keep (no op). 638 nm ring: the undercoupled regime is asserted for this
   ring (p.10) but not shown from its data (the gap-series argument on p.9-10 is for the UV rings), so Q_i > 170,000 and 5.3 dB/cm
   rest on an assumed regime (D403). UV ring: 75 dB/cm uses the loaded Q as the loss-limited Q plus the stated assumption that
   sidewall scattering and absorption dominate (p.10; Table 1 footnote "loss estimated from resonator Q factor"); the gap series
   shows undercoupling but not that coupling loss is negligible, so `author_estimate` is the safer basis. Either way the benchmark
   is unaffected (not telecom, platform `other`).
2. lu2018 edge coupler ">= 40 %" as `coupling_efficiency_pct` scope `device_total`. Recommendation: keep (no op). The value is a
   facet-to-facet chip transmission (two inverse tapers, waveguide, stitching scatter; coupling optics not stated for the value)
   that the paper itself calls not an accurate edge-coupling estimate; `device_total` keeps it out of the per-coupler / per-facet
   benchmark scopes, and halving to a per-facet number would be the batch's own estimate. The simulated grating peaks read off
   Fig. 3 stay `simulated` (D454); the 24 % red value is corrected above.
3. lu2018 directional coupler 50/50 at 14 um as one coupling-length series row (D165), basis `measured`. Recommendation: keep. No
   device at 14 um appears in Fig. 5(d) (points every 2.5 um); linear interpolation of the plotted port-1 fractions crosses 0.5 at
   about 13.3 um, and the authors state 14 um as the experimental result matched by the full-structure simulation (p.12). The
   distiller's note records the interpolation; `author_estimate` would be the alternative if the coordinator wants interpolated
   claims without a data point downgraded. Fab empty on all lu2018 rows (D154) endorsed.
