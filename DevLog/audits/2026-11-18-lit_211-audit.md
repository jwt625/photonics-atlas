## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. suzuki2018 fab AIST (judgment (a)). Recommendation: keep `org_national_institute_of_advanced_industrial_science_and_technology`.
   The decisive evidence is the p.1 footnote "The device fabrication was supported by TIA Super Clean-Room of the National
   Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology", a stated place of fabrication (D448), as in hatori2014 where a
   multi-org "we fabricated" plus the TIA-SCR acknowledgment kept AIST; the first-person "our ... pilot line" (D84a) adds to it.
   The one Furukawa Electric co-author (p.1) does not weaken this, since the footnote names the place independently of "our".
   Note that the gold electrodes were not made in the pilot line (p.2 Fig. 1 caption), already in notes. TIA Super Clean-Room:
   still missing from the registry (facility, p.1 footnote; same gap as hatori2014, ikeda2020, konoike2020 / 2022); org round.
2. guo2017a SE class (judgment (b)). Recommendation: keep `switch_fabric.other`. Besides D158, D407 covers it directly: a
   ring-assisted, wavelength-selective switch element is `switch_fabric.other` whether or not it sits inside the matrix, and
   Fig. 10 is explicitly a "stand-alone SE" (p.7). The per-SE 1.5 dB from the fabric sits on the same physical design (rule 9).
3. guo2017a EO powers as `p_pi_mw` (judgment (c)). Recommendation: keep. The paper ties the EO tuning of one MRR to the pi
   arm-phase difference that switches the element (p.2 Sec. II-A), `p_pi_mw` is allowed on switch_fabric, and the merged
   suzuki2020 / campenhout2009 switch rows carry switching power the same way. `power_consumption_w` would lose the per-element
   pi meaning. The PIN carrier-injection mechanism is already in the condition.
4. guo2017a 10.6 dB and 1.5 dB (judgment (d)). Recommendation: keep `author_estimate`. The 5.6 dB tap term has no stated
   origin; the only related number printed is the designed 1:10 tap ratio (p.3), so it reads as a design-based budget term,
   not a measurement (rule 4). Fab `org_astar_ime`: keep; "carried out using CMOS compatible processes in IME Singapore" (p.4) is a
   passive sentence that names the org (Contract v7).
5. suzuki2018 on-chip 6.1 dB (judgment (e)). Recommendation: keep `author_estimate` + `loss_breakdown`, 1.4 dB/facet not
   entered. The paper frames 1.4 dB/facet as an assumption (p.4 "we assumed"), matching the lower end of the ref. [10] range
   (p.1); the 6.1 dB is the own 8.9 dB measurement minus that assumed term, which is the D453 pattern (huang2019b,
   konoike2022), not a cited value; suzuki2020 (merged) has the same 3.7 dB on-chip value as `derived` only because its facet
   loss was measured.
6. Timing and power values (judgment (f)). Recommendations: keep all three. shokraneh2020 21 mW `derived` holds: the Fig. 7(a)
   I-V gives about 11 mA at the about 1.9 V first null of Fig. 7(b), so the authors' number rests on their own measurements;
   placement on the 2 x 2 row is acceptable (a move to the heater row is equally defensible; no op). "< 30 us thermal
   stabilization" as `rise_time_us` lt with the definition note matches roeloffzen2018 / suzuki2020 (`thermal_time_constant_us`
   is a first-order constant, not a settling time). suzuki2018 "~30 kHz" `bw3db_khz` `author_estimate` matches gui2021 /
   velha2016 (criterion and method not stated).
7. shokraneh2020 DC row and orgs, liu2017 (judgment (g)). Recommendations: keep the DC as `fabricated` with the simulated 50 %
   split (D80e; the DC is in every fabricated MZI, Fig. 5 / Fig. 8 inset). CMC is the MPW broker, not the fab: "MPW service
   managed by CMC" (p.5) and "subsidized MPW fabrication through A*STAR Institute of Microelectronics" (p.10); Contract v7
   lists CMC among brokers kept as fab orgs next to the foundry (D83d, D398; the benchmark drops brokers as columns, D900), so
   `org_astar_ime;org_cmc_microsystems` stays. liu2017 `wdm.other`, `row_kind: design`, platform `soi`: keep.
8. shokraneh2020 "-35 dB crosstalk" (new). Recommendation: delete (patched). The value is the absolute normalized leakage
   level of Fig. 11, read against the transmitted level it gives about 29-37 dB depending on path and wavelength. Keeping it
   would also contradict this batch's suzuki2018 treatment of "leakages less than -30 dB" (not entered). A per-path figure
   read of the ratio (about 29 dB for I4-O2) is not recommended: path-specific and not printed. Revert = drop the delete op.
