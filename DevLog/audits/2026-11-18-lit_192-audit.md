## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. chang2020 metalens, fab / facility, C band, rings (judgment (a)). Recommendation: keep as distilled. `free_space_emitter.metasurface`
   follows he2026 exactly (a-Si metasurface on fused silica held over a PIC, steering by emitter selection, platform `other`);
   the `micro_optics.metalens` rows (vocht2024, wang2023c, kim2023) are fiber / laser-facet optics, not steering elements.
   The 68 % focusing efficiency as `coupling_efficiency_pct` per_component, method unspecified (definition not printed) follows
   vocht2024. Fab Columbia (first person, p.7; miller2019), facility CUNY ASRC (p.9). C band inferred is supported by Fig. 2(b)
   and Fig. 5(a) (1557.5-1561.7 nm). Emitter + `resonator.ring` split per D396 with cross-notes is right; the 30 dB on / off
   extinction belongs on the ring row because the registry does not allow extinction_db on free_space_emitter.
2. yokota2022 0 rows (judgment (b)). Recommendation: accept 0 rows. Both reported results (2.0 kHz, sweep linearity) are
   properties of the DFB plus a discrete bulk FP etalon and lens in free space: D411 (bulk resonator out) and D642
   (fontaine2019 bench assembly out) apply, and D558 removed a value that needed an off-chip bulk lens. The solitary DFB chip
   has no printed value (threshold only as the "10 times" operating condition; Table 1 193 mA belongs to the simulated laser
   of ref. [51]). Record the ruling with the listed values as the decision.
3. hu2017 linewidth (judgment (c)). Recommendation: keep the row (< 130 kHz, `lt`, derived for kHz -> Hz) with the patched
   condition. The distiller's "entered as printed, not converted" is right, but the printed definition is contradicted by
   Fig. 6(b): the -20 dB markers span about 2.3 MHz, so 130 kHz is the 20-dB width / 20 (Lorentzian-equivalent, the
   convention mao2024 prints), not a 20-dB width. The abstract and conclusion call it simply the linewidth. Alternative:
   delete to notes as definition-ambiguous (D159 analogue); not recommended, because the figure settles the reading.
4. zhang2014a facet halves (judgment (d)). Recommendation: keep `loopback_half`, `derived`, method `cutback` (du2024 / D608).
   The Fig. 4 caption itself assigns the intercept to "the coupling loss with lensed fiber (both facets)", so the values are
   the paper's own; the 0.05-0.47 dB intercepts are inside the plotted scatter (circles about +/-0.3 dB at 2.8 / 3.8 cm), and the
   facet note says so. Views that rank edge couplers should treat these as fit-limited (no exclusion needed in data).
5. zhang2014a mirror basis and fab (judgment (e)). Recommendation: keep `author_estimate` (absolute loss rests on the nominal
   "around 1.1 A/W" responsivity, and the paper attributes the spectral shape to that responsivity: rule 4 assumed / nominal
   input, D462 / D520 analogue); pita2021 (D639) does not apply, because there the reference was the authors' own calibration
   measurement, here it is a nominal datasheet-like number. The L2-L4 figure reads (+/-0.03 dB) are noted on the rows. Fab
   empty (passive voice, all authors HHI; D80b), ChemOptics material note only (D428 / D431).
6. acoleyen2011a theta rows and efficiency (judgment (f)). Recommendation: accept the patch moving the three measured
   theta-direction rows to the order-150 scanner, the device they were measured on; the grating row keeps its simulated
   efficiencies, the simulated 63 deg envelope and the geometry (acoleyen2009 split). Revert = drop the 2 component ops, the 3
   deletes and the 3 adds. The 10-13 % input-waveguide-to-free-space efficiency stays a note: the sentence names no scanner
   (the context is the phi = 23 deg case of the Fig. 5(b) simulation), so D159 sample identity applies (D254 cited by the
   distiller fits less well; the outcome is the same). Fab org_imec and the ePIXfab process_name follow D616.
7. chang2020 steering power (judgment (g)). Recommendation: keep 83 / 56 mW on the switch-network row. The paper defines
   P_total as the switches plus the ring microheaters (p.5, Eq. (1)), and the switch-network row label includes the row
   heaters, so the row is the electrical consumer. Registry candidate (also miller2019): power_consumption_w on
   free_space_emitter, after which the two rows could move to the beam-steerer row.
