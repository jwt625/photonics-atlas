## Coordinator questions

1. shu2026 PZT tuner pm/V and rise / fall times vs rule 2 (distiller judgment 1). The EO atlas holds ring-modulator tuning in its
   `tuning_nm_per_v` column, and the same MRRs serve as the transmitter's modulators (p.10). But rule 2 lists the metrics that stay
   in the EO atlas (Vpi, EO bandwidth, modulator IL / ER, VpiL); `tuning_efficiency_pm_per_v` is defined for "Pockels bias" on
   phase_shifter / resonator and `rise_time_us` / `fall_time_us` on phase_shifter / switch_fabric; shu2026 is not an EO-atlas source,
   so there is no host row to duplicate; the paper presents the values as switch-element speed and trimming-tuner efficiency.
   Precedents keep such values: poulton2019 EO phase shifter (D582), kim2026 EO-tuner pm/V on a coupled-ring row; dostart2020a
   (lit_135) kept its GHz/V in conditions only. Recommendation: keep both on the tuner row (no op). Put shu2026 on the EO-atlas candidate list (PZT MRR modulator: 40 Gb/s
   eyes, ER 3.1-5.8 dB, 17.7 pm/V); if the EO atlas ingests it, move the pm/V there and link `host_device_ids`. Caveat for any
   speed ranking: the 45 / 37 ps edges were measured through an EDFA, APD and 80 GSa/s oscilloscope, and the paper gives no
   instrument-limit statement.
2. shu2026 WSS and weight-matrix values as conditions on the one `switch_fabric.other` row (distiller judgment 2). Recommendation:
   keep. The WSS experiment uses a tunable laser into the crossbar itself (p.8 Fig. 4 caption), without the 1 x N demultiplexer of
   the Fig. 4(a) architecture, so it is the same physical device in another configuration (rule 9); D396 splits rows by class, not
   by subtype. `switch_fabric.wss` would suit a separate WSS device, which the paper does not build.
3. shu2026 missing org "Hangzhou Institute for Advanced Study, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences" (p.1 affiliation 2; fab
   role through H.Y. in the p.18 fabrication sentence). Recommendation: an org-round entry, then a fab fix on all 3 shu2026 rows. Org
   choice: own org id as a child of `org_university_of_chinese_academy_of_sciences` if an exact ROR record exists at the org round;
   otherwise an alias of the parent under the D332 host-named-in-string rule ("Jinhua Institute of Zhejiang University" ->
   `org_zhejiang_university` precedent). Not patched here (Contract v7: missing orgs go to the report, never a new id).
4. fard2017 CMC acknowledged for "the contributions and technical support" (p.12). Recommendation: not a fab org (no op). D398 needs
   fabrication support or MPW access in the acknowledgement (dumais2016 "silicon photonics fabrication support", anthony2019
   "facilitating device fabrication"); the exact wording here was ruled out for simard2015 ("contribution and technical support",
   merged rows fab `org_university_washington` only), and eid2016 / tamura2026 likewise. The paper names IME as the fabricator.
5. sun2018 headline 0.8 mA/cm2 and device C 0.76 vs 0.79 A/W. Recommendation: keep the 0.8 mA/cm2 out (D159). The enlarged Fig. 4
   crop shows a two-colour circle (blue upper half, magenta lower half) spanning the device C and E curves, with a magenta label;
   the two curves differ by about 2x at the circle (roughly 8 and 4 nA at -2.5 V on the render), so no single device carries the
   value, and neither rough reading gives 0.8 mA/cm2 over a 30 um mesa (about 1.1 and 0.6 mA/cm2). The abstract / Table 2 attribute it to "this work" generally.
   Keep C at 0.76 A/W: the Fig. 6 labels are device-resolved by arrow colour, the text gives one value for the pair (D417 note in
   place). Keep the B 1.6 uA/cm2 row with its conflict note.
6. sun2018 responsivity rows on the 30 um siblings (audit). Recommendation: keep there with the new diameter note. The device
   letters fix the epi and ARC design, which the paper uses to explain the responsivity spread (p.5); moving the values to a
   diameter-free row would orphan them. Alternative: treat the diameter as unknown under D159 and drop the five rows.
7. poot2014 Vpi ~10 V `author_estimate`. Recommendation: keep. The 125 / 175 nm data stop at -6 V (about 92 deg); 10 V is the
   quadratic fit (-60 mrad/V2: pi at about 10.2 V) extended beyond the data, which is D435. Not an EO metric (electrostatic MEMS),
   so rule 2 does not apply.
