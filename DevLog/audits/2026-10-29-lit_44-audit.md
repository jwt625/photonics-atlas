## Rulings on the batch report's judgment calls

- hinumwagner2025 propagation loss "0.8 dm cm-1 to 1.2 dm cm-1" (p.4): endorsed as not entered. The unit is not
  printed as dB/cm anywhere else for this value (no other dB/cm string in the text); never correct a printed unit by
  guess. prop_loss_db_per_cm is allowed for resonator, so a dB/cm reading would otherwise qualify.
- hinumwagner2025 Q 4.2e5 as q_loaded with note: endorsed. p.9 "a Q factor of about 4.2e5 was calculated" from the
  spectrum; 852.837 nm / 2.0 pm = 4.26e5 is lambda / FWHM; no coupling-regime extraction is stated anywhere (Fig. 12 /
  13 captions call all measured Q "intrinsic" with the same method). Unqualified-method Q -> q_loaded (skill pitfall);
  q_intrinsic would need a regime (D403).
- hinumwagner2025 Fig. 9 FSR 1.2 nm vs Figs. 2 / 16: endorsed as entered with note. The Fig. 9(a) render shows dips
  spaced about 1.1 nm, consistent with the annotation; the conflict is with the caption geometry (R 80 um gives about
  0.7 nm by Eq. (1), Fig. 16(a) 0.68 nm), i.e. the printed value is unambiguous for the plotted device and the doubt
  sits on the caption, not on the sample (D159 not triggered).
- hinumwagner2025 LPCVD-1 / LPCVD-2 merged for the 12 dB row: moot, row removed (see findings).
- hinumwagner2025 fab empty: endorsed ("we fabricate full 200 mm wafers", p.2; authors at TU Graz and ams-OSRAM Group,
  several dual-affiliated; facility unnamed in the p.18 staff thanks; D83c / D72 unambiguity). Upper cladding (Table 1
  2.4 um vs p.8 1.6 um) not entered: endorsed. core_thickness design_target (selected 250 nm, p.3) vs box measured
  (only the post-CMP ellipsometry value, Table 1): acceptable.
- liu2026 splitter.adiabatic: endorsed (tapered SWG coupler with mode evolution, p.2-4). Measured excess 0.94 dB from
  the cascade-slope regression `measured` (D80a standard fit; p.8 Eq. (2)-(3)): endorsed. Fig. 5(b) / 5(c) readings
  verified on the render (3-dB upper 0.46-0.52; Gu/Gb 5 / 6 / 7 at 1550 nm about 0.71 / 0.79 / 0.83; spans as
  entered). Simulated 80 / 85 / 90 % entered as printed while Fig. 3(c) reads about 0.88 / 0.93 / 0.955: endorsed
  (the text ties each ratio to its Gu/Gb design at 1550 nm, so the sample is unambiguous; D159 not triggered; note
  records the plot reading). Band average "approximately 0.94 dB, variations below +/- 0.1 dB" not entered: endorsed
  (Fig. 5(d) spans about 0.2-0.95 dB). Fab empty (unnamed commercial 300 mm line): endorsed.
- tebeneva2024: kept in scope by the distill; overruled by D411 (bulk confirmed at p.4-5 Sec. 3). Rows deleted.
