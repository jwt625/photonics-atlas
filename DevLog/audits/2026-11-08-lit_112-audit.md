## Coordinator questions

1. lu2015a `bw_1db_nm` (10 rows) holds the paper's "+/- 1 dB bandwidth": the span where the extracted cross ratio stays within +/- 1 dB of its
   design value (p.9; for 50/50 a 39.8-63.1 % window), not a 1-dB-from-peak width. Recommendation: keep with the definition in condition (paper's
   own name; merged ma2000 stores an excess-loss-criterion width the same way) and move the rows to a criterion bandwidth parameter when the
   registry extension adds one (gap 1). Alternative: delete to notes now (D401 style).
2. Class of Bragg-mirror Fabry-Perot cavities: saber2017 (14) and zhang2015 (1) are `resonator.bragg`. Merged precedent is split: chowdhury2024
   WBG FP cavity, saghaei2026 DSBR cavities and spencer2015 QWS grating are `resonator.bragg` (chowdhury2024 endorsed in the lit_35 audit),
   while liu2018 Bragg-mirror FP cavities were moved to `resonator.photonic_crystal` in the lit_07 audit, whose subtype text names Fabry-Perot
   cavities. Recommendation: keep `resonator.bragg` here (sidewall waveguide Bragg gratings as mirrors, closest to chowdhury2024 / spencer2015)
   and record the rule: waveguide-Bragg-grating mirrors -> `resonator.bragg`; PhC, loop or other mirrors -> `resonator.photonic_crystal`;
   liu2018 to re-check under that rule.
3. halir2016 fab `org_national_research_council_canada` (4 rows): no fabrication site is named; p.6 thanks an NRC staff member for preparing the
   e-beam lithography files. Recommendation: keep as D84b borderline with the note (staff help with a step of device fabrication at that org;
   NRC co-authors). Alternative: empty (cheben2015: passive fabrication sentence, authors at two orgs; file preparation is not processing).
4. guan2017 Y-junction < 0.1 dB (p.6) is attached to "an improved design of our previous work [28]". Recommendation: keep as `author_estimate`
   (the sentence assigns the value to the improved design, not to ref. 28; no measurement shown). Delete under D259 only if the value is read
   as ref. 28's.
5. CMRR: D81 rejected `cmrr` as system-level. guan2017 measures it at the hybrid's own four outputs with on-chip PDs (22-die mean > 31 dB,
   1-sigma worst case > 28 dB, single die > 30 dB, simulated 30 dB; p.7, p.9-10), a component property like the merged `imbalance_db` /
   `phase_error_deg` rows. Recommendation: add `cmrr_db` for `splitter.hybrid_90` at the next registry extension; the earlier skipped value (lit_24
   oka2023 > 28 dBe, an electrical-domain figure) needs its own check before it is entered.
6. zhang2015 fab `org_cmc_microsystems` on "enabling the fabrication of the device" (p.4), no foundry named (2 rows). Recommendation: keep
   (D398 MPW-access fab org; merged pita2023 "assistance with the fabrication" and OFC Tu2J.5 "fabrication supported by CMC"); the benchmark
   drops brokers as columns (D900), so the rows carry no foundry column either way.
