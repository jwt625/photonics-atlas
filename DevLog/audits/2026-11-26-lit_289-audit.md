   reported there. The 0.13 dB sentence continues that measurement set (D80i borderline, same wafer and cutback).
   Alternative not recommended: add 0.13 dB as measured on beol_trans_si_sin1.

   not a value attribution (D408). Caveat for the record: Sec. II says the [39] platform "integrated ... TiN thermo-optic
   tuners"; whether [39] printed a P_pi cannot be checked ([39] not cached).
