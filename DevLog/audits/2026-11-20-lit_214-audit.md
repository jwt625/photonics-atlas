   flagged in the notes. Dropping the row would extend D259a to inferred reuse, which no ruling covers.

   21 % (77 K), below the printed values (table above). The 6 K argument for the distiller's attribution only shows that Fig. 13 plots I-V at
   80-400 K, not that the 5e18 device was never measured lower. Revert = drop the two delete_metrics.

   coordinator prefers one row per physical device across papers, the shared-figure rows (lu1996 m2-m6, m9) are the candidates; no
   precedent requires it (D607 covers a preprint of the same paper only).
