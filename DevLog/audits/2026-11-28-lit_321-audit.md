   total mode-overlap transmission into both outputs (Eq. (7), Fig. 4 caption), i.e. loss beyond the ideal 3 dB split, which
   is the registry `excess_loss_db` (D43); the source sign is in notes. No fabrication, so design row (rule 9).
3. ikeda2008 (judgment c). **(a) Butt facet 5 dB: keep `derived`.** The paper prints "coupling loss of 5dB (see Fig. 1(c))"
   and the Fig. 1(c) intercept is 10.241 dB, so the per-facet value is the authors' halving of their own cutback intercept
   (D80a: halving is derived whoever did it); D167 (`measured`) is for per-facet values with no traceable arithmetic. **(b)
   Inverse taper 2.5 dB: keep `author_estimate`** ("improved coupling loss of 2.5dB from fiber tip to the SiN waveguide", an
   n2-estimate input with no measurement described; D418 / D651, as xue2015 lit_299 Q3). **(c) Ring alpha 12 / 16 dB/cm: keep
   `author_estimate`** (single-spectrum fits; the paper itself says the values include bending loss and uses 4 dB/cm instead
   in its model, D403 / D454); n_g stays `measured`. **(d) Kappa: add** (patched). Eq. (1) defines kappa as a power coupling

   kappa (M = 1.15 literal); the swapped-back form gives M = 11.5 vs printed 11.4, so 0.9 % is also added, separable: drop
   allpass-m8 and its note edit if the coordinator does not accept a typo correction (D156 spirit), keep the 0.6 % row. **(e)
   Fab UCSD: keep** (first-person fabrication sentences, all authors in one UCSD department).
