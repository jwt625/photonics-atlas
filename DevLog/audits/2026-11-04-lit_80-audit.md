## Open questions for the coordinator

1. srinivasan2020 1550 nm RF rows. Recommended as patched: delete m12, m14, m16. The caption is a figure attribution, which is the D408 trigger, and the title of ref. [14] prints the same 32 GHz and gain > 8. The 1310 nm rows stay: 27 GHz and gain 11 are this paper's title and abstract result, and ref. [14]'s title does not carry them. Table I and Fig. 3(b) 1550 nm responsivities stay (no attribution). Weak point: p.4 says the measurement was performed at both wavelengths with no citation, and the caption could also be read as covering panels (a) and (b) together. If overruled, drop the three delete ops and the lsacm_apd note op.
2. Fab consistency, shahin2025 and akritidis2026. Recommended as patched: org_imec on both. akritidis2026 is the clear case ("at imec", p.6). shahin2025 p.2 ("fabricated on imec's 300-mm" platform) has the wording of merged deng2026a (p.2, also imec plus Ghent University-imec authors), elsaeed2024, song2024a, OFC Th3F.2 and M1E.6, all org_imec. D84a covers an own "our platform"; szelag2017 (D433) has only a platform description ("has been widely described", p.1) with no sentence fabricating the device on it. Alternative if D433 is read as covering any passive sentence: drop the shahin2025 op (empty), keep akritidis2026; then the merged imec rows above are a sweep candidate. srinivasan2020 org_imec is not in question (acknowledgment names fabrication).
3. vines2019 commercial InGaAs/InP SPAD. Recommended keep. It is a device under test measured by the authors under nominally identical conditions (p.5, p.6, Fig. 6(a)), with its own DCR, SPDE and afterpulsing: D155 applies, rule 13 is about setup instruments, and the comparison-table pitfall covers other works' numbers only. Fab empty (vendor unnamed). Note rewritten.
4. stanfield2019 Q_i and the 7 K row. Recommended keep both as they are. 2.2e6 `measured`: a doublet lineshape with loading and backscattering in the model fixes the coupling from the data (split dips have depth (1 - x)^2 with x = kappa_e / kappa, which is not symmetric under swapping kappa_e and kappa_i, unlike the singlet), so D403 is satisfied; the paper uses it to resolve the ambiguity it states for the 1.8e6 singlet. Caveat: no spectrum of that resonance is printed. 7 K row separate: p.14 names only a ring resonator device, while the abstract's exemplary device points to the non-undercut one and 0.26 is 30 % above its 0.2 GHz/V; D159 with the matsuo2013 separate-row construction (D422). The 200 fW row stays on the non-undercut row because p.15 assigns it there.
5. akritidis2026 a-Si:H on SiN transitions on `si_sin_multilayer`. Recommended keep: the mode moves between a SiN layer and a deposited Si layer through 20 nm oxide (rule 11, hybrid value), merged shoaa2025 polysilicon taper on SiN uses the same value, `core_material` names a-Si:H. The a-Si:H waveguide stays `other` (chiles2017, zhu2019), and the III-V taper and laser stay `other`.
6. blundell2025 `length_um`. Recommended keep the calculated L_pi rows (the paper calls L_pi the device length, p.3, p.17, and the IL rows use it), with the physical 50 um patch added as m3. Merged PCM rows use length_um only for physical lengths, so a chart on this parameter mixes both unless it reads the condition. Alternative: move L_pi to notes until `l_pi_um` exists (delete the five m1 rows; IL rows keep their formula in notes).
7. blundell2025 MMI rows. Recommended keep: T / T0 in `insertion_loss_db`, scope on_chip_excess, now with `excludes`. It is the only stated loss of those devices (p.16 prints up to 4 dB), but it is a programming-induced change, not the MMI's total loss, so these rows should not rank against splitter IL. If that is not acceptable, the 4 components go (rule 9) and their values go to the phase-shifter notes.
