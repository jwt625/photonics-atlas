## Rulings on the batch report's judgment calls

1. ikeda2020 WSS 3-dB bandwidth on the C-DC row: not endorsed. The text explains the bandwidth through the C-DC
   coupling coefficient but never states it as one C-DC's passband; the number is read from the WSS fiber-to-fiber
   spectra (p.5 "read from Fig. 4") and each channel passes two dropping C-DCs and the MZ switch (p.4). Notes per
   D407; gap confirmed. The C-DC row keeps its geometry through the added Bragg-wavelength range (m7).
2. skandalos2026 UCL as second fab org on laser / SOA: endorsed as borderline. "M.P. and J.S.P. performed the
   metallization of the devices" (p.8 contributions; J.S.P. is at UCL) names a fabrication step by a UCL author:
   D80b two-org fabricators. The QD epitaxy at UCL alone would only be a note (rule 12). Caveat: the acknowledgment
   places the fabrication of the integrated lasers at the Southampton Nanofabrication Centre (p.8), which under the
   D83c location logic would point to Southampton only; listed as an open question. Interface and SiN waveguide rows
   stay Southampton only (no metallization).
3. akiyama2021: design row for the simulated 4-64 ch CATs endorsed (row_kind design, D80e: 8-64 ch not fabricated);
   n_channels range fixed. P2pi: changed to tuning_power_mw as printed (no per-2pi parameter in the registry;
   tuning_power_mw "for the stated range" is the per-2pi home in merged rows).
4. li2023 edge-coupler platform empty: endorsed (D168 starnault2026 logic; the facet guiding stack is not stated).
   zheng2023 IL bounds normalized to the straight reference as derived: endorsed (D80a, p.8 "normalized by
   subtracting the transmission of the straight waveguide").
5. Not-entered items endorsed: ikeda2020 MZ switch 0.13 dB (ref. [4]) and C-DC 0.20 dB computed from it (rule 3,
   D80i); li2023 PSR < 0.5 dB over 110 nm: the sentence cites [31] (Gan, Liu, Guo et al., co-authors) -> D259 /
   D259a; li2023 ~7 dB entire DPC (contents not stated); akiyama2021 MMI 160 x 20 um (roles not stated), projected
   1.87 dB, ~12 mW per channel (D254); kwon2020 EAM metrics (EO atlas); skandalos2026 cited [41, 42, 44] values.
6. Fab: akiyama2021 empty (MPW, no org; p.10), ikeda2020 AIST (D84a, "our 300-mm CMOS R&D foundry", p.3), li2023
   empty (NanoLN wafer only), zheng2023 empty (four orgs, passive "fabricated"), kwon2020 ETRI ("we fabricate",
   p.2, all authors ETRI): all endorsed.
