## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. cheng2023b D259a vs D408 (judgment (a)): keep the distiller's removal; no op. Figs. 2(a), 5(a) and 6 carry "Adapted with
   permission ... [32]" (the authors' OECC 2020 paper, whose title prints 40.1 GHz at 223 K), so D408's caption clause removes their
   values. The restatements are the same measurements, not new data: the Table 1 headers (Ith = 0.70 / 1.05 mA) and Table 2 "This
   Work" repeat the Fig. 2(a) inset thresholds, and the Fig. 5(b) 14.7 mA trace with its 40.1 GHz label is the Fig. 5(a) -50 C
   trace (same bias, same label). D417 (muthuganesan2023: Table 1 values that are reprinted Fig. 4 data from the authors' ref. [17]
   deleted) is the matching precedent for a table restating a reprinted figure. Entering 0.70 / 1.05 mA and 40.1 GHz under the
   literal reading would put the conference paper's headline into the atlas as this paper's result. Fig. 5(b)'s other traces
   (4.2-10.5 mA, incl. the 17.2 GHz row), Fig. 2(b), Fig. 3 and Table 1 element values have no attribution and stay (D408).
2. cheng2023b Table 1 rows (judgment (b)): keep all 32 rows; no trim. Rule 9 makes bias an operating-point condition, and the
   bias dependence of Cd and Rm,total is the result the table reports (chen2016a per-bias S22-fit rows precedent). Element choice:
   Cd and Ca as `capacitance_ff` follow zeng2019 (model elements, element named in condition); Cp as `capacitance_ff` follows
   hu2023a's "parasitic capacitance" row (li2015 kept its pad Cp in the condition instead; either is defensible, the conditions here
   name the element); Rm,total (series DBR resistance) as `series_resistance_ohm` fits the registry definition (equivalent-circuit
   S11 fit allowed); Rj is a shunt junction resistance and Rp a pad-branch resistance, correctly out. If the coordinator wants
   benchmark views free of pad parasitics, the 10 Cp rows are the only candidates to drop. 58.7 pm/K `derived` from 0.0587 nm/C is
   D256 (no pm/K form printed).
3. serries2001 (judgment (c)): keep `wpe_pct` for "total power efficiency" eta_P (optical output over electrical input; registry
   definition "wall-plug / power-conversion efficiency"; operating point and facet count not stated, as the condition says).
   Keep statistic `best` on the per-temperature maximum powers (46 merged maximum / rollover power rows use best, wu2025b) and `ge` for
   "at least 120 mW" (qualifier ge on 166 merged metric rows). Fab: the abstract's first-person "We have fabricated and analyzed" with all five authors at
   one institute is rule 12's authors' own org; the registry has no Fraunhofer IAF (only org_fraunhofer_gesellschaft,
   org_fraunhofer_heinrich_hertz_institute, org_fraunhofer_izm; no alias), so fab stays empty pending the org round, parent not
   substituted. Recommend the org round add "Fraunhofer-Institut für Angewandte Festkörperphysik" (printed form; English "Fraunhofer
   Institute for Applied Solid State Physics IAF" if an exact ROR record is confirmed) with parent org_fraunhofer_gesellschaft, then
   a fix batch sets fab on both serries2001 rows.
4. papatryfonos2014 LC-DBR (judgment (d), first part): keep `laser.dbr`, row_kind design, platform inp. The paper's design object is
   the LC-DBR laser ("laterally coupled DBR lasers were designed", p.2; kappa chosen for kappa L ~ 1 of the laser), kappa_per_cm and
   fill_factor are allowed on laser, and kappa sits on laser rows in merged data (lu2024, nader2025, vaskasi2022). reflector.bragg
   would fit a separately characterized mirror (ferrotti2016, siddharth2024), which this paper does not have. inp follows "the
   structures reported here" (InAs / InP Qdash).
5. papatryfonos2014 Fig. 1 thresholds (judgment (d), second part): keep, `extracted_from_figure`. Locating the L-I onset by
   extrapolating the linear segment to the baseline is a plot reading, not arithmetic on printed numbers, and it stays inside the
