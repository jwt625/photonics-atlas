## Coordinator questions

1. west2019 fab `org_mit`: recommendation keep. The paper has first-person process statements ("Here we realize directly-etched
   waveguides" p.4; "the chlorine-based RIE chemistries we use to etch alumina" p.5), which meet D80b ("we fabricate"); all authors
   sit in MIT units (EECS and Lincoln Laboratory, a child of `org_mit` in the registry), so D154 gives the parent. Lincoln Laboratory
   alone would need a named site or fabricator (D518 niffenegger2020 had author contributions; morgan2021 printed "MITLL's 200 mm
   CMOS foundry line"); west2019 has neither, and the 200 mm / ASML / Centura tool list is not a place statement. mehta2016 (same
   group, MIT + Lincoln) is merged with `org_mit` too. Facility: none acknowledged.
2. west2019 loaded Q 275k / 2.7 GHz on the 400 nm gap ring: recommendation keep (no D159). The Fig. 4(a) caption does not name the
   gap, but two independent checks tie it to the printed pair: 1/Q_L = 1/470.2k + 1/660k gives 274.6k, and the fitted dip of about
   15 dB in Fig. 4(a) matches the 15.5 dB that Q_i 470k / Q_c 660k imply (under-coupled, as the caption says); any other gap of the
   same R 90 um ring would change Q_c and therefore Q_L. D159 is for a value that could belong to either of two entered components;
   here no other gap ring is entered and the numbers fit only this one.
3. liu2018b cascaded rings: recommendation keep one row per (width, gap) design with the resonance in `condition`. Both rings of a
   pair share width, gap and bus; only the radius differs slightly and is not printed, and the paper never says which ring gives
   resonance I or II, so a per-ring split would invent an assignment. The alternative (D159, drop the per-resonance values) would
   discard the paper's headline Q_int 210k. Q_int basis split (390 nm and supplement author_estimate, 455 nm derived) follows D403
   exactly (455 nm regime shown by ER vs gap; 390 nm assumed, and the paper itself later re-reads the 0.8 um TM00 device as
   over-coupled). The 455 nm 0.14 um-gap value on the Fig. 2 design row: keep (same width / gap design; die not stated, noted).
4. xie2020 "insertion loss" as `excess_loss_db`, per_component, reference_structure: recommendation keep (see Points checked: the
   simulated curves are drawn against a -3 dB line and the normalized measured traces sit 0-2 dB below 0 dB).
5. deng2019 Table I "This work" (average measured SR 78 %/22 %, maximum variation 6.2 %): recommendation keep out under D159. The row
   is a composite: its temperature column is the 3-dB device (p.8 text and Fig. 14), while 78/22 matches no plotted value exactly
   (closest Fig. 13 point is the 10 nm variant at 24 % at 1310 nm, simulated 79/21). Entering 22 % on dw10 would rest on that
   closeness alone. The 1.3 % / 1.6 % temperature variation of the 3-dB device stays a registry gap (below).
