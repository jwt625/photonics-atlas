## Rulings on the batch report's judgment calls

- sohn2018 isolation_db 15 dB: not endorsed (see table). Registry isolation_db is an isolation magnitude of the
  isolator; the paper itself calls the 15 dB a contrast / mode conversion asymmetry and shows the carrier passing in
  both directions. Removed; gap below.
- sohn2018 17 % as coupling_efficiency_pct (scope de_embedded, measured): endorsed, no derived 7.7 dB IL row. One
  convention = the printed form: the registry keeps linear efficiencies in % parameters (component_parameters.yaml
  header; skill rule 5) and coupling_efficiency_pct is defined for mode_converter as the coupled power fraction;
  cheng2025 printed "-4.6 dB" and therefore keeps insertion_loss_db (D82d: a dB value computed from % would be derived
  and redundant). Normalization to the waveguide input (p.9) is stated without method: basis measured kept, as
  cheng2025.
- carpenter2020 fab org_university_of_southampton (borderline): endorsed under D84a ("Our approach to manufacturing
  PPLN waveguides is reported in our earlier works", p.3) plus D84b (thanks for cleanroom processing help, p.8); all
  authors ORC Southampton, two also Covesion (dual affiliation, D83c: no other fabrication place stated). Covesion as
  wafer supplier is a note (rule 12).
- stone2022 fab NIST: endorsed, on a stronger ground than the JQI parent: "we fabricate an array of SiN microrings"
  (p.4) and App. C (p.7-8 "We deposit ..."), and every author lists NIST (affiliation 2) besides JQI (NIST / UMD), so
  NIST is the unambiguous common org (D72 / D80b); D154 (registry parent of JQI = NIST) agrees. No facility named.
- wang2026c garbled pages 8-9: endorsed; no value taken; facility empty is the default either way.
