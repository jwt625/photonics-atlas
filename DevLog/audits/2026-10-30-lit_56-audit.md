## Open questions for the coordinator

1. lee2012 Q_int basis under D403 (6 q_intrinsic rows, `measured`). Evidence: the taper gap is adjustable through
   under-coupling, critical coupling and strong over-coupling (p.1; Fig. 2 caption p.2), spectra were taken in the
   under-coupled regime (p.2), and the record value reproduces on the under-coupled branch only: FWHM ~0.3 MHz gives
   Q_L ~6.4-6.7e8, with the inset dip minimum read as 0.15-0.25 the under-coupled formula gives ~0.86-0.96e9 (printed
   875M), the over-coupled one ~2.1-2.7e9 (near or above the paper's fitted material-Q bound of 2.5 billion). The
   ring-down cross-check (p.2) concerns thermal effects, not the regime. Unlike ji2017 / he2023 (fixed bus gap,
   regime stated by design) and ye2019 (regime not stated), the regime here is set and identified in the measurement
   by the adjustable gap, although the gap-sweep data are not shown. Recommendation: D403 satisfied; keep `measured`
   (D80a: the paper prints only the fitted Q_int, no explicit arithmetic; ye2019 critical-coupling precedent), with the
   cross-check now in the d7p5mm component notes. Strict alternative: `author_estimate` on the 6 rows if D403 requires
   the determining data to be shown.
2. biberman2012 printed Q0 vs figure cross-check. Recommendation: endorse the staged handling (printed values kept,
   `author_estimate`, he2023 / lit_49 precedent) with the refined notes: the TE mismatch is largely explained by the
   -3 dB width convention of Fig. 2(a) (~2.05e7 from the half-depth width); the TM value (1.4e5) is reproduced by
   neither branch (~2.1e5 / ~2.6e5) and sits near the mode-solver 1.3246e5.
3. wang2012 CMC Microsystems ("for making this project possible", p.12) and the MPW wafer (p.9). Recommendation: not a
   fab org. The sentence has no fabrication wording (D398 requires an MPW access provider named for fabrication
   support); generic CMC thanks were not fab orgs in simard2013 (lit_49), eid2016 ("provision of services"),
   murray2015, simard2015 and lu2015 (merged rows carry the fabricating org only). ePIXfab appears only as a citation
   for "the same foundry" (p.8, ref. [27]), not as an acknowledged service. SFU Nanoimaging facility (p.12, assistance
   with the FIB cross-section images of Fig. 1(b)): not a facility org on these rows; FIB milling is destructive and
   the imaged device is not identified as one of the measured gratings (koyaz2026 / lit_45 UNIL Cellular Imaging
   Facility precedent: characterization of a sample other than the measured device, not entered).
4. giuntoni2009 tunable reflector and dolgaleva2011 scope.
   - giuntoni2009: no other stated performance value for the 2.2 um rib (Fig. 8: d 150 nm, D 0.3, L 800 um, period
     225 nm, 30 / 40 / 50 C, TE and TM) besides the ~77 pm/K shift (both polarizations, p.7) and the 80 pm/K
     calculation from cited coefficients (ref. [13]); reflectivities are figure-only. Recommendation: no row now;
     widen `thermal_drift_pm_per_k` to reflector in the registry extension and gap-fill one component (2.2 um rib) with
     77 pm/K `measured` (qualifier approx, both polarizations in condition) plus the design geometry from the Fig. 8
     caption / legend.
   - dolgaleva2011: in scope under D411. The parts are fs-laser-written waveguides (mode diameter 11 um, fiber-like
     mode) with Bragg gratings inside a 5 cm fused-silica chip, measured with guided light and characterized with
     facet coupling losses; not a bulk free-space or polished crystalline part. Merged precedents: amorim2017 (fused
     silica, reflector.bragg / directional couplers / waveguide, platform `silica`) and camus2020 (BGG glass).
     Recommendation: keep as staged.
