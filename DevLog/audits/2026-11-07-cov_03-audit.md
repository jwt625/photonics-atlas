## Rulings on the distiller's questions

1. ma2000 fab: keep org_northwestern_university. "we report our design, fabrication and measurement" (p.1 Sec. I) is a
   first-person statement that the authors fabricated the couplers ("our process" class, D84a; first-person making
   account, D399 chen2000), and all four authors sit in one department (p.1 footnote).
2. beals2026 pump power: agree, not entered. `pump_threshold_mw` is the optical pump threshold; 232 mW (active kick) vs
   627 mW (heater kick) are minimum powers to reach the soliton regime with a control technique (p.5 Fig. 3(a) caption),
   above the OPO threshold, which the paper does not print. Values stay in the comb notes.
3. sun2024 values next to citations: keep as this work's, basis as entered. The coil value sentence ("The cavity has ...",
   p.2) and the ring value sentence ("The ring resonator has ...", p.2) carry no citation; refs 9 / 10, 12 sit on the
   device-type sentences before them, and ref. 10 is another group's paper, so D259 / D408 (value sentence or figure
   attributes the value) do not apply. The 1600 nm pair fits this experiment's 1550-1600 nm reference-laser span. Weak
   point on record: ref. 9 (same coil group, title "4.0 m coil resonator") may be where the coil Q values were first
   measured; the cached text cannot settle it.
4. Optelligent: register as a company (packaging; p.12 Methods, p.13 Acknowledgements) with no role on these rows. Agree.
5. tian2025 non-uniformity statistic: keep the two Fig. 6 max-min rows (`il_nonuniformity_db` is defined as max minus
   min, readings confirmed above) and keep the printed standard deviations 0.269 / 0.701 dB (and 0.057 / 0.129
   normalized) in notes. Do not put them on `il_nonuniformity_db` with `statistic: std` (a different quantity from max
   minus min) nor on `insertion_loss_db` with `std` (merged std rows are sample-to-sample spreads of one port, e.g. the
   ectc 2025 FAU rows, not port-to-port spread). Add the registry parameter the distiller proposes (port-to-port standard
   deviation of insertion loss, dB; splitter, wdm), then enter p.5 values as measured with n_samples 8 and the
   mirrored-channel note.
