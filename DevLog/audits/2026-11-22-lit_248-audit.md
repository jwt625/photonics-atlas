## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. (a) priyadarshini2024 measured ~60 nm on the Wm 1.5 mirror row: keep. `stopband_width_nm` is reflector-only and the cavity's
   rejection band is set by its two N = 61 mirrors; the abstract ties ~60 nm to the ~35 um device (D1), so the D254 family issue of
   the p.15 D1-D3 sentence does not apply; "contrast level not stated" is correct (p.21 adds "> 40 dB" over 1525-1575 nm, a 50 nm
   window, which is the extinction row, not a level for the 60 nm). The cross-note to D1 is present. Locator fixed.
2. (b) Cavity IL de_embedded / direct: keep. p.16 defines IL as the resonance peak minus the passband level of the same trace, and the
   traces are normalized to a same-length reference waveguide (p.13), so couplers and access waveguides are out; `direct` describes
   the peak-to-passband reading better than `reference_structure` (paredes2021 D708 precedent for normalized traces). The excludes
   text already says it.
3. (c) Bistability onset as `power_handling_dbm`: keep the row (a pump filter's usable input power is its key limit and the registry
   allows `resonator`), but drop `lt`: the printed 0.5 mW is the onset itself, and merged onset row cmp_ofc2026_tu2j2_spgc_si-m3
   ("began to show absorption induced loss at approximately 15 dBm") stores the onset without a bound. Condition now says reversible
   bistability, not damage. Applied in the patch. Basis `derived` (mW -> dBm, D256) correct.
4. (d) Microheater rise / fall kept: keep. "are measured to be ~3.67 us and ~0.6 us" (p.18) is a measurement on this device;
   "closely following our earlier published work [28]" compares, it does not reprint (D259 / D80i not triggered). Definition (10-90 %)
   not stated, noted.
5. (e) h = 150 nm as `slab_thickness_nm`: keep. The Fig. 2(c) render shows the h arrow spanning the unetched slab beside the rib and H
   spanning the full 220 nm; 220 - 70 nm etch = 150 nm (p.13). "Rib height" in the legend is the paper's naming. Note p.6 prints
   "H = 200 nm" once (typo against 220 nm on p.3, p.9-10 captions and p.13); no row uses it.
6. (f) yao2018 one design row per tilt angle and dB-converted bounds as simulated: keep. Each tilt has its own optimized Lmmi (rule 9
   sibling designs, different length); conversions of a simulated value stay `simulated` (D83f); the total-power reading of the
   % bounds is confirmed by the Fig. 7(a) peak (see Points checked).
7. (g) yao2018 "total insertion loss" as `excess_loss_db`: keep. It is the sum of both output monitors against the input (p.4-5,
   Fig. 7(a) consistency), i.e. the loss beyond the ideal split; scope `per_component`. Platform `other` (bulk z-cut crystal, implanted
   layer) is the right enum value.
8. (h) xu2007 platform `soi`, widths in `linewidth_pm`, finesse `derived`: keep all three. Etch depth is not stated, so `soi_strip`
   would be a guess (D422 / D423). nm -> pm is a unit conversion (D256: `derived`). Finesse computed by the authors from their own
   FSR / FWHM is `derived` in merged rows (dumon2004, puckett2021, si2026); measured would also be defensible, but follow the
   majority of computed-by-authors rows.
9. (i) R20 / R5 at 1470 nm inferred: keep 1470 nm for both; the R5 1470-1494 nm range rested on swapped Fig. 4 axes (patched to
   1470 nm, same as R20). The sentence order ("At 1470 nm ... Keeping the same MMI design and reducing the ring radius ...") and the
   following "this trend ... applies to the resonators with other radii" support a fixed 1470 nm.
10. (j) Fig. 2(b) split ratios `extracted_from_figure` +/- 3 pp, and R50 extinction ~15 / ~12 dB both kept: keep. The enlarged
    render confirms 0.43-0.53 and about 0.64; the 3 x 8.5 um coupler sits in the stand-alone coupler paragraph (p.5). The two
