## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. wang2018c (judgment (a)). Recommendation: keep as distilled. The printed 1.2 / 0.2 dB are twice the "3 +/- x dB" deviation
   (D401); the same group and method were ruled in D454 (wang2016a: printed +/-0.7 dB in notes, `imbalance_db` = figure-read port
   difference). The vector reads (TE max 1.30 dB, TM max 0.19 dB) are the more exact port difference: in dB the two lossless
   K curves are not symmetric about -3 dB (-2.41 / -3.71 dB at the TE maximum), so 2 x 0.6 dB understates the TE port
   difference by about 0.1 dB. Table 2 "IL < 1 dB" as `excess_loss_db` `author_estimate` (the comparison column is loss beyond
   the 3-dB split; no IL measurement described); fab Applied Nanotools: endorse.
2. zamek2011 (judgment (b)). Recommendation: keep Q0 900 / QC 360 / Q 260 / IL 0.7 dB `author_estimate` (QC reproduces from
   Eq. (5) with the attributed kappa, see above; D403), the abstract 2100 as the simulated design value with the D417 note, fab
   `org_university_of_california_san_diego` (first-person "We first used electron beam lithography", p.7; all six authors at the
   UCSD ECE department, p.1) and `org_ucsb_nanofabrication_facility` as facility only ("The fabrication was performed at UCSB
   Nanofab and UCSD Nano3 facilities", p.9; D634 li2017a: a named facility is not a fab org and its owner is not fab). "UCSD
   Nano3": D634 already sent "UCSD's Nano3 cleanroom" (nejadriahi2021, 2021) to the org round as an alias of
   `org_san_diego_nanotechnology_infrastructure`, and merged weigel2018 rows map "SDNI / UCSD Nano3 cleanroom" to that id.
   Recommendation: rule both strings once in the org round. If SDNI is the later umbrella name of the same Nano3 cleanroom, the
   D80d time-correct rule argues for a separate facility org "UCSD Nano3" (parent `org_university_of_california_san_diego`) used
   for the 2011 printing, with D634's 2021 printing either on that org or on SDNI; then fill both zamek2011 rows in the fix batch.
   The facility history is not in the repo and was not checked (no network); the distiller's empty facility for Nano3 stands
   until then.
3. Resonator classes (judgment (c)). Recommendation: keep. `resonator.photonic_crystal` is registered as "photonic-crystal /
   Fabry-Perot cavity": it fits the zamek2011 Au-mirror standing-wave cavity and the zheng2017 two-SLM cavity (wu2018 cascaded
   Sagnac precedent, D454). The zheng2017 coupled MRR + FP Fano system is `resonator.other` (arianfard2020 Fano device, D611; not
   `coupled_rings`, one resonator is not a ring), its ring `resonator.ring` (D400: not a WDM channel filter). The zamek2011 Au
   plug as `reflector.other` with R 0.9 simulated / ~0.8 author_estimate and the D396 cross-notes are right.
4. zheng2017 (judgment (d)). Recommendation: accept the split of the ER range row into four rows (patched; revert = drop the m2
   op and the three fano adds). Split 46.24 % and power coupling 1.32 % `derived` per D80a: endorse; the fitted t = 0.68 equals
   the preset simulation value (p.4), already noted. Loop-mirror "reflection ~0.95" (p.7): keep out. Eq. (2) with the fitted
   t = 0.68, k = 0.733, a1 = 0.97 gives |r| = 0.967 (field) or |r|^2 = 0.936 (power, a1 as a field factor); the printed 0.95 is
   neither, and "assessed" gives no method, so a `reflector.loop_mirror` row would rest on a guessed definition (rule 1, D159
   analog). GC ~12 dB scope unspecified and fab empty: endorse.
5. chung2023 GC basis (judgment (e)). Recommendation: per-coupler reading kept ("coupler efficiency", singular; a pair total of
   3.93 dB would be about 2 dB per O-band GC, not excluded but less likely), basis changed to `author_estimate` (patched) under
   D44 / D544: the paper states the efficiency without any measurement or calibration, and the value may be the MPW GC figure.
   Alternative: keep `measured` under D167 if the coordinator reads D167 as covering any directly stated per-coupler value
   (revert = drop the two GC ops). A rule line that separates the two precedents would settle future batches.
6. chung2023 bandwidth and fab (judgments (e), (h)). Recommendation: keep bw_1db_nm 70 `gt` with the 3 +/- 0.5 dB criterion in
   the condition (chen2022a; the criterion is a 1-dB-wide window per port, the closest registered width). Fab
   `org_taiwan_semiconductor_research_institute` only: "chip fabrication support provided by Taiwan Semiconductor Research
   Institute (TSRI)" (p.5) next to "MPW foundry service" (p.4) is the D398 hung2020 case without a named foundry; no foundry is
   inferred (Contract v7). process_name as printed: endorse.
