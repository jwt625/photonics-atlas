## Coordinator questions

1. nie2019 identity (asked by the distiller). The cached text is the authors' accepted manuscript ("Compiled March 7, 2019", template DOI line):
   same five authors and affiliations, and the numbers of the source row's paper (16-stage cascade, 68.5 dB, stop band below 3 nm, 783.6 nm). The
   title differs only in "rejection ratio" vs "extinction ratio" and hyphenation. Recommendation: same work, no D397 stop (D430 / D433 / D462
   precedent for author versions); keep the component-note sentence; journal version unread.
2. karunakaran2024 `q_intrinsic` basis (asked). Measured is not available for an extracted Q0. The regime is determined from the paper's own data:
   Fig. 1(b) visibility at 1970 nm rises monotonically over the four gaps including this 700 nm ring (about 0.23 to 0.78), and p.9 calls the
   coupling Q "found experimentally"; this differs from smith2023 (D541), where the regime came from other rings only. Recommendation: `derived`
   for both values (patched; D403, D465, D520), the 1.55 um value with the note that the paper words the under-coupled reading as an indication.
   Alternative: `author_estimate` on both if the coordinator reads "nearly critically coupled" as leaving the regime open (at 1970 nm the
   under-coupled solution would give Q0 about 1.0e6 instead of 2.7e6, and four gap points cannot exclude a crossing between 600 and 700 nm).
   The 0.11 dB/cm row stays author_estimate either way (ng not stated, D76).
3. hu2020 family values and the 376 nm twin (asked). The 2.5 dB through-port average and the 0.5-1.5 dB drop-port loss summarize the four
   devices: Fig. 6 shows out-of-band through levels up to about 5-6 dB on the 382 nm device and drop peaks from about 1.4 to 0.3 dB across the set.
   Recommendation: delete the 8 per-row entries (D254, D541), keep the values in the component notes, and add the per-device drop-port peaks as
   figure reads (patched); keep the about 5 nm 3-dB width on each row (each panel is consistent with it; D465 construction). Keep the D396 split of
   the 376 nm device into `switch_fabric.other` (D158 / D407) and the `wdm.bragg_cdc` filter twin (perez2017 two-class precedent, D523). To keep
   the batch's version instead: drop the 8 delete_metrics, the 4 drop_loss add_metrics and the 4 component-note ops.
4. lin2023 bounds stated once for six devices (asked). "All of our fabricated devices feature an insertion loss lower than 0.3 dB" (p.1, p.6)
   and the < 3 % PSR variation and < 0.25 dB simulated IL are bounds that the paper states for each device, not a typical value placed on one
   representative row; D254 does not apply (govdeli2025 construction, D465). Recommendation: keep on all six rows. Measured split ratios: the
   audit pixel read of Fig. 3(a) agrees with the batch within 0.5 point on all six devices; keep the +/- 1 point notes. The PSR variation rows
   now carry 1520-1590 nm.
5. nie2019 grating period basis (audit). p.1-2 say Wa and Wb are measured from the SEM image; the next sentence gives Wg, period and duty cycle
   with no measurement statement. Recommendation: `design_target` on both period rows and the notes corrected (patched). Alternative: restore
   `measured` and the old notes if the SEM statement is read as covering both sentences.
