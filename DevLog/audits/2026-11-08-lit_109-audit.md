## Coordinator questions

1. chen2016a 400 nm Ge device (ge400 row, 6 metrics): own new measurement or a reproduction of ref. [11]? The paper
   cites [11] for a 400 nm Si-LPIN GePD at p.2-p.3 (20 GHz, over 1 A/W across the C band at -1 V) and shows its
   simulated field "for reference" (Fig. 2(c)). Fig. 9 and its p.8 discussion carry no citation and call the curves
   "experimental"; Fig. 9 is assembled in this paper (its 160 nm traces are the Fig. 5(a) curves with the same > 50 GHz
   labels and the Fig. 4(a) spectrum), and the 400 nm label (19 GHz at -1 V) differs from the 20 GHz quoted for [11].
   D408 removes values only where the sentence or figure attributes them to the earlier paper. Recommendation: keep the
   row (patch adds the evidence to notes and drops the assumed reference plane). Strict alternative: delete the
   component (it rests on the same design and platform as [11] and no sentence says it was measured for this paper).
2. chen2019 0.07 A/W for "the devices": D159 applies? The value comes from one top-illuminated FTIR spectrum (Fig. 5)
   whose device is not identified, and is stated once for the family (abstract, p.2, conclusion). Entering it on all
   three diameters triplicates one measurement and asserts per-diameter data the paper does not show; D254 (a value
   stated once for a family of siblings stays a note) covers this case. Recommendation: delete the three rows (patch
   does), value and the Fig. 5 inset readings in component notes. To keep them, drop the 3 delete_metrics ops and the 3
   chen2019 component notes ops.
3. smith2023 q_intrinsic and ring-loss rows `derived` vs `author_estimate` under D403. Eq. (1) is symmetric in a and r
   (p.5); the authors assign the regime from cross-device trends (TE: width trend, Fig. 5; TM: extinction / coupling
   trends, Figs. 7-8), print the under-coupled alternative for TM (2.32e6 -> 7.73e5), and the only gap series that
   reaches critical coupling (Fig. 10) covers widths 0.4 / 0.7 um, not the 1 um ring. No data of the w1000 ring fixes
   its regime. Recommendation: author_estimate for the 8 w1000 rows (patch does; D418 / D454 strict reading). The w500
   TE Qint stays `derived`: its dip reaches about zero (Fig. 4(d)), where both branches give 2 QL (D463 puckett2021,
   D418 ye2019 precedent). If the coordinator accepts sibling-trend regime determination as "from the data", drop the
   8 basis ops.
4. liu2024 FFC worst-case bound: the plot shows the dw = -0.2 um cascade at about -2.5 dB (0.06 dB per bend), above
   the printed < 2 dB / < 0.05 dB. The patch keeps the printed bound with a conflict note (D417) and adds the nominal
   trace as m11. Recommendation: accept; alternatively drop m1 to notes if a printed bound contradicted by its own
   figure should not stand.
