  is printed (the derivation is the de-embedding). The merged garciablanco2025 m3 (13.71 dBm on-chip, measured) is the

  population in condition / notes.

  liu2018 mirror rows hold reflectivity_pct (not allowed for resonator; values author_estimate, method not stated);
  liu2022 microring (loss extraction) and spiral (OFDR) rows.

  yan2024 four-port device stays nonreciprocal.isolator with the circulator role in notes (one physical device, D32;
  headline metrics are isolator metrics). white2024 laser.sil for the DFB + ULS hybrid: endorsed. white2024 DFB chip as a
  device under test (gain element of the reported hybrid, own free-running linewidth), not setup equipment (rule 13).
- Figure readings: confirmed on the renders (white2024 17.7 dB at ~42 mW; liu2018 9.3 / 11.5 dB +/- 1 dB and kappa
  45 /cm; zhang2017 isolation dips; tao2024 Fig. 7).

  flag cleared) or a plot axis gives a value (akin2024 ring SHG). yan2024 cl_band inferred on rows measured over
  1520-1610 nm: acceptable, unchanged.

- Fabrication orgs: liu2022 / riemensberger2022 org_epfl + CMi, tao2024 Jilin, akin2024 UIUC (no facility; grants only):
  endorsed. lu2020 empty (passive fabrication, program acknowledgements only), yan2024 empty (unnamed foundry, no D80b
  statement): endorsed. suntsov2021 (authors state they fabricated, single university), zhang2017 (author contributions
  name Glasgow device fabrication and Minnesota garnet sputtering onto the chips) and liu2018 (facility "performed at"
  statement) are blocked only by missing registry orgs (question 4). white2024 Stanford + UW kept (question 2).
