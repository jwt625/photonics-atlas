## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. zhang2022c fab (judgment (a)). Recommendation: HUST on all four rows (patched). The first-person "we fabricated a spot size
   converter (SSC) at the laser output port" (p.1) names a step of one monolithic flow: Sec. 2.2 (p.3) patterns "the SSC and
   gratings" in the same EBL step and RIE etch, then the common MOCVD regrowth, ridge, contacts and cleave. The SSC cannot be
   made apart from the laser wafer; the per-component rule (D515 / D463) separates parts made in different flows or by
   different orgs, which is not the case here. All seven authors at HUST (p.1), so rule 12 applies. Alternative (distiller):
   SSC row only; revert = drop the three component ops.
2. zhang2022c SSC 64 % / 44 % (judgment (b)). Recommendation: keep `measured`, `per_facet`, `direct` (D167: printed result,
   no arithmetic shown; the paper's "calculated" is the ratio of fiber to Ge-detector power, Fig. 5(c) ratios agree). The
   detector-area caveat (p.5) is stated for the laser without SSC.
3. koscica2025 dual-core coupler (judgment (c)). Recommendation: keep one row. The 2.8 / 5.8 um widths are the ends of a
   simulated width sweep (p.4-5); the paper never ties a fabricated dual-core device to a width (the ring DBR laser and the
   Fig. 5(b) FIB-SEM device have no stated width), so two rows would each claim a fabricated design the paper does not
   identify. Conditions carry the widths.
4. Platforms and orgs (judgments (d), (e), (f)). Recommendation: keep. `iii_v_on_si_epi` for the in-pocket lasers including
   the SiN-mirror DBR lasers matches skandalos2026 (laser.dbr, SiN-waveguide DBRs, `iii_v_on_si_epi`) and wei2023; transitions
   `other` per wei2023. AIM Photonics fab on the 6 foundry rows (p.2 passive sentence naming the foundry, Contract v7);
   UCSB Nanofabrication Facility on every koscica2025 row (D604 "a portion of this work was performed"); laser rows fab
   empty (D634 / D82c; the p.2 first-person "we use small coupons ... for experimental growth and fabrication development"
   names no org and the authors sit at UCSB, Aeluma, AIM Photonics / SUNY Poly and Analog Photonics, D154). su2023 Ligentec
   AN800 MPW on the 4 Si3N4-chip rows (p.2 "fabricated by Ligentec AN800", liu2022d / D617 precedent). New: the two koscica2025
   transition rows carry AIM fab but their losses are set mostly by the post-foundry III-V facet etch and gap fill (p.3);
   list them in `exclude_values` of `config/benchmark.yaml` (D933) so the 5.4 dB and the simulated overlaps do not read as
   AIM foundry transition performance.
5. su2023 coupling losses 2.5 / 3 dB (judgment (e)). Recommendation: keep `author_estimate` ("final coupling losses ... about",
   p.2; method and origin not stated), D418 (nader2025, D167 not extended) and D617 (guo2021 butt coupling ~2 dB). The 0.2
   dB/cm waveguide bound stays `author_estimate` (stated with the foundry process; `specification` would also fit, no change).
6. koscica2025 derived values (judgments (g), (h)). Recommendation: keep the ~125 mA mean threshold `derived` (250 / 2 from a
   stated relation, qualifier approx; n_samples 13 patched); keep 87 % reflectivity, 1.1 THz FSR and 30.5k Q `author_estimate`
   (D82a: design-or-measurement origin not stated; D256a for the THz -> GHz rescale).
7. feng2024 range and libsch2006 Table 1 (judgments (i), (j)). Recommendation: keep both. feng2024 1250-1300 nm from the text
   with the figure reads in notes follows D417 (tossoun2024: identity clear, numbers disagree); the 400 mA trace (near
   threshold) shows broad emission near 1285 / 1305 nm, which may be what the text range covers. libsch2006: `lt` + `best`
   matches the Table 1 header "best components fabricated"; lens pair `per_interface` (module-to-board), mirror
   `per_component`; band unspecified (Avalon VCSEL wavelength not printed in this paper; 985 nm belongs to the earlier
   Terabus modules, 850 / 980 nm losses are cited).
