## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. kim2017 one row, -0.078 dB headline (judgment (a)). Recommendation: keep one row and the headline. The paper selects wM 1.7 um
   and analyzes only that design (spectra, tolerances, Table 3); Table 2 points carry only a linear throughput, and the one
   point checkable against the figures (0.984 for the chosen design) disagrees with Fig. 6(a), (c), (d), which all read about
   -0.078 dB. Rows for wM 1.8 / 1.9 um would show 0.061 / 0.057 dB, better than the paper's own headline, from a table column of
   doubtful precision. Notes now record the figure support.
2. celo2017 one row per layout (judgment (b)). Recommendation: keep. The 26 mdB is printed once for both lithographies (p.2), so
   a per-lithography split would duplicate it; lithography is a process condition of the same mask layout, as in the wgx 193 / 248
   rows. The 6-die 29 mdB and 1545 nm 19 mdB values belong on wgxs: they follow the OPC sentences, Fig. 5 wgx traces are at
   40-55 mdB at those wavelengths, and the abstract states the OPC loss as < 30 mdB. Platform patched to `soi_strip`; fab empty
   correct (D433, dumais2017 same group). The +/-5 mdB wavelength dependence stays in notes (the +/- form does not fix a
   peak-to-peak `wdl_db` without assuming symmetry).
3. celo2017 crosstalk qualifier (judgment (c)). Recommendation: keep 42 / 39 dB with `ge`. "Maximum values of crosstalk over the
   C-band" are the worst-case (least negative) crosstalk, so suppression is at least 42 / 39 dB at every measured wavelength; the
   value is attained, hence `ge` not `gt`. This mirrors badri2019 (D639: band-maximum loss `le`) and dumais2017. The simulated
   32 mdB "for a perfectly fabricated crossing" (p.2 Sec. IV) describes the ideal sharp-corner geometry that neither mask
   reproduces as fabricated: notes only (D159) is right.
4. chen2012 subtype (judgment (d)). Recommendation: keep `crossing.mmi`. The paper builds it by inserting a multimode taper next to
   the intersection of its taper-integrated MMI-based crossing (p.1) and explains the loss as an MMI-based crossing plus taper
   coupling losses (p.2); chen2010a is `crossing.mmi`. Caveat for the layout view: `mmi_length_um` 0.86 um is the uniform
   section per branch (condition says so), not the across-crossing length that chen2010a's Lm gives; the across value
   (2 x (860 + 50) + 1200 = 3020 nm) is not printed, so the row stays as is.
5. noe2016 (judgment (e)). Recommendations: (i) in scope: Ti-indiffused LiNbO3 is an integrated-optical waveguide chip
   (8 SBC electrode sections on one waveguide, Fig. 1), not a bulk polished crystal part (D411); platform `other` (D515).
   (ii) Keep the one row as `author_estimate`, scope / band `unspecified`: the "~3 dB" carries no citation and appears nowhere
   else. If the coordinator reads the whole p.6 recap sentence as restated earlier work (its 100 krad/s and > 350 Grad items are
   refs. [6], [7]), delete the component (it has no other metric). (iii) EOSPACE is the fab (vendor of the purchased device
   under test, rule 12); org missing.
6. Tolerance bounds as metric rows (judgment (f)). Recommendation: keep. Precedent gao2025 m12-m15 (width deviation +/-20 nm and
   sidewall angle in the condition, simulated, `lt` / `gt`, D573). 1550 nm `wavelength_inferred` is conservative but sound:
   kim2017 Fig. 6(d) and chen2010a Fig. 7 reproduce the design-wavelength values at zero deviation; chen2010a also defines all
   FDTD runs at lambda0 = 1550 nm (p.2), so clearing the flag there would also be defensible.
7. Org round: EOSPACE (fab, vendor), name as printed "EOSPACE", lit:noe2016 p.1 Sec. II.A. Already open in
   `data/_staging/supply/org_requests.csv` as vendor "Eospace" (supply scan lit_13, also eo:renaud2023, eo:zhang2024); one
   org serves both. After it exists, set `fab_org_ids` on cmp_lit_noe2016_eospace_sbc_pol_transformer.
