## Questions raised by the distiller (evidence-based recommendations)

1. shoji2010 four-MZI 2x2 switch: recommend switch_fabric.other (endorsed). The device is one 2x2 switch whose four MZI
   elements are all set together to bar or cross, and the two inputs never share an element (p.3); it is the
   double-MZI element of Goh's PLC matrices generalized to Si (p.2, ref. [1]), i.e. a matrix cell, not an NxN
   reconfigurable mesh. D158 keeps mzi_mesh for NxN meshes or cells characterized inside one; dheer2018 dilated 2x2
   element (switch_fabric.other) is the direct analogue. The separate single-MZI reference row (switch_fabric.other,
   test structure) is consistent with D158.
2. campenhout2009 switching times: keep rise_time_us / fall_time_us with the extinction-level definition in condition
   (patched). The registry definitions are "10-90 % rise time unless noted" / "90-10 % fall time unless noted";
   merged precedent stores other definitions with the definition in condition (han2015 "time to 90 % power",
   seok2016 / seok2019 MEMS switching times, qiao2017 1.2 / 1.0 ns derived). When the switching-time parameter
   proposed in lit_47 (definition in condition) is added, these four rows move there. Von / VpiL / FCA-only on-state
   loss in notes: confirmed. lu2016a (lit_42) deleted the p-i-n FCA loss as EO-tuner (modulator) loss and kept P_pi;
   VpiL is listed in skill rule 2. Note for the ruling: `v_pi_v` is allowed for switch_fabric and lit_06 ruled
   carrier-injection switches "not EO modulators" (qiao2017), so Von = 1.0 V (steady state) / 1.02 V (pulsed) could
   enter as v_pi_v on the switch row; recommendation: keep it out as briefed (consistent with lu2016a), see Q2 below.
3. Orgs:
   - LioniX BV (a2011 p.7 Acknowledgements "fabricated by LioniX BV, Netherlands"; p.4 TriPleX): fab empty endorsed.
     `org_events.csv` holds no LioniX rename / acquisition / merger event (only an unrelated Kotura acquisition), and
     the registry treats LioniX BV as a separate, time-aware entity (D278; lit_16 mu2016 and lit_28 bauters2013 rulings;
     `org_lionix_international` note). Mapping to LioniX International BV would assert a lineage the registry does not
     hold. Add LioniX BV in the org round (then fab on both a2011 rows; also mu2016, bauters2013 x5).
   - AIST Nano-Processing Facility (shoji2010 p.5 Acknowledgments: supported "by IBEC Innovation Platform at the
     Nano-Processing Facility, AIST"): a support statement naming a shared facility (rule 12 facility role; konoike2020
     "supported in part by the TIA SCR" precedent, lit_30). Recommend registering the facility (name as printed
     "Nano-Processing Facility, AIST", facility, JP, parent `org_national_institute_of_advanced_industrial_science_and_technology`)
     and setting it as facility_org_ids on the five shoji2010 rows; the "IBEC Innovation Platform" is the support
     program, not the facility. Do not merge it with TIA SCR without a source. Fab stays empty (passive "were
     fabricated", p.3; unlike tanizawa2015 / nakamura2020, no "we fabricated").
4. Smaller calls:
   a. a2011 dg = 20 um as pitch_um: endorsed (registry "channel pitch" allowed for wdm; zhang2019a "end spacing between
      adjacent input / output waveguides" 3.7 um, hulyal2025, stanton2017, fan2025 store the FPR I/O pitch the same
      way).
   b. feng2011 on-chip loss: "< 5.0 dB" from the results (p.4) endorsed; abstract 5.0 dB (p.1) and conclusion 4.5 dB
      (p.6) in notes. The Fig. 4 render shows channel peaks between about -4.5 and -5 dB, consistent with all three.
