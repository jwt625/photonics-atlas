## Rulings on the batch report's judgment calls

1. yoshida2023 R / I / mu not entered: endorsed, registry gap. kappa_per_cm is "grating coupling coefficient"; R and I are
   the real and imaginary parts of the combined Hermitian coefficient (kappa_1D + kappa_2D-) e^-i theta_pc and mu is the
   magnitude of the radiative (non-Hermitian) coupling (p.3 Eq. (1) text, Fig. 1 labels), so none is a single grating
   kappa and storing them in kappa_per_cm would mix definitions. Values: pulsed device R 15, I 25, mu 38 cm-1 (fit to
   measured band data, p.3 Fig. 2(b)(c), p.4); CW device R 24, I 14, mu 44 cm-1 (estimated from the embedded air-hole
   shape, p.5, p.7 Methods).
2. ishii2003 "about +/-50 um" as tol_1db_lateral_um: endorsed for the VCSEL-to-waveguide value (469-m1). Sec. V (p.5) is
   introduced as estimating "the optical coupling tolerance", the paper defines tolerance at a 1 dB decrease (p.3, Fig. 7
   caption "Tolerance for 1-dB coupling loss"), and the Fig. 14 -1 level crosses at about +/-45-50 um; the printed form is
   +/-, so 50 matches the half-width definition without conversion. The axis unit of Figs. 14 / 15 is not labelled
   (noted). The design-row value (m2) is a full range of about 100 um halved: kept (arithmetic on a simulated value stays
   simulated, D83f), qualifier approx added, asymmetry noted. The Fig. 15 value (469-m2) is deleted for sample identity
   (D159), see the findings table.
3. wang2023c focal-spot FWHM as beam_diameter_um: not endorsed; rows deleted (see findings). Two definitions would be mixed:
   the registry parameter is a beam diameter at the interface of an expanded / collimated beam (rows elsewhere are 1/e2
   MFD-type diameters, e.g. dietrich2018 23 / 22 um), the paper's value is the intensity FWHM of a focused spot at the
   focal plane (skill rule 5). Registry gap below.
4. bernabe2012 1560 nm on the measured GC row: kept, with wavelength_inferred true (patched); the value is "close to 6 dB"
   at the Fig. 5 peak and the wavelength is a plot-axis reading.
5. dietrich2018 SMF-SMF mirror, expander and LIMOS rows at 1550 nm inferred: endorsed. Measurement wavelengths are not
   stated for these experiments; all design MFDs refer to 1550 nm (p.10 Methods); wavelength_inferred true with the
   reason in notes (skill rule 8).
6. Platforms: endorsed. Printed IP-Dip / IP-Dip2 reflectors and lenses `polymer` (dietrich2018, he2026 reflector, ishii2003
   epoxy parts); the a-Si / fused-silica metasurface, the GaN metasurface and LD, and the Si3N4-to-SU-8 flip-chip couplers
   (no hybrid enum value) `other`.
7. mu2016 LioniX B.V. fab: fab_org_ids stays empty. p.3 Sec. III "The Si3N4 chip is fabricated by LioniX B.V." is a rule-12
   statement, but LioniX B.V. is deliberately not in the registry (D278, time-aware vs org_lionix_international); the note
   on the five mu2016 Si3N4-chip rows records it. No change.
8. Other fab / facility calls: endorsed. yoshida2023 org_kyoto_university (p.8 author contributions, fabricators and
   co-fabricators all Kyoto; D395); bernabe2012 org_cea_leti ("We designed and processed", p.6 Sec. 4, all authors
   CEA-LETI); tyler2019 org_cea_leti (p.4 Sec. 3 "fabricated using LETI's 200 mm wafer fabrication platform", all authors

   org_aim_photonics (foundry, p.6 / p.9), metasurface empty (H.L. at MIT and 2Pi Inc., place not stated, D83c),
   facility org_center_for_nanoscale_systems on the reflector and metasurface (p.17 "carried out in part using the
   facilities of MIT.nano and the Center for Nanoscale Systems"); dietrich2018 empty (fabricator at KIT IMT, KIT IPQ and
   Vanguard Photonics, D83c; KNMF funding-only, D83e); ishii2003 empty ("We have developed two techniques", p.4, D80b);
   wang2023c empty (four orgs; no facility in the acknowledgements).
9. Classes: endorsed. he2026 freeform reflector as free_space_emitter.other (replaces diffractive surface emitters, emits a
   vertical Gaussian beam); dietrich2018 facet TIR mirrors micro_optics.other and ishii2003 45 deg waveguide mirror
   micro_optics.vertical_taper (registry: "reflector to PD or fiber"); he2026 AIM switch tree switch_fabric.other
   (hierarchical tree, not an NxN mesh, D158); wang2023c GaN LD kept as a component row (it is the device the metasurface
   is integrated on, not setup equipment).
