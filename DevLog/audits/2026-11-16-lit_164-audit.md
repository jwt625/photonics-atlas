## Coordinator questions

1. schuler2016 65 GHz on sample B (a). Recommendation: keep (no op). D159 covers a value whose sample identity is ambiguous; here the
   paper ties it: the abstract pairs "The device exhibits a 3 dB-bandwidth of 65 GHz" with the sample-B responsivities, the
   heterodyne paragraph (p.8-9) follows the sample-B impulse measurement with "our detector" and no change of device, and the summary
   (p.9) repeats the pairing. Fig. 4(b) not naming the sample is the only gap; the row notes say so. Alternative (strict D159): delete
   pd_sample_b-m3 and keep 65 GHz in component notes. Responsivities `author_estimate` (a): endorse. p.6 states the no-loss
   assumption explicitly ("assuming no losses in the 2 mm long waveguides and the mode coupler"), an assumed input under rule 4, while
   the paper's own 1.5 dB/cm cutback implies about 0.3 dB in 2 mm; the sample-B normalization is not restated but no other is given.
2. youngblood2014 3.6 vs 57 mA/W (b). Recommendation: keep one row (rule 9: one row per design; the paper never says the second device
   differs in design, the abstract quotes 57 mA/W as the device's responsivity, and the conditions name the device). Precedents:
   rao2019 "second device" value kept on its design row (lit_12 audit), kao2026 per-design row with the device not named (lit_85 audit). D159 is
   not triggered: "a different device" is an explicit identity, not an ambiguous one. Splitting would leave a second row with one
   metric and no geometry, and would make the Fig. 4 bandwidth device ambiguous between the two rows. The component note now says the
   geometry rows are the main device's (patched).
3. youngblood2014 GC ~20 % (b). Recommendation: keep `author_estimate` with "origin not stated" (rule 4: loss-budget term of unstated
   origin, used as the input of the responsivity normalization, p.9 Methods), `per_coupler`, C band inferred.
4. youngblood2014 bare Si MZI ER > 40 dB as `splitter.other` (b). Recommendation: keep (D74: `extinction_db` allowed for the
   interferometer built from the splitter, type in label / notes; own measurement, p.4 and Fig. 2(b)). It is the only quantitative
   result for the passive splitters (an ER > 40 dB bounds the split imbalance of the unbalanced MZI). Alternative: drop the row as a
   calibration structure; not recommended. Modulator values out as EO-atlas candidate (b): endorse (rule 2: gate-tuned absorption,
   64 % depth, 2.5 GHz modulation bandwidth, 18 dB added loss are modulator metrics); list youngblood2014 with the D168-style candidates.
5. shiue2015 responsivity basis. Recommendation: `author_estimate` for 0.36 A/W and 78 mA/W (patched): the in-waveguide power reference
   is never derived (p.5), the only coupling number is the 11 dB fiber-to-fiber total (p.4), so the per-facet split behind P_in is an
   unstated loss-budget term (rule 4; derose2011, hu2023 precedents); this also aligns the three papers of the batch. Alternative: keep
   `measured` / `derived` as distilled (drop the m1 / m2 ops) if the coordinator treats a waveguide-referenced responsivity as the
   paper's own calibration.
