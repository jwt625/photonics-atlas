## Coordinator questions

1. kim2021a `nonreciprocal.isolator` vs `nonreciprocal.other`. The device is a two-port add-drop chain whose only performance number is the
   forward / backward transmission contrast (S21 / S12, about 3 dB); `isolation_db` ("isolation magnitude") is exactly that quantity, and
   `nonreciprocal.other` is defined for multi-port routers (D169). Merged precedents classify two-port non-magnetic contrast devices as
   isolators even when the paper's wording differs (otterstrom2019 Brillouin racetrack "nonreciprocal transmission", lira2012, sohn2021,
   tian2021). Recommendation: keep `nonreciprocal.isolator` with the existing note that the paper says "non-reciprocal contrast", not
   isolator. Alternative: `nonreciprocal.other` (one field change on cmp_lit_kim2021a_aln_synthetic_hall_isolator).
2. bonneville2020 gain basis. Every gain value rests on two author estimates: the 0.5 +/- 0.3 dB/cm Film 3 loss (a range bounded by the
   film background and the Film 2 ring upper limit, informed by insertion loss; p.9) and the -4.5 dB per facet coupling estimate (p.8). D166
   (`derived`) covers gain computed from the source's own measured losses; these are estimates, so rule 4 gives `author_estimate` (D429
   ahmadi2022 precedent: net gain on an estimated / cited loss stays author_estimate). Recommendation: keep `author_estimate` on all gain rows
   (m1-m4).
3. bonneville2020 -8.5 dBm (deleted in this patch). cai2021 m3 / m5 store a 0-dB crossing as `psat_in_dbm` because that paper defines it as
   its saturation power; bonneville2020 states only "net internal gain was achieved up to ~-8.5 dBm launched signal power" and the inset
   shows compression from about -15 dBm. Recommendation: accept the delete (value in the component notes). Alternative: keep the row with
   the condition as written (drop the delete op and the notes change).
4. zhou2021a platform `lnoi_strip`. Support beyond the figures: p.2 "The 600-nm-thick Er3+-doped Z-cut LN waveguides ... are located on
   the top of a 2-um-thick silica layer" (the waveguide height equals the stated thickness and sits on silica), the trapezoid on silica in
   Fig. 1(d) SEM and Fig. 1(e) simulation, and the rim interference "indicating the varying thickness at the edge" (p.2). No etch depth or
   film thickness is printed separately. Merged PLACE rows differ by what each paper states (li2025, zhao2025a `lnoi_rib` with a stated
   partial ridge; wu2023a, zhou2025 empty where the etch depth is not stated). Recommendation: keep `lnoi_strip` (the p.2 sentence is a
   textual statement of a full-height waveguide on silica); optionally add the p.2 sentence to the platform note. Alternative: platform
   empty on the 4 rows (D422 / D423) with a `family_override` if the benchmark needs one.
5. zhou2021a `bw_3db_nm` > 40 nm (m6). The printed "3dB gain bandwidth of more than 40 nm centered around 1540 nm" (p.3) contradicts its own
   Fig. 6: with the 12.9 dB peak at 1545 nm only 1530, 1535 and 1545 nm lie within 3 dB (1540 nm is 8.4 dB). The p.2 "more than 40 nm
   bandwidth of internal net gain" (m5, `gain_bandwidth_nm`) is consistent with Fig. 6 (positive over 1520-1570 nm). Recommendation: keep m6
   as printed with the existing conflict note (D417; oser2019 / tossoun2024 precedents: identity clear, the paper's numbers disagree).
   Alternative: delete m6 and keep the statement in the component notes, which avoids a 3-dB bandwidth the data do not show reaching the
   benchmark.
6. vu2010 registry title. `data/registry/sources.csv` (`lit:vu2010`) and `references/vu2010/source.json` print "28dB/cm internal gain";
   page 1 prints "2.8dB/cm" (also abstract and conclusion: 14 dB over 5 cm). Recommendation: correct the title in both files to "...with net
   gain and 2.8dB/cm internal gain" (coordinator op outside the batch; no component row depends on it).
