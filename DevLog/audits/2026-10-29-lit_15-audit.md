## Rulings on the batch report's judgment calls

1. gao2019 org_neophotonics: not endorsed as a block. The acknowledgment (p.7) thanks named persons for optical
   assembly and testing without stating their affiliation, so D84b ("an org's own staff") cannot be applied; "we have
   demonstrated" / "developed prototype" and passive assembly do not qualify (D80b). Fab emptied on the laser,
   gain_siph_butt and gain_soa_lens. Kept on gain_soa_flipchip on a different basis: p.4 Sec. II-C "the initial gain
   chip/SOA sub-assemblies we have built", all authors NeoPhotonics (p.1), D72 / D80b.
2. isichenko2023 org_honeywell: endorsed. p.7 thanks a named person "at Honeywell for the fabrication", which places
   the fabrication at Honeywell (D84b; heim2025 precedent); not a facility (D72). Borderline note kept.
3. notaros2019 "fabricated at CNSE SUNY" (p.2 Sec. II) -> org_suny_polytechnic_institute: endorsed. The paper prints
   the co-author affiliation as "Colleges of Nanoscale Science and Engineering, State University of New York
   Polytechnic Institute" (p.1), so the paper itself places CNSE inside SUNY Poly; the registry has no CNSE org or alias
   (searched organizations.csv / org_aliases.csv for CNSE, Nanoscale, Albany, SUNY). Proposed alias "CNSE SUNY" /
   "Colleges of Nanoscale Science and Engineering" -> org_suny_polytechnic_institute (question 1). org_mit on the laser
   and film rows endorsed: film deposition "performed at MIT" (p.2) and "via reactive co-sputtering at MIT" (p.3) is a
   fabrication step at a named org (two-org fabrication, D80b).
4. boust2020 113.5 GHz as repetition_rate_ghz: not endorsed; moved to channel_spacing_ghz. The registry definition is
   a pulse repetition rate; D82g puts the line spacing of a non-pulsed comb in channel_spacing_ghz; the body (p.4)
   says line spacing and p.4-5 call the state an unstable MMS comb with sub-combs that are not exact FSR multiples. The
   abstract's "repetition rate" wording does not create pulses (D83b's wording criterion is subordinate to D82g here).
   comb_b_sil 2.04 THz stays channel_spacing_ghz: printed as line spacing / FSR, the 17 pulses are deduced from the
   spectrum, not characterized (D83b).
5. Hybrid pump-plus-resonator platforms: endorsed. boust2020 combs and gao2019 laser `other` with the stack in
   core_material (D71); couplings take the receiving waveguide layer. isichenko2023 SIL row `sin` is also acceptable
   (the diode is a fiber-pigtailed purchased part; merged laser.sil rows use sin / ge_silica).
6. dostart2020 bend loss per 180 deg as insertion_loss_db per_component: endorsed. bend_loss_db_per_90 is "loss per
   90-degree bend"; halving would be a derived value that the source never states; precedent dong2021 euler_bend-m1
   (per 180 deg, insertion_loss_db, "not entered as bend_loss_db_per_90"). Measured 0.003 dB and design 0.001 dB
   checked against p.3 Fig. 2(j) and p.11-12 Supp.
7. Other calls: fard2021 Si3N4-to-SU-8 taper as waveguide_platform.transition, platform other (D48, D71) endorsed;
   0.73 dB derived (authors' (4.84 - 2.44 - 0.18 - 0.75) / 2, Table I render checked), 0.52 dB author_estimate
   (assumed 1.73 dB/cm), edge facet 2.44 dB device_total (two different interfaces; Fig. 6 caption "dB/cm" typo)
   endorsed; org_aim_photonics on interposer rows ("fabricated at AIM Photonics foundry", p.2) endorsed.
   dostart2020 org_aim_photonics (MPW run, p.12 Supp. Sec. 3; rule 12 MPW service) endorsed. loi2019 fab empty
   endorsed ("as shown in our previous work" would satisfy D84a, but authors span Tyndall, X-Celeprint and CIT, D72 /
   D83c). lu2016 VERTILAS as vendor of the purchased VCSEL (p.4) is a rule-12 fab org, blocked by the registry
   (question 2). loi2019 MFD as 2 x simulated w0 stays simulated (D83f). gao2019 vernier filter as wdm.mrr (bw_fwhm_ghz
   needs wdm) acceptable.
