  Lc >= 1.0 mm; mean IL/facet over 50 I/Os with Lc >= 1.0 mm). Values whose caption ties them to one design (Lc 1.5 mm:
  Fig. 17 point, Fig. 18 band bound, Fig. 19 tolerance, Fig. 20 IL/facet) move to Lc 1.5 mm rows (rule 9; lit_05 xu2022

  states it for the flip-chip bonded samples and the whole optical path (p.10 Sec. VI-F); Fig. 25 shows the Lc 2.5 mm
  TE example only.

  PWG difference contains two couplers plus an S-bend (p.7); the paper designs the unit to measure "a single" coupling

  stated; since every text value is an upper bound, the per-coupler bound holds whether or not the authors halved, so
  per_component with "halving not stated" is not an overstatement. Not loopback_half (no stated halving, on-chip
  component, skill rule 6). Basis measured (D167 analogue).
