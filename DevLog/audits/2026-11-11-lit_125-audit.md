## Coordinator questions

1. johnson2020 fab `org_university_of_bristol` (asked). p.5 Sec. 3 is a first-person fabrication account ("For this fabrication, we used"
   the resist; "we are left with" the capping layer after etching), and all three authors are at the University of Bristol (QETLabs and the
   Quantum Engineering CDT, both University of Bristol, p.1). That is rule 12's first-person statement plus an unambiguous single org, not
   affiliation alone (D80b); same construction as zhang2023b / lee2025 (D554) and chen2000 (D422). The acknowledgment thanks a named person
   for fabrication assistance without an org (no facility). Recommendation: keep the fab; the component note may drop "borderline".
2. lin2021 488 nm transition labels (asked). Not a D159 case (one component, the question is the polarization of each label) but a D156 test.
   The 400 dpi render shows a consistent convention at the other four wavelengths: triangles sit on the dotted (TE) lines and squares on the
   solid (TM) lines (405 nm triangle at 50 transitions on the dotted 0.41 line, 640 nm squares on the solid 0.07 line to 200 transitions). At
   488 nm the triangle endpoint is the higher one (about 27.4 vs 27.1 dB at 100 transitions), the 0.28 label sits beside it and 0.27 beside the
   square. Recommendation: keep TE 0.28 / TM 0.27 (notes now record the evidence). Alternative under a strict D156 reading: one row 0.27-0.28 dB
   with `polarization: both` and the two rows removed; the values differ by 0.01 dB, so either choice changes little.
3. shi2023 0.48 dB/cm (asked). The Fig. 10(c) value is insertion loss divided by length with both oil-matched facets included; the paper calls
   that facet term very small (p.8), and the 10 cm and 6.55 cm cases of the same channels give the same 0.48 dB/cm average, which is what a
   negligible length-independent term would produce. Recommendation: keep it as `prop_loss_db_per_cm` with basis `derived` (division by length,
   D80a) and the condition stating that facets are not subtracted (patched). Alternative: delete the row and keep the value in notes (add a
   delete_metrics op), leaving the waveguide row with only the 4.85 dB fiber-to-fiber value.
4. D417 cases (asked), all confirmed at the locators:
   - hammond2022 single-polarization centre wavelength: p.11 text (+13 nm from 1542 to 1555 nm) and the Fig. 5(a) label (lambda0 = 1555 nm)
     agree; only the Fig. 5 caption prints 1570 nm, the value of the dual-polarization device in Fig. 6. Keep 1555 nm.
   - hammond2022 dual-polarization high-resolution simulated efficiency: p.8 text -5.6 dB, the Fig. 6(a) simulation label -5.6 dB, and p.11
     "-7.0 dB, which is 1.4 dB less than the simulated result" all give 5.6 dB; the Fig. 2 caption's -3.0 dB repeats the single-polarization
     caption wording (and the dual device's standard-resolution value). Keep 5.6 dB.
   - tahara2024 etch depth: not a self-contradiction. p.4 gives d = 96 nm for the high-efficiency design, p.7 gives 97 nm for the gratings of the
     fabricated structure (p.6 also gives 97 nm as the flat design's d). Keep 96 nm `design_target` with the rewritten note; no second etch-depth
     row (a fabricated-depth row would also be `design_target`-like, since 97 nm is not stated as measured).
5. tahara2024 fab on the SSC and Si-waveguide rows (audit). The fabrication sentence (p.7) names "the optimized structure" (the GC). The SSC and
   2.1 mm waveguide sit on the same fabricated chip (Fig. 8), but their loss values come from separate 5.1 mm and 35.9 mm samples whose
   fabrication is not stated. Recommendation: keep AIST on both (same chip and process family; the YNU-AIST team states one process), with the
   per-component caveat in mind (D515 / D463); strict alternative: empty fab and `process_name` on these two rows.
6. lin2021 other Fig. 4(c) tip widths (audit). The measured WEC sweep at Wbase 0.75 um also has points at WEC about 0.09 / 0.14 / 0.33 um (TM about
   5.45 / 4.17 / 4.76 dB, TE about 5.38 / 3.55 / 6.41 dB); the text names only 0.24 and 0.19 um. Recommendation: not added, since each new
   component would rest on a figure-read tip width (x position with +/-0.02 um bars) as well as figure-read losses. Alternative: three

7. shi2023 Shanghai Optoweave Technology (audit). The p.9 acknowledgment thanks a named person of this company for advice and help on OPCB
