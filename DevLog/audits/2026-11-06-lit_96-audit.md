## Points checked as asked

- zhao2025 1 dB net gain (`author_estimate`): kept. p.4 subtracts an on-chip L-band waveguide loss of about 1 dB whose origin is not stated;
  it matches neither the OFDR minimum of 2.5 dB/m over 0.56 m (1.4 dB, p.3) nor the 2.5 dB/m assumed for the analytical curves (Fig. 2 caption).
  Rule 4 (loss-budget term of unstated origin) applies; D166 `derived` would need the subtracted loss to be the paper's own measured value. The
  on-off gain peak m2 re-read at 600 dpi: highest measured point 2.27 dB at 1645 nm (tick spacing 4.56 px/nm), batch 2.3 dB / 1645 nm +/- 3 nm ok.
- zhao2025 theoretical OPA designs (Fig. 4(b), W 1600 / 1828 nm, 2 m, 0.6 dB/m assumed): keep as `row_kind design` (rule 9; bolk2018 design
  row D467, su2017 D459). Gain "about 20 dB" `simulated` correct. Band edges re-read on the render: red curve about 1197-1430 nm, green about
  1435-1690 nm, batch within +/- 5 nm. The range is the gain-band extent, not where 20 dB holds (plateau tops only); notes say so. Geometry miss
  (H1 300 nm) added on both.
- nikolai2025 107 %/W/cm2 and 4 dB per facet (`author_estimate`): kept (Q1). p.5: pump coupling calibrated assuming identical input and output
  facet losses of about 4 dB per facet; the SH collection coefficient is fitted to energy conservation in the high-power data and then applied to
  the low-power slope. The value rests on an equal-split assumption plus a fitted output coefficient (li2025a D462 precedent). The alternative is
  D80a (model extraction with stated assumptions -> derived); the total transmission behind the 4 dB is not printed.
- nikolai2025 derived > 11.1 % off-chip SH ratio: kept (Q3). Arithmetic on two printed values at the same operating point (abstract: above 0.5 W
  off-chip SH under 4.5 W pump); lower bound direction correct; reference planes in condition (SH after the dichroic mirror, pump in fiber).
  Fig. 4(b) shows the DM-corrected about 0.6 W at the 4.5 W point, consistent. The paper prints no ratio, so the row is a computed metric; its
  value as a cross-paper comparison is limited by the mixed planes.
- raghunathan2025 6 % / 5 % (`author_estimate`): kept. The abstract calls the 5 % on-chip in photon flux; the text describes no coupling-loss
  correction at the 1283 / 1260 nm input and 704 / 698 nm output (D460 franken2025 precedent). Q values 1.5e6 (780 nm in text, 778 nm on the
  Fig. 2(a) panel and in Fig. 4(a); D417 note kept), 4.5e5, 2.8e5 confirmed. Fab empty (no fabrication statement, D80b); facility CNF ("performed in
  part at", p.8) correct.
- yan2025 fab empty and platform `lnoi_rib`: both kept. Fab: C.Y. "designed and fabricated the devices" (p.7) with affiliations NTU and Sichuan
  University, site not printed (D433 hiratani2024). Platform: Fig. 1(a) labels the stack (+LN / -LN on SiO2 on Si) and draws a ridge on a slab;
  Fig. 1(d) mode plots and Fig. 1(e) SEM show the same; D422 / D423 forbid defaulting an unstated platform, and here the figure states it (Q7).
