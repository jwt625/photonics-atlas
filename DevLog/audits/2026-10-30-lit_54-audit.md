## Rulings on the distiller's questions (numbered as in the task)

1. schuck2013 row structure. Evidence: the waveguides are 600 nm wide for visible and 1 um wide for telecom light (p.2
   Fig. 1 caption, Fig. 1(b) label "w_wg = 0.6-1 um"); couplers are wavelength-specific (two GC rows); Table 1 indexes
   detectors by nanowire width / length and wavelength only (p.4). So the 768 and 1542 nm DEoc of one nanowire design are
   almost certainly different physical detectors. The dark-count detector is "a 75 nm-wide NbTiN nanowire of 40 um length"
   (p.2) and the jitter detector "a 40 um long, 75 nm wide nanowire SSPD" (p.6, Ic 16 vs 18 uA: possibly another device
   or cooldown); neither waveguide width is stated, and the authors combine that dark-count curve with the DEoc at both
   wavelengths for NEPoc (p.4). Options: (a) keep one row per nanowire design (as distilled) with the per-wavelength
   waveguide widths in notes, D165 analogue (a row defined by the nanowire design, wavelength-specific waveguides as
   conditions); (b) split per waveguide (9 rows) and, per D159, move the 5 dark-count, 2 Ic, 2 jitter and 1 decay rows
   of the 75 / 40 design into notes, dropping the paper's headline milli-Hz result. Recommendation: (a), kept unpatched;
   skill rule 9 read strictly favours (b), so this needs a ruling.
2. Cryogenic GC losses: `per_coupler` with `method unspecified` endorsed for both papers (skill rule 6: scope = what the
   number refers to, method = how it was isolated). pernice2012: the Table 1 system efficiencies equal OCDE x 0.5 x
   10^(-L/10) with L about 13.2 dB for all four detectors, i.e. the authors apply one input-coupler loss (device-specific,
   taken on the control port, p.9); note added to m1. schuck2013: "transmission of light from the fiber array into the
   waveguide" via one grating coupler, calibrated per device (p.5). `unspecified` would fit only if it were unclear
   whether one or two couplers are meant, which is not the case here.
3. mauthe2020: (a) mapping endorsed: the printed affiliation is "IBM Research Europe" in Rueschlikon, the site of the
   registry's IBM Research - Zurich lab (org_ibm_research_zurich, ROR 02js37d36); org_aliases.csv has no alias for it.
   Recommend registering the printed variant "IBM Research Europe" (Rueschlikon) as an alias of org_ibm_research_zurich
   (not as a new org; "IBM Research Europe" alone also covers other European IBM labs, so the alias row should carry the
   site in notes). (b) 0.68 A/W / 62 % not entered: endorsed (D159; "the measured device", width not stated, p.4; the
   responsivity setup uses cross-like structures, p.6; Supp. Note 10 not cached; it would be `author_estimate` anyway,
   since the incident power is calculated from fiber position and device area). (c) > 25 GHz only on the 200 nm row:
   endorsed (p.4 ties the value to a 200 nm device at -1.5 V; Fig. 6(a) 60 / 500 nm curves have no stated bias, and the
   abstract / conclusion statements are the same measurement).
4. pfeifle2012 zero rows confirmed. Non-modulator candidates: slot-waveguide propagation loss "more than 17 dB/mm" and the
   LC-added 16 dB/mm (p.13) are the loss of the phase shifter's own waveguide section (EO-row prop_loss_db_per_cm,
   skill rule 2: EO atlas); excess loss < 4.5 dB for 35 pi (p.13) is a modulator IL; "< 1 dB/mm" is cited (ref. 19, rule
   3); "< 0.3 dB for a 100 um device" is a projection; grating couplers and strip-to-slot converters (p.9) carry no
