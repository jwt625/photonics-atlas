## Questions raised by the distiller (evidence-based recommendations)

1. streshinsky2013 PSGC and D396. Recommendation: keep the single fiber_chip_coupler.grating row with the isolation as
   crosstalk_db (no polarization.pbs row). Evidence: the registry lists "2D/PSGC" under fiber_chip_coupler.grating;
   crosstalk_db is allowed for fiber_chip_coupler; merged PSGC rows (wang2022b, OFC Tu2J.2) are single grating rows;
   the isolation is not a separately characterized function but a bound computed from the coupler's own PDL in the
   same coupling path (p.9), so a pbs row would hold only those two moved values. D396 targets one device measured in
   functions of different classes with class-specific measurements (disk as resonator / SHG / laser). If the coordinator
   wants PSGCs in the polarization view, the alternative is a polarization.pbs row with per_db ge 24 / ge 8.4 moved from
   m5 / m6 (not duplicated), cross-noted. Basis of the isolation: derived is right. Eq. 3 (render p.9) is
   PDL = 2 x 20 log10(1 + 10^(-P_iso / 20)); inverting it gives 24.0 dB for 1.06 dB and 8.4 dB for 5.6 dB (checked). The
   worst-case assumption (all PDL from cross-polarization interference) makes the result a lower bound that holds for
   any other PDL source (p.9), which `ge` encodes; no assumed input enters the bound, so not author_estimate.
2. jones2013.
   - Gap 410 vs 400 nm: keep 410 nm measured with the conflict in notes. 410 nm is printed twice (p.1, p.12), 400 nm in
     Sec. 5 (p.11) and as the upper end of the tested range (p.10); the ellipsometry error is +/- 50 nm (p.5); the Fig. 9
     largest-gap points are plotted at about 410-420 nm (render p.9). Not D159 (one device, identity clear); D417
     tossoun2024 precedent.
   - Crosstalk: the printed 49.4 dB TE exceeds the stated 46 dB TE noise floor (Eq. 5, render p.11), and Fig. 9 data at
     that gap read about 43-44 dB TE and about 47 dB TM, i.e. the opposite TE / TM ordering. The paper does not state
     whether Fig. 9 plots port 1 or the geometric mean of Eq. (4). Recommendation: keep both values as printed with
     `ge` (stated identically three times, p.1 / p.11 / p.12) and the conflict in notes (patched); alternative if the
     coordinator reads it as a TE / TM identity ambiguity: D159, delete m1 / m2 and keep both numbers in component
     notes (the row keeps simulated crosstalk / loss and the measured gap as metrics).
   - Loss-budget rows (p.11: propagation "estimated to be 1.4 and 4.0 dB/cm", bends "estimated to be < 0.1 and ~1.5 dB
     per turn", "around 12 dB is attributed to" each facet): budget terms of the < 30 dB system loss with no stated
     method, so author_estimate with "origin not stated" (rule 4), as entered. The waveguide of the 1.4 / 4.0 dB/cm is
     not named; attaching it to the Si routing strip (the routing layer, p.4) is noted on the row; acceptable.
   - Classes: crossing.multilevel for the nitride-over-silicon vertical crossing (registry subtype "multilevel"; merged
     fu2023, nan2025, shang2017) and waveguide_platform.transition for the 100 um Si-to-Si3N4 adiabatic transition
     (skill rule 10, D80 on-chip inter-layer transitions; notaros2019 precedent): both endorsed; crossing.other is not
     needed.
