## Rulings on the batch report's judgment calls

- chen2024a QC-DC class: splitter.directional_coupler endorsed for the coupler (D165: a device used for its coupling
  ratio stays a DC; IL / ER / split ratio are splitter values). Its PCM segments are characterized as non-volatile
  actuators (endurance, four levels, p.5), so per D396 (one physical device, functions of different classes -> one row
  per class) and the meng2023 sibling-row precedent (D254) a `phase_shifter.phase_change` row qc_dc_pcm now carries
  endurance_cycles and n_levels. switch_fabric.other (D158) does not fit: the device is a 2x2 coupler, not an MZI
  switch element. The endurance-test contrasts 8.5 / 4.5 dB stay on qc_dc as extinction_db (splitter allows it, D74).
  The registry-gap request (endurance / levels for splitter) is withdrawn.
