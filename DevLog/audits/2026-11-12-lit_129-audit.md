## Coordinator questions

1. wu2025 measured IL scope (asked). The paper attributes -1.05 / -1.09 dB to the PSGC itself (p.1, p.8, p.9 caption); the Fig. 6(d) sketch
   shows the PSGC with two conventional GCs in the path and no de-embedding is described. Recommendation: keep `per_coupler`, method
   `unspecified`, basis `measured` (D167, D441: a per-coupler value stated directly without shown arithmetic). Alternative: scope
   `unspecified`; no op either way.
2. wu2025 fabricated stack (asked). The fabrication text names only the commercial SOI, EBL and the Bosch etch; the Al reflector / 530 nm oxide
   are design values (Table 2). The rows that carry them (m8-m13) are `simulated` / `design_target`, and the component notes state the gap.
   Recommendation: keep as is; flag before any showcase or benchmark use of the 1.05 dB value as a single-etch, no-reflector result.
3. vaerenbergh2021 1-dB bandwidth (asked). Sec. 5.1 prints 20.5 nm (67-die median, with 28.1 nm 3-dB and the no-envelope 18.9 nm in the same
   passage); abstract and conclusion print 21.3 nm; p.7 prints 21.2 nm for the five-die 139 nm family (on the ID DOE row). Recommendation:
   keep 20.5 nm with the conflict note (D417); the section that defines the measurement is the most specific.
4. vaerenbergh2021 PDK 8 deg GC fab (asked). "the foundry's PDK" (p.11) refers back to the pilot line foundry named on p.2 / p.3 as CEA Leti;
   p.11 also says the 1.35 dB targeted-OPC design was not included on "our immersion lithography wafer", which implies the measured PDK GC was.
   Recommendation: keep `org_cea_leti` (D453 / D455 style: org named with the process); scope `unspecified` stays.
5. gordillo2019 class (asked). The paper's title and abstract call it a connector, the body mostly "plug-and-play couplers" / "funnel
   couplers"; the registry lists 3D-printed out-of-plane couplers under `fiber_chip_coupler.other`, while the merged jung2025 split put the
   printed alignment pins in `fiber_interface.connector` and the printed TIR coupler in `fiber_chip_coupler.other`. Here one printed part does
   both (funnel receptacle plus polymer waveguide and TIR mirror onto the GC), and the fiber couples in-plane. Recommendation: keep
   `fiber_interface.connector` (the defining feature is the mechanical receptacle; the 0.05 dB is relative to the bare GC, so it should not
   rank among coupler losses). Alternative: `fiber_chip_coupler.other` (one-field op on cmp_lit_gordillo2019_funnel_connector; all its
   parameters are allowed there).
6. Basis of printed relative / extracted values (audit, patched). gordillo2019 0.05 dB and vaerenbergh2021 R1 -16.5 dB were `derived`; both are
   printed by the paper from its own data with no arithmetic shown or done by the distiller, so `measured` (rule 4, D167 / D452, D80a), with
   the caveats (different normalization wavelength; fringe-amplitude estimate) in notes. Recommendation: accept.
7. zhang2025d IL conflict (asked). Table 2 Exp. prints "<2" dB (220 nm ER > 30 dB band) and "~2" dB (315 nm ER > 10 dB band); the conclusion
   says ILs below 0.9 dB over the measured > 220 nm band, which repeats the simulated abstract bound; Fig. 8 shows the wanted-port traces
   within about 0-2 dB. Recommendation: keep the Table 2 values (m8 and the added m25) with the conflict note (D417); do not enter the 0.9 dB
   as measured.
8. tian2019 height step row_kind (audit). Sec. 2 (p.6) describes the abrupt 150-to-220 nm step at the through port as part of the device, and
   the taper-etched through waveguide ends 150 nm high by construction, but the fabricated layout of the output is not described and the
   values are simulated only. Recommendation: keep `design` (no op). Alternative under D80e: `fabricated` (one-field op).
