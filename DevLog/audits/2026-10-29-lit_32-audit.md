## Rulings on the distiller's judgment calls and the coordinator's questions

1. bogaerts2007a fab org_imec vs brouckaert2007 fab empty: both endorsed, each consistent with its own text.
   bogaerts2007a: "We fabricated the duplexer chip" (p.7 Sec. 5) plus a thanks to named staff "in IMEC" for the process
   development and to the P-line for the actual processing (p.12 Acknowledgments) = own-staff fabrication help placed
   at IMEC (D84b borderline, note kept; bogaerts2006 precedent in lit_06). brouckaert2007: "We fabricated" (p.2 Sec. II,
   p.4 Sec. IV, process in refs [9], [15]) by authors under one joint affiliation line naming both Ghent University and
   IMEC (p.1), and no acknowledgment section: the fabricating org is not unambiguous (D72 / D80b; dumon2004 /
   mangal2018 precedent). The difference is the acknowledgment text, not the group.
2. akiyama2022 O-band with wavelength_inferred true: endorsed. No wavelength in the text; the measured spectra (Fig. 7
   axis 1304-1316 nm, Fig. 9 1305-1315 nm) and the praseodymium-doped fiber amplifier (p.5) place it in the O-band;
   reason in each note (skill rule 8, schema: plot axis is an inference source). The 43.8 dB signal-to-crosstalk-floor
   contrast (p.4) stays out (contrast between two dashed levels of Fig. 7, not a per-port crosstalk); the Fig. 8
   -44.5 dB label marks the highest 1-ch crosstalk point (render checked), extracted_from_figure ok.
3. ryf2002 adjacent-port crosstalk 58 dB: kept without qualifier. The p.2 render shows the glyph; it is the en dash
   (minus) that the PDF's font mis-maps, the same mapping that garbles the apostrophe in "fabric's" on that page; no
   "less than" wording. Note corrected (above). The abstract's "A38dB" glyph is a different character, but m3 uses the
   p.2 wording "less than -38dB" (gt), so it does not depend on it.
4. sattari2020 conclusion "> 50 dB" vs results "> 20 dB" (active-state port extinction): endorsed as entered. Not a
   numeric conflict: the results bound the active-state extinction from below (> 20 dB, m4) and give the actuation-curve

   isolation figure. 70 nm "ridge height" of the shallow section entered as etch_depth_nm: endorsed (ridge height above
   the slab = shallow etch depth). Fab org_imec (standard iSiPP50G at IMEC, foundry) + org_epfl (authors' post-processing
   at CMi, D83c) and facility CMi ("performed at", p.2): endorsed.
