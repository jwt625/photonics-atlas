## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. buf2015 fab (judgment a): keep empty. The fabrication verbs are first person ("We pattern", "we perform", "we developed a
   specific process", p.3, p.7-8), but "we" spans STMicroelectronics (11 authors, three sites) and Luxtera (3), no sentence
   names the fab or site ("developed into a 300 mm manufacturing facility", p.1), and the ceramic-package sentence ("being
   used within STMicroelectronics", p.4) is about packaging. D80b / D154 / Contract v7 leave it empty; inferring ST from the
   300 mm process is outside knowledge. Note for consistency: the merged OFC rows cmp_ofc2026_tu2d4_* carry
   org_stmicroelectronics on the weaker basis "the paper presents it as ST's platform" (PIC100G); guerber2017 / guerber2018
   have an explicit "ST Crolles" statement. If the benchmark should place PIC25G under ST, do it in config/benchmark.yaml
   (family_override / affiliation-foundry rule, D914 / D925), not in the data. Keep process_name "PIC25G" (printed; named
   process, no named foundry, Contract v7).
2. buf2015 bases and exclusions (judgment a): keep all as entered. Table I and Fig. 6 statistics and the SPGC values the text
   says were "measured in epoxy / in air" are measured; SMW 1.4 dB/cm and PSGC 3.1 dB at 1490 nm sit in the same "typical
   performances" paragraph with no measurement wording and no data shown: author_estimate per D544 (velha2016). Table I 3.2
   dB over the text 3.25 dB: keep (Table I is the stated mean with its std; the conflict is in notes, D417). DSOI GC values:
   keep out; ref. [26] is the authors' own GFP 2014 paper (Baudot ... Boeuf), so D259 applies to 0.85 / 2.2 dB, and the
   0.6 dB average improvement has no parameter. HSPM values: keep out (Si PN depletion phase modulator, skill rule 2 EO-atlas
   scope; D250 rejected per-length phase-shifter loss). Recommend recording the HSPM values as an EO-atlas candidate rather
   than a component-registry gap.
3. sapra2019 design rows and the ref. [23] comparison (judgment b): keep all 24 design rows. The ref. [23]-stack designs are
   the authors' own optimizations on another group's layer stack, not values of ref. [23]: rule 3 / D259 bite only on Wang et
   al.'s -3.6 dB / 84 nm, which are correctly not entered. Keep the 25-deg tie: the Fig. 9 25-deg marker reads -2.297 dB
   against the printed -2.29 dB, and the compared method used 25 deg (p.5); no competing component exists, so D159 does not
   apply. Fab / facility on the 3 fabricated GCs and the waveguide as entered; design rows without orgs are right.
4. sapra2019 measured 3-dB widths (judgment b): keep 52 / 94 nm as figure reads. The paper's reference ("distribution
   centre") and the peak give the same widths here: the -3 dB span centres (about 1512 / 1513 nm) sit within 0.05 dB of the
   peaks on both curves. Caveat for the record: the paper's "7-19 nm" measured-simulated difference (p.5) does not reproduce
   for the 100 nm-target device against Fig. 3 (116 nm vs 94 nm = 22 nm); the reference simulated width is not stated, so
   no change (D417-type note optional). Keep 34 dB/cm derived (D256) and the 1550 nm inferred wavelength.
5. shi2014 back reflection (judgment c): keep `derived` (D80a: model extraction from own measured ripple, the authors'
   arithmetic) with `gt` 20 and O band inferred; the inset shows the ripple at the spectrum peak (~1302 nm), so 1302 nm
   inferred would also be defensible. Fab org_astar_ime and empty process_name kept (no PDK name printed).

   su2026 use silica); the three merged fang2010 rows (cmp_lit_fang2010_suspended_sio2_ssc, _design_30nm_tip,
