## Rulings on the distiller's questions

1. cavalier2011 SiN test-waveguide loss (about 15 dB/cm) and end-fire coupling loss (about 15 dB): keep the exclusion.
   Evidence: the p.3 roadmap states that the waveguide-development and nanowire studies, "already described in previous
   works" (ref. 8, the authors' IEEE TAS 2011 paper), are briefly recalled; the p.4-5 waveguide paragraphs end with a
   citation to ref. 8 (p.5, monomode guiding "as required for the application"); the test waveguides are not the device
   waveguides (p.4: better photomasks were chosen "for our final SWIFTS device"). D408 keeps values of a journal
   extension unless the value sentence or figure attributes them; here the attribution is explicit for the whole
   recalled study and repeated by the closing citation, and the paper is not an extension of ref. 8. The about -32 dB
   total attenuation is built on those values (excluded too). Tc / Jc (Sec. IV.B, Fig. 5) are new measurements on this
   chip and not affected. Recommendation: exclusion as staged.
2. derose2011 37 GHz at 0 V / 30 uA: keep on the 1.3 x 4 um2 row. The paragraph's only singular device is the
   1.3 x 4 um2 detector, the cited figure (Fig. 5(b) and inset) is that device, and the sentence contrasts the 0 V roll-off
   at 0.3 mA, which is the inset's 0 V point (render: a few GHz at 0 V, 300 uA). D159 covers identity ambiguous between
   components; here the text offers one candidate. 45 GHz bias: keep the Table 1 footnote (1 V, the systematic condition
   of all 15 rows); the Fig. 5(b) caption 2 V is now in the metric notes; the inset shows about 45 GHz from 0.8 to 2 V.
3. Responsivity basis: endorse all three. derose2011 `author_estimate` (losses "calibrated out", calibration origin not
   stated, rule 4; merged precedents lischke2021_ge300_lateral_pin_pd_1310-m3 and nozaki2016_phc_pd_l1p7-m5).
   vivien2009 `author_estimate` (the reference branch is measured, but the input power assumes an equal star-coupler
   split and applies a rib loss of about 1 dB/cm whose origin is not stated, p.5; unlike pospischil2013, where the
   reference waveguide power was used directly). yin2007 `derived` (about 126 uW at the Ge from the paper's own 3.0 dB
   facet loss and 0.55 dB/cm FP loss, p.4; D166 / D80a). QE rows follow; ref_plane on_chip added (patch).
4. Fab orgs: endorse all.
   - mcnab2003 / vlasov2004 `org_ibm_watson`: "etched in a standard 200mm CMOS line at IBM Watson Research Center"
     (mcnab2003 p.6 Sec. 3, vlasov2004 p.2 Sec. 2.1) names the org and site (rule 12); MRL staff thanks are staff
     thanks at a company cleanroom (D80c, D404), no facility.
   - campenhout2010 `org_ibm`: fabrication used the shallow-trench isolation module of standard IBM CMOS technology and
     a standard IBM NiSi module (p.1): the authors' org's own process (D84a); the site is not stated, so the parent, not
     org_ibm_watson chosen from the affiliation (D154 spirit).
   - derose2011 `org_sandia_national_laboratories`: the photodiodes are integrated into the Sandia CMOS compatible silicon
     photonics process flow, described step by step (p.3-4), and the conclusion says "We have fabricated" in that flow
     (p.7): rule 12 (named org's process) and D84a; IQE and MIT co-authors have no stated fabrication role.
   - vivien2009 `org_cea_leti` (borderline, both rows): thanks to the staff of the LETI 200 mm clean room "for the
     fabrication" of the optical structures (p.6) place the fabrication at CEA-LETI per D84b (xie2024 / bhasker2026);
     three co-authors are at CEA-LETI. D82c concerns facility owners and does not apply; per D84b this is never a
     facility basis, so facility_org_ids stays empty. Ge growth site not stated (noted).
5. (new) vlasov2004 basis: confirm `derived` for the 7 bend rows and the loss-spectrum row (D80a: difference of two
   structures, cascade / N). Alternative: keep `measured` as a cut-back; not recommended, the merged `measured` bend
   rows are multi-point slope fits (papadovasilakis2023a, song2024a) while dangel2013 (a loop divided by 4) is `derived`.
6. (new) mcnab2003 F-S converter platform `polymer` (D82f): confirm.
7. (new, no patch) derose2011 platform `soi`: the paper never says SOI or gives a BOX; it states silicon and nitride
