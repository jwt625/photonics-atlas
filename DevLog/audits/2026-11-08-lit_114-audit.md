## Coordinator questions

1. gehring2019 -2.07 dB mean / 0.35 dB std (19 devices): per coupler or per pair? Recommendation: per coupler, `loopback_half`, derived
   (patched). The paper uses "coupling efficiency" / "coupling loss" for the single-coupler quantity throughout (p.2 abstract "coupling
   efficiency well above -8 dB" is the p.6 per-coupler "each coupler ... less than -8 dB"; Fig. 2 and Fig. 3 axes "Coupling [dB]" are
   the square-rooted per-coupler spectra, p.5). A per-pair reading gives about -1.04 dB per coupler, better than the optimized geometry
   presented as best (Fig. 2(a) about -1.62 dB at 1550 nm; Fig. 3(c) peak about -1.6 dB at 1550 nm). This is the paper's own usage
   plus its data, not a guess (rule 6). Drop the m3 / m4 ops to keep `unspecified`.
2. ma2015 receiver-level PDL on the PSR row? Recommendation: notes only (patched: 4 rows deleted, values in component notes). No
   single-PSR test structure exists (p.6); the measured 0.7 +/- 0.2 dB is the receiver's, and p.8 attributes the excess over the 0.1 dB
   PSR simulation to the edge coupler, the DCs and the tuner losses, so the value would rank the PSR on other components' PDL
   (system-level values stay out: lit_42 / lit_112 audit logic). The prototype row keeps its simulated IL / PDL and geometry (D80e).
3. lacava2016 p.4 has no render: are the p.4 values confirmable elsewhere? Yes. p.4 carries the method (CET = IL / 2 on the Fig. 2
   structure; its interleaved fragments still read so), the 750 nm TOX and the setup. The method and both numbers are restated on p.1
   (abstract: 7.5 dB for input 2D-GC to two identical 2D-GCs, worst case with waveguide loss neglected, -3.75 dB per 2D-GC); 750 nm is
   on the p.3 Fig. 1 inset render ("750nmTOX") and p.2 ("750 top oxide"); the 1510-1590 nm ECL, SMF-28 and the 15 deg incidence are on
   the p.5 Fig. 3 render. No entered value depends on p.4 alone; the 0.01 nm OSA resolution (p.4 only) is not entered.
4. Basis of per-coupler values the paper prints after a stated halving / square root (gehring2019 m1, m3-m5; lacava2016 m1; zhang2014
   dbr m1, nodbr m1; ding2014 al m1) and of the zhong2026 "calculated" 0.081 dB per facet: derived or measured? Recommendation: derived
   (patched), following D516 (marchetti2017 / zhou2022: stated arithmetic -> derived; zhou2022 is the same equal-coupler halving as
   zhang2014) and rule 4; D167 / D452 measured stays for papers that show no arithmetic. Merged practice is mixed (loopback_half rows:
   134 derived, 98 measured; jia2014 kept measured in lit_113). Drop the `basis: derived` ops to keep measured.
5. jung2020 collimator pairs `micro_optics.other` -> `fiber_interface.other`? Recommendation: yes (patched), same group, same C-lens
   platform and same role as the merged jung2018 collimator pairs (D535). The isolator (`nonreciprocal.isolator`), BPF / dichroic WDM
   (`wdm.tff`) and splitter (`splitter.other`) rows keep their functional classes (jung2018 isolators precedent).
6. zhong2026 worst ICXT -39 dB after the 115 km link: keep as a FIFO row? Recommendation: delete, value in notes (patched); p.7 says the
   degradation from -46.21 dB comes from accumulated link effects. The B2B pair value (m5, two spliced FIFOs) stays with that condition.
