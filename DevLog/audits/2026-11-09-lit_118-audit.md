## Coordinator questions

1. hsu2025 39 GHz. The value is the 3-dB E-O response of VCSEL plus 1 km graded-index SMF (Fig. 4(c), Fig. 6(a)); p.4 attributes the rise
   from 22 GHz to the VCSEL chirp interacting with the fiber's negative dispersion, and p.5 calls it the bandwidth of the VCSEL "connected
   with the fiber channel". `bw3db_ghz` on a laser row is the device's modulation bandwidth; a fiber-length-dependent channel response is a
   link value (D542). Recommendation: keep it in the component notes only (as batched); the BTB 22 GHz rows carry the device value.
2. osorniomartinez2025 PHIX. p.3: the chip was packaged in the standard characterization package offered by PHIX B.V. (8 deg facet polish,
   PM fiber-array attach with epoxy, TEC); PHIX B.V. is also an author affiliation (p.1). The edge-coupler row is defined as the packaged
   fiber-chip interface, whose loss depends on the polish and attach, so PHIX on it follows D399 (assembly maker on the assembled row only).
   The EDWA row is a chip-level amplifier made from the ref. [13] wafer batch with no fabrication org stated; putting PHIX there would place a
   packaging house in the Al2O3 foundry column of the benchmark. Recommendation: PHIX on the coupler row only (as batched). Alias gap:
   "PHIX B.V." -> `org_phix_photonics_assembly` is not in `org_aliases.csv` (org round).
3. hsu2025 coupling row class. The lens is on the setup fiber (SMF-28 lens fiber, p.3), not a fabricated optic on the chip;
   `micro_optics.facet_lens` is defined as a facet-attached (3D-printed) microlens, and merged micro-optics VCSEL rows carry a fabricated
   optic (dietrich2018, maier2022). Merged lensed-fiber rows are `fiber_chip_coupler.edge`; a surface emitter is not an edge coupler, so
   `fiber_chip_coupler.other` (tapered / lensed-fiber interface, D138) with platform `gaas` (emitting layer, D82f) fits. Recommendation:
   keep.
4. zhao2016. (a) HHI fab on the SOA gain chip: p.1 Sec. II says the gain chip is "provided by" Fraunhofer HHI; no author is at HHI, so
   this is an external supplier of a device under test, not a wafer / film supplier and not a co-author chip hand-over (white2024 left
   empty for that reason). Rule 12 / Contract v7 vendor clause -> fab `org_fraunhofer_heinrich_hertz_institute` on that row only.
   Recommendation: keep. (b) < 0.1 dB/cm: the value sentence (p.1 Sec. I) carries no citation; the preceding sentence cites the platform
   paper [12]; the authors present it as a platform advantage without a measurement. D80i borderline: `author_estimate` with "origin not
   stated" (as batched). Recommendation: keep; the stricter reading (rule 3, cited) would delete double_stripe_wg m1 and with it the
   component row, since m2 / m3 are geometry only (rule 9).
5. (a) zhang2023b fab: abstract "We design, fabricate, and demonstrate" plus the first-person flow on p.5-6 (we perform, we deposited), all
   six authors at UC Davis -> fab UC Davis is rule 12 / D80b, same as merged lee2025. Recommendation: keep (note wording patched).
   (b) li2025c: the text cites "Fig. 3(b)" for 8.78 dB at 30 mA and 60.1 dB at 60 mA, but the caption assigns (b) to 50 C. The 20 C SMSR
