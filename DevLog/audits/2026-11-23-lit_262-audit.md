## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. Measured IL scope for xu2021c and wang2014c (judgment a): keep `unspecified` + method `unspecified` (rule 6; valicourt2015 D723,
   park2011 D725). Evidence both ways, none decisive: xu2021c defines "BBGC" as the whole input-taper-waveguide-taper-output structure
   (p.4, Fig. 1(d)-(e)), but Table 2 lists -3.69 dB in the per-coupler "coupling efficiency" column next to single-GC simulations;
   wang2014c overlays the measurement on the single-GC simulation (Fig. 7) and compares the 11 TM devices with the single-GC 2.7 dB
   (Fig. 8(b)), and its own two-GC circuit simulation (Fig. 5(b)) sits near -4 to -7 dB, which suggests a per-GC normalization, but no
   halving or reference is printed. `per_coupler` (pernice2012 D425 pattern) is the alternative only if the coordinator reads
   Table 2 / Fig. 8(b) as the paper applying the value to one coupler.
2. maire2008 clad / no-clad as separate component rows (judgment b): keep. The top-cladding deposition changes the stack (`cladding`
   air vs 530 nm SiO2 is a component attribute) and the resonance angle; one row with conditions would have to carry two cladding
   values. Contrast atsumi2018 (anneal of one structure, one row). Fab empty is right (passive sentences, four unrelated orgs).
3. Free-space gratings as `fiber_chip_coupler.grating` with the beam angle in `fiber_angle_deg` (judgment c): keep (romerogarcia2013,
   radulaski2018, logan2018 merged). maire2008 is a waveguide input coupler tested with a 30 um free-space spot; zhu2017a couples a
   waveguide to an objective, not an AMO emitter (`free_space_emitter` would also lose `fiber_angle_deg`).
4. wang2014c back reflections (judgment d): patched to `simulated` (see table); alternative is to keep `author_estimate` with "origin not
   stated". 1550 nm stays `wavelength_inferred`. Fab `org_university_washington` kept. Org round: Washington Nanofabrication Facility
   (WNF), facility, lit:wang2014c p.11 acknowledgment, 2 rows (TE / TM); still missing from the registry (also in the D-follow-up list
