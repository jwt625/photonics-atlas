## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. roeloffzen2013 removal list (judgment a). **Agree.** Every result figure other than Figs. 9, 10 and 24 is captioned "from
   [N]" with N an earlier paper of the same authors (D259 / D259a); the matching text values carry the same citations. The
   16 x 1 chip is not attributed in its section to any earlier paper (it cites [33] for the antenna system and [6, 7] for
   the architecture only), and the abstract calls its measurements new, so D408 keeps them. Ring delay arrays as one
   `resonator.ring` row each (D165): keep.
2. roeloffzen2013 20 W / 120 heaters (judgment a). **Keep on the 16 x 1 row with the conflict note** (patched to state the
   evidence). The sentence names the 16 x 1 OBFN chip, and 120 heaters is consistent with its 40 doubly tunable RRs plus
   tree, OSBF and carrier reinsertion, while Fig. 8(a) labels only 31 tuning elements; "Fig. 8" reads as a figure-number
   slip (the paper has another one: p.24 "Fig. 23(c)" for Fig. 24(c)). Device identity is clear, so D159 does not apply;
   D417 conflict-note precedent. `tuning_power_mw` for a chip-total heater power follows merged precedents (akiyama2021,
   doerr2005, hosseinnia2026). Basis `author_estimate` (origin not stated): keep.
3. maesenovo2013 2D / 3D siblings (judgment b). **Keep as two simulated design rows** (different pitch and length, rule 9).
   Bandwidth range: **keep 1260-1675 nm** - it is the printed span the device is said to cover, it lies inside the 450 nm
   claim (450 nm exceeds the 415 nm O-U span), and Fig. 4 confirms all three bounds over it for both designs; the endpoints
   of the 450 nm itself are not printed. 3D bounds: **keep** - the printed bounds belong to the text's SWG-MMI and its 3D
   verification, and the Fig. 4 SWG (3D) points satisfy each bound over 1260-1675 nm (they do not over the full 1.24-1.75 um
   axis). Lengths as period count x pitch: **keep `derived`** (the paper's own 122 x 190 nm = 23.18 um sets the
   convention; the 2D taper prints 10 um for 53 x 190 nm = 10.07 um, i.e. rounded). Widths on the 3D row: **add** (patched;
   p.7 lists pitch and MMI length as the only adjusted dimensions). If the coordinator reads that sentence less literally,
   strike add_metrics m8-m10 and keep m7 (core thickness, stated for the whole paper).
4. herr2014a (judgment c). **`resonator.other`: keep, but not as "spiral"** (notes patched): the shape is not stated, and the
   inset pattern does not fit a 76 GHz FSR, so the micrograph may be illustrative. The component id keeps the word "spiral"
   (internal id, not shown to users; renaming would need delete + re-add for no data gain) - rename only if the coordinator
   wants ids to carry no unverified shape. **MgF2 out under D411: agree** (crystalline mm-scale resonator, tebeneva2024
   precedent). Si3N4 values from the preprint only; no D259 attribution on the comparison measurement.
5. maesenovo2013 platform. **Keep `soi`** (not `soi_strip`): Fig. 2(a) draws a strip on SiO2 but the text never states a full
   etch (D663 / D716 need a stated etch; D422).
