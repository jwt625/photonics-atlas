## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. xu2019c Fig. 12 responsivities on the 40 um row (judgment (a)): keep. The device is fixed by the paper's own numbers, not only by
   section order: the Fig. 11 photocurrents of the D = 40 um device at -1 V divided by the stated incident power give about 0.044 /
   0.028 / 0.015 A/W at 1742 / 1877 / 2000 nm (Fig. 11(b), 3.6 dBm; about 0.10 / 0.064 / 0.034 mA) and about 0.19-0.20 A/W at 1550 nm
   (Fig. 11(a), 0-5 dBm), against the printed 0.047 / 0.029 / 0.015 / 0.214 A/W. D159 needs an identity ambiguous between two rows;
   here it is resolved (zhou2020 precedent: identification from the paper's own numbers). Cross-check added to the row notes.
2. hoessbacher2014 class (judgment (b)): keep `phase_shifter.other`. D158 / D407 cover elements that route light between output
   ports (1x2 / 2x2 MZI, ring switch elements in a matrix); this is a one-input, one-output amplitude element (VOA-like, D155), and
   the merged non-volatile / resistive actuators sit in `phase_shifter.other` (tossoun2024 memristor ring, cheung2023 charge-trap MZI,
   feng2011 / morichetti2021 VOAs). `endurance_cycles` exists only for phase_shifter, so `switch_fabric.other` would drop the
   50-cycle row.
3. hoessbacher2014 "below 200 nW" (judgment (c)): keep `tuning_power_mw` lt 0.0002 `author_estimate`. The bound rests on "no
   significant current" under a 100 nA compliance at about 2 V, not on a measured current; the exact merged precedent is cheung2023
   ctm_mzi m6 (< 20 pW from the instrument floor, author_estimate). dong2019 / stanfield2019 / lihachev2024 (D640) place hold and
   switching powers of non-thermal actuators in the same parameter. The liu2020a "< 300 nW" owner-list item should follow the same
   rule (bound from a floor or compliance -> author_estimate; measured I x V -> derived).
4. Platforms (judgment (d)): keep. hoessbacher hybrid rows `other`: the paper calls the stack a plasmonic MIM (abstract), but the
   guided mode is a hybrid Si-strip mode with 39 % of the field in the 30 nm SiO2 (p.2); merged hybrid-plasmonic rows on a dielectric
   core are `other` (huang2013 HNSW, ofc2026 Th3F.7 HPSS, lotkov2024 ITO stack on SiN), while `plasmonic_mim` rows are metal slots / MIM
   gaps (ding2019, ono2016 slot, salamin2018, zhou2022c). The interface row `soi` follows the photonic-side convention for
   photonic-plasmonic converters (kohli2023 / kohli2025 / salamin2018; lit_108 and lit_141 audits); ono2016 and ofc2026 Th3F.7
   converters carry `other`, so the corpus is split; owner list: one convention for photonic-plasmonic converters. gao2011
   `iii_v_on_si_epi` with `photodetector.vertical` (sun2018 / iadanza2024 precedents). 1 dB/um -> 10000 dB/cm is `derived` (pure unit
   conversion, D82d / D256), correct.
5. gao2011 (judgment (e)): keep all three calls. Internal QE 57 % is an absorption figure computed from the absorption coefficient
   cited to ref. [9] (rule 3). The first-batch "about 2 GHz for all sizes" is a family statement over four diameters of a different
   pad / PBO design with no bias stated (D254; a D254 gap-fill family row is possible but adds little). The 8.6 / 4.1 GHz electrical
   bandwidths are equivalent-circuit calculations with an estimated Cp (D82a, yin2007 m17): `simulated`.
6. Organizations (judgment (f)): keep all. xu2019c: fab empty (passive epitaxy and process sentences, D80b; one Applied Materials
   author is not a fab statement); the acknowledgment thanks a named staff member at Si-COE, NTU, for RF-measurement help: staff thanks
   and not fabrication, no facility (D72, D84b). hoessbacher2014: facilities BRNC and FIRST from "partly carried out in" (rule 12; the
   same ids as salamin2018 / flory2020; the BRNC owner is not a facility, D82c); fab empty because there is no author-contribution or
   first-person fabrication statement (salamin2018 / flory2020 had one) and the acknowledgment names facilities, not a place where
   samples were processed by a named org (D448 not extended; sabatti2024 / didier2026 facility-only precedents). gao2011: fab HKUST
   from "We have fabricated two batches of devices" with all authors at HKUST ECE (D72 / D80b); the HKUST Nanosystem Fabrication
   Facility is in the registry but not named, so no facility.
7. xu2019c J_bulk 42 mA/cm2 basis (not raised by the distiller): keep `measured`. The value is the 1/D = 0 intercept of a linear fit
   over 1/D = 0.01-0.05 um-1, i.e. outside the data; a literal D435 reading would give `author_estimate`, but the construction is the
   intercept of a standard two-term fit of the paper's own series, the same as a cutback intercept (per-coupler or per-interface loss),
   which the corpus enters as `measured` (D80a; hoessbacher2014 6.5 dB in this batch). Recommendation: confirm, so D435 stays limited
   to performance values projected past an instrument or time limit.
