## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. liu2020b alpha ~1 dB/m (p.2). The sentence cites refs. 46-47 for the Damascene process, not for the number; the value is consistent
   with the paper's own kappa_0 (8.5 MHz gives about 1.6 dB/m for an assumed group index near 2.1, audit arithmetic, not entered). Recommendation:
   keep `author_estimate` (D80i borderline, D578 rees2020 precedent). Alternative: delete m6.
2. liu2020b 38 mW and 7 mW `derived` on-chip. 38 mW is printed as the bus-waveguide power next to 76 mW in the lensed fiber, i.e. the
   authors' fiber minus ~3 dB de-embedding (D394 / D166 analogue). The 7 mW sentence does not repeat the reference plane; the row
   follows the sentence's bus-waveguide reading and says so. Recommendation: keep both as distilled. Alternative for 7 mW: `ref_plane`
   empty with the ambiguity in notes.
3. liu2023a IL < 0.22 dB as `excess_loss_db`, and +/- deviations as `coupling_variation`. An IL of 0.22 dB per stage including a 3 dB
   ideal split is impossible for 50:50, so the printed IL is loss beyond the ideal split (GC response calibrated off). The +/- values are
   linear fraction deviations over 1450-1600 nm (not "3 +/- x dB" per-port values), so D401 does not apply; lu2015a (D539) and cheung2019
   are the precedents. Recommendation: keep both. Note for the record: the raw Fig. 7(a) / 7(b) dB levels do not reproduce a 3.2 dB
   per-stage slope at every wavelength, so the bound rests on the authors' processing (entered as printed).
4. li2022a fab. "We developed a fabrication process" and the passive process flow name no fabricator; the authors sit at two orgs
   (Fraunhofer CAP, Strathclyde), so D80b leaves fab empty. CORNERSTONE made only the SiN receiver chip (p.3, acknowledgment). The
   measured Q belongs to the transfer-printed LN ring; `org_cornerstone` on the ring row would place an LN membrane ring in the
   CORNERSTONE SiN foundry column of the benchmark. Recommendation: keep fab empty on both rows, CORNERSTONE in notes (D515 / D463 per
   component; D399 assembled rows only where stated).
5. krasnokutska2019 R30 printed vs figure. Printed TM Q ~9000 vs Fig. 2(b) 8600 (5 %), TE Q ~1200 vs 1550 (29 %, beyond reading
   uncertainty), TM FSR 5.7 nm (p.5 Discussion) vs Fig. 3(d) 5.54 nm (the TE point is 5.75 nm). Recommendation: keep the printed values
   with the figure reads in notes (D417: tossoun2024, song2020, chen2021a precedents); no extra figure rows for R30 Q or TM FSR.
6. krasnokutska2019 3 pm/V on the R70 ring row vs rule 2. Rule 2 keeps modulator metrics (Vpi, EO bandwidth, modulator IL / ER) in the
   EO atlas and forbids duplicating an EO row; the EO atlas has no row for this paper (checked `../eo-modulator-atlas/data` and
   `references`), the value is a DC resonance shift of a filter ring, and `tuning_efficiency_pm_per_v` is defined for "Pockels bias"
   (lit:li2024 PSBG precedent, measured). Recommendation: keep. If the EO atlas later ingests the paper, link `host_device_ids` and drop
   the row only if the EO row holds the same value.
7. krasnokutska2019 fab RMIT University. "The photonic components were fabricated by the process developed and described in our previous
   work [24]" (p.2 Sec. II) qualifies under D84a; all three authors are at RMIT, so the org is unambiguous. Recommendation: add RMIT
   University in the org round, then a fix batch sets fab on all 10 rows.
