## Rulings on the batch report's judgment calls

1. fang2021 ring classes and the D396 question. The four rings stay `resonator.ring`: their printed values are ring
   responses (non-volatile resonance shift, contrast). The electrically switched device is one physical device
   characterized in two classes (D396): the ring row keeps tuning_range_nm 0.4 nm and extinction_db > 30 dB
   (abstract p.2 "contrast over 30dB" between the high and low transmission states, so the condition is supported);
   the Sb2S3 / ITO cell gets its own `phase_shifter.phase_change` row with the SET power 13 mW (p.13), the 8 um cell
   length and the 200 nm ITO thickness, matching the jayatilleka2021 (trimmable ring + Ge-implant trimmer) and
   song2016 (MRR + PMC cell) precedents. The annealed passive ring series (20 nm, 66 nm, SiN 750 nm) get no cell row:
   their only cell-level values are per-length (0.060 nm/um, 0.16 dB/um), which have no registry parameter (gap below).

   "plotted in Figures 3d-3e", both of the 20 nm series; no other component row competes for it, so D159 (ambiguity

   note says so (array-level row, D165).
