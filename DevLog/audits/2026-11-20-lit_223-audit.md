## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. chen2015a AWG-only 5~6 dB (judgment 1, question (a)). Recommendation: own AWG row (patched). The bi-directional AWG is a
   separate physical design inside the hybrid (rule 9) with its own printed loss estimate, design geometry and footprint; one
   wdm.awg row with a de_embedded 7 dB and a per_component 5~6 dB mixes two scopes that a consumer of `excess_loss_db` cannot
   tell apart. The same batch already splits suzuki2017 budget terms onto element rows, and the move also lets the printed
   AWG-part footprint (440 x 190 um) enter. Spacing 3.2 nm / 400 GHz and the 18 channels stay on the hybrid (whole-device spec,
   Fig. 9 caption). Revert = drop the add_component, its 11 add_metrics and the 6 hybrid delete ops.
2. chen2015a PDL 0.1 / 0.4 dB (judgment 2). Recommendation: keep `author_estimate`. "It is estimated" from the PBS (Fig. 5)
   and PR (Fig. 7) results, no arithmetic printed, and the inputs are approximate statements ("about 1dB", "<1dB", "1~2dB"),
   so the value cannot be reproduced from printed numbers (rule 4: derived needs explicit arithmetic on own measurements).
3. chen2015a TM cross-port "almost zero" as a 0.2-0.5 dB figure read (judgment 3). Recommendation: keep
   `extracted_from_figure`. The render supports the range within the stated +/- 0.2 dB, and "almost zero" has no number;
   the p.9 "<1dB" for PBC / PBS in the TM group is consistent.
4. chen2015a FSR "about 29μm" (p.4). Recommendation: keep out, note on the AWG row (moved there). The unit is a misprint
   (FSR > N x 3.2 nm = 28.8 nm), but entering 29 nm would be the auditor's correction of the source (rule 1), and the value is
   a calculated design figure of low use. PBS test structures tied to the p.4-5 design (judgment 4): keep (p.6 "identical
   designs"; no sibling rows, so no D159 case).
5. suzuki2017 P_pi ~40 mW (judgment 7). Recommendation: keep on the heater row, `measured`, condition rewritten (patched). The
   sentence is in the measured double-MZ section; "bar state to the cross state (pi phase shift)" can only be an MZ-switch
   transition (Fig. 4: the element's bar-to-cross change turns heaters off), and the 2 x 8 x 40 mW budget matches 40 mW per
   MZ switch (suzuki2020 merged row uses the same 2 x 8 x P_pi form). Not derived from the budget.
6. suzuki2017 2.5 dB/facet (judgment 6, question (b)). Recommendation: keep `author_estimate` with method unspecified, and
   keep the 6.2 / 3.2 / 1.4 / 0.2 / 0.2 / 0.1 dB terms `author_estimate` + `loss_breakdown`. p.4 "estimated to be 2.5
   dB/facet" names no reference structure, cutback or arithmetic (origin not stated; nader2025 D418, velha2016 D544).
   suzuki2018 (D666) left its 1.4 dB/facet out only because the paper calls it an assumption; suzuki2020 is measured only
   because it names a guide waveguide. 0.8 W `author_estimate` (0.16 W term of unstated origin): keep.
7. suzuki2017 fab AIST on all 9 rows; TIA Super Clean-Room (judgment 5). Recommendation: keep
   `org_national_institute_of_advanced_industrial_science_and_technology`. First-person "We fabricated" (p.1 abstract) with
   all eight authors at AIST, plus the acknowledgment "The device fabrication was supported by the TIA Super Clean-Room of
   AIST" (p.9), a stated place (D448; suzuki2018 D666). The polarization-diversity row by "We prepared ... and created" (p.7,
   D399). Facility stays empty until the org round adds TIA Super Clean-Room (queued D453 / D666). `process_name` as printed.
   Double-MZ element as `switch_fabric.other` (judgment 8): keep (D158 / D407).
8. yang2018b (question (c)). Recommendations: `switch_fabric.other` on `soi_strip` keep (D158; p.3 "All the silicon patterns
   are fully etched"); delay-line row keep as `waveguide_platform.straight`, fabricated, with the simulated 40 fs DGD (D80e:
