## Rulings on the batch report's judgment calls

- zhang2019 fab org_mit + org_mit_lincoln_laboratory: endorsed. p.12-13 contributions: the first author (MIT) fabricated the
  devices; two Lincoln Laboratory and three MIT co-authors "contributed to device fabrication". "Contributed to ... fabrication" is a D80b fabrication statement (ronn2019 precedent, lit coord2),
  so both orgs are stated; parent and child are entered because both are printed as separate affiliations, not
  inferred. Two Lincoln co-authors who only "helped with" fabrication would be D395 assistance. Facilities MTL + Harvard CNS ("fabrication
  facility support", p.12): endorsed. Ring switch as resonator.ring (no on-chip actuator; fang2021): endorsed.
  Fig. 5(d) readings verified (42 / 33 / 33 / 44 dB amorphous; crystalline 0 to about 3 dB).
- ding2019 L = 19 um bw3db_ghz > 110: endorsed. A 1.5-dB bandwidth of 110 GHz (p.3) implies the 3-dB point lies
  beyond 110 GHz, and Table 1 / p.4 summary print > 110 GHz with 360 mA/W. Zero-bias responsivity with bias_v 0:
  endorsed (text p.2 and caption say zero bias; the inset label VB = 0.1 V is noted). Slot absorption 0.8 dB/um as a
  waveguide_platform.straight prop_loss row (cut-back over PD lengths, Fig. 2(C)): endorsed, no PD absorption
  parameter exists; ~1 dB/um author_estimate (Supplementary, origin not stated in the main text): endorsed. Fig. 3(A)
  readings (50 mA/W at 2.0 V, 245 mA/W at 2.4 V) and Fig. 4(B)-(C) FB+ESA points above about -1.5 dB to 110 GHz
  verified. Fab empty (passive "was fabricated", p.5; DTU / SDU / HUST authors): endorsed.
- huang2014 fab org_astar_ime: endorsed as borderline D84a. All authors IME (p.1); "we presented the fabrication and
  characterization" (p.7), the lithography "our scanner" (p.5) and "future fabrications" of "this platform" (p.5)
  place the process on the authors' own line, the D84a "our process fab" case, not a passive step list. 400 nm PECVD /
  LPCVD rows "from our earlier runs" (p.4) without a cited paper: kept as own data (D408: D259 applies only when the
  value is attributed to an earlier paper). SiN width 1 um from the simulation statement (p.3) with condition: kept.
  Heated ring as resonator.ring (platform test filter, not a WDM path; D400 logic): endorsed. Fig. 4(d) Lo = 0 um
  reading (about 1.85-2.2 dB) and Fig. 5(b) resonance near 1556.9 nm verified.
- li2026b n_levels 137 with the authors' caveat: endorsed. Fig. 2(h) title "137 states", Table 1 (p.9 render)
  "137 phase positions (weighted electrode)" under "Demonstrated states or phase positions"; each position is a
  retained non-volatile phase level; the "not digitally addressable" caveat (p.8) is in notes.
- li2026b endurance 10^6 on the 1 mm MZI row: kept (not a D159 case in my reading). The cycled device is not named,
  but the endurance paragraph (p.8) sits between the MZI multilevel paragraph ("The MZI supports multilevel ...",

  normalized delta n, the MZI read-out of Fig. 2(f), while the ring is read as a wavelength shift and the weighted

- li2026b MZM-embedded non-volatile electrodes as phase_shifter rows with retention only (80 h, p.10 and p.14):

  0.0026 pi shortest-segment increment (p.12) not entered: endorsed (D159; the 2 mm weighted electrode and the MZM
