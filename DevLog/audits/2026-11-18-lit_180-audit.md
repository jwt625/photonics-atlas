## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. (a) bino2018 in scope (D411)? Recommendation: keep. D411 removes "discrete bulk (mm-scale polished crystalline)"
   resonators; this is an amorphous fused-silica rod, fiber-coupled by two tapers and presented as an isolator / circulator
   device, the same kind as the merged ilchenko1999 silica microspheres (405-470 um) and karki2019 fiber-pigtailed bulk
   isolators. The 1 mm size is the open point: record a D411 clarification that the exclusion is about polished crystalline
   (and other machined bulk) parts, not size alone, so later WGM rods / spheres are decided the same way. Alternative: rule
   the rod out as mm-scale; then delete the 3 components (all 8 metrics go with them).
2. (b) bino2018 values. Recommendation: keep all as entered. `q_intrinsic` 1.5e8 and `q_loaded` 3e7 author_estimate (D403,
   D76: no spectrum or fit); isolation > 24 dB as printed three times with the "> 20 dB" conclusion and the about -21 dB
   figure points in notes (D417; the printed value is the paper's headline and may come from points not plotted);
   IL "around 7 dB" (caption, fit line) with the "-5 dB" text in notes; directivity T23/T21 as `isolation_db` (T23 about
   0.95, so it equals the port 2 -> 1 isolation to within about 0.2 dB) and T13 as `crosstalk_db`. Only change: circulator
   IL basis `measured` (patched; D256, the 3.3 dB is printed). Alternative for the isolation: enter 20 dB `gt` (conclusion,
   agrees with the figure) and keep 24 dB in notes.
3. (c) zhou2023a version and derived values. Recommendation: accept the arXiv v1 text as the work of the source row (D430 /
   D433); the row has no DOI and its url / arxiv_id are this preprint, so "CLEO 2023" is venue metadata the coordinator may
   correct in the registry; no value depends on it. Loss rows `author_estimate` instead of `derived` (patched; D454: fit
   rates from a normalized single-length transmission of unstated reference, one rate assumed over the whole spiral);
   alternative: keep `derived` (D80a fit inside the data range) and drop the two triridge metric ops. 59 GHz -> 0.462 nm at
