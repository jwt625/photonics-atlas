## Rulings on the batch report's judgment calls

1. **korcek2023 fab / facility.** Fab changed from empty to C2N + CEA-Leti, and facility emptied.
   - Fab: the p.11 contributions name the fabricators: R.K. (Zilina), Q.W. (Univ. Grenoble Alpes, CEA, LETI), and S.E. and C.A.R. (C2N). D80b sends fabricators named at several orgs to all of those orgs (niels2026 imec + Ghent). This is the same kind of statement that gave ronn2019 its C2N fab. University of Zilina has no registry row and is reported.
   - Facility: "done within the C2N micro nanotechnologies platforms" is a facility statement, but the platform has no registry row. Its owner C2N is a research institute, not a facility (D82c). This matches the lit_24 ruling 2 and merged liu2018 / gonzalezandrade2019. RENATECH is a program (D80c), not entered.
2. **gao2026 ANPI.** Facility emptied; fab stays HUST.
   - p.27 says the devices were fabricated by S.L. and G.Y. (HUST) and that the LT fabrication was "performed at the cleanroom of Wuhan ANPI Corporation".
   - ANPI is not stated to have fabricated, so it is not a fab org (rule 12).
   - The named facility is the company's own cleanroom with no registry facility row. Its owner is not a facility_org_id (D82c). This is the same logic as C2N above. All merged facility_org_ids are facility-type orgs (330 of 333; the rest are research institutes).
   - Name mapping: "Wuhan ANPI Corporation" -> org_wuhan_anpi_optoelectronics is plausible, not verified, and not needed for any field. Circumstantial support: the registry row comes from EO li2026aa, a HUST-WNLO TFLN paper with a "Wuhan ANPI Optoelectronics Company Ltd" affiliation; same city.
3. **he2019 fab Harvard.** Endorsed. "We fabricated the converter" (p.2) plus "performed at the Harvard University Center for Nanoscale Systems" (p.4) places the fabrication at Harvard (D83c). CNS facility endorsed.
4. **korcek2023 890 nm period.** Endorsed. The Fig. 6(b) legend (render img_p06_1) prints 830, 842, 860, 872 and 890 nm. The 890 nm curve peaks near 1580 nm at about -5 dB, matching the text's 1582 nm / -4.9 dB. The text's second "830 nm" (p.5) is a typo. Basis design_target from the legend label; locator "p.5; p.6 Fig. 6(b) legend" is correct.
5. **korcek2023 FDTD values on the DC 70% row.** Endorsed. The design point is DC 70% (p.3). The -5% bias remark (p.5) is the authors' explanation for the DC 65% device and does not reassign the design.
6. **kopp2023 class wdm.other.** Endorsed.
   - The device is a 980 / 1550 nm pump-signal combiner. Its function is band multiplexing (registry wdm.other: "other WDM / band multiplexer"). fiber_interface covers attach / fan-out / connector parts.
   - D80f lists fiber-only WDM in scope; precedent garciablanco2025.
   - The 70 dB return loss stays a registry gap (back_reflection_db for wdm). A class change only to admit a parameter is not done.
7. **zhang2025 cascade and crosstalk differences as derived.** Endorsed: D80a, lit_05 wang2026 ruling. The cutback slope (m5) as measured is endorsed (standard fit).
8. **atsumi2018 4.2 dB loopback_half measured.** Endorsed (D167): the per-coupler value is stated, with the halving not shown (p.7). he2019 1.7 dB/facet as derived is also acceptable, because 3.4 dB fiber-to-fiber is printed in the same sentence (p.1, D80a).
9. **zhou2023 / he2019 best values with the measured span and wavelength_inferred.** Endorsed: the peak wavelength is not stated.
10. **gao2026 EO metrics left to the EO atlas.** Endorsed (skill rule 2). Listed as an EO-atlas candidate: MZI Vpi 2.46 V and ER about 24.6 dB (p.9 Fig. 2(e)), packaged EO bandwidth about 1 GHz (p.8), and the TW modulator 2.03 dB S21 drop at 67 GHz / projected 86 GHz (p.8).
11. **aamer2012.** Fab empty endorsed: fabrication is passive (p.2) and the authors are at four orgs (D80b). The rotator IL 1-2.5 dB attribution to the 25 um device follows the conclusion (p.3 Sec. V); the note records the Sec. IV wording. Class polarization.other kept for a pure rotator (registry psr = splitter-rotator / rotator-combiner), but see question 3.
12. **atsumi2018 Sec. 2 dimensions** (220 x 430 nm, 50 nm tip) kept while the Sec. 2 simulated 0.8 dB / 420 nm is excluded (D259). These dimensions describe the design the paper fabricated: the Fig. 2(b) inset shows the taper before IIB, and only the taper length differs (9 / 7 um, entered). D259 targets restated results, not the device's own geometry.
