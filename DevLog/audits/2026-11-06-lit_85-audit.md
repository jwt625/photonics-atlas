## Questions raised by the distiller (evidence-based recommendations)

1. li2025a efficiency rows (48.6 %, about 20 %, > 710, > 100 %/W/cm2) as author_estimate. p.4: pump and signal per-facet losses are
   measured (6.5 / 6.8 dB), but the idler loss is set equal to the signal loss by assumption ("estimated to be the same"), and every
   on-chip idler power, hence every CE / NCE, rests on it. Rule 4 names an assumed input as author_estimate. wang2018 and zhao2025a
   calibrate the coupling at each wavelength they use (p.3 / p.3), so measured there and author_estimate here is a real difference, not an
   inconsistency. Recommendation: keep author_estimate (no patch). Alternative: derived (D394 de-embedding) if a stated equal-loss
   assumption between nearby wavelengths is taken as the authors' own measurement; not measured.
2. wang2018 fab org_harvard_university. p.2-3: first-person account of electrode patterning, poling and waveguide EBL ("we"), authors
   at five orgs; p.4 acknowledgment: device fabrication performed "in part" at Harvard CNS. This is the koyaz2026 construction (D419: first-person poling plus a stated fabrication site, fab = the site's org) and
   gundavarapu2018 (D448: a stated place of processing). D433 hiratani2024 does not fit (site unstated); D395 / D399 cannot pick an
   author org from "we" alone. Recommendation: keep Harvard as fab and CNS as facility (no patch). Weak point: "in part" leaves the
   site of the rest unstated. Alternative: fab empty, facility CNS only.
3. sohn2021 Fig. 4 isolator wavelength: keep 1526 nm (three printings incl. the panel label, above). No patch; the component note
   already names the conflict.
4. sohn2021 Fig. S4 device (p.19). The S4 transmission panel puts the TE00 dip near +6.5 GHz with delta 1.5 GHz, so its mode pair is
   spaced about 6.5 GHz, not the 5.05 GHz pair of Fig. 3(b) (Table S2), and its TE10 is overcoupled where Fig. 3(b) is critically
   coupled; the caption says "the 775 nm isolator" and p.8 "a device operating near 780 nm"; Table S2 lists only three devices as "all
   parameters for the tested devices". It may be another mode pair (another FSR order) of the Fig. 3(b) racetrack or another racetrack;
   the paper does not say. Recommendation: D159 as distilled (values in the 773 nm row notes). Alternative, if read as a separate
   device: a new isolator row (775 nm, overcoupled TE10) with isolation_bw_nm 0.00044 nm derived (label BW 5 dB = 218 MHz at 775 nm,
   level 5 dB) and isolation_db about 9.5 dB extracted_from_figure; the 25 dBm / 5.05 GHz drive and Gph 1.07 GHz in condition.
5. kao2026 net gain 10 dB derived without qualifier. 23.5 dB on-chip gain (p.3, p.5) minus the 13.5 dB fiber-chip-fiber loss
   (p.3, p.6) is exactly 10.0 dB; p.1 prints "up to 10 dB", p.3 / p.5 "over 10 dB", opposite bounds on the same number, and p.3 states
   the 13.5 dB comparison as the basis. A qualifier would pick one wording against the arithmetic. Recommendation: keep derived, no
   qualifier (no patch); the note already quotes both wordings. Alternative: statistic best (the maximum-pump operating point), which
   would then also apply to m1.
6. (new) Lensed-fiber per-facet scope. wang2018 and zhao2025a print per-facet losses obtained from the linear transmission at both
   wavelengths (p.3 / p.3); li2025a measures through the device under test (p.4). The batch scopes all
   five rows per_facet, method unspecified, measured. That matches D167 (shi2024, calibrated with a lensed-fiber pair, per_facet)
   and 19 merged lensed-fiber rows; lorenzen2026 (D452) went loopback_half because its Methods name the total insertion loss of a passive
   guide. No total and no halving are printed here. Recommendation: keep per_facet (no patch). Alternative: loopback_half on the 5 rows
   (li2025a m1 / m2, wang2018 m1, zhao2025a m1 / m2), includes "half of the device-waveguide propagation".
7. (new) kao2026 period row and SHG identity: patched as above. Alternative: if design curves read off plots should not be entered, drop
   kao2026 shg-m2 and li2025a m12 together.
