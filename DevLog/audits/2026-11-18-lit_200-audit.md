## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. shang2015 0.167 dB and 0.01 dB (judgment (a)). Recommendation: keep both as distilled. 0.167 dB is the paper's measured
   per-crossing value, printed three times, with the device identity clear; the Fig. 6(b) slope (0.069 dB per crossing) is
   recorded in the row note (D417, tossoun2024 / gu2020 precedents; D567 printed per-element value with no division shown
   -> `measured`). Method `cutback` matches the 15 / 30 / 45-crossing structures the paper describes. 0.01 dB `derived`
   (D80a: the authors' own cascade / N arithmetic is derived; mean, n 20).
2. shang2015 sibling crossings (judgment (b)). Recommendation: keep crosstalk only. Audit slopes, not entered: Fig. 6(a) 500 nm
   90 / 60 / 30 deg about 0.52 / 0.55 / 0.89 dB per crossing; Fig. 6(b) 700 nm 30 deg about 0.45; Fig. 6(c) 900 nm 90 / 30 deg
   about 0.09 / 0.28. Since the one printed value (0.167) is 2.4x the slope of its own data, any figure-derived sibling value
   would sit next to a normalization the paper contradicts (rule 1).
3. shang2015 heaters (judgment (c)). Recommendation: keep the three `design` rows with `simulated` P_pi 70 / 80 / 83 mW
   (D454). D80e makes a part `fabricated` when it exists on the chip; here the heaters exist but none of the three
   overcladding designs is tied to the fabricated chip (overcladding not stated; Fig. 1(a) is the "proposed" design).
4. shang2015 orgs (judgment (d)). Recommendation: fab `org_university_of_california_davis` ("We fabricated the devices",
   p.5, all authors UC Davis) and facility `org_marvell_nanofabrication_laboratory` (p.9) as distilled; UC Berkeley not a fab
   org (D634: D448 does not make a facility's owner a fab; fu2023, same group and facility pair, has no Berkeley fab).
   "North California Nanotechnology Center, University of California, Davis" (p.9, facility role, all 10 fabricated rows):
   the same group's fu2023 (2023) prints "Center for Nano-MicroManufacturing (Davis)" for its UC Davis facility, so the
   2015 name may be a predecessor of `org_center_for_nano_micro_manufacturing`; nothing in either paper states a rename.
   Org round: verify the facility history; if confirmed, add the printed name as an alias and fill `facility_org_ids` in the
   fix batch; otherwise a new facility org with parent `org_university_of_california_davis`. Alias candidate: "Marvell
   Nanolab facility, University of California, Berkeley" -> `org_marvell_nanofabrication_laboratory`.
5. zhao2019 PBS IL (judgment (e)). Recommendation: keep `per_component` + method `unspecified` (7 merged polarization IL rows
   use the same pair; Fig. 6 spectra sit near 0 dB, so the couplers were normalized out, but no reference structure is
   described). `de_embedded` + `unspecified` would carry the same information. The O-band ER conflicts are patched as notes
   (above); the coordinator may prefer to delete m1 / m5 under D159 if the undefined ER is judged ambiguous, but D417 (device
   identity clear, numbers disagree) fits better.
6. hwang2007 scope (judgment (f)). Recommendation: keep all 5 components. The parts are custom-made fiber-coupled passive parts
   (90-deg bent fiber block, fiber- and MT-ferrule-embedded OPCB, guide-pin VCSEL / PD interfaces): D80f / D411 in scope, with
   optical-PCB precedents dangel2013 / shi2023 (MT connectors and OPCB waveguides) and the VCSEL-to-fiber
   `fiber_chip_coupler.other` precedent hsu2025. fontaine2019 (D642) differs: a bench assembly of purchased bulk optics (SLM,
   mirror, lenses) with a free-space camera output, no fabricated chip-scale or fiber part. The VCSEL-to-PD link row is a
   sum of passive terms (Fig. 5(a) position 0 is the VCSEL output reference; 0.8 + 3.57 + 3.0 = 7.37 dB, 0.8 + 1.5 + 3.0 =
   5.3 dB) and carries the abstract value plus the layer difference and crosstalk; keep it as `device_total`
   (gradkowski2023 link-level `fiber_interface` precedent). Alternative: delete the link row and move m3 / m4 to fc_opcb_path.
   Platform empty is fine (merged fiber_interface rows use empty / silica / other; D422). Crosstalk `author_estimate` (D80i;
   note patched). Isu-Petasys Co. ("for the manufacture of optical PCBs", p.3 acknowledgment): fab on `fc_opcb_path` only
   (the OPCB is its product; the link and the blocks were assembled / made by the authors; D399 / D515 fab per component),
   not on `vcsel_pd_link` as the batch report proposes.
7. watanabe2013 (judgment (g)). Recommendation: keep m1 `author_estimate` (Gaussian-overlap calculation with measured spot
   sizes, offsets and an MCF MFD of unstated origin; loss_breakdown, per_facet), m4 / m5 `specification` (supplied fiber the
   fan-in is designed for; doerr2010 / bundalo2022 precedent). m1 conditions patched (polarization and wavelength belong to
