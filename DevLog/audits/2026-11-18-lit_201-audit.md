## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. ji2022 scope and platform (judgment (a)). Recommendation: keep as `fiber_interface.fan_out`, platform empty. An all-fiber
   fused-taper FI/FO is a fiber-coupled passive part (D411; Contract v4 / D80f "fiber-only passive parts in scope"); the
   registry subtype is "MCF fan-in/fan-out". Merged all-fiber precedents have platform empty: zhang2022 fused-taper FIFO,
   shikama2017 fiber-bundle FIFO, ma2026 CDCF fan-in (D422 / D423: no default).
2. klaus2012 and lee2014 scope (judgment (b)). Recommendation: keep both, platform `other`. The line drawn by D642
   (fontaine2019) is consistent: fontaine2019 was a bench transformation of purchased parts ("off-the-shelf without any custom
   fabrication") with fiber at the input only and a camera at the output, so its IL / crosstalk described a lab arrangement.
   klaus2012 is a designed and fabricated device with fiber at every port (19 laser-welded SMF collimators and the MCF),
   measured fiber to fiber and used as the MUX / DEMUX of a 19-core transmission experiment (p.2); lee2014 is a 25 x 88 mm
   packaged module. Both are fiber-coupled passive parts under D411, as the merged free-space fiber parts jung2018 (C-lens
   collimator pairs / isolators) and jung2020 (platform `other`). The caveat for klaus2012 is size: a 30 cm x 43 cm assembly
   with internal xyz / tip-tilt stages; record in the decision that D411 does not depend on size when every port is fiber,
   so later bench-scale but fiber-terminated MUX / DEMUX devices are treated the same.
3. cheng2023a orgs (judgment (c)). Recommendation: keep fab empty and the HUST WNLO facility on all 8 rows. Rule 12 lists
   "support for the fabrication" as facility wording; the fabrication sentence is passive with no org (p.5, D80b / D433);
   D448 applies when an acknowledgment names where samples were processed (gundavarapu2018 "processed at"), which this
   sentence does not; D634 / D82c: a facility's owner is not made fab through the facility. Same facility and same wording
   handled this way on li2026aa. Alternative (D448 read broadly): add `org_huazhong_university_of_science_and_technology` as
   fab on the 8 rows (all authors at WNLO / HUST, EBL at the facility); then the 8 component notes need the sentence
   "borderline D448 fab candidate ... for the audit" replaced.
4. klaus2012 7-channel row (judgment (d)). Recommendation: keep IL < 1.5 dB and crosstalk ~50 dB as `author_estimate`,
   scope unspecified. D254 removes a "typical" value given once for a family from one representative row; the conclusion
   (p.4) states the bound for each of the two devices, the D592 "in all cases" pattern (lin2023), and the 19-channel row
   carries its own measured values instead. Note for the decision: the same sentence is slightly loose for the 19-channel
   device (Fig. 6(a) channels 4 and 6 at about 1.52-1.53 dB against "< 1.5 dB"; printed 1.3 +/- 0.2 dB), so the bound reads as
   a rounded summary. Alternative: drop the two 7-channel performance rows and the component (no 7-channel data shown);
   then the row has no performance metric left (rule 9).
5. cheng2023a figure reads (judgment (e)). Recommendation: keep the PER band edges and the 12 GC minima as
   `extracted_from_figure`, GC scope unspecified. The re-traced values are inside the stated tolerances (above). Fig. 12(a)-(b)
   shows each GC pair joined by a waveguide and the caption says "Measured ILs spectra" of the GCs without saying per pair
   or per coupler; halving would be a guess (rule 6 `unspecified`).
6. ji2022 / klaus2012 fab (judgment (f)). Recommendation: keep empty. ji2022 "the FI/FO devices we fabricated" (p.5) with
   authors at Fudan University, Shanghai University / Jiangsu Hengtong and City University of Hong Kong; klaus2012 "We
   designed and fabricated" (p.2) with authors at NICT and Optoquest Co. Ltd. Both sentences name no org and the authors
   sit at unrelated orgs (D154, qi2024; D395). klaus2012 mentions laser welding "during manufacturing" (p.4) without naming
   the maker. lee2014 stays empty too (passive "were fabricated", all authors at Hitachi, D80b).
7. klaus2012 0.01 dB (judgment (g)). Recommendation: keep `per_coupler`, `simulated`. The overlap-integral loss is computed for
   one MCF-lens-lens-SMF path of one device (centre beam, p.4 Fig. 8) with Fresnel terms excluded, which is "a fiber coupler
   isolated individually"; the condition already says centre beam and model parameters; wavelength 1550 nm inferred from
   the 1.55 um test source (rule 8).
8. cheng2023a added crosstalk rows (this audit). Recommendation: accept m13 / m14 (worst plotted mode leakage about -14 dB
   near 1474 nm, both inputs) as figure reads; the paper's only wording is "low crosstalk", and the worst-case mode
   crosstalk is the figure of merit a reader compares across `mdm_mux` rows. Revert = drop `add_metrics` and the component
   note op.
