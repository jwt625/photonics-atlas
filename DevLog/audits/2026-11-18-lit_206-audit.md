   papers carry such values as `derived`; `author_estimate` would be wrong because the subtracted terms are measured and printed
   (contrast yuan2021 / cao2023, where they are not). (ii) Fab `org_amf` keep (foundry named with its MPW flow; all authors are AMF R&D,
   p.1). (iii) Edge coupler 7.00 dB `per_facet` `cutback` `measured` keep (D167; Fig. 10 fit legend prints the per-facet value). (iv)
   Transition keep as `waveguide_platform.transition`, `fabricated`, `simulated` (D80e): it is the SiN routing waveguide entering the
   SiN-over-Si-rib section of the fabricated APD; if the coordinator reads "transition" strictly as light changing layers, the
   alternative is `waveguide_platform.other`, not deletion.

   coupling structure"), so D259 / D408 apply; the legend's [14] (Chen et al., Opt. Express 2023, another group) would make the red
   points cited data under rule 3 anyway. This JLT paper is not a journal extension of that OFC paper (different multiplication layer
