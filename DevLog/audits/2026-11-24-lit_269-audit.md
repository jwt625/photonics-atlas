## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. Class (judgment a): keep `resonator.ring` for the tien2011 notch / add-drop rings and the qiu2018a MPF ring. tien2011:
   Table 1 is a radius / thickness series characterized for Q and loss, and the filters serve microwave-photonic and
   sensor use, not a WDM channel path (D400). qiu2018a: one ring used as the optical notch of a single-passband MPF. The
   merged split is by structure, not application: taddei2019 keeps its single-ring notch configuration as
   `resonator.ring` and only the 8-ring CROW bandpass as `wdm.mrr`; lu2024a is a 2 x 8 MRR filter array; burla2015 is
   `resonator.ring`. Optional skill line under rule 10: "single rings used as microwave-photonic notch / bandpass
   filters stay `resonator.ring`; multi-ring filters and ring arrays in a channel path are `wdm.mrr`".
2. puckett2019 (judgment b): keep 216 M as `q_intrinsic` `author_estimate`. Two independent checks agree: f / 1.76 MHz =
   1.09e8 = 216 M / 2 (critical coupling, Fig. 1(a) dip near zero), and 0.123 dB/m gives Q_i = 216 M at a group index of
   about 1.52, i.e. the printed loss is the intrinsic loss (arithmetic only; ng not printed). Storing it as `q_loaded`
   would contradict the printed FWHM. Keep the Brillouin laser as a second row of the same ring (D396) and fab empty
   ("fabricated as in [5]", no org, no acknowledgment; D433).
3. tien2011 (judgment c): keep `q_intrinsic` `measured` on all seven rows: p.4 fixes the coupling regime from data ("by
   comparing the spectra of similar resonator structures with various power coupling ratios"; D425 lee2012). At 1060
   nm the figure supports the Q value: Fig. 3(a) (22 MHz, 10.6 dB) matches Q_i 18 M with 0.8 % coupling; the printed 16 MHz
   is the outlier (note patched). 1310 nm: 28 M with 0.55 % under-coupled predicts about 14 MHz and a 15 dB notch, while
   Fig. 3(b) shows about 34 MHz and 3.8 dB; that pair reads either under-coupled with Q_i about 7 M and about 0.65 %
   coupling, or over-coupled with Q_i about 34 M and about 3 % coupling. The paper's three 1310 nm numbers cannot all hold;
   the notes say so (D417). Alternative if the coordinator prefers: only t50-m3 (28 M) and t50-m8 (0.55 %) to
   `author_estimate`; the other Table 1 rows have no contradicting data. Radius: keep 2 mm (Table 1 + text; with ~5 % per
   coupler and ~1 % round-trip loss, 2 mm gives about 300 MHz and Q_i about 7.4 M, 1.5 mm about 400 MHz and 5.6 M; arithmetic
   check). 2.9 dB/m: keep out (ref. [15] attribution, Fig. 1 shows 3.66 dB/m at 2 mm, now entered). LioniX BV: keep apart
   from org_lionix_international (D278 / D545) and add a time-correct org in the next org round; fix batch then sets fab on
   the 7 tien2011 rows (5 + 2 added) together with a2011, bauters2013, mu2016, zhuang2016.
4. qiu2018a (judgment d): MPF RF FWHM as the ring linewidth: keep (for PM-to-IM conversion by notching one sideband the RF
