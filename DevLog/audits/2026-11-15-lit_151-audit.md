## Coordinator questions

1. Mode-converter pair class (distiller call 6). The paper names "mode converters" between the suspended spring
   waveguides and the fixed waveguides (p.33-34) and never says which modes they convert; the Fig. S15(b) micrograph alone
   cannot settle it. Recommendation: keep `waveguide_platform.other` (single-layer junction, xiao2022 / cheng2025 D417
   precedent; `mode_converter.converter` would assert a mode-order conversion the paper does not state). No op.
2. Test-structure wavelengths (call 7). No wavelength is printed with the Section 11 values; the paper uses exactly these
   values for its "excess loss at 1550 nm" path estimate (p.45). Recommendation: keep 1550 nm with
   `wavelength_inferred: true` and the reason (rule 8). No op.
3. Platform of the routing / shuffle rows (call 8). The crossing (Fig. S17 caption) and shuffle bends (p.42) are stated
   ridge; the spirals are the "routing waveguides" whose losses the paper applies to the ridge array paths (p.45 Table 2),
   but their own cross-section is not printed. Recommendation: `soi_rib` on all five (patched; lit_138 / merged soi_rib
   practice, 56 merged straight / bend / crossing rows use it). Strict alternative for the spirals only (D422 caution):
   keep `soi` and drop their etch rows (ops named in the patch header).
4. Benes values (call 5). Recommendation: keep m4 (measured 58-path range), m5 (loss-budget estimate, `derived`, every
   input an own measurement, Table 2 reproduces) and m6 (Table 3 min / max over 28 intact paths, `derived`, printed
   precision). Flag for views: m5 is a prediction; a "best excess loss" view should filter `basis: measured`. Alternative:
   delete m5 (op in the header).
5. 82-switch statistics (call 3). Same design, separate chip (array chip) from the Fig. 3 device; rule 9 / D32 put
   operating points and populations on the design row. Recommendation: keep on `swx_switch_2x2` (condition names the
   array chip). No op.
6. Varied-width crossing vs the authors' earlier work. Main text cites ref. 51 for the crossing type and the Supplement
   says its simulated loss was validated in that work (p.43); the PSO design, 4 mdB simulation and 28 mdB measurement here
   carry no citation. Recommendation: keep (D408 / D80i: value sentences uncited); cross-note added.
7. Mechanical resonance basis (patched to `simulated`, D83f). Alternative if the SHO estimate (computed mass from the
   designed volume) is read as a model estimate: `author_estimate` on m21 only.
8. 2x2 MEMS element subtype. Recommendation: keep `switch_fabric.mems` (merged single-cell MEMS precedent above). No op.
