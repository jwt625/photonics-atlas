## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. hulme2015 psi-axis headline (23 deg x 1 deg, 5.5 dB) and laser tuning 34.5 nm / 0.28 nm/mW (judgment a). **Keep D159.**
   Neither text nor figures name the measured array or laser: Sec. 6.1 and 6.4 say only "the laser"; Figs. 10 and 15 carry no
   design label; Fig. 1, Fig. 7(a) and Fig. 14 show one grating array per PIC but not which spacing; Fig. 9(c) shows "Laser"
   without saying which design; the handwritten "SW2 Right" on the PCB (Fig. 7(c)) is not a design name. The only hint is
   physics: 23 deg equals the grating-lobe spacing of a uniform 4 um pitch at about 1.57-1.60 um (asin(lambda / 4 um) about 23
   deg), and Fig. 15 shows repeated side peaks along psi. That is an auditor inference, not a statement, so it stays in this
   audit and out of the data. Note for the record: "> 70 % background suppression" (p.13) is 10 log10(1/0.3) = 5.2 dB, while
   the conclusion prints 5.5 dB. Option not recommended: an as-measured "array design not stated" row (matsuo2013 unnamed-device
   precedent, D422) would carry the headline but has no geometry; the 34.5 nm laser tuning would stay out anyway (two physical
   lasers on one chip, a genuine D159 case).
2. guan2018 (judgment b). **Agree with all.** Rings R1 / R2 `resonator.ring` (D400: filter inside the laser cavity, not a WDM
   channel filter); WSR `reflector.other` with `extinction_db` (kramnik2024 precedent for Vernier side passbands; `slsr_db`
   would also fit, no change). Coupling: 0.73 dB measured / 2.8 dB measured best / 0.18 dB, 96 % and 2.82 dB simulated, each on
   its own row. The ring spectra were taken "on wafer-scale through grating coupler" (p.6), which suggests separate test copies,
   but the paper does not say so; `test_structure` left empty. Fab empty: foundries not named (Contract v7; Elenion is an
   affiliation only, the affiliation-foundry rule is config-only).
3. suh2018a (judgment c). **Keep both.** Q ~3e8 `author_estimate` (no extraction method or coupling regime, D403; suh2018
   precedent D802); 145 fs `derived` (sech^2 spectral fit parameter, D84c).
4. huang2019d (judgment d). **Keep the four FSR-only rows** (FSR is a performance metric, rule 9; herr2012 kept four rows with
   `author_estimate` FSRs, D801; they anchor the TRN gap-fill). Fab `org_epfl` + facility CMi: **keep** (D448, D83c).
