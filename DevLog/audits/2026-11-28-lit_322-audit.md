- TE loss ranges: Fig. 12(a) TE between about 0 dB (1.60 um) and -5.5 dB (about 1.553 um); Fig. 12(b) between about -1.3 to
  -1.5 dB (1.605-1.61 um) and -5 dB (1.539 and 1.572 um). Entered 0-5.5 and 1.5-5 dB, `extracted_from_figure`, scope
  `de_embedded` ("Here the coupling loss is excluded", p.10), method unspecified: agreed.
- Geometry: H 700 nm (abstract, p.2), hr 140 nm (Fig. 12 panel labels; Fig. 9 label and caption for the design), wco as
  printed in um -> nm `derived` (D157), 1 mm -> 1000 um `derived`. Trench width wtr has no registry parameter (in label
