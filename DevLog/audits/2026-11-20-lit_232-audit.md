## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. yen2020 Table I channel mapping (judgment (a)): keep the 1530-1570 nm span plus channel label on the CH2-CH4 rows. The evidence
   favours the Fig. 5(a) numbering (CH2 = 1550, CH3 = 1570, CH4 = 1530 nm): Fig. 5(a), Fig. 6 and the Fig. 7(a)(b) panel titles all use
   it, and the p.6 crosstalk observation fits Table I only under it; only the Fig. 7(c) legend differs. The distiller's alternative
   (adopt the Fig. 7(c) legend) is the less supported reading and is not recommended. Assigning the Fig. 5(a) wavelengths would still
   be an inference about a table that states no wavelengths (D159 spirit); evidence now in the component notes (patched).
2. Loss scope `unspecified` (judgment (b)): keep on all three. yen2020 spectra are normalized (passbands near 0 dB with edge couplers
   and a lensed fiber) but the reference is never stated; akiyama2021a "loss values to 4 output ports" states no reference plane;
   xu2017a "insertion losses of the grating couplers ... ~5 dB" (plural) does not say per coupler or per pair. Rule 6: never a guess.
3. Directional couplers (judgment (c)): no yen2020 DC rows. The paper gives only five nominal cross-coupling coefficients (0.5, 0.3,
   0.23, 0.17, 0.05 at 1550 nm, p.6) with no geometry and no simulated or measured coupler value; the cheung2019 rows carried Table VI
   geometry (gap, width, Rin, angle) and `simulated` coupling, which is what made them layout-viewer rows (D153). Kept in notes.
   xu2017a 0.5 / 0.2 / 0.04: keep `author_estimate`. The [10] citation supports the flat-top synthesis scheme; the ratios are this
   paper's optimized design values, the bent-DC geometry is tuned to them (Fig. 3), and the paper does not say the ratios were computed
   (D82a; D80i borderline own design).
4. akiyama2021a (judgment (d)): 50 dB without qualifier, keep ("suppressed down to -50 dB" is one printed value; Fig. 3 consistent).
   Platform empty, keep (D422 / D423: "Si nano-waveguide PIC" states no SOI / BOX; the akiyama2021 sibling's 220 nm SOI is another
   paper, rule 1). TSMC 180 nm in notes only, keep: the EIC is an electronic controller die, fab is per component (D515 / D463), and a
   TSMC fab or process_name would place the CAT in TSMC's foundry column of the benchmark.
5. n_stages (judgment (d)): keep 4 / 2 / 2 as entered, each with the counted unit in the condition. yen2020's "four-stage" is the
   paper's own term (Fig. 5(a) Stage-1 to Stage-4 includes the clean-up S1 / S2 stages; tree depth 2); xu2017a and akiyama2021a count
   tree levels (akiyama2021 precedent). The registry definition "cascaded MZI stages" does not say which unit; merged rows already mix
   them. Recommend a registry note (count as the paper names stages; counted unit in condition) rather than re-basing rows.
6. Fab (judgment (e)): yen2020 empty, keep, and route "OCP Asia, MOLEX" (as printed, p.8 Acknowledgment, role fab by the D398
   wording "chip fabrication support") to the org round; no hit for Molex or OCP in `organizations.csv` or `org_aliases.csv`. The
   paper does not say whether the comma-joined string is one entity or two; the org round should settle it from a primary source.
   When the org exists, set it on both yen2020 rows with a note that the foundry itself is unnamed (borderline: the paper does not call
   it a foundry or MPW broker). TSMC is a funder only (p.1): not fab. xu2017a org_zhejiang_university: keep (first-person "We
   fabricated", p.3; both authors at one Zhejiang University unit, p.1; rule 12, D72 / D80b); no facility named.
7. Row kinds (judgment (f)): keep. The three bent DCs are parts of the fabricated filters (Fig. 5(a)-(c) micrographs) with only
   design / simulated values: `fabricated` (D80e). The straight DCs and the straight-DC demux exist only in the FDTD / transfer-matrix
   comparison (p.2-3): `design`.
