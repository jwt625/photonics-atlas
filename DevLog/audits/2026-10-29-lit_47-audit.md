## Rulings on the distiller's questions

1. tossoun2024 IL and ring consistency. Recommendation: keep as printed with the flag (no D159 removal).
   - IL: 10 log10(0.945) = -0.245 dB and 10 log10(0.937) = -0.283 dB, not 0.047 / 0.048 dB; no simple formula maps the
     two alpha values to two numbers 0.001 dB apart, so the printed IL cannot be reproduced. But the main text (p.14),
     Table 1 (p.20, 0.048 dB) and p.6 ("about 0.05 dB") all attribute it to the memresonator: the sample is unambiguous,
     the doubt is on the arithmetic. lit_44 precedent (liu2026 ratios, hinumwagner2025 FSR): printed value tied to its
     device is kept with a note. Qualifier approx added.
   - FSR / radius: at 1323.6 nm, FSR 2.808 nm gives a group index of about 9.9 for the printed 20 um diameter, 5.0 for
     a 20 um radius, 4.0 for 25 um; the Fig. S5 broadband spectrum of "the memresonator" (spacing about 3.6-3.7 nm) gives
     about 7.6 for 10 um and 3.8 for 20 um radius; Fig. S2 (p.35) labels its 3 measured rings 10 / 20 / 25 um radius.
     The physics points to a 20 um radius device and an S2 FSR from yet another ring, but the paper itself states the
     20 um diameter twice (p.14, p.23) and uses 2 pi x 10 um in its own L_pi arithmetic (p.41). Correcting it would be a
     physics-based guess. Keep, flag in notes (patched with the numbers). Alternative if the coordinator prefers strict
     D159: delete ring-m5 (radius) only; the S2 performance values stay attributed as printed.
2. chang2026a cavity "P_pi" 1.72 mW as tuning_power_mw: endorsed. It is FWHM x pi / eta, not a pi phase shift
   (p.7); merged tuning_power_mw rows hold non-pi powers with the range in condition (dong2010 per FSR, oz2023,
   kawahara2025 pi/2). bw3db_khz 729 / 500 kHz `derived` from rise / fall: endorsed; precedent rows with the same
   construction: cmp_hess2026_ring_tops-m1, cmp_lit_maeder2022_tops_w3p1_l0p8-m3, cmp_lit_zhu2019_* (0.35 / max(rise,
   fall)), cmp_lit_wang2022b_tin_heater_phase_shifter-m6, cmp_lit_kim2019_l_shaped_to_phase_shifter-m4. Here
   0.35 / mean(rise, fall) reproduces both values (formula not printed).
3. (a) adya2024 IL 1.5 dB on the 12.5 nm row: endorsed. Table 1 "This work" pairs IL 1.5 dB with 1.4 dB/um,
   103 / 3.38e3 nJ and 20,000 events, all specific to the 12.5 nm device (p.6-7), so D254 (typical value of a family)
   does not apply. (b) 256 ohm heater resistance in notes: endorsed (Fig. 4(a) "a PIN micro-heater", variant not
   identified; D159 / D254). (c) tamura2026 before-PCM extinction: overruled, D159 (see findings); alternative "keep"
   only if the coordinator reads "before PCM deposition" as the same chip (cheung2023 before / after bonding precedent).
   (d) tossoun2024 fab empty: endorsed. "were fabricated" is passive (p.5), "we integrated" (p.4) is a development
   statement (D80b), author contributions list no fabrication, no facility acknowledged (p.24); cheung2023 differs
   because it states in-house fabrication and names the fabricating authors.
4. Registry gaps: all skipped values confirmed at their locators (table below).
5. Missing orgs: both confirmed absent from organizations.csv and org_aliases.csv (no Washington Nanofabrication / WNF
   and no ShanghaiTech core-facility entry; SMDL is org_shanghaitech_material_and_device_lab and already entered).
