## Coordinator questions

1. poulsen2025 CMC Microsystems as fab (asked). The acknowledgement (p.10) thanks CMC for technical support including access to design
   software, foundries and select measurement equipment, and the next sentence names AMF as the fabricator; p.6 states an MPW run.
   "Access to foundries" is MPW-brokerage wording of the kind counted in geravand2025 ("access to MPW services") and anthony2019 ("access to
   the MPW was facilitated by"); wang2012 ("for making this project possible") and eid2016 ("provision of services") had no foundry
   wording. Recommendation: keep `org_cmc_microsystems` next to `org_amf` (D83d / D398, Contract v7 brokers), borderline: the sentence is
   generic boilerplate, not tied to this device's run. The supply-review filing as a plain acknowledgement is a different contract
   (vendor usage) and does not decide fab. Alternative: fab `org_amf` only on the 3 rows.
2. zhang2021a 0.02 deg (asked). Recommendation: delete (patched; D411). The value is the divergence of the chip plus a bulk off-chip
   cylindrical lens (p.9), it would rank the OPA against chip-only divergences, and the chip's own 32 deg row stays. Alternative: keep with
   the lens in the condition (drop the delete op and the notes op).
3. zhang2021a damage thresholds as `power_handling_dbm` (asked). Recommendation: keep both. The parameter definition takes the criterion
   "as stated", the condition names it (90 % survival of a fitted destructive-damage curve, peak power of 10 ns pulses, coupling loss not
   subtracted), and merged rows already hold failure-bounded or onset criteria (rasel2026 29 dBm before failure at 30 dBm, chung2017 1 dB
   extra loss, ofc2026 Tu2J.2 onset). Caveat for views: the Si value (2.15 W) sits far above the onset of nonlinear saturation (output
   peaks near 390 mW input, p.2), which the condition and component notes state. If the coordinator wants destructive thresholds kept
   apart, register `damage_threshold_dbm` (criterion and pulse regime in condition) and move both rows.
4. wang2026g bandwidths (asked). Recommendation: `derived` (patched). The printed values reproduce 0.35 / mean(rise, fall) of the
   paper's own measured times to the printed precision; no assumed, nominal or cited input enters; merged chang2026a (729 / 500 kHz),
   guo2024 (32 kHz) and wang2022b (30 kHz) bandwidths of the same construction are `derived`. The > 68.6 / > 66.0 kHz bounds keep `gt`
   (detector-limited). Alternative: keep `author_estimate` (drop the 6 metric ops).
5. wang2026g shared stack on 12 rows (asked). Recommendation: keep. Each row is a physical design (rule 9) built on the one stack the paper
   prints for all designs (p.2, p.4: 450 nm x 110 nm Al2O3, W heater 3 um x 100 nm at 1.06 um, 2 um BOX, 1.06 um top cladding); the
   `heater_ps` template needs `core_thickness_nm` and `width_nm` per component; D254 concerns performance values stated once for a
   family, not a shared stack (sun2022a and merged multi-design papers repeat geometry the same way).
6. wang2026g "imec Xplore lab" (asked). The registry facility `org_xplore_multiuser_cleanroom` was created from khalil2026 (imec authors,
   Leuven; "Xplore multiuser cleanroom team" assisted sample fabrication; host not stated there). wang2026g names the same Xplore facility
   with imec as host for die-level module fabrication. Recommendation: same facility; facility set on the 12 phase-shifter rows (patched,
   "performed at", rule 12; not on the two MMIs, which are pilot-line parts); org round: alias "imec Xplore lab" (printed_variant) and
   `parent_org_id` org_imec on `org_xplore_multiuser_cleanroom`. Alternative: drop the 12 component ops until the alias exists. Benchmark:
   the 0.85 mW rows are die-level post-processed Al2O3 at 360 nm; list them in `exclude_values` of `config/benchmark.yaml` if they would
   otherwise set the imec thermo-optic P_pi cell (D933).
7. poulsen2025 post-processing at the University of Western Ontario (asked). Sec. 3 describes the release steps without a first-person
   subject, so D440 alone does not apply; the acknowledgement (p.10) states the devices were post processed at the University of Western
   Ontario Nanofabrication Facility, which is a stated place (D448). Recommendation: org round adds University of Western Ontario (fab,
   post-processing step, on all 3 poulsen2025 rows, which are all released structures, p.2, p.6-7) and its Nanofabrication Facility
   (facility, child org), then a fix batch; note the post-processing on the rows (already in the notes); benchmark `exclude_values`
   candidate for any AMF cell these rows would enter (D933).
