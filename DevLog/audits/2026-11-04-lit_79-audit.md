## Open questions for the coordinator

1. gissibl2016 `extinction_db` from two figure readings. Recommendation: delete all four (patched). The paper prints no contrast, only a selectivity bandwidth; contrast from the figure paths:

   | panel, end facet | T ratio | 1530 | 1540 | 1550 | 1560 | 1570 | 1580 | 1590 | 1600 nm |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | (c) left-handed, incoupling | RCP / LCP | 5.2 | 5.9 | 4.3 | 3.5 | 2.3 | 0.9 | 1.4 | 2.1 dB |
   | (c) left-handed, outcoupling | RCP / LCP | 4.5 | 6.6 | 3.9 | 3.6 | 2.5 | 1.0 | 1.1 | N/A |
   | (d) right-handed, incoupling | LCP / RCP | 5.1 | 4.9 | 5.7 | 5.0 | 5.2 | 4.8 | 1.9 | 0.3 dB |
   | (d) right-handed, outcoupling | LCP / RCP | 6.6 | 7.5 | 8.2 | 5.6 | 5.5 | 4.0 | 1.6 | -0.1 dB |

   A single number is therefore a choice of wavelength, and the chosen 1560 / 1570 nm is not the contrast peak on any of the four. p.5 also says absolute values are not exactly calibrated (T reaches 1.0). No merged extinction_db row is a ratio of two figure readings: the merged figure-read rows read one dip or stopband depth on a dB axis (zhuang2025 m3 as the off- and on-resonance levels of one curve), and the log10 rows convert a printed linear value. The chiral rows stay with center_wavelength_nm and rod width; the 50 nm goes to the gap above. If you overrule: keep the rows, set the right-handed condition to T_LCP / T_RCP, set left m2 to 3.6 dB, and say in the notes that the value depends on wavelength.
2. `beam_diameter_um` beyond "at the interface". Recommendation: keep focal-spot FWHM (wessling2022) and widths at a stated distance (gissibl2016), and widen the definition to "beam diameter or width at the stated plane (interface, focus, or distance); level (1/e2, FWHM) and plane in condition". Merged use is already this wide: kasztelanic2018 m1 focal-spot FWHM, huh2016 m1 to m5 at 15 and 7 mm, huh2016 focusing lens at the focal point, lim2021 (D437), fowler2025. Limit: a width of the beam, so the ring peak-to-peak row is out (patched).
3. Sign of `lens_radius_um`. Recommendation: store the positive magnitude, printed sign in notes, basis unchanged, as for loss and crosstalk (registry conventions store those as magnitudes with the source sign in notes). All 18 merged rows are positive, and the printed negative sign reads as an optical-design convention for the convex exit surface drawn in p.4 Fig. 2(a); the paper does not define it. Add to the definition: "magnitude; printed sign and convex or concave in notes". The "angles keep the sign" convention does not extend to radii.
4. gagino2024 EOPM. Recommendation: keep `phase_shifter.other` with `phase_shift_pi` = 2, 6.4-9.1 V in condition (grottke2020 m2 precedent: 2 pi at V2pi). Do not halve into `v_pi_v`: its definition sends 2 pi values to notes and the phase-voltage relation is not printed (supplement not cached). The class label "non-EO" does not fit a reverse-biased EO element; merged lu2016a and gao2025a carrier-injection rows already sit there, and rule 2 is not triggered since the EO atlas has no row for this paper and the paper gives no modulator data. List as an EO-atlas candidate. The cleaner end state is `v_2pi_v` (gap table), which would replace the constant 2.
5. Figure-read radii doubled into `beam_diameter_um` (gissibl2016, 9 rows). Recommendation: keep extracted_from_figure with the reading and doubling in notes, since the reading uncertainty dominates and the arithmetic is trivial; the alternative under "a value you compute is derived" would be 9 basis edits with no gain in information.
