## Coordinator questions

1. riedhauser2025 kappa_0 `measured` (m1, m2, m7; Q0 m3 and loss m4 `derived` from it). The paper fits every resonance with the doublet
   model Eq. (S5) with kappa_ex, kappa_0 and mu free (p.11-12), and a resolved doublet's dip depth breaks the kappa_0 / kappa_ex swap
   symmetry (D455 / D465 ji2024); the Fig. S2 example is clearly resolved (mu 998 MHz vs kappa / 2 = 568 MHz). Unlike ji2024 there is no
   broadband kappa_ex trace, and the text says "most" (not all) resonances are split, so some resonances inside the per-device medians may be
   regime-ambiguous. Recommendation: keep `measured` (the published statistic comes from the regime-resolving fit model, unconverged fits
   discarded), with the caveat now in the m1 note. Alternative: `author_estimate` on m1 / m2 (and m3 / m4 then `author_estimate` as D403
   carries), keeping m7 `measured` (resolved doublet).
2. riedhauser2025 ridge height 110 nm (m11 `measured`) and 1.6 um top width (m13 `derived` from that). The sentence states the dimensions
   with a bracketed pointer to the SEM top view and FIB cross-section (Fig. 1(a),(b)); the figures carry only scale bars (no dimension
   labels; `img_p03_4.png` is the raw FIB frame), and the paper does not say "measured" or "designed". "Width ... at the top of the ridge"
   describes the as-etched trapezoid, which a mask design value would not. Recommendation: keep `measured` (as-fabricated dimension
   evidenced by the cited SEM; nie2019 D552 precedent treats an SEM sentence as measured). Alternative: `design_target` on m11 (m13 stays
   `derived`, m12 stays `derived`).
3. xiang2022 0.93 A/W `measured` with the GC loss in `excludes`. The paper de-embeds its own test-structure GC loss (5.4 dB) without printing
   the arithmetic (p.3). Merged precedent is split (de-embedded with an own reference structure and no arithmetic: srinivasan2020 m1-m5,
   shahin2025 m1-m4, chen2016a `measured`; "calculated by the authors" with numbers: hu2023a, wang2022a `derived`). D394 makes a de-embedded
   on-chip power `derived`, but that ruling concerns an output power, and D167 keeps a directly stated value with no shown arithmetic
   `measured`. Recommendation: keep `measured` (srinivasan2020 / shahin2025 APD precedent, the closest class match); the same applies to the
   70 A/W figure row. Alternative: `derived` on m1 (D394 analogue), which would then also apply to the gain rows' R0.
4. xiang2022 fab empty with `process_name` "standard 180 nm MPW process (silicon photonics foundry not named)". p.6 is a passive sentence
   naming only "silicon photonics foundry" (no org), p.3 is a capability sentence, p.2 cites an unnamed "foundry manual"; the authors are all
   at Zhejiang University with no first-person fabrication statement. Recommendation: keep fab empty (D80b, D433) and the process_name (D536
   node wording; the parenthetical follows the baghdadi2021 style). The coordinator may trim the parenthetical to keep only printed words
   ("standard 180 nm MPW process"); no op written.
5. runge2015 fab empty: "100G photodetector chips have been fabricated on a 3-inch InP wafer" (p.1, passive, no org), all five authors at

   fabricated / all authors at one org are not enough").
