## Questions raised by the distiller (evidence-based recommendations)

1. deng2024 R = 0.7 / 1.2 um rings: recommend resonator.ring (patched). Evidence: p.7 Sec. 3 builds a radius series
   (0.7-2 um) to show single-mode resonance at 0.7 um and FSR / roundtrip-loss scaling (Fig. 5(b)-(c)); the WDM filter
   uses only R = 2 um rings (p.7-9). D400 restricts wdm.mrr to channel filters in a WDM path; eid2016 is a dedicated
   add-drop filter device, so it is not a precedent for test rings. Consequence: the 167 GHz drop FWHM moves to
   linewidth_mhz (resonator has no bw_fwhm_ghz, D250). If the coordinator prefers wdm.mrr, drop the two component ops
   and the r1p2-m3 op from the patch.
2. sun2022 "9/cm" (p.3 Sec. 3, "measured internal loss for PMC waveguide at 1550 nm"): the paper defines neither the
   coefficient nor the method. Internal loss in cm-1 is the modal power attenuation coefficient in the
   semiconductor-laser convention (a field coefficient would give 78.2 dB/cm). Recommend keeping 39.09 dB/cm derived
   with the note as written (single-step unit conversion under the standard convention; the value is the paper's own
   measurement, not a cited one). Alternative if the convention is judged an assumption: basis author_estimate; deleting
   it would leave the PMC row without a performance metric (component deleted, D49). No psr row: endorsed (judgment
   call 3).
3. taballione2019: 0.2 dB/cm and ">= 60 %" as author_estimate endorsed. p.3 "exhibit a propagation loss of 0.2 dB/cm
   for a total on-chip transmission >= 60 %" follows the sentence citing [33] for the cross-section, and p.10 frames 0.2
   dB/cm as what the chosen cross-section [33] ensures; no measurement is described (D80i borderline -> author_estimate
   with note). The 60 % equals 0.2 dB/cm over the ~10 cm longest path (2 dB -> 63 %), i.e. arithmetic on the same
   estimate, so author_estimate (not derived) for the 2.22 dB row. Fab empty endorsed: LioniX co-authors, but no
   fabrication statement anywhere (rule 12, D80b); the acknowledgment names only a discussion.
4. Orgs: Applied Nanotools is in the registry (`org_applied_nanotools`, foundry, CA): fab set on the valdez2025 rows
   (patched). Missing and needed: University of Washington Nanofabrication Facility (lu2015 p.10, facility role; also
   eid2016, chen2024a, fang2021, adya2024, lim2026 notes) and Europractice MPW service (ribeiro2016 p.3, fab role per
   D398 / D83d; also desmet2020). Missing but not needed for components: James Watt Nanofabrication Centre, University
   of Glasgow (sun2022 p.4: staff thanked for help in fabricating = staff thanks, rule 12; sinclair2020 / zhang2017
   precedent), QuiX BV (taballione2019 p.1 affiliation only, no role).
5. Registry gaps: confirmed at their locators (next section).
