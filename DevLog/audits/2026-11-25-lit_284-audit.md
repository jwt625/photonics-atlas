## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. (a) li2018b tuning range and SMSR, D159. Confirmed ambiguous: Sec. 3 presents the 1527-1573 nm tuning (> 40 dB SMSR, < 1.5 dB
   power variation) before "Laser devices with two different gain waveguide lengths (4.2 cm and 8.4 cm) are fabricated. The
   device with an 8.4 cm long gain waveguide is chosen for the following efficiency and power measurements" (p.7-8). "Following"
   ties only the power data to 8.4 cm; Fig. 3 caption and the abstract speak of "the laser" without a length. Recommendation: keep
   D159 (not entered, bounds in notes of both rows). No alternative attachment is supported by the text.
2. (b) li2018b sibling assignment. Agree. 4.2 cm row: p.8 ties the fine-tuning cavity to "4.6 cm including the 4.2 cm gain
   waveguide" and p.10 measures the linewidth on "the tunable laser device with a 4.6 cm round-trip cavity length"; 340000 Hz
   `derived` is D256. 8.4 cm row: power and Fig. 3(d) explicitly (p.7-8); threshold read 32-33 mW, row value 33 +/- 3 mW kept.
   Gain lengths added as `length_um` (misses).
3. (c) li2018b heaters and bend. Keep `thermal_time_constant_us`: the paper fits the rising and falling edges "with an exponential
   function" (p.9), which is the registry definition (first-order constant, not 10-90 %); `rise_time_us` would mislabel them.
   Keep the Euler bend as `insertion_loss_db` per_component per 180 degrees, `measured` with "estimated" quoted (rule 4; deng2024
   and manganelli2017 merged precedent): halving into `bend_loss_db_per_90` would be `derived` and assume two identical 90-degree
   halves of a single Euler 180-degree bend.
4. (d) auth2019 Table 2 average powers. Keep `author_estimate`, ref_plane `unspecified`. p.5 calls 0.48 mW the average power
   "solely by the passively mode-locked laser" (no booster), and the Fig. 2(a) fiber-coupled cells at the three Table 2 biases read
   2.7-3.8x lower, i.e. the values match the fiber power divided by the nominal "around 30 %" coupling (rule 4: rests on a nominal
   input). Alternative if the coordinator prefers not to infer the derivation: `measured` with ref_plane unspecified and the same
   conflict note; `fiber` is excluded by Fig. 2(a).
5. (e) duprez2016 mode-transformer > 90 %. Keep as `author_estimate`. The value sentence (p.3) carries no citation; only the next
   sentence refers the "principle of operation" to [4,11]. D408 limits D259 / D259a to a value sentence or figure that attributes
   the value to the earlier paper, which this one does not.
6. (f) duprez2016 mirror FSR sides. The batch reading of Fig. 7 is wrong (patched): Fig. 7's own arrows and brackets, its caption,
   the p.7-8 below / above rule and the p.8 laser-2 sentence all put the larger FSR on the right mirror; only Table 1 (whose columns
   are internally consistent with a_sp via Eq. (1)) puts it on the left. Recommendation: keep every value as printed with the side
   names of its own source and the D417 conflict note (patched), mirror rows named by the Table 1 columns. Alternative (stricter
   D159 reading): drop the side words from the four mirror labels; not recommended, since lengths, a_sp and FSR are tied per column
   in Table 1 and the reflectivity / geometry rows do not depend on the side.
7. (g) duprez2016 thresholds and grating coupler. Keep the Fig. 6 threshold readings (106 / 85 mA, `extracted_from_figure`; the
   paper only says "high threshold current values"); the mode-hop caveats are in notes and the zoomed reading agrees. Keep the 6 dB
   `scope: unspecified`: the sentence gives one loss for "the fibers" of both outputs and does not say per coupler or how it was
   measured; `per_coupler` would be a guess (rule 6). `measured` under D167 is fine.
8. (h) Fab / facility empty on all 18 rows: confirmed. li2018b: "The device is fabricated on a 300 mm silicon wafer ... same as
   previously reported [53-56]" (p.7) is passive with no org; acknowledgment thanks two named staff for Er-film deposition without
   naming an org (D84b / D72), funding DARPA only (D80c). auth2019: "epitaxial growth was completed" (p.3), passive, details in
   [35]; no acknowledgment section, funding only. duprez2016: "a 200-mm SOI wafer is processed", "the III-V heterostructure was
   grown" (p.6) passive, process "similar to" [4,13]; acknowledgment thanks staff for characterization help; IRT Nanoelec is a
   grant program (D80c). No first-person statement with an unambiguous org (li2018b and duprez2016 authors span several orgs) and
   no facility anywhere (D80b, Contract v7).
