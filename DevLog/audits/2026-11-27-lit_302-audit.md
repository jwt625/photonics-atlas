## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. li2019a lens-based system row (judgment a). **Delete** (patched). The FOV and divergence are fixed by a purchased bulk
   plano-convex lens (f 1.04 cm) through Eqs. (3)-(4); the paper itself presents them as lens-tunable ("reconfigured design of
   the beam divergence and steering angle by choosing a lens with a proper focal length", p.12). D558 deleted exactly this kind of
   value (zhang2021a 0.02 deg, chip plus off-chip bulk cylindrical lens). chang2020 is not a precedent for a bulk lens: its
   metalens is a fabricated chip-scale metasurface with its own row. Keeping the row would put a 0.06 deg divergence next to
   chip-defined OPA divergences in the free-space-emitter views. Alternative (not recommended): keep m1-m3 with a lens note, which
   would overturn D558. Switch as `switch_fabric.other` (D158) and fab empty: agree.
2. mehta2020 orgs (judgment b). **Keep** LioniX on all three rows, ETH Zurich added on the fibre-attach row only, no facility,
   process_name empty (see Points checked). The ETH fab entry rests on the first-person attachment sentence plus author
   contributions, not on affiliation alone.
3. skipper2025 205 mA (judgment c). **Keep on the selective-buffer row.** Fig. 4(a) read at full resolution: the lowest-threshold
   curve is the red solid line, lifting off at about 205-210 mA; the lowest dashed curve (green) starts near 280 mA; the caption
   assigns solid to the selective buffer. The figure fixes the sample identity, so D159 does not apply. "Best over 16 mW" stays
   out (blue solid about 18.4, green dashed about 17.1 and red solid about 16.6 mW at 1 A all exceed 16 mW: D159 / D254). AIM fab
   on the transition rows only, UCSB facility on every row: agree (koscica2025 / D604).
4. wang2021c grating-emitter family (judgment d). **Keep the family row and the eight reads** (verified, above). Axis "(mm)" read
   as um: keep; 600-2000 mm would be 0.6-2 m emitters, while the text says the length "can be at least 2 mm" and the OPA uses
   1.5 mm. **Keep SIMIT fab on this row only**: first-person "We fabricated several grating emitters" (p.4) with all authors at
   SIMIT (two also UCAS) follows liu2023a / liu2024b (SIMIT + UCAS, first person -> SIMIT); the other five rows rest on the
   passive "The OPA is fabricated on SOI integrated with silicon nitride" (p.2) and stay empty (fab per component, Contract v7;
   "we use silicon nitride to fabricate input port" p.1 is a material choice, D80b).
5. newman2019 two comb rows only (judgment e). **Agree.** The clock laser's only number is a contribution to the two-photon line
   width (~475 kHz), not its own linewidth; vapor cell and PMT are not photonic component classes; comb span, Q and pump
   thresholds are in Supplement 1, which is not cached.
