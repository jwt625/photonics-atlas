   platform owner named in the fabrication sentence is the fab. D278 does not bar LioniX B.V.: it rejected the string as affiliation-only for
   hosseini2015; its time-aware point (do not map a pre-2017 LioniX BV to `org_lionix_international`) still holds. Recommendation: fab empty
   now (patched notes), LioniX B.V. reported as missing fab org; in the org round add a time-correct LioniX BV org (D80d / D458 style) and

   paper repeats as the device's spacing. Recommendation: keep both rows (m4 design `author_estimate`, m21 figure read) with the D417 flag; to
   skip, drop m21 and the cross-note sentences in m4 and the component notes.
