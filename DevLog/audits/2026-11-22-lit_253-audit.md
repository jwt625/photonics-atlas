## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. akca2018 "overall crosstalk" as `crosstalk_cumulative_db` (judgment a): keep. The paper defines it as a "combination of non-adjacent
   receiver waveguide crosstalks" (p.2), i.e. an aggregate over the non-adjacent receivers, which is what `crosstalk_cumulative_db`
   ("cumulative (integrated)") holds; `crosstalk_nonadjacent_db` is the per-channel level. The printed definition is in every condition;
   "combination" is not explicitly a power sum, so a reader can still see the ambiguity. Merged precedents: pathak2014 / jeong2020 /
   palmer2026 (summed or integrated other-channel crosstalk) use cumulative.
2. akca2018 stage AWGs (judgment b): keep the four stage rows as fabricated (D80e; the stages exist on the measured chip, Fig. 2(b), and
   Fig. 6 labels their channels S1-S3). S1 / S3 carry design channel spacing, FSR and center wavelength (registry kind `performance`,
   basis `author_estimate`), so rule 9 is met; they are distinct designs (order 18 vs 19, focal length 933 vs 903.6 um), so merging them
   into S2 would be wrong.
3. akca2018 bases and fab (judgment c): keep. "around 1.5 dB/cm at 1550 nm" is a present-tense statement of the fabricated waveguides
   with no budget role and no citation on the value (the [12] in that sentence attaches to the reflow step): D167 / D450 analogue,
   `measured`. "10 dB ... is estimated" for facets without spot-size converters is a budget expectation with no data: `author_estimate`,
   scope `unspecified` (per facet or both not stated). Fab empty: passive fabrication sentence, and the acknowledgment thanks two named
   people for fabrication support without naming an org (D84b needs a named org; D80b).
4. joo2018 (judgment d): (i) footprint on both switch rows: keep. The ~0.7 mm2 is stated for "the fabricated SiN thermo-optic switch"
   (p.3), in the abstract and in the conclusion for the devices generally; the two layouts differ only in the coupler lengths (44 vs
   32.6 um) with the same 1 mm heaters, so this is a shared layout size, not a "typical" family performance value (D254 targets the
   latter). (ii) Rise / fall assignment: correct on the Fig. 9 image (9.55 and 9 us labels sit on the 10 % -> 90 % edges, 7.7 and 8.48 us
   on the 90 % -> 10 % edges). (iii) Table II as simulated `split_ratio_pct`, first number: keep; the order of the printed ratio follows
   the design 50:50 annotation and the note says the port is not named. (iv) Fab ETRI: keep; first-person "we designed and fabricated"
   (p.1) and all three authors at ETRI meets rule 12.
5. liu2019c (judgment e): keep all choices. Laser-to-PLC two-lens path as `chip_chip_coupler.other` (hu2017) and PLC-to-receptacle lens as
   `micro_optics.other`; four lane lasers as one population row (D165) with lane in condition; TOSA outputs at `module` (packaged output,
   cf. juodawlkis2009 in lit_235); per-lane CE `author_estimate` (each rests on the "about 0.5 dB" receptacle term of unstated origin;
   D437); the 0.5 dB itself `author_estimate` as a budget term (D437 over D167). HUST only on the two assembled lens rows (D399: "we aligned
   the 2nd lenses", "We have sucessfully fabricated a TOSA", single affiliation). Vendors: Shijia Photons Technology as fab of the AWG
   row once registered (rule 12, purchased device under test; name as printed). "Mitsubishi": see missing orgs; recommend leaving the
   DFB row's fab empty until the org round decides.
6. pitris2018 (judgment f): keep. Measured AWGR IL `de_embedded` + `reference_structure` matches the extraction plus reference-waveguide
   normalization (p.5); simulated rows `per_component` (Gaussian-approximated AWGR alone). Fab org_imec with process_name
   "imec-ePIXfab silicon photonics passives technology" as printed (Contract v7: named process with the foundry). ePIXfab as a second fab
   (access provider, D398) once registered.
7. valicourt2015 (judgment g): keep all. (i) AWG IL from the Fig. 5 peaks (`extracted_from_figure`, scope unspecified): "< -2dB" /
