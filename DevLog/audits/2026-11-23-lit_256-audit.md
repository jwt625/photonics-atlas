## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. atabaki2010 tuning efficiency (judgment a): keep it out. The re-read narrows the conflict to ~3.2x (Fig. 4(a) ~0.9 nm/mW vs Fig. 5(a)
   34.6 GHz/mW ~0.28 nm/mW, same 0.5 um heater, same experiment class); the paper does not reconcile them (the p.8 pad / connector
   correction is applied to the simulation, not to the experiment). Entering either value would pick a side (D159 spirit, D156). Note
   patched with the corrected reading.
2. atabaki2010 725 ns as `rise_time_us` `simulated`, statistic `best` (judgment a): keep. p.11 "rise-times as low as 725 ns can be achieved"
   and p.12 "Using this model, it is shown that the fastest reconfiguration time ... is around 725 ns": a model prediction, not demonstrated
   (the demonstration is < 1 us, entered as measured). Optional: add `qualifier approx` for the p.12 "around". tau_diff 1.5 us `derived` is
   right (D80a model-parameter extraction).
3. atabaki2010 trench row as `design` / `simulated` (judgment a): keep. The 6.2 us sentence is in the modelling section and is followed by
   "Other simulations on the effect of trenches", and the authors state they avoid trenches; no fabricated trench device is described.
4. atabaki2010 Q_i ~60k `author_estimate` (judgment a, D403): keep. The p.6-7 sentence gives no fit or coupling-regime determination; the
   Fig. 4(a) dips (about 9-10 dB below a rippled baseline) do not fix the regime.
5. yu2016a ~0.83 nm/mW on the r_out 2.2 um device (judgment b): keep. Fig. 6 is not a D156 case: each marker sits beside its own micrograph
   and label block (the r_out 2.2 um block is level with the lower r_d = 3 um marker), and the text ties 1.13 nm/mW to r_out 2.3 um (p.7),
   so the tie holds twice. Re-read 0.84 nm/mW (+/-0.02): within the stated reading uncertainty, value not changed.
6. yu2016a `heater_gap_um` 0 (judgment b): keep. The registry definition is "heater-to-waveguide gap as stated", and the paper states direct
   contact with no isolator layer (abstract, p.2); 0 is the stated geometry, with the wording in the condition. Merged graphene / MoS2 heater
   rows hold 0.011-0.24 um spacer values, so a 0 row is comparable.
7. yu2016a graphene absorption as `prop_loss_db_per_cm` 1.6 `simulated` on the disk row (judgment b): keep. It is a WGM propagation loss
   of this disk (mode solver, Fig. 4(b) star), the condition says "graphene-absorption contribution only"; wavelength reason patched.
   Alternative: drop it, since a reader of the resonator loss column may take it as a total loss.
8. lin2022c fab Southeast University (judgment c): keep (first person "we designed and fabricated", abstract / p.1 / p.6, all nine authors
   at one centre; D595 / D647). "According to the design manual and our simulation" (p.2) hints at a foundry rule set but names no foundry,
   process or PDK, so Contract v7 gives no other org and no `process_name`; the component notes record it. Strict alternative: fab empty
   on all six rows (if the coordinator reads "design manual" as evidence of an outside fab).
9. lin2022c switching power as `p_pi_mw` on the switch rows (judgment c): keep (zhu2018 / lit_254 Q5 precedent). Eq. (4) is the heater1
   power between the off (pi) and on (2pi) states, i.e. a pi shift of the spiral phase shifter; `derived` is right (authors' arithmetic on
   their measured V and R, D80a). Optional cross-note on spiral_ps / straight_ps that the measured P_pi sits on the switch rows.
10. lin2022c MMI "< 0.5 dB, according to our test" (judgment c): keep `excess_loss_db`, `measured`, method `unspecified`, `lt`. "Our test"
    is a measurement claim; no input states an assumption, so `author_estimate` does not apply; rule 6 makes a 1x2 / 2x2 value below the
    ideal split an excess loss. Spiral 1.2 dB `author_estimate` (isolation not described): keep.
11. song2016a (judgment d): keep fab empty (passive "fabricated by PECVD", p.2; the "platforms provided by IMEC [6]" sentence cites
    refractive indices; D80b, D433 szelag2017). Alternative `org_imec` only if the coordinator reads the index sentence as naming the
    platform of these chips. One row per bend type with the radius as condition: keep (bogaerts2010 precedent); per-radius k / widest widths
    are uncertain anyway (text vs Fig. 8 caption conflict, recorded). Table II mean / std rows with n 4: keep. Platform 1.0 dB/cm
    `author_estimate`: keep (origin not stated). Four 1550 nm Table III `design` rows: keep (printed values, distinct designs).
12. koos2007 (judgment e): keep fab empty (first-person "We fabricated" with authors at three unrelated universities; D154 qi2024). 0.35 dB
    per 180-degree COB as `insertion_loss_db` `per_component` `cutback`, not halved: keep (cherchi2013 precedent; halving a 180-degree
    contour that is not two 90-degree bends would invent a per-90 value). Optional: `wavelength_nm` 1480 / `wavelength_max_nm` 1580
    (spectral span of the Fig. 4 meanders) instead of 1550 inferred; the paper does not say whether the slope is per wavelength or averaged,
    so the current form is acceptable.
13. Optional platform (not patched): yu2016a six rows `soi` -> `soi_strip`? The graphene "crosses the step from the silicon core to the top
    surface of the SiO2-BOX layer" and the Ti contacts sit on the BOX (p.2-3), which implies a full etch around the disk, but no etch depth is
    printed. Recommend keep `soi` (D434 applied only on a stated etch).
