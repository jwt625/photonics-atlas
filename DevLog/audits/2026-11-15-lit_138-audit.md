## Coordinator questions

1. boller2019 Sec. 5 dual-gain rows vs ref. [149] (asked). [149] (Epping et al., ASSL 2019, "High power, tunable, narrow linewidth dual
   gain hybrid laser") has seven authors, all co-authors of boller2019. The measurement sentence "we achieve a maximum fiber coupled output
   power of 105 mW" (p.12), the 117 mW sentence, the tuning / SMSR sentence, the linewidth and RIN paragraphs and the Fig. 9 / Fig. 10
   captions carry no citation; [149] sits only on the next sentence, the record claim "these values are the highest power ever achieved".
   D408 (journal re-presentation of the authors' conference work keeps its values unless the value sentence or figure attributes them) and
   D80i (own result clearly reported) both point to keeping. Recommendation: keep all Sec. 5 rows (no op). Strict alternative: delete m1 / m2
   only (the two values the cited sentence names); deleting the whole section would go beyond D408 (op lists in the patch header).
2. rees2020 0.1 dB/cm and 100 um bend radius next to ref. [31] (asked). The citation closes the preceding sentence ("as described by
   Roeloffzen et al [31]"); the loss sentence names "This single-mode waveguide" of the authors' feedback chip, with no measurement shown.
   D80i borderline; the merged zhao2016 row (same double-stripe platform, < 0.1 dB/cm after a citing sentence) stays `author_estimate` (D554).
   Recommendation: keep m1 `author_estimate` and m2 `design_target` (no op). The Vernier ring row's 100 um is the paper's own design rule
   ("the requirement of bend radii of at least 100 um", p.4): keep regardless.
3. boller2019 Sec. 6 / Sec. 7.1 exclusions (distiller judgment). The Sec. 6 laser is introduced as "presented here [108]" and the 40 Hz value
   cites [108]; Sec. 7.1 shows Fig. 12 "[168]" and the Fig. 13 calculation cites [168]. Recommendation: keep out (D259a strict, as zhang2023).
   Alternative for Sec. 6 only: keep the values whose sentences carry no citation (42 mA, 23 mW at 320 mA, > 70 nm, > 60 dB, 210 Hz at 3 mW)
   if the coordinator treats a co-author thesis [108] like the D408 conference case; not recommended, because the device itself is attributed.
4. Same laser in two sources (boller2019 Sec. 4 and rees2020): identical values and identical figure traces, no cross-citation either way,
   both arXiv postings within a month (2019-11 / 2019-12). Recommendation: keep both (D259 needs an attribution, none exists); cross-notes now on
   both rows. Benchmark / view note: the two rows are one physical device; a duplicate-device flag in views would avoid double counting.
5. rees2020 gain-chip row (distiller left out). The HHI fabrication statement is explicit (p.3), but the only performance value, gain bandwidth
