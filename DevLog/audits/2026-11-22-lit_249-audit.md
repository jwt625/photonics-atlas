## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. xiao2019 single gratings as `mode_converter.converter` (judgment a): keep. G01 / G02 / G12 are contra-directional mode-converting
   gratings whose stated figures of merit are modal crosstalk and pass-through loss of the other modes (crosstalk_db is not allowed on
   reflector), and the cascade rows are mode converters built from them; one class for the family keeps the spectra comparable.
   Counter-precedent: li2023a antisymmetric multimode Bragg grating sits in `reflector.bragg`, but that device is a delay-line reflector.
   Close the loss by the registry (slsr_db, kappa_per_cm on mode_converter, below), not by a D396 second row per grating.
2. xiao2019 fab empty (judgment b): keep empty. "When we fabricated the cladded PMMA surface grating" and "our fabrication condition"
   (p.11) are first person, but the authors are not at one org (seven at Nanjing University, Jia Li at the University of Miami only,
   Xiangfei Chen at both). Rule 12 needs an unambiguous own org; D154 (qi2024) and D433 (hiratani2024, dual affiliations, site unstated)
   leave such rows empty. The waveguide sentence is passive with no org (D80b). If the coordinator reads "our fabrication condition" as
   the Nanjing group, org_nanjing_university (exists) is the only candidate; not recommended.
3. zou2014 IME acknowledgment (judgment c): keep all four rows `design`, fab empty, acknowledgment in notes. The paper reports no
   fabricated device and no measurement; every value is transfer-matrix / FEM / ATLAS / COMSOL output. org_astar_ime on design rows
   would place simulated values in an IME foundry column of the benchmark (Contract v7: fab is per component as stated; D80e makes
   `fabricated` depend on the component existing on the paper's chip, which this paper never shows).
4. zou2014 bandwidth and band (judgment d): keep. `stopband_width_nm` + "level not defined" matches 19 merged rows; D82g sends a
   stated 3-dB reflection band to bw_3db_nm, which is not the case here. 17.4 nm = 20 - 2.6 nm stays `simulated` (D83f). c_band
   inferred from the plotted reflection band (about 1541-1561 nm) is acceptable under rule 8.
5. cheng2019 (judgment e/f): keep both forms. The dB bound and the % efficiency are both printed (the % is the abstract headline), they
   are different registry parameters (no double count within a parameter), and the notes tie them as one statement. "Up to 97.9 % /
   98.6 %" -> `le` per rule 7; the values equal the design-point dB values (0.09 dB = 97.9 %), so the bound is tight; acceptable. The
   "TE1" / "TE0-to-TE1" typos inside Sec. 2.2 belong to the TE0-TE2 converter (section, Fig. 7 / Fig. 9 captions, 1.4 um width); kept on
   that row with the note.
6. xiao2019 cascade peak -0.2 dB at 1550 nm inferred, `best` (judgment f/h): keep. The peak position is not printed, but all gratings
   are designed at 1550.0 nm (p.7) and Figs. 9-11 show the field at 1550 nm with full conversion; Fig. 9(b) / 10(b) passbands are flat
   at about 0 dB from about 1540 to 1555 nm. Alternative `band: c_band` would lose nothing important; not required.
7. xiao2019 three measured 3-dB widths on the fabricated row (judgment g/i): keep. T21 passband, T11 notch (G01 reflection) and T00
   notch (G01 + G02) are three responses of one fabricated device; rule 9 / D396 keep one row per design within a class, conditions
   distinguish them. Separate G01 / G02 rows would be sub-parts with no separate fabrication or measurement.
8. yao2012 (judgment j/h): keep. (a) FDTD Q 1.74e7 on the fabricated row: D80e; the paper presents the fabricated cavity as this design
   ("the cavity has been fabricated"), and the 126 / 1017 / 2012 nm vs 120 / 1000 / 2000 nm difference is in the notes. The l_band
   inference is weak (Fig. 2(d) places the design resonance at about 1560-1615 nm for background index 1.33-1.54, the simulation
   background is not printed), but the note states the basis; `band: unspecified` would also be defensible. (b) Fig. 4(a) peak 1583.5 nm
   checked on the render. (c) Wx as a third `width_nm` row with "along the propagation axis": no better parameter exists (length_um
   would read as device length); keep.
