## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. rueda2019 0 rows (D411). Recommendation: keep 0 rows. The resonator is "machined from single crystalline lithium
   niobate" (p.4), R 2.45 mm, 0.4 mm thick, prism coupled in a copper cavity: a discrete bulk mm-scale crystal resonator,
   as tebeneva2024. The 260 mV Vpi / EO-comb values are EO-atlas material at most (not this table).
2. dmitriev2022 Q-factor as `q_intrinsic` derived and kappa_0 / kappa_c measured. Recommendation: keep. The printed Q
   matches omega / kappa_0 within 2-4 % for both rings and misses the loaded value by 1.7x / 2.4x, so intrinsic is the
   reading the numbers allow (D519); the Methods state a backscattering (doublet) fit that yields kappa_0, kappa_c and
   gamma separately (D587 / D465 accepted such fits as fixing the regime). Caveat for the record: neither doublet is
   resolved as two dips in Fig. 3(e), (f); for the 150 GHz ring gamma / kappa is about 0.19, so the swap of kappa_0 and
   kappa_c is only weakly constrained by the lineshape (1 THz: about 0.72). Alternative (strict D403): 150 GHz ring
   m2 / m3 / m5 -> `author_estimate` with that reason; the 1 THz rows stay.
3. dmitriev2022 edge coupler "insertion losses as low as -1.1 dB on both sides of a chip" (p.2). Recommendation: keep
   `author_estimate`, `per_coupler`, statistic best. The sentence is a capability statement ("ensuring") with no
   calibration or method anywhere in the paper; D167 (shi2024) rests on a described lensed-fiber calibration, D418 kept
   nader2025 edge-coupler losses `author_estimate` for the same lack of origin.
4. dmitriev2022 diode characteristics and pump-to-comb efficiencies `author_estimate`. Recommendation: keep. Diode
   values are stated as properties of purchased parts without a measurement or datasheet attribution (lihachev2022 D431,
   not `specification` since no datasheet is named). eta_p2c divides by P_pump "reduced by the coupling losses" whose
   value and origin are not printed (rule 4); the printed approximate powers give 25.7 / 42.5 %, noted. Consistency
   note: merged cmp_lit_hu2022_cr_eo_comb-m1 (on-chip powers after a facet-loss correction, "method not described") is
   `measured`; a later sweep may want to align it.
5. Seminex. Recommendation: org round entry "Seminex Corp., USA" (fab role, vendor of the FP laser diodes; p.11 Supp.
   Note 2 "FP diodes manufactured by Seminex Corp., USA"; p.8 Methods "DFB and FP (Seminex Corp., USA)"), then fab on
   `cmp_lit_dmitriev2022_fp_laser_diode` only. The Methods parenthetical follows "FP" and Supp. Note 2 names Seminex for
   the FP diodes alone, so the DFB vendor stays not stated (component note already says so).
