## Coordinator questions

1. morishima2021 random mating (average 0.07 dB, 0.21 dB or lower for more than 97 %, max 0.39 dB, 2688 samples; p.7-8, Fig. 9, abstract).
   The paper says its preliminary fabrication results were presented in ref. 8 (p.1 Introduction; ref. 8 = the same authors' OFC 2020
   paper Th3I.2, not in the registry); the Fig. 9 caption ends with "[8]" and the Grade B sentence that carries the statistics ends with
   "[8]". Figs. 1 and 2 (schematics) also cite [8]; Tables 2-3 and Figs. 4-8 do not. The p.7 sentence ("we conducted a random mating
   test") and the abstract present the test in the first person. Recommendation: keep out, values in notes (D259a / D408 caption clause;
   govdeli2025 Fig. 4(a) in D465 and srinivasan2020 in D455 are the precedents: a caption citing the authors' earlier paper
   removes the figure's values). To add them back, the distiller's report lists the rows (insertion_loss_db 0.07 mean and 0.39 with
   qualifier le, n_samples 2688, `per_interface`, `band: unspecified` because p.7-8 print no wavelength for this test).
2. hu2021 (a) per-facet losses 0.54 / 0.59 dB (and the 0.92 / 0.99 dB, < 1 dB, 0.72 dB rows): keep `measured` + `loss_breakdown` (D167;
   D441 precedent). The subtracted terms are the authors' own (splice loss, no number printed; ridge-waveguide loss 0.99 / 1.54 dB/cm from
   Supplement 1 with the waveguide length not printed), so the arithmetic cannot be reproduced and no term has an unstated origin (D428
   does not apply). (b) "polarization extinction ratio" lower than 0.05 dB at 1550 nm: keep `pdl_db` with qualifier lt. The quantity is the
   TE / TM coupling-loss difference (0.59 - 0.54 dB), a polarization-dependent loss; `per_db` is the suppression of the unwanted
   polarization by a polarizer, which this coupler does not provide. The printed "< 0.05" is kept even though the two printed losses differ
   by exactly 0.05 dB (rounding).
3. zhu2025 576 nm span (IL < 1 dB and CT < -30 dB over 1257-1833 nm): keep `simulated`. p.10 says the source range was extended to
   1200-1900 nm and "additional simulations" were run; the abstract calls it further analysis; the conclusion says the tested bandwidth is
   limited by the setup and the 576 nm comes from simulation results. Fig. 15 shows smooth IL / CT / RL traces with markers over 1200-1900 nm,
   including an RL trace the grating-coupled setup cannot measure, and the measured window is GC-limited to about 80 nm (p.10). The single
   "measured" in p.10 is a wording slip.
4. chen2022a "IL < 1 dB over 1460-1580 nm" (p.9, abstract, conclusion): keep it out as a loss (D401). p.9 derives it from the per-port
   window of -3 +/- 0.5 dB (the paper's "1 dB operation bandwidth" definition), i.e. a split-deviation criterion, not a separately measured
   total loss; the measured IL is the cascade fit 0.66 dB at 1550 nm (m1). The 120 nm `bw_1db_nm` row with the criterion in the condition
   follows D539 (lu2015a) and stays; registry candidates `split_deviation_db` (D401) and `criterion_bw_nm` (D539) would hold both.
5. tao2025 fab (new): p.3 "our fabrication process" / "our fabrication experience" is own-process wording (D84a), but the nine authors are
   at Nankai University and at SIMIT / UCAS (p.1); no sentence says which org fabricated the coupler, and ref. 43 (ion-sliced SiCOI) is cited
   for the wafer technique only. Recommendation: fab empty (rule 12: org not unambiguous; D80b), note patched. Alternative: none without a
   statement naming the org.
6. hu2021 Fig. 7(b) tip-width siblings (see Misses): recommendation: do not add (one figure-read point per sibling, D159 ambiguity with
   the headline device). Alternative: 8 sibling rows `fiber_chip_coupler.edge`, `insertion_loss_db` `extracted_from_figure` (+/- 0.03 dB),
   `lower_tip_width_nm` from the axis.
