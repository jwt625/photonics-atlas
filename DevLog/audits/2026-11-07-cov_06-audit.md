## Rulings on the distiller's questions and on orgs

1. li2022 EO tuning efficiency (report Q1): convert, do not add a GHz/V parameter. Merged practice enters printed
   GHz/V or MHz/V as `tuning_efficiency_pm_per_v` with lambda^2 / c, basis derived at a stated or inferred operating
   wavelength (andreou2019 0.29 GHz/V, stanfield2019, lihachev2022), author_estimate only where a nominal wavelength
   had to be assumed (kim2026, D256a). Here the device lases at 1581.12 nm (p.4, p.6) and any wavelength of the
   1576-1596 nm tuning range moves the result by under 2 %. Added as m8 (patch). ji2026 620.6 GHz/W is outside this
   batch; the same conversion into tuning_efficiency_pm_per_mw would apply.
2. zhou2025 fab (report Q2): recommend org_east_china_normal_university, borderline. D84a counts a statement that the
   device was made with the authors' own process; the paper fabricates the MCI chip with PLACE and refers the method
   details to the authors' own earlier work (p.7). Every author lists an ECNU affiliation (p.1; Y. Cheng also SIOM and
   others), so the org is unambiguous; nan2025 (same group, same pointer) is merged with ECNU. li2026a / li2024a stayed
   empty because their authors were spread over several orgs, which is not the case here. Patched; drop the two ops to
   keep fab empty.
3. xiang2021a SIL row fab (report Q3): keep org_ucsb with the note. The contributions name C.X. and J.P. (UCSB) as
   fabricators of the laser device (p.8); the ring chip was provided by W.J. (UCSB) and M.P. (Anello Photonics) with no
   fabrication statement, so the ring row stays empty; heim2026 precedent (UCSB on the laser rows the UCSB authors
   built, D399 / D460).
4. Registry candidates (report Q4): confirmed as skipped values with locators (babichev2025 p.3 Table I and EQE 37 %,
   p.5 T1 about 100 K; fan2025a p.4 MSR 13.5 dB; guo2023 p.6 2.6 pJ / 0.51 W, TBP 0.445). internal_loss_per_cm,
   internal_efficiency_pct and threshold_current_density are already D431 candidates, pulse_energy_pj D433; new ones:
   transparency_current_density_a_per_cm2, characteristic_temperature_t1_k, external_quantum_efficiency_pct (laser),
   peak_power_w, a Vernier-filter mode suppression ratio (main to next Vernier peak) on resonator / wdm, and
   time_bandwidth_product.
5. Ring classes in laser cavities (D400): fan2025a and li2022 Vernier pairs -> resonator.ring (patched); xiang2021a
   stand-alone SIL ring resonator.ring correct.
6. Fab / facility orgs otherwise as staged: babichev2025 and chen2020 empty (passive voice, D80b); fan2025a empty
