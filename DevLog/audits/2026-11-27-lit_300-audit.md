   inconsistency flagged; tossoun2024 / tsuchizawa2005 precedents). The main-text sentence (p.3) and the Methods "Sample details"
   (p.9) both name the operated device and neither is marked as rounded; choosing one would be a guess. Both rows are
   `author_estimate`, so no benchmark cell takes them. **FM-transduction cutoff: delete** (patched, see above); if the coordinator
   prefers to keep it, it needs its own parameter, not `bw3db_ghz`. **Fab `org_epfl` + CMi facility: keep** (first-person author
   contribution naming the place; D80b, D83c).
2. kim2020 conflicts (judgment b). **Index: keep core 1.397** (the design paragraph is the one consistent with guiding; the
   fabrication paragraph's order would make the core the low-index layer); reason added to the TOPM note. **37.9 Hz vs 116 Hz:
   keep the printed 37.9 Hz / 4.2 ms with the conflict note.** New fact from the Fig. 7(a) render: the figure itself carries both
   numbers (a vertical dashed line at about 38 Hz and the asymptote corner at about 116 Hz), and the measured points (-0.8 dB at
   10 Hz, -3.5 dB at 100 Hz) do not resolve which is right; supplement in the m8 note.
3. kim2020 fab (judgment b). **Keep empty.** "We designed and fabricated" with authors at three orgs is the D154 / D395 case
   (rule 12 needs an unambiguous authors' org); the acknowledgment thanks individuals for discussions only.
4. yi2015 (judgment d). **Figure reads: keep** (reproduced, above). **KNI: keep facility empty.** KNI appears in the funding list
   and in the acknowledgment sentence "gratefully acknowledge ... DARPA ..., NASA ..., the Kavli Nanoscience Institute, and the
   Institute for Quantum Information and Matter" with program numbers around it: a funder list, not a statement that work was
   performed there or of fabrication support (D80c, D83e; yang2018a precedent). Contrast guo2023, where the acknowledgment says
   the nanofabrication was performed at KNI.
5. liang2021 (judgment e). **On-chip power and WPE `author_estimate`: keep.** Both follow from the -7.5 dB GC correction, whose
   origin is not stated (rule 4), and from a symmetric-output assumption (per-side vs total not stated; note added). **MOSCAP
   microring modulator: leave to the EO atlas** (rule 2): it is on a separate wafer ("fabricated at the same time [4]"), its
   design is in ref. [4] (ECOC 2020), and the paper's own data are link-level (25 Gb/s eye, ER 5.8 dB, Vpp 4 V, -20 dB chip
   reflection through two GCs). The EO atlas has no liang2021 / srinivasan2020 row (checked `devices.csv`), so list it as an
   EO-atlas candidate.
6. yoon2023 (judgment c). **Budget terms `author_estimate`: keep** (rule 4, zhou2023b). **45 / 10 deg not entered: agree**:
   the p.4 sentence ends "[22,26]" and the abstract restates it; ref. [26] is lit:kang2019 (already merged with its 45.4 deg
   measured value), ref. [22] the 2019 Opt. Lett. radiator paper; the uncited "+/-22.5 deg" on p.4 is the same quantity (D259).

7. kim2020 OPA loss through the packaged lens and mirror (distiller's note). **Keep** with the `includes` list: the value is a
