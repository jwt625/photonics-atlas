## Rulings on the batch report's judgment calls and the coordinator questions

1. barwicz2017 class split and granularity: endorsed. chip_chip_coupler.butt for the coupler ends, one
   mode_converter.converter row per length and per feature-size variant (skill rule 9; Fig. 4 caption: measured mode
   conversion loss "for various converter lengths", (c) the ~13 nm reduced feature size). Figure readings are
   extracted_from_figure with the reading uncertainty (skill rule 4): Fig. 4 readings confirmed (table above), three
   Fig. 2(d) readings corrected. Coupler rows with simulated values only stay row_kind fabricated (D80e). The text peak
   -0.9 dB on the 250 um converter: endorsed, the 250 um label points to the red top curve of Fig. 4(b), which starts at
   about -0.92 dB at 1510 nm (D156 satisfied: marker ties label to curve). Fab org_ibm_watson (p.3 Acknowledgments:
   chips "fabricated in" the IBM Watson Microelectronics Research Laboratory); no facility (own lab, D404).
2. theurer2020 DFB section (D259a / D408): endorsed. D408 keeps values of a journal extension unless the sentence or
   figure attributes them to the earlier paper; here the figure does: Fig. 5 (18 DFB lasers, the -2.1 dB average /
   0.35 dB std of p.3 and of the abstract) is captioned [16], and so are Figs. 2, 3, 6, 7, 8 (single-laser -1.6 dB,
   > 60 mW, 4-array -2.25 dB); p.2 states the > 60 mW DFB result as previous work [15], [16] and the new contribution
   as the extension to the tunable-laser PIC. The abstract's 2.1 dB is therefore the [16] measurement, not a new one.
   The -1.3 dB DFB theory (p.3-4) rests on DFB and TriPleX MFDs "derived from far field measurements as shown in [16]"
   (skill rule 3, computed from cited values). The gain-chip -0.3 dB (p.6) is this paper's computation with its own
   gain-chip MFDs and the cited TriPleX MFDs: kept simulated with the note.
3. hassan2020 class: changed to mode_converter.converter (see findings). The paper's own word "metalens" stays in the
   label.
4. sotirova2024 fiber_interface.fan_out: endorsed (registry subtype "glass waveguide fan-out"; 127 um VGA pitch to 8 um).
   Coupling-efficiency scope unspecified: endorsed (evaluated by imaging the chip output, p.11, so the value includes
   propagation; neither per_coupler nor fiber_to_fiber). System-level crosstalk at the ion (m2 > 30 dB, m3 ~50 dB best):
   kept with the condition naming the AOM / relay-optics path (D83a analogue: path values stay with their path stated);
   the paper attributes the variation to AOM leakage and optics scatter (p.10). Fab org_oxford on the SPIM rows
   (p.13 "B.S. designed and fabricated the SPIM-WG photonic chips"); conventional chip empty (not attributed).
5. Intracavity rings resonator.ring (D400) in xu2021, theurer2020, kumar2024: endorsed. xu2021 Vernier double-ring
   feedback circuit as reflector.other for its calculated 1.7 dB reflection loss: endorsed (D396 one row per class;
   paper: behaves like a Sagnac loop mirror, p.2; merged precedents differ: heim2025 loop_mirror, chen2023 wdm.mrr).
   The 1.7 dB is a model calculation from own fitted parameters with the coincident-resonance assumption: derived
   (D80a model extraction) accepted.
6. hassan2020 Vistec Electron Beam GmbH: D80b applies, not D395 / D84b. The contributions (p.6) name three
   fabricators at two orgs ("The fabrication was carried out by K.H., J.-A.D., and P.B."; K.H. / P.B. CEA-Leti,
   J.-A.D. Vistec Electron Beam GmbH, p.1 affiliation 2). There is no "with assistance from" wording (D395) and no
   staff thanks (D84b). Fab = org_cea_leti + Vistec Electron Beam GmbH once the org exists; not mapped to
   org_vistec_lithography (different entity, no name-only mapping). Applies to metalens_tm_fem_2p3um and
   abrupt_junction (same fabricated chip); the EMT design row stays empty.
7. Other fab rulings checked: xu2021 org_karlsruhe_institute_of_technology on the ECL and PWB only (p.10 "jointly
   fabricated by P.M. and Y.X.", both KIT; D399); RSOA and SiP chip empty (no fabricating org named). chen2025 and
   andreou2019 org_smart_photonics (MPW run, D83d). kumar2024 org_intel (Intel's own 300-mm process, all authors Intel,
   D84a). theurer2020 and maier2022 empty (no statement naming a fabricating org; maier2022 App. C names printing
   machines only).
8. Bases checked and endorsed: chen2025 output 15.4 dBm derived (own 13.3 dB link loss taken out, D394), net gain and
   edge-coupler loss author_estimate (on-chip input from a foundry-provided responsivity); maier2022 Tx means derived,
   Tx per-channel / std and all Rx values author_estimate (assumed equal VCSEL powers; datasheet PD responsivity);
   kumar2024 wafer-level medians not entered (design under test not stated, D159); andreou2019 2.32 pm/V derived from
   0.29 GHz/V at 1548.6 nm (lambda^2 df / c = 2.32 pm, checked).
