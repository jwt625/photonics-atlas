## Coordinator questions

1. chen2023b resonator-assisted IL (0.051 / 0.065 / 0.070 dB) and crosstalk (-50.1 / -58.0 / -56.2 dB): `author_estimate` or
   `derived`? Recommendation: keep `author_estimate`.
   - Eq. (1) and Eq. (5) convert Q_L to a and t through n_g L / lambda, and the paper prints neither the group index nor the ring
     circumference. The ratio of the two rings does not cancel n_g L: a_c / a_r depends on it through each ring's Q_L and T_t.
   - This is the D76 clause ("author_estimate when an input (ng) is not stated"), applied the same way in D459 (jung2026:
     derived because n_g is printed).
   - Some merged component-loss-from-Q rows are `derived` without the inputs listed in their notes
     (cmp_lit_chen2024a_sbs_ps_ring-m2, cmp_lit_zhang2026d_hex_mesh_7cell-m2, cmp_lit_jayatilleka2021_ge_implant_trimmer-m1). This
     audit did not check whether those papers print n_g and L. If the coordinator prefers `derived` (the inputs are the authors' own
     device quantities, not assumed or cited), set basis `derived` on sym45 m1 / m3 and asym_yz m1 / m3 / m4 / m6 and keep the
     unprinted-input note.
   - No op.
2. chen2023b fab. Recommendation: CityU fab (patched).
   - p.2 "we design and fabricate ..." is a first-person fabrication statement, and all authors are at City University of Hong
     Kong. That meets D80b / rule 12, as in chen2021 ("We fabricate the designed PRS") and xie2024 (CityU fab plus NFF facility).
   - The NFF facility stays.
   - Alternative: drop the 3 component ops to keep fab empty.
3. ma2023 crossing IL (< 1.8 / < 1.8 / < 2.5 dB) from subtracting the < 2.7 dB back-to-back reference. Recommendation: keep
   `derived`, scope `per_component`, as printed.
   - The subtraction is the authors' own explicit arithmetic on their measurements (D80a), and the headline values are exactly
     4.5 - 2.7 and 5.2 - 2.7.
   - A difference of two upper bounds is not a strict bound. The row note already says it is the authors' arithmetic on bounds,
     which is enough.
   - The 4-channel rows reuse the Fig. 6(d) reference value (now noted).
   - No op.
4. wu2017 excess loss in `insertion_loss_db`. Recommendation: keep.
   - For a PBS the ideal transmission to the designated port is 100 %, so EL and IL coincide.
   - The registry definition of `insertion_loss_db` names polarization devices, and `excess_loss_db` does not list polarization.
   - Merged PBS precedents store printed "excess loss" the same way: cmp_lit_fukuda2006_dc_pbs_single-m5,
     cmp_lit_fukuda2006_dc_pbs_two_stage-m3 and cmp_lit_lu2015_pbs_point_symmetric-m1.
