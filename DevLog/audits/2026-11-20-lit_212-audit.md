## Coordinator questions (recommendations)

1. lamont2008 gain basis (judgment (a)): keep `simulated` on all four gain rows. The peak output powers are outputs of the NLSE model
   ("calculated from the simulation", p.5; "estimated from simulation", Fig. 4 caption); the model is checked against the measured
   spectrum but nothing in the gain is read from data, and its inputs include calculated dispersion and a Raman spectrum from a
   different (similar) waveguide. D83f keeps the net values (simulated minus the stated coupling losses) `simulated`. `author_estimate`
   would also fit rule 4 (rests on a cited / assumed Raman input), but `simulated` says more precisely what the number is; no op.
2. lamont2008 180 nm (judgment (b)): keep `measured`, with the patched condition. The text assigns the 180 nm to the recorded spectra
   (p.6, Fig. 5(a)) and the span is visible there (signal 1550-1630 nm plus idlers down to about 1450 nm); the caption's "simulations
   suggest over 180 nm" is the model agreeing. The gain level over the span is simulation-only, which the condition now says. If the
   coordinator reads "gain bandwidth" as requiring the gain criterion to be measured, the alternative is `author_estimate`; not
   recommended (the printed number is a measured span).
3. ayan2023 CE dB -> % `derived`, reference plane not stated (judgment (c)): keep. The paper calls the values measured (p.5) and does
   not describe a coupling-loss correction of Pi or Ps; D462 (li2025a `author_estimate`) applied because every idler power there
   explicitly rests on an assumed idler coupling loss, which is not the case here. ding2022 (same idler / signal ratio, no ref plane)
   is the matching precedent. The estimated coupled pump power is a condition, not an input of the ratio. ref_plane stays empty.
4. ayan2023 Fig. 3(b) rows as the only performance values of the 2.5 um 0.5 / 1 / 1.5 m siblings (judgment (d)): keep all seven. They
   are experimental points (caption: "experimental and theoretical CE"), the markers are vector and exact to 0.05 dB, the 2 m 1.7 W
   marker reproduces the printed "around 3 dB", and without them three sibling designs would have no row (rule 9, D153). Patched: one
   value (-5.05 dB) and the notes.
5. ayan2023 2-4.7 dB/m population row (judgment (e)): keep as a separate population row. D159 covers a value whose identity is
   ambiguous between two component rows; here the paper ties the range to "our samples" of this study's Damascene spirals as a family,
   and no single FWM row is claimed (same construction as the merged jung2026 single-mode / multimode regime rows). Polarization cleared
   (patched); band stays inferred.
6. sabapathy2012 `glass` vs lamont2008 `other` (judgment (f)): keep both. Platform follows the guiding stack: a fs-laser index change
   inside bulk glass is `glass` (camus2020 BGG; 33 merged fs-written rows carry `glass`, borosilicate and fused-silica hosts alike), while a deposited and etched
   chalcogenide film rib on silica is `other` (34 merged chalcogenide-film rows, e.g. suk2025 As2S3, shen2019 GeSbS). Judgment 7
   (sabapathy2012 relative gain in gain_db with the definition in the condition, D580 precedent): keep.
