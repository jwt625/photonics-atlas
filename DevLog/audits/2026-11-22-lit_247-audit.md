## Judgment calls (distiller) and recommendations

- (a) hadijelhouati2019: keep. The expander is a wire-to-slab-beam mode converter; the back-to-back value is two of the same design
  with nothing else in the path but the slab, so `device_total` on the converter row (D163 analogue) is the honest scope; the per-device
  2.15 dB stays the primary value. The fabricated uniform tap sends a fixed fraction of the wire power into a second waveguide (the paper
  compares it with directional couplers, p.13): `splitter.other` with `split_ratio_pct` ~10 % at 1565 nm, normalized to a straight wire,
  is right; `free_space_emitter` would be wrong (in-plane, captured by a 21 um waveguide).
- (b) virot2017 second "W_Ge = 0.8 um" block -> W 1 um: keep; three independent text statements (above) fix it, and the note is on all four
  W 1 rows.
- (c) virot2017 family rows: keep (D165), derived A/cm2 -> mA/cm2, n_samples = Table 1(a) cells of the width (3 / 3 / 4 / 4). The L x 260 nm
  normalization reproduces all four means and three of four std values; W 1 std printed 0.669 vs 0.699 recomputed (patched note; keep the
  printed value).
- (d) Bandwidth wavelengths: keep 1550 nm `wavelength_inferred` for virot2017 (abstract "near 1.55 um", grating couplers at 1.55 um,
  responsivity at 1550 nm) and zhang2014b ("we report device performance at 1.55 um", p.4); keep `band: unspecified` for chen2016d and
  rosa2016 (no wavelength in either text, rule 8). virot2017 responsivity `ref_plane unspecified`: keep. 1.16 A/W at 1550 nm is 93 % quantum
  efficiency, which a fiber-referred value through a grating coupler could not reach, but the paper never says where P_in is referred;
  an inference from a physical bound is not a statement (rule 1).
- (e) chen2016d: keep all four. Junction R rests on the nominal 50 ohm cable and junction C on the simulated ~10 fF pads (`author_estimate`);
  the total C is plain arithmetic of Eq. (1) / (2) on the measured bandwidth and measured total R (`derived`; recomputed exactly). The
  RC-only model (transit time ignored, p.5) is the paper's own model choice, not an assumed input value, so it does not move the total C to
  author_estimate. 28.8 mA as `ge`: right (the paper says type C had not saturated); A and B keep `approx` ("about").
- (f) zhang2014b: Fig. 5(b) legend 1.20 / 1.74 A/W `measured` with the legend locator: keep (printed labels of linear fits of own data).
  Absorber length: change (patched). p.5 states the Set B detector is 16 um long, p.8 uses 16 um slabs for "the" device, and Set B is the
  electrically characterized structure; the identity is not ambiguous (D159 does not apply).
- (g) rosa2016: keep the split by trench / heater / pad metal, `p_pi_mw` for the switching power (pi phase shift between self-images,
  p.3), 254.2 mW with the 245.2 note (Fig. 7(a) dip near 250-260 mW, consistent), the Fig. 7 cross-port extinctions as figure reads, and the trench
  flat-heater times withheld (D159: Ti and Cu flat-heater trench devices both exist, p.7). The no-trench flat times on the Ti-pad row: keep
  as a noted judgment call. It is the only no-trench flat-heater device the paper describes, and pad metal mainly changes the power wasted in the pads (p.6-7), while the times are set by
  the heater and MMI; the weak point (a no-trench meander device with unstated pads exists, so more no-trench
  devices were made than Fig. 7 shows) is in the row note. Misses 2-5 added.
- (h) ahn2007: keep the speed device as its own unnamed row (matsuo2013 precedent, D422), PD platforms `sin` / `sion` by the feed waveguide
  (bernard2021 precedent) and empty for the 7 x 10 um and impulse rows, family-row responsivities `derived` (Eq. (1): P_in from the measured
  reference-waveguide output and the measured cutback loss, arithmetic on own measurements; D394 analogue), Fig. 3 lengths as separate rows
  with figure reads, levelled-off 1.08 / 0.96 A/W on the family rows. One fix: the 60 nA moved to the 7 x 10 um row (patched).
