## Request (2026-11-07, Wentao)

1. TSMC SiPh is nearly empty in the benchmark although NVIDIA, Lightmatter and Ayar Labs build CPO on TSMC: source TSMC
   customer and TSMC papers and proceedings and their chip performance. Second-order values count (e.g. grating
   coupler loss from the envelope of a measured spectrum).
2. Tower: some specs may be deducible from public specs of Tower customers' products (e.g. "SiLith").
3. Ingest the references of the owner's private reference collection (a separate private repo; never named in this
   public repo) into the atlas, reusing its existing extraction results (text extracts, metadata, metric tables) to
   avoid re-processing.
4. Subagents do the work; the coordinator dispatches, audits and merges.

| S4 ingest | after S1-S3: register new sources (copy cached PDFs / reuse extracts, no re-download), distill batches `cov_NN` (<= 8-10 papers), fresh-context audit per batch, serialized merges | batches, audits, merges, benchmark rebuild |

Rules for all streams: the private collection, its name, its notes, analyses, vendor or investor materials never
appear in this repo; only public publications (papers, proceedings, datasheets) and facts read from them. Values
derived from customer product specs are not literature measurements: how they enter the benchmark is a coordinator
decision after S2 (candidate: a marked `inferred_values` overlay like D910).
